Truly tiny printed text

I have a project where I have to strip in an obscene amount of legalese onto a ticket. Think 3-4 pts. It will be printed on an offset press. I know that I am asking the impossible but is there any font out there that has any chance of printed cleanly at this size? If it could be read with really strong reading glasses (or by Superman) it would be a major victory.

I am thinking condensed sans of some type. I will need a book and bold weight in both standard and italic.

The character of the font is not important other than it’s ability to be read smaller than anything should ever be printed.

tia

I recently used microprinting for a check-security feature; the text appears to be a hairline rule, but when viewed at 8x magnification it can be read clearly. I used .75 pt. Helvetica Neue 55, and ran plates at 4,000 dpi. By the way, I used to work for Siegel+Gale, who specialized in both corporate identity and legal-language simplification; the law in some states (here in the U.S.) requires that legal language must be above a certain point size for some consumer contracts (a ticket might be an example) to be binding.

Unfortunately, these specialized faces are not available for Retail license, though you might try contacting the foundries and seeing whether or not they would license for your needs. — Kent.
