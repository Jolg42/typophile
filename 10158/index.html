Anonymous @ 28 Jan 2002 — 1:14am
Today Sans is amazing. Somewhat mannered, but still extremely capable. I personally wouldn't call Cronos a copy, but I think it does "rip off" some significant innovations from Today. And it doesn't make me happier to see Adobe get a TDC award for it, not to mention a \*patent\* on it. (But they also got a patent on \*Garamond\*, so...) Here's something too: [http://cgm.cs.mcgill.ca/~luc/cronos.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414093409/http:/cgm.cs.mcgill.ca/~luc/cronos.html) \> Slimbach & Co.'s Myriad always seemed like a Frutiger "appropriation" to me And to Frutiger, from what I understand. \> Today is the most tightly-spaced sans I've ever worked with. That's actually a good thing, even -no, especially- in text setting. The letters have to meld into words (they have to become less letters). I've come to believe that contemporary standards of spacing are too loose. The metal boys couldn't space much tighter than they did - but we can. There is certainly such a thing as too-tight spacing, but Today I feel is just right. And I'll note here that the good readability of Times is due to its very tight spacing - overcoming its deficiencies (which could be attributed to its unintelligent conversion to digital) in terms of lightness and contrastiness. Tight tracking in headlines is largely a matter of taste - but in headlines, largely function. hhp
