Latinotype™ does not appear to have been registered with the US Patent and Trademark Office, but maybe it has been registered with the Chilean PTO maybe? In the U.S., ™ can be used at any time, even without federal registration, to put others on notice that you are claiming rights in the mark. ® is used when a Trademark has been officially registered after going through the required steps with the USPTO.

Janus assumed an additional «y» (and) with his comma placement but it's not there or something else to indicate it's position in the hierarchy.
