Hi Neha, you might like [Legacy](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018095246/http:/www.fonts.com/FontPackages/SuperFamilies/11-01-2004.htm). Anonymous @ 3 Dec 2009 — 7:21pm
Shinntype's [Figgins Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018095246/http:/www.shinntype.com/figgins.html) has a small x-height and large contrast between cap and lower case stem widths. You could use it with an oldstyle like Centaur or Bembo, or with [Scotch Modern](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018095246/http:/www.shinntype.com/scotch.html), which it was designed to partner.
