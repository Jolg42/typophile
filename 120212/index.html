Looks like old time poster lettering. The letter-forms are similar to Quaint Gothic from the Dickinson Type Foundry in Boston USA circa 1900. Also known as Desdemona by the K.K. Hof & K. Brendler Foundry. Digital Quaint Gothic SG [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/quaint-gothic-sg/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141009102627/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/quaint-gothic-sg/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/quaint-gothic-sg/") Also Desdemona Black [http://www.will-harris.com/store-h/desdemona.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20141009102627/http:/www.will-harris.com/store-h/desdemona.html "http://www.will-harris.com/store-h/desdemona.html") Don
