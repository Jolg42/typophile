When you say "rounded", do you mean soft ends? If you want something heavier than this, you're getting into [VAG](http://web.archive.org/web/20100417082334/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/creative_alliance/vag_rounded_std_complete_vp/) and [Frankfurter](http://web.archive.org/web/20100417082334/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/itc/frankfurter_std_virtual/) territory. Or perhaps you mean "rounded corners", in which case [Magistral](http://web.archive.org/web/20100417082334/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/paratype/magistral_extra_bold_ot/) or [FF Max](http://web.archive.org/web/20100417082334/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontfont/ff_max_ot_black/) may fit the bill. But either way, there's nothing good that is rounded, heavy, _and_ this wide.
