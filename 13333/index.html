[Matt Jalbert’s version](http://web.archive.org/web/20060604052158/http:/www.exuberance.com/type/artcraft.html) is closer, but not a match. Best of luck trying to deal with Castcraft and I have no idea if the CG font is available anywhere these days. As to the curvy tops of the letters, that is a distinctive feature of the typeface. A font without those wouldn’t be Artcraft. Surely that feature was removed by the logo maker.
