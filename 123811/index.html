Not a typeface, but hand lettering (as was most poster display type of the era). The lettering appears to be based on an old woodtype style called Latin: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/latin/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/latin/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/latin/") Some digital typefaces with a similar vibe (but by no means a match to your sample): [http://www.houseind.com/fonts/edbenguiatfonts/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056/http:/www.houseind.com/fonts/edbenguiatfonts/ "http://www.houseind.com/fonts/edbenguiatfonts/") [Ed Roman] [http://www.google.com/fonts/specimen/Fontdiner+Swanky](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056/http:/www.google.com/fonts/specimen/Fontdiner+Swanky "http://www.google.com/fonts/specimen/Fontdiner+Swanky")

AFAIK nobody has made this lettering into a font. The capital letters are fairly standard Roman letters with pulled out serifs. Fonts that include this feature include Ronaldson ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056im_/http:/typophile.com/files/RonaldsonAsTMW_5765.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/ronaldson/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/ronaldson/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/ronaldson/") and Tiffany ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056im_/http:/typophile.com/files/TiffanyMedAsTMW_5064.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/tiffany/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/tiffany/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/tiffany/") The other letters are an unusual mix of roman letters with big ball terminals and sloped letters. For some ideas see Everafter Bold ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056im_/http:/typophile.com/files/EverafterBoldAs_anho_4640.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typetrust/everafter/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typetrust/everafter/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typetrust/everafter/") and Oxtail Black ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056im_/http:/typophile.com/files/OxtailBlackAs_anho__5811.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/macrhino/oxtail/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/macrhino/oxtail/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/macrhino/oxtail/") Don
