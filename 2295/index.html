(x) AD&D, TSR, Dark Sun product line - Opti Packard {Randy}

This is the font used by the publisher of AD&D, TSR, for their 1st edition releases in the Dark Sun product line. I have found similar fonts, but none that match it precisely.

I have chosen letters for the sample that are some of the most distinctive of the font as a whole. If this still is not enough for a positive ID, I can provide a whole page of scanned text.

Good luck…I don’t think you can do it. (Just challenging you to excellence.)

OptiFonts are handled these days by [Castcraft](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026054753/http:/www.castcraft-software.com/homepage.html). Their website is, to say the least, outdated and the only part that functions usefully is the Designer Collections link. Since Opti Packard is not in any of the four Designer Collections and the Online Catalog images don't appear, you would need to contact Castcraft. Over the last few years, I've contacted them by e-mail 5 times and received a reply once. Hopefully you'll fare better. I'm sure that they could provide the font in the format of your choosing. As to which font would be most accurate, it seems likely that the OptiFont was used. Since Dieter Steffmann's version is free, you can check it out for yourself. It doesn't fare the best on-screen, so be sure to print a sample paragraph or two to see it on paper, where it looks splendid.
