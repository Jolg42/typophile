Free (GPL like), legal font license required

I want to publish a typeface I’m working on (which I hope will form part of a small family) and need to include a license. I’ve been trying to find an appropriate free license, along the lines of the GPL/copyleft which might cover any nasties/abuses happening later.

I’m aware that there are issues with GPL licenses in fonts. (See here: http://www.typophile.com/node/31738) So it needn’t be a GPL license. I just can’t find an appropriate, simple license text specific to font distribution.

Essentially it needs to cover my free distribution, future free distribution (license intact) and my copyright.

Anyone with a similar license they use?

\* The [FreeType license](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/freetype.sourceforge.net/FTL.TXT) was designed as a license for the [FreeType](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/www.freetype.org/) rendering engine (a software library) not as a license for fonts. \* Recently [Bruce Perens](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/perens.com/about/introduction/) in his blog article [Warning: SIL Open Font License](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/perens.com/blog/2009/02/17/64/) has expressed some concern about SIL's [Open Font License](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/scripts.sil.org/OFL) claiming: _"There appears to be an unintended loophole that would allow the conversion of any font under the license to public domain."_ \* There is a " [Font Exception](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html#FontException)" which can be tagged onto the end of the [GPL](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/www.gnu.org/copyleft/gpl.html) to make it suitable for fonts ~ however the author of that subsequently wrote an article on [Font Licensing and the GPL](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015164046/http:/www.fsf.org/blogs/licensing/20050425novalis) in which he expressed some reservations. - Chris
