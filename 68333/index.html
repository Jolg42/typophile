Hi Dösi, in addition to Dan’s article (which omits the Kurrent genre, the script equivalent of _Gebrochene Schriften_ – I suppose deliberately, as it never was significant in typography), there’s also the classification that [Judith Schalansky](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Judith%20Schalansky) uses in her [Fraktur Mon Amour](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/typographica.org/2010/typography-books/fraktur-mon-amour-2nd-edition/) – basically a similar approach, with a finer differentiation of experimental (display) styles, see [this overview PDF [3.8mb]](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/www.fontblog.de/picts/FrakturMonAmour.pdf) by Fontblog.de. As you speak German, see also the [TypoWiki](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/www.typografie.info/typowiki/index.php?title=Gebrochene_Schrift), [Wolfgang Beinert](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Wolfgang%20Beinert)’s [Typolexikon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/www.typolexikon.de/g/gebrochene-schriften.html), and [this article](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/www.beepworld.de/members66/vau-ef-be/gebrochene.htm) by [Bernhard Schnelle](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Bernhard%20Schnelle). Offline reading recommendation: [Fraktur](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/www.typografie.de/product_info.php?products_id=389&cPath=) by [Albert Kapr](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Albert%20Kapr). <cite>a guide out there that breaks up blackletter forms to it's very basic elements</cite> Do you mean a list of form details like the [Elefantenrüssel](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015081207/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/elsnerflake/neue_luthersche_fraktur_ot/?&sample_text=ABFLMNPRVWY&update=0) [‘elephant's trunk’]? I’m not aware of such a guide. F
