EDITED: Looks like an outdoor sign. My guess is that it's not a font as such, but rather sign-maker's lettering. The effect could be duplicated with a fairly heavy, monoline, boxy or gas-pipe style wide sans. For example Valfle Extended ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424032557im_/http:/typophile.com/files/ValfleExtended_MHS_5668.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/suitcase/vafle/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424032557/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/suitcase/vafle/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/suitcase/vafle/") I trust a kindly typophile could recommend something that is a better match. For the \_M\_ and \_H\_ paste on small Clarendon style serifs. As for the the \_S\_ the paste could consist of the serif and terminal treatment from any of several "wild west" wood type designs. Good choices include Jeff Levine's Saddle Tramp JNL ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424032557im_/http:/typophile.com/files/SaddleTrampJNL_S_5551.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/saddle-tramp/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424032557/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/saddle-tramp/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/saddle-tramp/") Equally suitable, Blackoak ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424032557im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Blackoak_S_6709.gif) already suggested by Kevin. In both of these cases some tinkering would be needed to get the terminal to match. Don
