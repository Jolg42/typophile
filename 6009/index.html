Anonymous @ 10 Aug 2004 — 8:54am
This is actually a semi-serif... Good for you - we need those! It reminds me a little bit of Costa (my fav Porchez font, finally released for retail sale - yay!) but it's certainly original nonetheless. I'm actually liking it a lot - but it needs work. The even tone is nice - it's an honest, but sensitive design. And the stroke terminals are great. Lowercase: - The "a" is obnoxious, sorry. It has the worst of Gill Sans, and that Jannon imbalance to boot. - I don't think there's a good reason to give the "f" a descender. - The "g" is nice - maybe give the head more size versus the tail - the glyph looks way too small compared to the rest. - I think the "k" needs some of the bowing. - I'm unsure about the "w". - The "x" needs some of the bowing too, and maybe the "z" as well. The caps are really nice. - The "L" and "T" seem too wide. - Consider bowing in the "K", "X", "Y" and "Z". - The "S" needs more balance and polish. Others: - Interesting numerals. The "3" is too much though. - I like the 3rd ampersand. A lot. - Superb "at" sign. - Flatten the UC accents. - The acute and grave seem a hair too strong. Spacing: it needs work, but with some tightening I think the wordspace you have is good here. -- One thing about the pairing with the serif version though - I don't see them as compatible designs at all - certainly not sister types. hhp
