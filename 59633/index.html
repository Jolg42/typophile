Here is what I did last year in your shoes ;-) (not including the Latin groups…) One group for all Deva glyphs One for all the Deva conjuncts One for all the Deva ligatures One group for each of the full consonant forms and their derivatives (i.e., one group would include the Ba, the BRa and Bba) One group for all the full Vattu forms One group for all the half forms Another for all the half Vattu forms (I actually did this group twice…) Then a group for the above the base marks Another for the below the base marks I think that is it! But you have my VOLT project file, so you can take a closer look.
