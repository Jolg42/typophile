It’s well done, Chuck, and I agree with a few of the comments above. My small suggestions is to watch out for the very slight downward decline of the optical baseline. In handwriting styled faces and logos a downward arch or baseline connotes depression and pessimism by handwriting analysis. You can improve the subliminal signal by giving it a slight incline, or raise the last character “n”, or by slightly rotating the entire logo counterclockwise by just a few degrees. This is less of a pure design critique and more about the power of creating symbols and marks.
