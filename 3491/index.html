Daniel, Up yer scuppers with the "fake antique" offense, avoid anchors at all cost. Done to death. Since this is a "one off" it is well suited for letterpress. A "one off " on a Vandercook can give you superior results without inhaling reams of paper. I recommend fine hand made paper, or a pedigree watercolor stock. Good laundry is always important. Something acid free. Remember�no acid. Remember what happens to photographs. Have it set by hand, or set on by Monotype. Since you are in Melbourne talk to these fine folk. [http://home.vicnet.net.au/~typo/collect/typemach/monocomp.htm](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518124024/http:/home.vicnet.net.au/~typo/collect/typemach/monocomp.htm) Pick a typeface, probably Caslon Oldstyle, if they have the matrices. [http://lanstontype.com/CaslonFountEight.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518124024/http:/lanstontype.com/CaslonFountEight.html) Talk to bookbinder, see what he can do with tradtional sailmaker's cloth, and twine. Avoid gold stamping. Quarter bound with natural leather, sailmakers cloth. If your binder has a problem with it, talk to your sailmaker. Use the right materials, keep it understated, don't take it too far, don't get corny. I am a bit of an "old gaffer" myself. You want to hoist some sails?
