Current Sans Serif favs

Hello,
Interesting in knowing what are your current favorites for contemporary (humanist, geometric, or gothic) san-serif typefaces might be. Usage could be anything, but primarily wondering what faces you’ve purchased in the last 3 or so years that you like and why.

Here’s mine:

• Bryant Pro: Nice contemporary friendly face. Very clean on and off screen

• Galaxy Polaris: Great workhorse for the studio, but a little limited. Basically our helvetica.

• Stratum: Good ultra modern font, that straddles a nice line between ultra-tech but not overly so.

Feel free to broaden the criteria for selection.

Cheers,
Peter.

Well you stole a couple of mine (Bryant and Galaxy Polaris), but here are some more recent favorites: **[Amplitude](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=amplitude)** by [Christian Schwartz](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Christian%20Schwartz) After three years, it's still my favorite workhorse. Amplitude looks right in almost any setting — clean and readable at text sizes, vibrant and powerful in headlines. With an inktrapless version and italics on the way, soon you'll be able to use Amplitude for anything. **[FF Strada](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_strada_text/) and [FF Strada Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_strada_condensed/)** by [Albert Pinggera](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Albert%20Pinggera) Its warm curves can make it friendly if it needs to be, but you can use it to set serious text too. Wonderfully readable at small sizes, unique detail when large. One of few contemporary typefaces drawn by an Italian. **[Maquette](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/arstype/maquette/)** by [Angus Shamal](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Angus%20Shamal) A new large x-heighted alternative in the grotesque style of [Gotham](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Gotham) and [Proxima Nova](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174807/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Proxima%20Nova).
