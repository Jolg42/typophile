Mainly because of /M and even if /R leg is wrong, I would have started with [Eurostile](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317170328/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/Eurostile), adding a leg to /P to recreate /R, moving /E crossbar downward and cutting the whole thing with a horizontal bar. Interesting alternatives: [Francker](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317170328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/francker), [QType](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317170328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/ff-qtype), [Clonoid](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317170328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/clonoid), [Olney](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317170328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/philatype/olney), [Kursk 105](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317170328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/talbot/kursk-105), [Geom Graphic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317170328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/geom-graphic) EDIT: ...Or you rely on Fivos' expertise... Definitely Borg 9 which actually looks definitely coming from Eurostile.
