WARBY PARKER: can't find a perfect match and even with a [better sample](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/myfonts-wtf.s3.amazonaws.com/33/3315c6e0d665d87a66154cc7c66758bf.33234.png); it's something close to [Gotham](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/gotham/overview)/ [Proxima Nova](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/marksimonson/proxima-nova) (used on their website)/ [Neutraface No.2](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.houseind.com/fonts/neutraface2) ( [Neutraface](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.houseind.com/fonts/neutraface) used in shops) but slightly rounded; I'm aware of [Gotham Rounded](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/gotham-rounded/overview) and [Proxima Nova Soft](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/marksimonson/proxima-nova-soft) but they don't match either... More alternatives: [Arquitecta](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/latinotype/arquitecta), [Celias](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typedynamic/celias) eyewear: sorry but really too small for my eyes... I know they're using [Utopia](http://web.archive.org/web/20140916034105/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/utopia) Display (and Proxima Nova) on their website. At this size, it could the job even if I'm quite convinced it's not the right one.
