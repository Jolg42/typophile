Hi there: Bodoni is inherently beautiful. However if your going to choose a 'classical' Bodoni type, the Bitstream Bauer Bodoni is widely accepted one of the most elegant. For a modern spin on Bodoni, Emigre's Filosophia is a most fabulous choice. Read this article to help you make up your mind: [http://www.fonthaus.com/xheight/bodoni.cfm](http://web.archive.org/web/20131020165626/http:/www.fonthaus.com/xheight/bodoni.cfm "http://www.fonthaus.com/xheight/bodoni.cfm") Those are my pix. Hope I have helped you. Mikey :-)
