Anonymous @ 30 Jan 2013 — 10:01am
You don't need to do anything but assigning the correct Unicode values to your characters. InDesign, Illustrator, and all other type related software worth its salt are oblivious of any of the encodings you mention. The reason is that these "encodings" are meant as a Unicode-to-8 bit table translation. What will happen when you select one of them, is that FontLab will re-order your _Unicode_ characters into the encoding specified, delete the rest (?) and export only the characters in that specific encoding. Is it required to select an encoding in FontLab? If so, choose something sounding like "Unicode (BMP)".
