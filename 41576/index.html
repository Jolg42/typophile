Hi Jürgen, it’s [Whitney](http://web.archive.org/web/20080203163634/http:/typography.com/fonts/font_overview.php?productLineID=100026) by [Tobias Frere-Jones](http://web.archive.org/web/20080203163634/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Tobias%252BFrere-Jones), identified in [this older thread](http://web.archive.org/web/20080203163634/http:/www.typophile.com/node/19127). You can always [run a search](http://web.archive.org/web/20080203163634/http:/www.google.com/search?q=site:typophile.com%20visa) if you know what you’re looking for. For your second question: There’s [a list of Corporate fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20080203163634/http:/www.typografie.info/typowiki/index.php?title=Hausschrift-Liste_Unternehmen-zu-Schrift) at German Typografie.info. An older Typophile thread mentions several [corporate initiated typeface designs](http://web.archive.org/web/20080203163634/http:/www.typophile.com/node/27315) And there’s the [Website about Corporate Identity](http://web.archive.org/web/20080203163634/http:/users.ncrvnet.nl/mstol/index.html) by Hans Stol with a lot of useful information.
