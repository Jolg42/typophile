Maybe a food-service professional would recognize what the picture is, but to me it's too abstract--just a corner with the four little round things. The color scheme and the typography say _clean_ and _high-tech_, but they don't say _food_. I get kind of a biotech or pharmaceutical vibe from this, although that's partly because the name _Univerzal_ sounds like a prescription drug. I realize they won't let you change the name of the company. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20121128170355im_/http:/typophile.com/files/aramark_5373.jpg) Here's the logo of one of the biggest food-service outfits in the US, Aramark (possibly somebody Univerzal would like as a client). They've been using this logo for 15 or 20 years, so it's not exactly on the cutting edge, but it manages to look modern and techie without looking cold and antiseptic.
