Anna Giedryś' [Signika](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317054140/http:/www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/signika) Quoting R. from [thos post](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317054140/http:/typophile.com/node/117175#comment-580410) > Here’s one handy thing to know: If you want to identify a webfont, you can select the text that uses it, right-click on your selection and then select ‘Inspect Element’ from the context menu (in Firefox and Chrome). The font name will be shown on the right-hand side in the panel at the bottom of the screen. You might want to click on the tab ‘Fonts’ in Firefox or ‘Computed’ in Chrome for a neater overview. Other handy option for Chrome user only: Chrome [WhatFont](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317054140/http:/chengyinliu.com/whatfont.html) app by Chengyin Liu. Click the toolbar app icon and simply hover html text to reveal the webfont name.
