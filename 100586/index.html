Some opinions: - Are you sure it's a good idea to use DIN for the text? Is there not a lot of text? - Caslon and Perpetua are very old-fashioned. Mrs Eaves is more interesting, but it's horribly spaced, so you would need to manually respace every single setting. - I don't know what "squiggy" means. :-) - Instead of extreme contrast between text and titles I might aim for more harmony, choosing a squarish face for the titles (maybe Melior, or preferably something more recent). But first make sure you want to use DIN. hhp

Frutiger’s [Egyptienne F](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417091314/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/egyptienne-f/) may be a good serif match for DIN, but unlike Melior (which I love) it doesn’t look so old-fashioned, at least to me. And I second Hrant, you may want to consider using the serif face for text and DIN for the headlines.
