A commercially available match may be impossible. GQ is known for commissioning proprietary type. Consider [ITC Bailey](http://web.archive.org/web/20080519014621/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontpackage&startrow=1&records=&currpage=1&totalrecords=4&displayfontid=ITC.10354.0.0&fontRendererText=Men+of+the+Year) (similar proporitions) or [Torus](http://web.archive.org/web/20080519014621/http:/www.psyops.com/html/spec_torus.html) (angled stroke endings).
