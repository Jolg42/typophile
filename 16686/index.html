looks cool. here's my two cents: the S looks like it needs to be heavier and wider at the bottom. your E seems upside-down and the inside stroke is a little thin. The B is looking a little small (could be a bit wider). to make it more balanced, you might want to match the counters of the B to the those of the O,D,N, and G. a good resource (that someone recently told me about) is: [type basics](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021020930/http:/typeworkshop.com/index.php?id1=type-basics) -------------- nc
