Scale of image too small to comment on details but it seems that terminals have been clipped, perhaps from a Caslon derived font. The particular clipped g is found in a few fonts. For example, the Rita Smith font by Claude Pelletier [http://www.dafont.com/ritasmith.font?fpp=50&text=%26g](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804095734/http:/www.dafont.com/ritasmith.font?fpp=50&text=%26g "http://www.dafont.com/ritasmith.font?fpp=50&text=%26g") Claude based his font on an old design, Primavera of Rita Major. The Walmart retail chain also uses a clipped g for its George label: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804095734im_/http:/typophile.com/files/george21_4439.jpg) As for the & it looks like 8 with a tail.
