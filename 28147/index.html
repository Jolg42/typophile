Do you have Arial installed? If it isn’t installed, IE (in Windows at least) will take the next installed font... in alphabetical order. It’s kind of strange though, since Google specifies the general sans serif if Arial isn’t installed, and in your case that should be Helvetica. Arial is actually kind of nice on-screen, abominable as it may be in print. Just goes to show how different online and print design really is.
