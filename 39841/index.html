Here are four letters that look like a reversed h: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709010152im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/revh_3735.gif) [**uni0265**](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709010152/http:/www.fileformat.info/info/unicode/char/0265/index.htm) is “latin small letter turned H”. [**uni03BC**](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709010152/http:/www.fileformat.info/info/unicode/char/03bc/index.htm) is “greek small letter mu”. [**uni0427**](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709010152/http:/www.fileformat.info/info/unicode/char/0427/index.htm) is “cyrillic capital letter che” and [**uni0447**](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709010152/http:/www.fileformat.info/info/unicode/char/0447/index.htm) is “cyrillic small letter che”, and there may be others. The above font is Gentium. Michel
