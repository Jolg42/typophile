Anonymous @ 28 Feb 2007 — 1:24am
Postscript Autohinting is far away from perfect. If you take a look at some other fonts you notice that these kind of problems are almost always there, more or less anyway. I’ve experimented a lot with this lately and found out that if you only autohint the vertical stems, it can sometimes can work very good – you get sharper appearance on screen but this typical horizontal distorsion is not there (when letters jump up and down). Take a look at this thread for more hinting stuff.
