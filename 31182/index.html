I like it a lot. I doesn't shove the book reference in your face as much. And I like your old check-out ticket - I think we were the last generation of students to have those. I think it blends the contemporary black-and-white with the old-school very well. My only concerns are: The T in the headline because it's so decorative, I found it kind of distracting against the other characters that you used. And some of the "stamped" characters are hard to read because they are too light (presumably because they don't have enough ink on them). But other than that, I like it a lot. -Scott
