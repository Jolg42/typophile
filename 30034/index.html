If, on the front of the card, you have only the word CANUS printed in caps, why not only capitalize CANUS in the first 2 lines across the back? That way you would avoid both the gap between the 2 currently capitalized words (which is drawing too much attention) and the issue with the Sl combination. I’m assuming you’re aware of the spacing issue after the (0) in the phone number. And why “dot” in the email & web addresses? Why not just use periods? You may be stuck with (at) if you’re setting metal, but there’s no reason not to use periods here (other than to be “different”). Also, while this may simply be due to the blurry photographs, the inking on these looks a bit muddy; it looks like all of the “e”, “a” and “4” counters are filled in, etc.
