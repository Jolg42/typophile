For body copy, it might be nice to recall the traditional “schoolbook” type of vintage children’s books: [Plantin Schoolbook](http://web.archive.org/web/20071102112341/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/monotype/plantin_schoolbook/) [ParaType Schoolbook](http://web.archive.org/web/20071102112341/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/paratype/schoolbook_ot/) [Century Schoolbook](http://web.archive.org/web/20071102112341/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/bitstream/century_schoolbook_ot/) [Bembo Schoolbook](http://web.archive.org/web/20071102112341/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/monotype/bembo_schoolbook/) - a bit more formal
