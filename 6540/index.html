I'm no expert on pixel fonts, but I'd say that in your original concept, either your e's are top-heavy, your s's are bottom-heavy, or both. (The middle of the word "hopelessly" emphasizes the disparity.) Just looking at the set, it looks like the font wants to be a bit top-heavy - you didn't rethink the o, and most of the other letters seem to be ambivalent. The word "six" makes it look like your s is upside- down, too. I'd keep the old c for a bit, switch to the middle e, and try to redesign the s to match them as much as possible. Then let's see another sample. It's always hard to judge a letter in isolation. As for your second font, maybe a second thread, just so things don't get confused?
