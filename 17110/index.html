Extended Deadline — TDC52 and TDC2 2006 competitions

Call for Entries
TDC52 & TDC2 2006
The annual typography and type design exhibition competitions of the Type Directors Club

TDC52 typography

Jury: Andy Altman, Why Not Associates; Mike Joyce, Stereotype Design; Alexa Nosal, Leap; Emily Oberman, Number Seventeen; Woody Pirtle, Pentagram Design;
Steve Sandstrom, Sandstrom Design; Brady Vest, Hammerpress; Diego Vainesman, Chairman

TDC2 2006 type design

Jury: Frank Blokland, Dutch Type Library; Yvonne Diedrich, ydt_fonts; Mark Jamra, TypeCulture; Teri Kahan, Teri Kahan Design; Ilene Strizver, Chair

Extended Deadline is Friday 13 January 2006
Entries from 1 January 2005 to 31 December 2005
(Full guidelines & PDFs at the URLs above)

Entries selected by the juries will be on display at the exhibition opening in New York in the summer of 2006, exhibited in the TDC travelling exhibitions, and published in _Typography 27_, the Annual of the Type Directors Club.
