The case made for flush left (aka ragged right, if I am not mistaken) is twofold: 1. A consistent beginning of each line, which aids continuation after the end of a line 2. Identical wordspaces, which facilitates reading by consistency (the eye [brain] knows when the next word starts and how much to ‘jump’ to the next word) In your example both points can not be satisfied, hence these are less legible than flush left.
