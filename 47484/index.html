All the cemeteries in Berlin are a treat. There are several along the base of Prenzlauer Berg, just outside of Mitte (a short walk from Alexanderplatz). Kreuzberg has a massive multi-cemetery complex from behind the Bergmanstraße onwards. The design bookstore fave seems to be Bücherbogen on Savignyplatz. I quite like the museums, but I wouldn’t say that they are quite typographic. Although current branding and ads for the Bode Museum and the Pergamon Museum are. There are a number of monuments with inscriptional lettering to see, including the memorial to Soviet troops in Treptower Park as well as a memorial to WWII Polish partisans in the Friedrichshain Volkspark.
