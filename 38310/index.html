Adobe Press’s _Classroom in a Book_ series is always a good investment, but their [Illustrator book](http://web.archive.org/web/20081204090928/http:/www.adobe.com/training/books/illustrator.html) does not focus exclusively on type or logos. Leslie Cabarga has a new book called [_The Secret Life of Logos_](http://web.archive.org/web/20081204090928/http:/www.fwbookstore.com/product/1619/how), but I think it is more about designing a good logo rather than how to draw one in Illustrator.
