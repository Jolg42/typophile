Well, in German, Typography can actually be writen one of many diffent ways. In his 1998 book, _Kurtstexte_, Kurt Weidemann writes the following about Typo and spelling (please forgive me, this is both a translation and a mental summary): _Tipografie_ would refer to any kind of typography that involves someone typing, i.e., a compositor at his keyboard, or a DTPer, but not an author with Microsoft Word (necessarially). _Typographie_ refers to the New Typography of the 1920s. Mostly because this is how Jan Tschichold spelled the word then. _Typografie_ is the "in" spelling of the word a the moment. It is also the correct spelling, according to _die neue deutsche Rechtschreibung_, I think. It implies the contemporary streamlinization of Typography, and is spelled just as it sounds (at least, that is what a German would tell you!).
