It's called "Marble Heart", and it was an ATF typeface, based on an 1870s type called "Gothic Double Shade" from the Boston Type Foundry (which was an ATF predecessor). this info comes from Mac McGrew's great book on American Metal Typefaces. It is also shown in the Solotype Catalog & the Phil's Photo "Homage to the Alphabet" book, which means it made it into film type. There is a similar style called Orleans Open that was digitized by FontBank as "OldWest", but I do not think I know of a digital verson of Marble Heart. Maybe Mark Simonson or Mike F. would know. - Mike Yanega

Marble Heart was recently digitized for posterity. It is m-u-c-h older than McGrew thought: A specimen appears in the Hænel catalog of 1847! According to Bauer, Hænel imported roman types from GB and France. Since Nicolette Gray does not discuss it, it was probably French: [http://forums.typeheritage.com/topic/marble-heart/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420022543/http:/forums.typeheritage.com/topic/marble-heart/ "http://forums.typeheritage.com/topic/marble-heart/") [http://www.flickr.com/photos/51266334@N08/sets/72157635201975897/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420022543/http:/www.flickr.com/photos/51266334@N08/sets/72157635201975897/ "http://www.flickr.com/photos/51266334@N08/sets/72157635201975897/") --Anna

Hi Don, The original mission of The Type Heritage Project [THP] was to publish a series of textbooks illustrating at least 1,500 already-existing digital revivals of display types originating c1800-WWI: [http://typeheritage.com/textbooks/overview/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420022543/http:/typeheritage.com/textbooks/overview/ "http://typeheritage.com/textbooks/overview/") In the planning process, I realized the importance of preserving as many of these gems as possible--before maintainable letterpress equipment becomes any scarcer! The THP forum website recruits, coordinates and supports volunteer partners committed to THP's second mission: [http://forums.typeheritage.com/undigitized/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420022543/http:/forums.typeheritage.com/undigitized/ "http://forums.typeheritage.com/undigitized/") So far, THP partners have produced hundreds of new revivals: [http://forums.typeheritage.com/status/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420022543/http:/forums.typeheritage.com/status/ "http://forums.typeheritage.com/status/") "Margarethe" is a revival of Marble Heart including a super-rare lower case perhaps not shown by ATF. It was renamed because I must [reluctantly!] advise revival developers to do so: [http://forums.typeheritage.com/topic/legalities/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420022543/http:/forums.typeheritage.com/topic/legalities/ "http://forums.typeheritage.com/topic/legalities/") Later this year, THP will launch an eCommerce font boutique specializing in revivals and others drawn from historical materials. A semi-functional model may be previewed here: [http://www.vixemporium.com/fontcart/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420022543/http:/www.vixemporium.com/fontcart/ "http://www.vixemporium.com/fontcart/") Until then, these fonts are not available unless the partners decide to offer them elsewhere. Anna
