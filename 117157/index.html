This Italian lettering reminded me of the underlying letterforms of the Ferrari logo, which has been made into a font by Michael Hagmann as Ferro Rosso [http://www.dafont.com/font-comment.php?file=ferro\_rosso&text=P+I+A+N+O+F...](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724113339/http:/www.dafont.com/font-comment.php?file=ferro_rosso&text=P+I+A+N+O+F+O+R+T+I "http://www.dafont.com/font-comment.php?file=ferro\_rosso&text=P+I+A+N+O+F+O+R+T+I") Clip the length of the serifs and automotive styling overlapping bars, add a few serifs where they are missing and it would look fairly close if you edited the leg on R. Presumably if there is a closer matching font one of the other font detectives will tell you about it. Don
