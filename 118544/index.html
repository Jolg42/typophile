Occasionally a crossbarless \_A\_ might suggest that a word has ancient roots in Greek. But contemporary readers who recognize Greek letters are almost non existant. So usually a word with a crossbarless \_A\_ suggests the opposite of ancient. Perhaps "ad Astra" the face of the future. In any case, the crossbarless \_A\_ is now such a design cliché that I would be reluctant to use it for anything that does not have an ultra sleek, high tech or futuristc character. Even there, it's in the "been there, done that" category. Don
