10 Comments

I think it's a good project; there's a certain 'genericity' in certain characters (BPRf, for example) but I guess it's just a matter of tweaking the curves, etc. There's a lot of inconsistencies in color (weight); and overall it seems a bit too light (specially the thins). You're right about the bdpq. The bowls need more weight. Other comments: - O: the inner shape looks 'squarish'; - Q: tail is too odd and thin; - W: that white shape in the middle (where the two strokes cross each other) is too small, enlarge it or remove it; - X: top heavy; - g: extremely odd shape for this typeface; - y: it needs the inner left serif (IMHO).
