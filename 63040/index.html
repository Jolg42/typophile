@ Rachna: Here's a little trick when you've got a font that is close but not quite exact. Goto 'identifont.com' and enter 'Albertus' on the home page where it says 'Find a font'. When the detail shows click the 'Show more similar fonts...' link. In the left hand column a list will pop up that should be similar to the font you're looking at. This feature isn't available for every font but when it is it can come in very handy.
