Starting by having a look to [Typecache Font Cluster: Stencil Serif/Slab Serif Fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/typecache.com/font-clusters/06/) could be helpful. None of them are actually close. They share only "serif+stencil" specifications. Dala Floda is an original design and deserve its price. Anyway, here's a selection: [Caslon Fina Stencil](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/applyinteractive/caslon-fina-stencil/), [Avia](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/avia/), [Nadia](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typeinnovations/nadia/), [Typonine Stencil Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typonine/ty-stencil-pro/), [Q-Stencil](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.playtype.com/font/q-stencil), [Sensaway](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/gestalten/sensaway_pro_heavy/), [Stencil Two](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.tendollarfonts.com/product/stencil-two-typeface), [DeVinne Stencil](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415100537/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/de-vinne-stencil/)
