Good mornin, I don’t know about a tutorial, but may i suggest messing around with the envelope distort option in the object menu. You have the option to make with warp, or to make with mesh. The warp gives you some basic shapes that it’ll distort to, which you can then mess with afterwards by using the white arrow to pull individual points on the mesh. However make sure you choose the colour of the text first as you’ll be unable to do so afterwaards without expanding the object. Good luck. Graham
