Check out your local big box book store and/or craft store and ask for the ’Dover books’ section where you’ll find all sorts of books full of scanable clip art based on old engravings like this. Online, you can check out the Briar Press’s archive: [http://www.briarpress.org/cuts](http://web.archive.org/web/20100106212703/http:/www.briarpress.org/cuts "http://www.briarpress.org/cuts")
