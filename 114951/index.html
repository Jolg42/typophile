May I suggest my own Proza? It was initially intended as a sans-serif companion to Garamond, and still shares its humanist skeleton. You can read all about it here: [http://ilovetypography.com/2013/11/01/making-fonts-proza-typeface/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804005304/http:/ilovetypography.com/2013/11/01/making-fonts-proza-typeface/ "http://ilovetypography.com/2013/11/01/making-fonts-proza-typeface/") For similar proportions you might try this: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/jannon-sans/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804005304/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/jannon-sans/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/jannon-sans/") It's based on Jannon, rather than Garamond, but it's probably the closest of a match you'll find. Good luck!
