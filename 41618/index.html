Well I'd personally avoid woodcarver (assuming you're talking about [ICG Carver](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415023031/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/imageclub/carver/)). It doesn't fit the mold of old letterpressed woodtype and it's pretty illegible at any size. I think [Corner Bakery](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415023031/http:/www.cornerbakery.com/) does a decent job of incorporating different faces for a rustic quasi-show card feel. Perhaps combine Rosewood & Rosewood Fill with some other woodtype faces like [Brothers](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415023031/http:/emigre.com/EF.php?fid=83), [Council](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415023031/http:/emigre.com/EF.php?fid=86), or some of the [Adobe woodtypes](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415023031/http:/store1.adobe.com/cfusion/store/html/index.cfm?store=OLS-US&event=searchFonts&type=category&cat=theme&code=old-west)
