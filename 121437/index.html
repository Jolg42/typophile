BAKERY is interesting. Some letters are almost a perfect match for Abril Titling Bold but the bowls on \_B\_ and \_R\_ are squared in a style developed by Imre Reiner as Corvinus in 1929-34. So what changes to Abril would produce an alphabet that was consistent with BAKERY? Consider Abril Titling Bold BRSPDCG ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417025058im_/http:/typophile.com/files/AbrilTitlingBoldAsBAKERY_4110.gif) Compared to Corvinius BRSPDCG Skyline ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417025058im_/http:/typophile.com/files/CorviniusSkylineAsBAKERY_6588.gif) Don

BAKERY. Another squared bowls font, designed by by Wolff Olins in 1978 for Renault automobiles. The matching weight is somewhere between Renault Medium ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417025058im_/http:/typophile.com/files/RenaultMedAsBAKE_5097.gif) abd Renault Bold ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417025058im_/http:/typophile.com/files/RenaultBoldAsBAKE_4379.gif) Various foundries including [https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/renault/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417025058/https:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/renault/ "https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/renault/") Don
