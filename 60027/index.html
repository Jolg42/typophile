It never had a resize grip, that's why you can't change it back. Attach another panel at the bottom that _has_ a resize grip (drag it by its label towards the bottom of the character panel until you see a blue line, then release). Although you can change the width of the character panel now, with some experimentation I still can't make the new width "stick". As soon as I drag ways the attached panel it snaps back to default width. If that doesn't work, you can always try resetting the program preferences.
