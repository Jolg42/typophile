For the artists names, I'm probably wrong and even if it sounds weird, I see Arial, distorted a bit. For title, I'd have said something close/like Alternate Gothic but many details are off and mainly /J over curved terminal. Best one I could get is [Knockout](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317183327/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/knockout/overview) (from No. 66 to 68) using the alternate /R (as seen [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317183327/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/knockout/characters/knockoutno-68fullfeatherweight)).
