There are a couple of odd features to these letters, but the one that struck me most is that the foot serifs are off-center. I searched with 'tammi' as the sample, using 'slabserif' at MyFonts, and went through all 340+ fonts, and the only one I noticed there with off-center foot-serifs was [Corzinair](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018221229/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/corzinair/) from Typodermic. As you can see, it's close, but not exact. This says it's not a foundry that MyFonts sells, or it's a custom font. - Mike Yanega
