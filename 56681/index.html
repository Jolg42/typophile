Musing about a Russian Vyaz font

I was toying around with an idea of a new Vyaz’ font for a while now, but the sheer mind-boggling amount of ligatures and heavy scripting required to accommodate a proper and versatile face seemed unmanageable. Thus, I finally decided that a simple vector file would be a better alternative, with a basic modular design, a handful of letter alternatives, ligatures and decorative elements, providing the user with the freedom to construct their own combinations. here are the first sketches of this Idea:

Anonymous @ 17 Apr 2009 — 7:38am
Victor, _[vyaz’](http://web.archive.org/web/20150401064707/http:/typophile.com/node/14390)_ (_вязь_) is an [ancient Cyrillic calligraphy style](http://web.archive.org/web/20150401064707/http:/www.paratype.com/help/term/terms.asp?code=369). There was [an earlier discussion](http://web.archive.org/web/20150401064707/http:/typophile.com/node/14377) of _vyaz’_ on typophile.com, in view of the advanced typographic features of the OpenType technology (contextual glyph substitution on the fly). As to [_Gzhel’_](http://web.archive.org/web/20150401064707/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gzhel) and [_Khokhlomà_](http://web.archive.org/web/20150401064707/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khokhloma), those are traditional folk techniques of pottery-making and decorating, and painting wooden tableware, named after the villages they originated in.
