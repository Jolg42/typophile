A great font manager is "Font Suit" from Iseatech - [www.iseasoft.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20101227122027/http:/www.iseasoft.com/ "www.iseasoft.com") I use it myself. On restarting the computer or closing the software, the font is deactivated. So its slightly bothersome having to keep fontsuit open while working with its fonts.
