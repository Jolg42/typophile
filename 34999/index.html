In case it helps your research, this type of serif is called “Latin” (although often this includes serifs that don’t have totally sharp ends, like Azuza\*). BTW, a good example is Saracen: [http://www.typography.com/catalog/saracen/index.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20080430120106/http:/www.typography.com/catalog/saracen/index.html "http://www.typography.com/catalog/saracen/index.html") \* See the description here: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/parkinson/azuza/](http://web.archive.org/web/20080430120106/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/parkinson/azuza/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/parkinson/azuza/") Latin serif fonts were big in France in the second half of the 19th century. hhp
