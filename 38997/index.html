The O (oh) and 0 (zero) look virtually the same. Depending on what you're intending it for this might be immaterial, but if you want people to be able to code with it, one of the glyphs needs something to distinguish it (like a center dot or a slash). Maybe as an OpenType alternate, as slashed zeros often are?
