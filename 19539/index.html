Anonymous @ 11 May 2008 — 8:22pm
The New York is custom lettering that has been tweaked and refined over the years by, among others, Milton Glaser and Ed Benguiat. Here's a discussion between Roger Black and former NY mag AD Luke Hayman that mentions the evolution of the logo: [http://www.rogerblack.com/blog/conversation\_with\_hayman](http://web.archive.org/web/20130329131400/http:/www.rogerblack.com/blog/conversation_with_hayman "http://www.rogerblack.com/blog/conversation\_with\_hayman") The condensed sans serif is the now-retired Elderkin from Process Type Foundry: [http://www.processtypefoundry.com/help/updates.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20130329131400/http:/www.processtypefoundry.com/help/updates.html "http://www.processtypefoundry.com/help/updates.html") You can see a picture here, although the download link is dead: [http://www.fontfinder.ws/12204/Elderkin-Medium.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20130329131400/http:/www.fontfinder.ws/12204/Elderkin-Medium.html "http://www.fontfinder.ws/12204/Elderkin-Medium.html")
