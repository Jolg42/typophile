DVD release date change, NYC run still going, more screenings

Submitted by paul d hunt : 31.Oct.2007 12.20pm

  1. DVD release date changed
  2. New York run extended (this is the final week, really!)
  3. Screenings this week in Estonia, England, Denmark, Belgium, Georgia, more


Souvenirs from Monday’s event in Stockholm

DVD RELEASE DATE CHANGED TO 11/20
Due to some unavoidable manufacturing delays, the release date of the retail DVD of Helvetica has been pushed back two weeks to November 20th. The DVD will not be available in stores worldwide until that date. If you’ve pre-ordered the DVD from our site (or the limited-edition box set) your copy will be shipped one week prior to the new date. Institutional DVD orders are not affected. Our apologies on this, sorry for any inconvenience.

NEW YORK RUN EXTENDED… BUT THIS IS IT!

Helvetica has been extended for an 8th week at the IFC Center in Greenwich Village. But we’ve been assured this will be the last week… we have to give those other films a chance, after all. So if any of you New Yorkers have been procrastinating, this is it. Presented, of course, by our friends at Plexifilm, Veer, and A Public Space.
