It's interesting that the sample has three P's and one doesn't match the other two. The only other repeated letters (I) are identical. So this is a font, but finding it is a problem, because it falls into the 'anyone could have made this' category of font design. You could scan through [Part 14 of the Script Font ID Guide](http://web.archive.org/web/20130207035856/http:/www.bowfinprintworks.com/Script14Pg1.html) (Distressed Script), or look at Dafont (Brush) category. - Mike Yanega
