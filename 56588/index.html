Which logo do you like best- Budget Printing

Please tell me what logo you like best and why. If you would make a change to that logo what would it be?

These logo’s are for a printing Company I work with. They have been around for 30 years and want a logo update. They have 4 employees they do work for designers, the adverage Joe off the street and a few large corporations.

This printing co’s current logo has the letters BP very large hence my sphere with those letters. I’m really trying to pull them away from something that sounds like a gas station. the others are all built from the thought that they are the people that make a white sheet of paper look good (with ink) Hence, there is paper in the other three logos.

Please tell me what logo you like best and why. If you would make a change to that logo what would it be?

Thank you!

If you do stick with it, Tighten/fix the kerning/letterspacing - as a logo, it’s gotta be PERFECT. Perhaps run the two words together with the color change.

None of them works. Sorry. What I would do would be plain type “Budghet Printing” in two lines on negative space (for BW version) with tear paper edge. Would be more suitable and original. I also think that if you do budget printing your logo should be simple in terms of shapes, type, colours and everything. I’m not saying it should be dull and down to earth trivial. It should be exciting, but budget oriented. Think of anything that says to you “Budget” and see if you can work out an idea out of it. Your logos may be ok for some other business (but majorly reworked) just not for your company’s business. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ _Robert Koritnik_
