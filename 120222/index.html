Probably [Archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317063108/http:/fontfabric.com/archive-free-font) with a closed /R. Note that Archive has been revived under a commercial licence as [Institut](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317063108/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/brownfox/institut) with a closed /R this time. Alternatives: [Brutal](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317063108/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/brownfox/brutal-type) (same foundry, almost same design backbone, /G spur needing to be removed though), [Moderna Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317063108/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/los-andes/moderna-condensed) (open /R)
