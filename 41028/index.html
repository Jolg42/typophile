<cite>it’s too fine for the logo I’m working on - the hairlines disappear when the logo is small</cite> There are several faces with a range of [size-specific fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20081121130805/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Optical%252Bsize) to address this issue; e.g. [ITC Bodoni](http://web.archive.org/web/20081121130805/http:/www.stonetypefoundry.com/itcbodoni.html) (3 sizes) or [H&FJ Didot](http://web.archive.org/web/20081121130805/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/font_features.php?featureID=29&productLineID=100004) (7 sizes!). [Prillwitz](http://web.archive.org/web/20081121130805/http:/www.preusstype.com/fam-prillwitz.php) by [Ingo Preuss](http://web.archive.org/web/20081121130805/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Ingo%252BPreuss) is another – fresh – Didone. It has 3 different designs, one optimized for use on newsprint, one for text sizes and one for display sizes.
