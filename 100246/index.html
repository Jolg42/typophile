Whether the font is free or not, check the license agreement (or even ask the designer). The thing is, when you use a font for something that represents you (as opposed to a client) that might count as "personal use" (as opposed to "commercial use"). But it depends how the designer sees the situation, and it's always good to get an OK in writing. hhp
