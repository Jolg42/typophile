This isn't working for me: - The mark isn't aligned at all with the type. - The elements in the mark are all drawn in different styles, with different line weights, and look "cliparty". - The big guy looks like he's punching the little guy in the head. - "TLAfrica" means nothing to me. Try going with the full name "Transformational Leadership". Your mark doesn't need to be as literal as this, and it doesn't need as many elements. Simplify. - Lex
