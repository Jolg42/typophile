Anonymous @ 19 August, 2004 - 8:14am
Red Rooster has a number of condensed fonts with oblong O's like this. [Schindler](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408955) comes in 5 weights. [Raleigh Gothic Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408954) comes in 3 weights, but is more squared perhaps than you want. [Pall Mall](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408922) is another in 2 styles & 2 weights. [Keyboard](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408952) comes in 3 weights. [Helium](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408968) comes in 4 weights. [Grand Canyon](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408986) is all upper case but comes in 8 flavors. [Europa Grotesque](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408905) comes in 4 weights. [Coliseum](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408896) has 3 weights. [Chamfer Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408977) has angled corners, 3 weights and 2 styles (other is Small Caps Expert set). And last but not least is Block Gothic, which comes in [Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408957) & [Extra condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20051110021254/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=408889), with 5 weights of each. I just happen to have the Red Rooster fonts and I use these a lot when I want condensed styles.
