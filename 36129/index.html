TypeCon2008 Buffalo Town Hall Meeting, Tues., Aug. 21

SOTA’s going to be on location in Buffalo next week to meet with local crew and others interested in helping to develop the TypeCon2008 program and extracurricular activities.

All type lovers are encouraged to join us for our first official local planning/brainstorming meeting for next year’s ’con.

Tuesday, August 21st | 6pm
WJ Morrissey’s Irish Pub in Buffalo’s Historic Cobblestone District
30 Mississippi Street (one block away from HSBC Arena

Directions to TypeCon2008 Meeting

Some tasty/frothy refreshments will be provided!

Anyone interested is free to attend, but please RSVP in case we need more space.
Email: richard at p22.com and info at typesociety.org

If you can’t make it to this meeting but would like to participate, please email us at info at typesociety.org. Everyone is welcome in creating this volunteer-driven event!

I’ve been to Buffalo about half a dozen times and enjoyed every visit. This trip, we zoomed out to the Roycroft campus (in East Aurora, just 20 minutes from the conference hotel) and toured one of the Karpeles manuscript museums (Buffalo has two). Had dinner at Empire - great food, well-priced, and a menu of deliciously colorful specialty martinis. Went to a networking shindig on the fantail deck of the USS Little Rock - on the waterfront - a gorgeous sunset experience. Played trivia for glory and ate and drank on the cheap at a wings-specializing bar. Took the kiddos bowling at Kenmore Lanes (they have the best bowling alley signage I’ve seen in a long time). Down by the water again to sugar up the boys at a custard stand much beloved by seagulls. Drove around the city to scope out the beautiful architecture, ghostly lettering on brick walls, and plethora of funky neon. Will hit more museums and check out the wee-hours entertainment when I return in the fall. There are so many venues for special events and field trips that we are going to have a hard time fitting it all in. But we’ll do our best :)
