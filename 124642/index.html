Most authentic Palatino digitization?

This question is a bit of a continuation of this thread. In short, I am looking for a digitization of the Palatino typeface that is close to the original design.

Looking at this article, “The original Palatino,” I do like its liveliness and warmth. It seems to have been captured best by Berthold’s digitization (see this PDF which has the font somewhat incomplete embedded) but that digitization is no longer available. There is the Linotype version, and the TeX Gyre Pagella version, and URW Palladio. Which else? And which is closest to Zapf’s original?
