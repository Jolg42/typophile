You're in for a long slog. There is simply so much out there that it's better to start by describing what you want, such as: "I need - a sturdy, rugged serif for newspaper text" - a casual, friendly script with a nod to the American fifities." - heavy sans serif typefaces that look like they were hand drawn with a pen or brush." With more specific requirements we can be much more helpful with recommendations.
