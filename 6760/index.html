Anonymous @ 22.Sep.2004 4.35am
Take the innards out of 6 cheap ballpoint pens. Sand the edges of the tips for closer spacing on your lines. Tape them in a row with a splint if need be. Draw to your hearts content. This was the method pioneered by [Charles Dikkens](http://web.archive.org/web/20080213213931/http:/www.humorlinks.com/python/sketches/TheBookshopSketch.htm), the well know dutch composer. Time Estimate: 5 minutes Cost Estimate: 50 cents One issue for your pizza cutter is getting good ink coverage. It would be much simpler to cut your own punch, or if you're interested in an argument use a Benton Pantograph, make a matrix, cast some lead staves, set in a comp stick to the right length, fire it up and letterpress that badboy... use vegetable based inks. They're more organic... Time Estimate: 5 months Cost Estimate: Plenty Come to a bunch typophiles, what do you expect :-) Good luck.
