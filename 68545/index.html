Sometimes you will find fonts that have a "cameo" (reversed) weight... For example, [Fiendstar Cameo](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416045834/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/aviation/fiendstar-cameo/) [Blitzplakat](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416045834/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/facetype/blitzplakat/) However, be warned that searching for the term "cameo" on a font website will bring up fonts called Cameo as well as fonts with cameo (reversed) weights, and even some fonts with no reversed weight at all! So you will need a bit of patience... For example, here is [a search for "cameo" on FontShop's site](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416045834/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=cameo&x=0&y=0). A [search for the term "reversed" (on the same site)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416045834/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=reversed&x=0&y=0) also yielded some results. I hope this helps!
