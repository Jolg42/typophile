Logo is custom, but _GOOD_ uses [Vista Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090801/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Vista%20Sans) and [Trade Gothic No. 20 Condensed Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090801/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/linotype/trade_gothic_no_20_condensed_bold/) throughout each issue. The look was directed by [Scott Stowell of Open](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090801/http:/www.notclosed.com/pages/wemake/work_good.html).
