Hi Becky, [Mozilla](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709011238/http:/www.mozilla.org/) uses [FF Meta](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709011238/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontfont/ff_meta_bold/?sample_text=Firefox%20%26%20Thunderbird), and so does [Neues Museum Nürnberg](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709011238/http:/www.nmn.de/) (both found at [Simon Zirkunow’s Flickr photos](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709011238/http:/www.flickr.com/photos/bluesmuse/tags/meta/). Have you read the wiki entry: [FF Meta](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709011238/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/FF%252BMeta). Additional information about its origin can be found at [Erik Spiekermann](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709011238/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Erik%252BSpiekermann)’s own blog, he put [an article from Baseline 7, 1986](http://web.archive.org/web/20080709011238/http:/www.spiekermann.com/iblog/C61720386/E150639911/index.html) online.
