Anonymous @ 19 Oct 2005 — 8:43am
[Adobe Type Classics for Learning](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015092205/http:/www.adobe.com/education/ed_products/typeclassics.html) ($99!) is the best overall value for a starter set. Add to it with a larger library of "classic" fonts. Bitstream's [Type Oddyssey](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015092205/http:/www.myfonts.com/info/bitstream-type-odyssey-2/?refby=typographica) (1,450 fonts for $1495) or the [Linotype Essential CDs](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015092205/http:/www.myfonts.com/info/linotype-font-cds/#LinotypeEssentialsCDs?refby=typographica). Make sure you ask about an educational discount. Finally, top it with the best of contemporary typefaces with any of the [FontFont CDs](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015092205/http:/www.fontshop.com/products/libraries.cfm#fontfont). They offer a 30% educational discount.
