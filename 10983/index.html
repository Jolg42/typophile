Small caps for academic degrees

I’m working on an alumni directory. Haven’t really had to address the formatting of degree notations before, but now it’s an issue.

Anyone have any advice for treating small caps in this situation? Things like BS Ed, BS Ag, M Ed. Right now, we’re treating the first section (BS) as two small caps, and the 2nd section (Ed) as initial cap small cap.

Suggestions?

Although I am unaware of any particular style guide rules about such formatting, this sample illustrates a format that seems satisfactory (in terms of legibility and clear communication). My degree (from San Jose State) is rather unusual, so my goal is to provide a bit of explanation in my format choice. The person's name is size "x", the degree (in U&lc) is approx. 80% of x, the explanation (in this case GRAPHIC DESIGN) is at approx.size 60% of x. ![sample designation format](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023141028im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/30/8871.gif)
