Anonymous @ 15.Jan.2004 10.35pm
This is in fact exactly the sort of thing ATF produced a lot of around the turn of the century. Your best bet is a book called "Specimens of type, brass rules and dashes, ornaments and borders, society emblems, check lines, cuts, initials and other productions of the American Type Founders Co.", edited by A Lawson and J Bidwell (New York, Garland, 1981). But that's also a facsimile, so it won't be the best quality. Another way would be to track down an actual ATF specimen book, but an early one: I suspect the famous 1923 edition doesn't have this font. FYI, the Lawson book is based [mostly] on the specimen of 1896. A third approach is to see if this was ever made available as a phototype face (shudder), although procuring specimens of such would probably cost you something. hhp
