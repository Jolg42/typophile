The overshoot needs to be a bit more for very “round”, narrow curves and less for flatter ones, like the O in Eurostile or the C in Thesis. It helps me not to think in terms of the depth/height but the width of the extending portion: If you make the overshoot so big that is has about one third of the width of the O you are probably right.
