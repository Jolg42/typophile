It's an old font that was originally called 'Dragonwyck' (I think), which has been digitized as [Dragonwick](http://web.archive.org/web/20150227203959/http:/www.dafont.com/dragonwick.font?nb_ppp=50&text=Heart+India) by my old friend FontGrube. I think several digitizations exist, including one by Rick Mueller. - Mike Yanega
Anonymous @ 18 Feb 2008 — 9:29am
One clarification about Dragonwick, Mike - it was digitized by David Rakowski. [Fontgrube](http://web.archive.org/web/20150227203959/http:/www.fontgrube.de/en/index.htm) (Andreas Hofeld) took several fonts that others had made and improved them by fixing mistakes and filling out the character set. He was always very up front about this.
