Anonymous @ 3 Aug 2014 — 6:01am
As for Verdana and Tahoma, both are Matthew Carter designs. Tahoma's letters are narrower and it has tighter letter spacing. There is a still narrower version called Nina. Verdana Pro from Ascender has a Black weight in addition to the Bold. Compare: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317175543im_/http:/typophile.com/files/VerdanaProBoldInUse_5302.gif) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317175543im_/http:/typophile.com/files/VerdanaProBlackInUse_3879.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ascender/verdana-pro/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317175543/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ascender/verdana-pro/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ascender/verdana-pro/") If I was attempting to duplicate the \_CORKTOWN\_ sign I would create an intermediate weight of these two fonts. -------- UPDATE: However, IIRC, when white letters are placed on a dark background they appear thinner than when dark letters are placed on a dark background. It's worth converting Verdana Black to a white on dark version and comparing it to the \_CORKTOWN\_ sign. Don
