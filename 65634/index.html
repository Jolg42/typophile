You can approximate this probably custom lettering with [Stymie](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013174622/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/stymie/). The Black weight is closest except for the y. Oddly, the Black y is different from the other weights. I think if you added stroke to the Bold weight you would be able to fake it. The M is also a bit different, crotch-wise, but you could edit it or ignore the difference. - Mike Yanega
