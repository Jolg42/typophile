Digigirl logo

Looks like a font, but the odds are it is of Japanese origin. The only place I know to see these fonts are in the Font Pavillion collections at P22/IHOF. P22 says they have limited stocks of these and cannot get more from the Japanese vendors. Most have a Latin version as well as Japanese characters.

If someone else knows good links in English for Japanese fonts (with Latin characters), that could be a useful resource.
