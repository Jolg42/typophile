Hi Jim, all of them are quite classic. One could add [Stymie](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Stymie) and, of course, [Clarendon](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Clarendon) to this list. Lubalin Graph is Avant Garde’s slab soul brother, therefore _very_ geometric. Nice for headlines, not easy – to unusable – in text. Caecilia is at the other end of the gamut, having humanistic proportions: not so bold-faced, but more versatile. Not-so-often seen (can’t call them ‘hidden’) gems: [City](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/city-bq/familytree.html) by Georg Trump, [Boton](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/boton-bq/familytree.html). Similar but fresher faces would be [Giza](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/slab/Giza), [Dispatch](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/slab/Dispatch). Or [Silica](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/silica/), if you like to follow the Caecilia line. Check out this thread [on Serifa, Glypha, Egyptienne F and other slabs](http://web.archive.org/web/20140719103435/http:/www.typophile.com/node/31962), too. F

Anonymous @ 13 Dec 2007 — 12:52pm
Pick the heaviest weight of Serifa or Glypha, tighten up the tracking a bit (1 point range tracking at 24 pt text, 2 pt tracking at 48) and voila--superb titling slab at zero cost (I assume you already have Serifa or Glypha if you have the Adobe or Bitstream catalogs handy). Rockwell will work too, I'm just not a huge Rockwell lover. If you're willing to buy new stuff, then yes, FB Giza is the slab titling bomb.
