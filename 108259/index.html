Lithographed label, not typefaces. WILLIAM'S SOAP is in a lettering style called "concave." Here are a couple of digital fonts in the concave style -- not including concave Tuscan, which has spiked outlines: Concave Extended [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/solotype/concave-extended/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150130154353/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/solotype/concave-extended/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/solotype/concave-extended/") Fredericksburg NF [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/fredericksburg-wbw/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150130154353/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/fredericksburg-wbw/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/fredericksburg-wbw/") Don
