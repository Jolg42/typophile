[Trapping](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014025536/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Trapping) is when you over compensate for printing errors and fill-ins with your design (ok, that is oversimplifying things a bit…), like in [Bell Centennial Bold Listing](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014025536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/bell-centennial/bold-listing/). it isn't the term for the general joins within a letter, which I believe is what Tanya is asking for.
Anonymous @ 14 Dec 2005 — 6:21am
"is when you over compensate for printing errors" it is only "overcompensation" when you over do it. To me, trapping is also a way to open visual space at small sizes--even if ther was no ink plugging going on. Angle joins of M, W, and N are examples of glyphs that might need it. The bolder the weight, the more likely the need will arise. ChrisL
