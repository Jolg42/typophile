According to [creative director Shaz Madani](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317052320/http:/www.creativereview.co.uk/cr-blog/2013/november/riposte) > The title typeface is “a very slight alteration” of Gerard Unger’s [Amerigo](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317052320/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/amerigo), which has also been used throughout the first section of the magazine. “We’ve removed some of the curves on the tapered strokes for sharper, more chiseled like serifs… This has been balanced with a softer more friendly sans serif. I love this sans font for all its small quirky imperfections that give it so much personality,”
