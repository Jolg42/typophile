Escrow is used for the headline serif, but I believe the text serif is Exchange from Hoefler & Frere-Jones. See [this article](http://web.archive.org/web/20111221030452/http:/www.typography.com/about/clients.php) about clients. - Mike Yanega

More info on Exchange [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20111221030452/http:/new.typographica.org/2007/typeface-reviews/exchange).
