Some light from this [previous discussion](http://web.archive.org/web/20150112051504/http:/typophile.com/node/117181) by Mike F... > [...] [McCollough](http://web.archive.org/web/20150112051504/http:/www.linotype.com/155164/mccollough-family.html) for me, Don. > Designed about 1898 by Gustav F Schroeder for ATF and - according to [this Klingspor Museum PDF](http://web.archive.org/web/20150112051504/http:/www.klingspor-museum.de/KlingsporKuenstler/Schriftdesigner/Schroeder/GustavFSchroeder.pdf) - originally spelled McCullagh. ... and Don > Matamoros NF is based on a similar but different font -- note for example the lazy s counters in \_O\_ and \_Q\_, similar to Inland Foundry's \_Inland\_ -- from circa 1900 called Jason Wood by Dan X.
