I did quite a search for the NUTTELEX typeface, and I couldn’t find anything that had that stepped X. The bracketed vertical serifs on the E and T suggest a Clarendon, and my suggestion would be to use a medium weight Clarendon, with a modified E (remove the middle serif) and X (to step the legs). For the script ’Solidified’, this looks like a hand-drawn sign painter script that was made into an illuminated sign. A similar example is ’Blaze’ from Art & Sign (signfonts.com) shown with other sign scripts [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20071116152917/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/Script07Pg8.html). The style of ’f’ is common in sign painting scripts for some reason, and that’s what made me look at these. - Mike Yanega
