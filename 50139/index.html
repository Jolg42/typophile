hi, this is the engraving lady and yes, this is engraved, probably by hand. its not a font. do you have the plate (or die) or just the print. there are 2 categories for this kind of font available: script and calligraphic (calligraphy.) the "lines" are either the shadow cast within the "cut" or the guide lines scribed first. neither of which will help you. if your task is to replicate this, best and (historically "correct" according to the market) is to re-draw it in illustrator. it will be lovely. and maybe you can use the lesson learned for someothing else.
