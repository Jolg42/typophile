Adobe Type

Indices : Foundries : Adobe Type

Adobe Type sells more than 2,200 popular typefaces for individual sale. The entire library, Font Folio, is available in OpenType and may also be purchased on CD at a substantial savings. Adobe makes a selection of their library avaiable for students under the title Adobe Type Classics for Learning.

Online at http://www.adobe.com/type/
