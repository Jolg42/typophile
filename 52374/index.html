I wrote an e-mail to Radim Pesko and he graciously sent back this reply and image. "The font is called 'Larish' and was indeed drawn specially for Secession identity which was developed together with Willi Schmid, in 2007-2008. Its appearence is inspired by an old prints from Vienna of late 19th century. Even it is not re-drawn typeface, its shape it true to the spirit to its example. Larish will be completed with italic released in Spring 2009." - Mike Yanega ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150305002157im_/http:/typophile.com/files/spec040307small_3555.gif)
