Anonymous @ 16 Jan 2006 — 9:55am
_is this quote is correct?_ Yes. But slab serif faces were used for display settings, or as a contrast face in text, rather than the main style. Like we use a bold sans to contrast a regular serif face today. The only form of contrast that Morris tolerated was between caps and U&lc -- had he addrressed typography earlier in his career, or lived longer, he might have developed a larger palette of typefaces and started to use them together.
