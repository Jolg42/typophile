Anonymous @ 16 May 2014 — 8:10am
If that font is to be used as the mathematical bold fraktur font (unicode range 1D56C-1D59F), then it does not meet my requirements; that range is not meant for text, it is meant to give individually recognizable mathematical symbols. For me the symmetric group on n elements, and the alternating group on n elements are represented by ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414184859im_/http:/typophile.com/files/groupe_4669.png) in the standard and bold math frak fonts, and making the S and the A looking closer to those in regular alphabets is pointless.
