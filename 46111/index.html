Unfortunately you’re going to get a slightly different height and weight from the two versions. You need to buy the whole family from one foundry or the other. For those who want ease of use, I recommend [ITC Avant Garde Gothic Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20080612044317/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/itc/itc_avant_garde_gothic_pro_virtual/) which has the alternates and ligatures built into a single OpenType font for each weight. For those who want the alts but value [true italics](http://web.archive.org/web/20080612044317/http:/typographica.org/001029.php), the only choice is the [E+F digitization](http://web.archive.org/web/20080612044317/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=elsner+flake%20avant%20garde).
