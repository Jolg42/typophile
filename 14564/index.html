Chris, you might be in luck! Hiragino Mincho Pro was not designed in Cupertino by Apple. They licensed it. Maybe the company they licensed it from will license you a PC version? Some designer/distribution info is embedded in the font (I viewed this information with Linotype's FontExplorer X utility, which will be publicly available soon): The font was designed by JIYUKOBO Ltd. Co., Ltd.
