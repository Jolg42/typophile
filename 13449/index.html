Anonymous @ 5 July, 2005 - 8:09am
Check out the B’s in Galliard and Mantinia. The B on the book cover, the cross bar where the 2 bowls of the b meet. Galliards closes both bowls, Mantinia leaves the bowls connected and open. Also check out the ‘foot’ of the R’s. Galliards is rather plain, whilst Mantinia’s has a rather pronounced brush flair. I vote Mantinia.
