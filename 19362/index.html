Anonymous @ 19 Apr 2006 — 2:05pm
These are indeed not "fonts" in the sense that they would be available in digital format to use on your computer. These characters are made specifically for the plate-making machines that produce them. There are a number of typefaces available that either were modeled after or resemble various types of license plates. In this instance I would look into [Tobias Frere-Jones](http://web.archive.org/web/20150103215815/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Tobias%20Frere-Jones)'s [Garage Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150103215815/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/font_bureau/garage_gothic/?sample_text=ABTP%20357&sample_size=74) or [Christian Schwartz](http://web.archive.org/web/20150103215815/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Christian%20Schwartz)'s [Pennsylvania](http://web.archive.org/web/20150103215815/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/font_bureau/pennsylvania/?sample_text=ABTP%20357&sample_size=74). Also check out the collection of license plate fonts from [Lineto](http://web.archive.org/web/20150103215815/http:/www.lineto.com/).
