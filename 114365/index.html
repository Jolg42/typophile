RASHOMON looks like an adaptation of Font Bureau [Aardvark](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701122635/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/aardvark/?refby=bowfin). The A and H have been changed to have normal crossbars, but otherwise this seems like the source (or inspiration) for this lettering. - Mike Yanega
Anonymous @ 20 Feb 2014 — 2:59pm
There are two different sans serif fonts used on the poster but the red lettering looks a lot like [Block Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701122635/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/block-gothic-rr/?refby=bowfin) from Red Rooster. A little height/width tweaking seems like it would match very well. The gold sans at the top is something else that looks commonplace. - Mike Yanega

The gold sans is [Univers](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701122635/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/univers/), by Adrian Frutiger. Available from Linotype and Adobe. Best match is probably the Black (75) weight.
