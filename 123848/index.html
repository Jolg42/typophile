Can't find an exact match either. Take into account that this is probably a custom OEM font of the lens manufacturer designed for the lens production machine and not a commercially available digital font. You may like one of the following replacement candidates, here is a square looking one: **[Thud (Medium)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150319050901/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Suomi%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Thud%20-%20Medium&fid=3f6e746c92f5fae0c339a0d953677eda&fsize=60&text=DC%20ELMARIT%201%3A2.8%2F10.2%20ASPH&wrap=2)** by Suomi Type Foundry [2010] ...and some more round ones: **[Zetta Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150319050901/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=PlayType%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Zetta%20Sans%20-%20Regular&fid=705f45f9b8eb0dbbe506d35e98219f42&fsize=60&text=DC%20ELMARIT%201%3A2.8%2F10.2%20ASPH&wrap=2)** by Jonas Hecksher [PlayType] **[Coda](http://web.archive.org/web/20150319050901/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Google-Web-Fonts%20%2F%20%28Freemium%29&ffam=Coda%20-%20Regular&fid=fc5514afc8b9a2cc39776e68b28b39d7&fsize=60&text=DC%20ELMARIT%201%3A2.8%2F10.2%20ASPH&wrap=2)** by Vernon Adams [Google Web Fonts, 2010] **[Eurostile Medium](http://web.archive.org/web/20150319050901/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=URW%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=EurostileTMed%20-%20Regular&fid=562e0f2e786533bc0ea0cd8189456961&fsize=60&text=DC%20ELMARIT%201%3A2.8%2F10.2%20ASPH&wrap=2)** PS: I now see that Ryuk confirmed it's a custom font...
