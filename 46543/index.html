Cyrillic Fest 2008

„Свято Кирилиці” („Cyrillic Fest”) is a typography and calligraphy festival, which traditionally takes place in 24-25 May in Kharkiv (Ukraine). This year it included type seminars and presentation by Vitalij Mitchenko’s book, type games and exhibition of typefaces and calligraphy from Ukraine, Russia, Belarus and Bulgaria.
