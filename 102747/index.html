Anonymous @ 3 May 2013 — 8:12am
Fedra Serif is part of the [Typotheque](http://web.archive.org/web/20130620214203/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fwww.typotheque.com/fonts/fedra_serif_a) font collection. It's not available elsewhere (moreover freely) nor included in any Operating System font package or software bundle, to my knowledge at least. Here are some resources you might find useful: [list](http://web.archive.org/web/20130620214203/http:/www.identifont.com/similar?GIC) of similar to Fedra Serif A compiled at Identifont, " [More like](http://web.archive.org/web/20130620214203/http:/www.myfonts.com/morelike/38719/)" at MF, [Serif Fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20130620214203/http:/www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/list/style/Serif) section at Fontsquirrel and [Google Web Fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20130620214203/http:/www.google.com/fonts) directory.
