It's an interesting project. My first thought was a font that is probably not technically Medieval, but [P22 Aragon](http://web.archive.org/web/20150225002904/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/sherwood/p22-aragon/) has a nice old style with legible lower case. I'm sure you will get other suggestions, but this was something I've used and liked. - Mike Yanega
Anonymous @ 24 May 2009 — 10:32am
If Medieval means blackletter, you’re in a trouble, but if that is the case I’d choose some rotunda in the spirit of [San Marco](http://web.archive.org/web/20150225002904/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/san-marco/) (not really Medieval, it’s early Renaissance). If your Medieval concept accepts Carolingian times, I’d say something like [Alcuin](http://web.archive.org/web/20150225002904/http:/www.paratype.com/fstore/fonts/Alcuin.htm). For nice capitals: maybe overused, but [Charlemagne](http://web.archive.org/web/20150225002904/http:/www.paratype.com/fstore/default.asp?fcode=609&letter=C) could be an option.
