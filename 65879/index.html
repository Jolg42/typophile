‘doufat’ is Futura. ‘надежда’ is somewhere between Verdana and Myriad. ‘надявам се’ is probably Helvetica (not Neue). ‘希望’ look like Hiragino Kaku Gothic to me. ‘ελπίδα’ seems to be a hodge-podge of different typefaces, or a font without proper support for Greek. ‘희망’ is Batang. F
