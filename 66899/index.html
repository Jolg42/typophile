Hi Daniel, it looks like [Canada Type](http://web.archive.org/web/20131012115953/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Canada%20Type)’s [Quanta East and West](http://web.archive.org/web/20131012115953/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/quanta/?testdrive=dock%3Dfalse%26size%3D52%26w%3D720%26src%3Dcustom%26text%3DTHE%2520BABIES%2520ARE%2520COMING%26fg%3D000000%26bg%3Dffffff%26goodies%3Dfit%252Cot.liga%26seed%3D25) combined, i.e. without the stencil gaps. In Quanta’s description, [Patrick Griffin](http://web.archive.org/web/20131012115953/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Patrick%20Griffin) states that it is “[b]ased on yet another undigitized type spotted on a film set”.
