These letters are in the Trefoil -- three lobe -- variant of a style called Tuscan. Here are some in the style: Frontiersman [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/frontiersman/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317131344/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/frontiersman/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/frontiersman/") Hustlers [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/decade-typefoundry/Hustlers/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317131344/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/decade-typefoundry/Hustlers/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/decade-typefoundry/Hustlers/") Cactus Flower [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/cactus-flower-sg/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317131344/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/cactus-flower-sg/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/cactus-flower-sg/") Massell [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/massel/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317131344/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/massel/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/massel/") Surrey [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/surrey/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317131344/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/surrey/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/surrey/") Don
