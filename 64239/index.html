The SunDogs typeface seems a bit too techno. Soft rounded curves don’t say ’tough’ to me. Now [Ironmonger](http://web.archive.org/web/20091117163745/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/ironmonger/?testdrive=dock%3Dfalse%26size%3D66%26w%3D720%26src%3Dcustom%26text%3DTough%2520mother%26fg%3D000000%26bg%3Dffffff%26goodies%3Dfit%252Cot.liga%26seed%3D29) is a symbol of toughness. Another would be [Force](http://web.archive.org/web/20091117163745/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/outras/force/?testdrive=dock%3Dfalse%26size%3D66%26w%3D720%26src%3Dcustom%26text%3DTough%2520mother%26fg%3D000000%26bg%3Dffffff%26goodies%3Dfit%252Cot.liga%26seed%3D20) If you don’t like the slab serifs you could try [Tank](http://web.archive.org/web/20091117163745/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/tank/?testdrive=dock%3Dfalse%26size%3D66%26w%3D720%26src%3Dcustom%26text%3DTough%2520mother%26fg%3D000000%26bg%3Dffffff%26goodies%3Dfit%252Cot.liga%26seed%3D23) Search “construction” and “military” at MyFonts.com for a pile of options.
