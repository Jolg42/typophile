Anonymous @ 23 Mar 2009 — 11:14am
There are three things you can do. As it is a rare occurrence, a little manual work on the "d" is OK. 1. Reduce the size of lower case "d", then increase its weight by applying a "Stroke" value. 2. Use a "d" from a smaller optical size, if your typeface has one. 3. Choose a "d" from a related font that has the appropriate weight at the size. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023214938im_/http:/typophile.com/files/JaneSmith_5630.jpg)
