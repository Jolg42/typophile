There is a version of Papyrus called Parchment that was done by Rick Mueller and is available for download at TypOasis (from the Designer pages). Maybe that's the one Lex was remembering, however, comparing the outlines of Papyrus and Parchment, I believe Papyrus was clearly the source. The nicks and dings in the letters are in the same places in Papyrus, but Parchment is relatively smooth. The only obvious modification I see is that the E's middle arm has been shortened in the posted sample. This looks to me like the outline points of the middle arm were just shifted left. It's been distorted somewhat, but I am convinced this was made with Papyrus. - Mike Yanega
