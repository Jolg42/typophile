You’ve got at least two typefaces here, and I don’t either one is Century. “Review” looks like a Scotch or Transitional face. Caledonia would have been an option in ’66; Miller (from Font Bureau) is a modern interpretation. The small roman type (along with the numbers and small caps) looks like a version of Baskerville. Not sure about the italics, though.
