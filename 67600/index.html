Hi Mark, welcome to Typophile! [FF Schulbuch Nord](http://web.archive.org/web/20150126052944/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_schulbuch/) is essentially a ‘Helvetica Infant’. So is [AG Schoolbook 2](http://web.archive.org/web/20150126052944/http:/www.identifont.com/show?HG6). Monotype offers a bold version (only) that features a number of different letterforms (y, I) under the name of [Special Alphabets 10](http://web.archive.org/web/20150126052944/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/creative_alliance/special_alphabets_10/).
