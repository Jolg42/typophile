Anonymous @ 20 Jun 2014 — 1:25pm
Late 19th century book designer, type designer and fontographer who revived medieval designs and was a major influence on the style of published books. His fonts are a fusion of Blackletter and Roman. His books used very ornate designs often involving vines and leaves. Your \_O\_ is a comfortable companion to his designs. More [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William\_Morris](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725123833/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Morris "http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William\_Morris") There is a lively discussion of his influence at [http://typophile.com/node/114418](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725123833/http:/typophile.com/node/114418 "http://typophile.com/node/114418") Don
