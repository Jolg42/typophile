Filthy: [bespoke typeface designed by filthymedia](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/typophile.com/node/76822), very influenced by Times and Caslon (no. 224) Black Italic. Alternatives: [Dutch 801](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/dutch-801/extra-bold-italic) (Bitstream version of Times), [Nimbus Roman](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/nimbus-roman/d-extra-bold-italic) (URW verison), [Thames](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/softmaker/thames-serial/heavy-italic) (Softmaker version), [Caslon no. 224 Black Italic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/caslon-no-224), [Cabernet](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/marketplace.veer.com/font/Cabernet-Complete-JBT0001003), [Riccione Xbold Italic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef-typeshop/riccione), [Basilia Black Italic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/basilia), [Abril Fatface Italic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/type-together/abril), [Velino Text Black Italic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dstype/velino-text) CHIRON: most probably one among [Engravers Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/engravers+gothic)/ [Sackers Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/sackers-gothic)/ [Blair](http://web.archive.org/web/20141024022357/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/blair)
