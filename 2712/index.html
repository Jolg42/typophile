Anonymous @
Adobe Sans and Adobe Serif are multiple master fonts that ATM (Adobe Type Manager) uses to match the exact proportions of fonts that are missing from files generated by Adobe programs, therefore causing no layout oddities even though the original fonts are not present. I guess the "aaaaaaaaaaaaaarrrrrrrrggggggghhhhhhhs" are because the font isn't exactly a beauty ![:-)](http://web.archive.org/web/20100401075018im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/clipart/happy.gif) Edit: for clarity.
Anonymous @
Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaargh!!!... Adobe Sans Multiple Master substitution font. So basically this is not a font, it's merely a replacement courtesy of SuperATM, in order to allow you to read the text without having the typeface installed. For further information on this unnatural phenomenon, check my fine selection of rants and raves in [this thread](http://web.archive.org/web/20100401075018/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/83/3075.html#POST8270) from just over a year ago.
