Antonio di Bartolommeo Miscomini (c. 1445–1495) was a Bolognese punchcutter and printer who worked in Venice, Modena and above all **Florence** , where he cut roman and orthotic Greek types. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017033005im_/http:/typophile.com/files/4187284898_220cef8b96_5966.jpg) <cite><a class="freelinking external" href="http://web.archive.org/web/20131017033005/http:/www.flickr.com/photos/bookhistorian/4187284898/" rel="nofollow">1482 Firenze - Antonius Mischomini, type 3,4</a></cite> cc-by History of the Book, Amsterdam ( [more on Flickr](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017033005/http:/www.flickr.com/search/?q=Mischomini&w=43719536@N02)) I am uncertain if any revivals of his types exist.

Hermann Zapf's **Medici Script** takes its name from the famous Florentine family. While not particular to Florence, you might have a look at some script styles such as Adrian Frutiger's **Ondine** , or a _rotunda_ such as **[San Marco](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017033005/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/san-marco/)**.
