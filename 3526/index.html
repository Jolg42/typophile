I believe that Adibe's ATM Lite is required to use PS fonts in Windows 95, 98, and NT 4.0. PS support is built into Windows 2000 and higher (XP Home, XP Pro). You can get ATM lite for free here: [http://www.adobe.com/products/atmlight/main.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019042331/http:/www.adobe.com/products/atmlight/main.html)
