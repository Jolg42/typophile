These particular forms are often referred to as Granjon ornaments or Granjon fleurons, I believe. You'll find some digital versions in this collection: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/arabesque-ornaments-two-mt/](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106070435/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/arabesque-ornaments-two-mt/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/arabesque-ornaments-two-mt/") The early 20th-century designer & typographer Bruce Rogers was fond of using them to great effect. If you search on some of those terms, you may find some info. -- K.
