Anonymous @ 17 November, 2005 - 9:24am
According to Walter Tracy, ‘Letters of Credit,’ the round 0 in old style numerals follows scribal tradition, which was done to avoid confusion with the letter o. Incidently, as is usual in type design, what looks geometrical isn’t. In Adobe Caslon old style 0 the inner circle is an oval, and consequently the vertical arches are fatter than the horizontal ones.
