Hypothetically speaking, any royalty arrangement that gives the author of the work a minority share is still based in an antiquated manufacturing model. Since most authors of digital fonts not only have designed the work, but has also produced it, they are entitled to a majority of the income. What risk does the reseller assume in this model? Anonymous @ 16 Mar 2004 — 5:06am
Things to consider: **Promotion** How are they going to spend your money? Do they have a printed catalog? Does it suck? Do they have a decent website that offers respectful showings of your types? How much traffic does the site see? **Saturation** How large is the vendor? Do they already have a huge library in which your stuff could get buried? Will they guarantee that your fonts will get representative play in their promotions? **Sublicencing** I would ask which resellers, if any, also sell their fonts. [Something to read.](http://web.archive.org/web/20131020061417/http:/typographi.ca/000787.php#000787)
