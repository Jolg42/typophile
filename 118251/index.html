Anonymous @ 25 Jul 2014 — 1:41pm
Well, the question is why the other fonts are not giving you conniptions, not why this one is. 'aalt' is a substitution feature that acts as a catch-all for other substitution features. You can't stick 'kern' in it and expect anything good to happen. If you have no other, real, substitution features, then you don't need aalt.

Anonymous @ 16 Aug 2014 — 1:10pm
Or you could just completely ignore {aalt}. It was a workaround devised by Adobe to drive early versions of their glyph palettes. Not only does it not have any practical use for font users, it isn't even needed to drive the font palette, which can derive the same information from parsing all the GSUB features for one-to-one substitution lookups.
