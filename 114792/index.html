Most probably a custom lettering somehow mixing influences from [Ballantines](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/ballantines)/ [Shelley Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/shelley+script)/ [Edwardian Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/edwardian+script), [Mahogany MTI](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/atmahogany-script)/ [Monterey](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/monterey)/ [Feel Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/sudtipos/feel-script) and [Fenway Park](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jukebox/fenway-park-jf). Ready-to-go alternatives: LHF [Indian Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/fonts/indianscript2.php) and [Packard Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/fonts/packardscript.php), [Impregnable](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.aringtypeface.com/impregnable), [Intrigue Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.aringtypeface.com/intrique-script), [Motion Picture](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.aringtypeface.com/motion-picture), [Tangier](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/tangier) (even if very different) Freely available to download as vector [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20140320224711/http:/www.brandsoftheworld.com/search/logo?search_api_views_fulltext=kiehls)
