Hope this isn't spam-bait, as the answer is so obvious :-) Private Label... [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private\_label](http://web.archive.org/web/20100201080649/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_label "http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private\_label") "Private label products or services are typically those manufactured or provided by one company for offer under another company's brand." So its designing a product that can have another company’s logo slapped on it - Apple, Wal-Mart, Halliburton or whatever, rather than designing a product specifically for a brand.
