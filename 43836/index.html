(x "Floris") Jungle World: German weekly newspaper

I saw this newspaper while in Berlin a while ago and thought the layout was very appealing and unconventional. It’s distributed on a weekly basis, and the content is mainly politics (from a left wing perspective as far as I could understand). There’s one typeface that is used throughout the newspaper, both in display and text, and at first glance I thought it was Dolly. But it’s less fluffy and has more serif in it.

Thanks,
Malte

Hi Malte, your Dolly suspicion wasn’t completely off: It’s ‘Dutch style’, at least! ;-) Jungle World used Floris, a custom font family by [Luc(as) de Groot](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907160715/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Luc%252528as%252529%252Bde%252BGroot). Read more about it [in this thread](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907160715/http:/www.typophile.com/expert_level#comment-257418) (a recent Type ID Quiz).
