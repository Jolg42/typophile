Well, the S has the weight in the wrong place--should usually thicken in the center. Check out other s's for [examples](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417143205/http:/www.typedesign.com/fonts/benmed.html). Also, the top right curves on the m and n taper oddly. Curves should be thickest at the sides and thinnest at top, if you are going for a vertical stress--your zero has it right. In general the letters need to be more consistent with each other. [Underware has a good tutorial on letter design](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417143205/http:/www.typeworkshop.com/index.php?id1=type-basics) Also the wiki has some good links [http://typophile.com/wiki/Drawing\_How-To](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417143205/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Drawing_How-To) [http://typophile.com/wiki/How-To](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417143205/http:/typophile.com/wiki/How-To) Eron
