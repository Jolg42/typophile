The apple symbol is not “alt-a”, at least on the US keyboard layout. (It’s shift-option-k.) Alt-a (or option-a) is å. Maybe if you put your character in the å (aring) slot it will work the way you want. (Note also that the apple symbol is not normally accessible on Windows.)
