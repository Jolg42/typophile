I believe the piece you are looking for is "A Technique for Dealing with Artists." It's a little bit tongue-in-cheek, in a Jonathan Swift sort of way. The original article was published in 1941 by the Press of the Woolly Whale. It's since been republished in a number of places. One is the 1947 Typophile Chapbook XVII -- <cite>MSS. by WAD</cite>. It looks like it's also included in the more recent <cite>Looking Closer 3: Classic Writings on Graphic Design,</cite> from Allworth Press. I hope this helps. -- K.
