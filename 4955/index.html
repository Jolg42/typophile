The first thing I would do, is take it out of the box. I think this will help in evaluating the relationships between letterforms vs. the relationship between letterforms and the box. I think you're on the right track with the "r", but I'm not sold on the "F" yet. Outside of the black box, I think you can increase the letter spacing between some of the letters more easily (i-r, m-e for starters). Aloha! Scott

Oscar, a few issues here imo .... \* The dot on the i cutting into the F creates an odd counter shape and sharpness that doesn't fit in with the rounds. \* the Vertical stem widths appear consistent, but the horizontals are inconsistent. -- the counters on the m are too tall. --the crossbar on the F seems too thick. --the top horizontal stem on the F seems thick in relation to the other letters, too. You might set some guides, adjust, and then eyeball a print out. (Actually, not you but your friend.) \* the m seems too wide. bj
