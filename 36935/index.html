Strictly speaking, it is probably latin characters of [ShinGo](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414021514/http:/www.morisawa.co.jp/font/fontlist/details/fontfamily007.html) (means “New Sans Serif” in Japanese), a Japanese popular modern sans serif font. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414021514im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Shingo_3898.jpg) Of course, the latin characters of ShinGo is based on Eurostile but rounder than Eurostile. BTW “YOU ARE HERE” set in ShinGo is condensed around 94%. - Akira
