Artists also do challenging and difficult problem-solving, but the primary goal is self-expression or communication of a personal vision. Personally, I love art, but I also fascinated with how other problems can be solved in a beautiful way--design. Art is something we take time from our lives to experience, whereas design is part of the warp and woof of everyday life.
