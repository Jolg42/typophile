Great New Fonts! - Font Bros Update!

Submitted by paul d hunt : 27.Nov.2007 1.45am

Hi Friends!

We’re thrilled to announce the addition of over 20 amazing new display fonts! Check ’em out!

Blue Vinyl Fonts - Parsley Script is the perfect semi-formal brush script face for nearly all your design projects! Check it out at: http://www.fontbros.com/bluevinyl.php

CBDO - Our friend Charles Borges de Oliveira has blown us away with a hearty informal brush script called Avalanche! Check it out at: http://www.fontbros.com/cbdo.php

Fontalicious - In addition to reuniting the fontalicious.com URL with its former owner, we’re pleased to announce we’ve added the popular and highly requested Alba family! Check ’em out at: http://www.fontbros.com/fontalicious.php

Font Head - Introducing two great new dingbat fonts, Diaper Money and the very lovable and fun Quirkies! Inspired by the birth of his TRIPLETS, Ethan Dunham created Diaper Money for just that purpose! Check it out at: http://www.fontbros.com/fonthead.php

Sideshow - We’re thrilled to announce two new exclusive releases from Sideshow, Blackcat and Blackcat Fever, both 1950s inspired condensed retro brush scripts were created by Sam Gambino, the famous artist and illustrator! Check ’em out at: http://www.fontbros.com/sideshow.php

Wilton - Sporting the largest addition to this months update, Robbie de Villiers has been hard at work on tons of amazing new formal scripts and playful releases. Check ’em out at: http://www.fontbros.com/wilton.php

We hope everybody is enjoying a fantastic holiday season and can’t wait to share more great new fonts with you in the coming months!

Warmly,
Mike & Stuart
The Font Bros
