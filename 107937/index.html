Unfortunately, it's their own corporate typeface called [TeleGrotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051441/http:/www.urwpp.de/cgi-bin1/dalcgi/source/schnellsuche_e.htd?searchchar=telegrotesk) and made by [URW++](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051441/http:/www.urw.de/english/fonts/identitype/telekom.html). If you're working officially for them, they should provide you a complete package... If not, try switching with [URW++ Grotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051441/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/grotesk) Narrow, far from perfect (main big difference is the single storey / [a](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051441/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/grotesk/urw-grotesk-t-regular-narrow/glyphs.html#glyphs/473308/66)) but could do the job. Other alternative: [Founders Grotesk Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051441/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fwww.klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/founders-grotesk-condensed), [Grotesque Display MTI](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051441/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/grotesque-display-mt)
