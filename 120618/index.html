[Cover lettering for Holiday Magazine](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/notetoself.typepad.com/note_to_self/2011/07/holiday.html) October 1959 issue so probably never released as a font. Best would have been compressed or more contracted versions of [Austin](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/commercialtype.com/typefaces/austin) Hairline, [Miller Banner](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/miller-banner) or [Chronicle Display](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/chronicle-display/overview) Compressed. As digital alternative but far less elegant or contrasted: [Ambroise](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typofonderie/ambroise-std) Firmin, [Onyx](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/onyx-mt), [Torino](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/torino)/ [Torino Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.fonts.com/font/monotype/torino/condensed), [Roman Compressed](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ortizlopez/ol-roman-compressed), [Fashion Didot](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bagraphics/fashion-didot), [Bodoni Campanile](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/bodoni-rr-campanile), [Moderno](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/moderno) Compressed, [Bordeaux](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/bordeaux), [Arsis](http://web.archive.org/web/20141019212416/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/arsis)
