The font I used is called Optima by Herman Zapf. The registration font is likely not something you can get digitally. They look very constructed to me. You could buy fonts that would get you close ( [Interstate caps](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516143518/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/font_frames.tpl?cart=1084803745615003&fontname=Interstate) or [FF DIN](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516143518/http:/www.fontfont.com/shop/) caps) but it's probably just as good to use plain old Arial. You must have the flight sim bug pretty bad! Edit: Patricia, agreed. I seriously doubt the author of that logo was working from an actual font due to the general lack of basic modulation cohesion.
