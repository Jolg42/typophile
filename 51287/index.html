Anonymous @ 10 Nov 2008 — 12:59pm
I don't know if you need an exact match, but if you do, your sample isn't in HY Headline Bold (sample below, condensed to 90%), although it's certainly a similar style. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20130207035853im_/http:/typophile.com/files/hangullll_5133.jpg) Most Korean foundries have their own versions of "Headline (헤드라인)" designs of this style, such as HY Headline, Rix Headline, etc. They may be offered in angled (_gak_, 각) or round (_dunggeun_, 둥근) versions, hence the HY Kak Headline, _kak_ being an alternate romanization of 각. "Headline" faces are also called _jemok_ (제목) faces, so you may also have to look through some _dunggeun jemok_ (둥근제목, round headline) faces offered by different foundries. I haven't been able to find an exact match. It's not one of the usual suspects (Hanyang, Baekmuk, Sandoll, etc.) and I've exhausted the few specimens I have. Online samples are too small and most often don't include the glyphs "한글!". If you need an exact match, I can investigate further.
