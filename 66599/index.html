I don’t think that there is an exact match for the masthead of »Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung« (FAZ). You might browse Dieter Steffmann’s [collection](http://web.archive.org/web/20131012030147/http:/moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/index.htm) for a start. For the headlines within the paper, FAZ employed a special version of Fette Gotisch called »FAZ Fraktur« made by [URW](http://web.archive.org/web/20131012030147/http:/www.urwpp.de/deutsch/home.html), which unfortunately is not used anymore since about two years.
