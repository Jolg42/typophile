Kudos to Kris Sowersby

Our own frequenter of Typohile and brilliant young type designer from New Zealand, Kris Sowersby, KLIM (is milk spelled backwards:-)) is the newest member of Village and his Feijoa type is soon to be released.
I have seen and admired his work here in the critique area and now it is finally out on the market! BRAVO! BRAVO! BRAVO!!!

Great work Kris and the best of luck to you!

ChrisL
