Uh, just have in mind that that glyph is not a 3, nor a z. It's an IPA character (I recall Bringhurst says something about it on 'Elements'). You can find that glyph in IPA fonts (there are some free IPA fonts available from [SIL](http://web.archive.org/web/20050826041018/http:/www.sil.org/computing/catalog/show_software.asp?id=12)).
