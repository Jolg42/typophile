Mwahahahaha... As a display face, I think it's not interesting enough to pursue - except maybe as a learning exercise. But if you have technical leanings you might very well be on your way towards some primo uncharted territory: a font that exudes strong character at very small sizes. The semi-serif aspect might be key, the darkness is there, you just need to loosen it up and shorten the descenders. But most of all, you need to experiment with how exagerated curves behave at like 6 points. If you go this way, plan on spending some money on hi-res output though. hhp
