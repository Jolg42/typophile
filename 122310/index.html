This smacks of custom type design. This logo replaced an earlier version that was set in Lubalin Graph Bold: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/lubalin-graph/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414165834/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/lubalin-graph/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/lubalin-graph/") The type in the new logo may be a heavily customized adaptation of Lubalin Graph Bold Italic: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414165834im_/http:/typophile.com/files/LubalinGraph_4495.png) Plume Ad Italic, by Dalton Maag, comes close for the |F| (and it’s not bad as a starting point for the |X|): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/daltonmaag/plume/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414165834/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/daltonmaag/plume/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/daltonmaag/plume/") New York to Las Vegas might work for the |O| (and it’s also not a bad starting point for the |X|): [http://www.fontbros.com/families/new-york-to-las-vegas/styles/regular](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414165834/http:/www.fontbros.com/families/new-york-to-las-vegas/styles/regular "http://www.fontbros.com/families/new-york-to-las-vegas/styles/regular")
