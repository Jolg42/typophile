I couldn't find anything particularly similar, but the best candidate starting points I would pick are [Keyboard](http://web.archive.org/web/20140830184705/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/keyboard-rr/) from Red Rooster, or [Onyx](http://web.archive.org/web/20140830184705/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/onyx-mt/) from Monotype. Both have similar contrast and shape to Dollhouse, but both would need a lot of changes, particularly to the terminals. Onyx at least has some ball terminals. - Mike Yanega
