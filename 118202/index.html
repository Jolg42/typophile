Okay, it appears your sample has a larger counter in the O than Impact has. If you substitute Impact's zero for its O, it is still a good approximation. Placard MT Condensed Bold is another option: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/placard-mt/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140906071930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/placard-mt/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/placard-mt/") or Brown Gothic Black: [http://www.fonts.com/font/shinntype/brown-gothic/black?SiteId=15](http://web.archive.org/web/20140906071930/http:/www.fonts.com/font/shinntype/brown-gothic/black?SiteId=15 "http://www.fonts.com/font/shinntype/brown-gothic/black?SiteId=15") or Helvetica Inserat: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica-inserat/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140906071930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica-inserat/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica-inserat/") or CG Triumvirate: [http://www.fonts.com/font/monotype/cg-triumvirate/compressed?SiteId=15](http://web.archive.org/web/20140906071930/http:/www.fonts.com/font/monotype/cg-triumvirate/compressed?SiteId=15 "http://www.fonts.com/font/monotype/cg-triumvirate/compressed?SiteId=15") or Benton Sans: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/benton-sans/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140906071930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/benton-sans/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/benton-sans/")
