Anonymous @ 21 Nov 2003 — 9:23am
James, a lot of graphic designers (and all major design associations) _do_ frown on spec work. It devalues the work, invites amateurism, and opens the artist to all kinds of intellectual-property vulnerabilities. Design contests are often tolerated when run by nonprofit organizations, but that's about it. Good design involves more than just coming up with a neat idea. It requires research, investigation, and getting to know the client. If you want good design, hire professionals and pay them for their time and work.

Something I often suggest to potential clients with limited budgets is to approach local design schools with their project proposals.
