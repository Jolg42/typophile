It appears to be (very nice) artwork based upon an old typeface digitized by Lime as [Extra Ornamental No. 2](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415144322/http:/www.dafont.com/extra-ornamental.font). As such, I don't think a font was directly used. This appears in the Solotype Catalog (metal, wood and photo type) as Tuscan Floral.
