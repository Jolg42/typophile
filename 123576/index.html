Type made for such boards or marquees doesn’t always have an equivalent in the metal type/phototypesetting/digital type worlds. It most likely had a basis in large wood type of the 19th century. I found many similar fonts, but no spot-on matches. For the numbers, Octin Sports is very similar: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/octin-sports/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415143936/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/octin-sports/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/octin-sports/")

EDITED--AGAIN: The large image shows a product as displayed by church supply houses such as Michigan Church Supply [http://www.michiganchurchsupply.com/-c-100\_842.html?sort=3d&page=1](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415143936/http:/www.michiganchurchsupply.com/-c-100_842.html?sort=3d&page=1 "http://www.michiganchurchsupply.com/-c-100\_842.html?sort=3d&page=1") I obtained the remaining numbers from that source and combined all numbers into a single number-set. I removed the white on black optical illusion which alters perception of weight and produced the following reference: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415143936im_/http:/typophile.com/files/HymnsetBonW_5092.jpg) I trust it will be helpful in selecting a substitute font where most letters would be a rough match for the casual reader. Octin College Semibold [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/octin-college/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415143936/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/octin-college/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/octin-college/") could perhaps be the closest. Too wide, but it has a similar weight and has angles inside the counters. Octin College is the same as Octin Sports [already suggested] but lacks the non-conforming serifs, except on \_1\_. Runner up: Directory Board JNL. It also has a similar weight and, but has rounded rather than angular corners inside the counters. Don
