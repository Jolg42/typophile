Anonymous @ 19 Feb 2008 — 2:14pm
It is not Helvetica because the lowercase 'a' right side goes straight down and does not have an extenstion like Helv. Also the figure 'one' in Helvetica doesn't have a straight top extenstion but a curved one. The parens are spot on Univers as is the lowercase 'g', comma, and numerals.
