Hi Eric, some similar square tech sans faces (most of them of pretty rough & unpolished ‘charme’): - [Baksheesh](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.identikalshop.com/font_ind.php?baseProductID=107) by [Stuart Brown](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Stuart%20Brown) - Blender and Regular by [Nik Thoenen](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Nik%20Thoenen) via [Binnenland](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.binnenland.ch/type/) - [BDR Mono](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.gestalten.com/fonts/detail/?id=be0db8100998aaec010a4f5def8e0003) by [Lopetz](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Lopetz) - [AutoScape](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.lineto.com/The+Fonts/Font+Categories/Text+Fonts/AutoScape/) and [Lutz Headline](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.lineto.com/The+Fonts/Font+Categories/Headline+Fonts/Lutz+Headline/) by [Cornel Windlin](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Cornel%20Windlin) (also [Alpha Headline](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.lineto.com/The+Fonts/Custom+Typefaces/Alpha+Headline/), available again in 2012) - [Arame](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dimitrelima/0arame/familytree.html) by [Dimitre Lima](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Dimitre%20Lima) - [Screener](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.canadatype.com/showfont.php?id=38) by [Patrick Griffin](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Patrick%20Griffin) - [Super Duty](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typeco/super-duty/) by [James Grieshaber](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/James%20Grieshaber) - [T26 Carbon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/t26/carbon/) and [T26 OCRX](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/t26/ocrx/) by [Anuthin Wongsunkakon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Anuthin%20Wongsunkakon) - [United Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.houseind.com/index.php?page=showfont&id=531&subpage=un_fonts_sans) by [House Industries](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/typophile.com/wiki/House%20Industries) - [some of the Neubau fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.neubauladen.com/catalogue/NB-Typography/3). Also have a look at [License plate typography](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019134902/http:/www.typophile.com/node/27362). F
