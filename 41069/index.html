_How should I go about being able to use these fonts for logos without getting into trouble?_ Unless they come with a EULA when you download them which prohibits usage in a logo you are in the clear. _Also, will the company be able to Copywrite the logo I gave them If I used that font?_ No. _What do the other companies do when they make logos?_ Some companies you fonts out of the box. Some take those fonts and tweak them to suit. Some hand-letter their own. Generally speaking there are very few fonts which you cannot use in logos and most of those are dingbat or picture fonts.
