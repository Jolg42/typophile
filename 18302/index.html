I have a sneaking suspicion that it _is_ Univers Condensed (either 57 or 59) with some handwork done. What suggests this possibility to me is the A without the crossbar; the slash O with the slash going in the wrong direction; and the 3, which is really a 5, slightly modified (actually, it’s a combination of a 5 and a 7).
