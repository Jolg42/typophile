You can start by subscribing to MyFonts.com or Fontshop.com mailing list. Furthermore, you can try visiting each distributed type foundry’s websites and subscribing to their blog feeds. This forum also host a ’Release’ section at [http://typophile.com/forum/7](http://web.archive.org/web/20091012074035/http:/typophile.com/forum/7 "http://typophile.com/forum/7") ILoveTypography.com also reviews newly released typeface every once in a while.
