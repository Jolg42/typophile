Any suggestions for Golf logo.

Good afternoon Ladies and Gentleman,

I work on a Golf magazine here in the UK, imaginatively entitled ’Golf Pages’, and we are currently redesigning the Logo (and most of the magazine).

The old logo was in ITC esprit, and a little tired. However, the new logo suggested by the creative director is in Alba, and looks a little too-cool-for-school.

We’re trying not to alienate the traditionally older audience (or the PGA who officially sponsor the magazine) but also to give the logo a bit of life to it and make it a little less stuffy. I was thinking of a modern looking serif rather than sans, as i feel it suits golf a little more.

However…the company wants to use a font we already own (in other words they’re tight) and I don’t think any of those we own are right, so I might buy one myself if its about £20-30.

Anonymous @ 10.Jan.2007 7.43am
I agree that you should go with a fresher-feeling logo to alleviate the potential for stuffiness. However, I’m not crazy with the current revision. It does seem a bit self-consciously modern, which I’m not sure is ideal for your magazine. Also, the combination of the L and F seems awkward, as if the L is stunted, and it creates an odd shape that looks like a U with a big top serif that distracts. My suggestion would be some sort of slab serif — different from the norm, and the big serifs remind me mildly of clubs. I’d go with a modern interpretation, along the lines of PMN Caecilia. My favorite after a quick search is Freight Micro, but it’s out of your price range. For a logo, though, most would recommend that you do something custom, or at least tweak a font significantly.
