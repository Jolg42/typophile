Anonymous @ 30 Mar 2008 — 10:30am
I could imagine it might be in a foundry's interest to establish such a deal -- something like you might find in book publishing, where a publisher negotiates first right of refusal for a popular author's next several titles. But in today's market, I think most freelance type designers would not be interested in any such restrictions. Note that back in Dwiggins's day, there were no such thing as royalties on sales of type. WAD's agreement amounted to something like a yearly retainer. So this would have been a more attractive arrangement. Things work differently now. -- K.
