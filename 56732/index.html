Corinthia and Olicana are far and away the best quality of the typefaces you mention. Be sure to set the bride and groom's names in the online sampler and I think you'll find the connections on the other fonts are not so good. You might also like [Avalon](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/font_bureau/avalon/), [Biscotti](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/font_bureau/biscotti_ot/), [La Portenia](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/sudtipos/la_portenia/), [Oxida](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/sudtipos/oxida_ot/), [Enchanted](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/borges_lettering/enchanted_regular/), [Brisa](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/sudtipos/brisa/), [Kewl Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.veer.com/partner/link.aspx?i=1048&c=4&p=products/typedetail.aspx?image=UMT0000387#specimen), and [Ministry Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419210234/http:/www.veer.com/partner/link.aspx?i=1048&c=4&p=products/typedetail.aspx?image=UMT0000149).
