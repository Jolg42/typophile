Tabular Periods and Spaces

I’m chiseling away at rather large OT family that includes
Tabular figures as alternates to the standard proportional figures.
I’ve designed and included monetary symbols to fit with the
Tabular figures but it dawns on me that periods and spaces
should match too. Maybe…

Am I crazy? Periods are really common within a range of numbers.
For example:
4545.094598
3458394.9084

And as shown above, would throw off the tabular setting.
Is this reasonable with OT?

Anonymous @ 20.May.2004 1.37pm
You \*can\* do it. But I don't think you need to. The only reason for tabular figures is to make them line up from one line to the next in a table. In such setting, it is expected that the numbers will be aligned on the decimal (a period in English, a comma in many other languages). So the width of that alignment character becomes irrelevant. Any separator characters will then also line up, so as long as one is consistent in their use (spaces, thin spaces, commas, periods, whatever one uses to do numbers like "1,000,000") Now, that's the question of necessity. A tabular period and comma would put more space around them in setting numbers. I am curious to hear folks' opinions about whether this would be aesthetically pleasing. Regards, T
