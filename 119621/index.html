The German expressionist font used in your poster is called Habsburg. It was designed by Heinrich (Heinz) Keune at J.G. Schelter & Giesecke, Leipzig, in 1903. He also designed a narrower version called Wittelsbach. Here are two fonts as shown in a S&G Schriften und Initialen type specimen book from the era ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110939im_/http:/typophile.com/files/HabsburgSM_6529.jpg) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110939im_/http:/typophile.com/files/WittelsbachSM_6650.jpg) Adapted from photos by Ben Mitchell on Flickr [https://www.flickr.com/photos/ohbendy/13911086473/in/photostream/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110939/https:/www.flickr.com/photos/ohbendy/13911086473/in/photostream/ "https://www.flickr.com/photos/ohbendy/13911086473/in/photostream/") AFAIK neither font has been digitized. However, as previously suggested, Moritz is almost identical and would work as a digital substitute. Don
