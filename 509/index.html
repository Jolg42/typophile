(x) Bauhaus Font of 1929 - various {Stephen}

Here is a very interesting and stylish font from the cover of the Bauhaus quarterly magazine. Scan from Typography/Basic Principles by John Lewis, published in 1966. This is the only book on typography I have in the house, as I am a poor student. Sniff. Still, some of the typographic ideas are stimulating, and this font inspired my font Feynman Regular.

Matthew, Here are some of the digital interpretations of Schmidt's design that I'm aware of... The beautiful people at Type-Ø-Tones did the most authentic Joostian [alphabet](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/www.type-o-tones.com/p_fonts/p_joost.htm) I've run across. There's a bit of history at that page too. [Jocelyn](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/findfonts_frame.asp?con=getfont.asp?pid=243426) by Johannes Birkenbach is a another take on the look. Those zany lowercase 'a's can be found in much of Finnish designer Tomi Haaparanta's work. See [Tantalus](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/66.70.183.115/fonts/fontshow/db.cgi?db=default&uid=default&ID=878&ww=on&view_records=View%2BRecords) and [Target](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/66.70.183.115/fonts/fontshow/db.cgi?db=default&uid=default&ID=807&ww=on&view_records=View%2BRecords), both at T-26. And here are some other wild variations, mostly based on the Type-Ø-Tones' Joost forms... [Gemini](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/www.fontalicious.com/shopping/FONstore.cgi?user_action=detail&catalogno=fam-0003) and [Tiajuana](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/www.fontalicious.com/shopping/FONstore.cgi?user_action=detail&catalogno=fam-0004) by Ben Balvanz of Font-a-licious [FF Marten](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/www.fontfont.de/packages/marten1060/marten1060.html) by Martin Wenzel [Gaffe](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/www.philsfonts.com/phils/sectionssep00/typegf9.html) by Rodney Fehsenfeld. (I'm not sure if this is available from GarageFonts anymore. Many of Fehsenfeld's fonts are gone from their site. This link is from a Google search which led to an old page at Phil's Fonts.) [Leger](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717231326/http:/66.70.183.115/fonts/fontshow/db.cgi?db=default&uid=default&ID=980&ww=on&view_records=View%2BRecords) by Bill Dawson Stephen
