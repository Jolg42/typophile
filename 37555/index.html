That's easy, without any script: 1. Choose "Tools" / "Action Set". 2. Set "Apply action set to" to "Fonts in the Fonts List". 3. Click on "..." 4. Click on "+" and choose all your .pfb fonts. 5. Set "Destination format" to "OpenType PS (.otf)". 6. Choose a destination folder. 7. Click on "OK", then "Run". Adam
