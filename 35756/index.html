I know of two InDesign (CS2&3) scripts that might be handy for what you're trying to do: -- Add Guides - draws guides around the selected object or objects -- Make Grid - divides the selected frame (or frames) into grid(s) of frames You can get the scripts on this page: [http://www.adobe.com/products/indesign/scripting/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150406023558/http:/www.adobe.com/products/indesign/scripting/ "http://www.adobe.com/products/indesign/scripting/") (click on the "Scripting Resources" tab)
