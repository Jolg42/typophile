Discord - new brazilian monoline sans serif

Hello,

In the the last days I’m working in a new design. The inspiration is in the aesthetics of machines an in the geometric typefaces used in CAD softwares. In the project I tried a friendly-eye approach through proportions, spacing (no manospaced) and optic adjustments in the weight of the lines.

http://www.rafaelneder.com/discord/

Please tell me what you guys think

After [a long period of development](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414193332/http:/www.typophile.com/node/28316) finally I released Discord. Inspired in the machine aesthetics and in the human conflict with them, Discord is a flexible family of sixteen fonts designed to be used in a wide range of work. Ready for multilingual support and with advanced OpenType features such as Small Caps and Old Style Numbers.
