Some of Manfred Klein’s Blackletter fonts for FontFont have loops on the ascenders, but that is an unusual feature. I think your sample looks hand-lettered, however. Manfred has generously made most of his fonts available for free at [TypOasis](http://web.archive.org/web/20071201164933/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/klein/index.htm), and he has helped with some of the [Blackletter revival](http://web.archive.org/) fonts on that site too, but I don’t think this is among them. - Mike Yanega
