For "MASA" you can use **[Freshman](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317161154/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Dafont-1&ffam=Freshman%20-%20Normal&fid=4e0a731a29f56f42431919e8cf234b9e&fsize=60&text=MASA&wrap=2)** free font by William Boyd or **[Collegiate Black FLF](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317161154/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Dafont-1&ffam=CollegiateBlackFLF%20-%20Regular&fid=722174466b9ed1961ab5b9b6d810afad&fsize=60&text=MASA&wrap=2)** by Casady & Greene [1988] "SPADL DESZCZ?" could be **[Bebas Neue](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317161154/http:/www.dafont.com/bebas-neue.font?text=SPADL+DESZCZ%3F)** free font by Ryoichi Tsunekawa [Dharma Type, 2010] "LEPIEJ DESZCZ": I don't have an exact match, but it looks close to **[PT Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317161154/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Google-Web-Fonts&ffam=PT%20Sans%20-%20Regular&fid=f2eadd43911fbf66f2bf5c8b32f69724&fsize=60&text=LEPIEJ%20DESZCZ&wrap=2)** by ParaType [2009] now part of Google Web Fonts "niz" is a modified **[Adobe Caslon (Bold Italic)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317161154/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Adobe&ffam=Adobe%20Caslon%20-%20Bold%20Italic&fid=5019f08eb3750eedae035769c2e102ca&fsize=60&text=niz&wrap=2)**
