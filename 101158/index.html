Photos of blank business cards, brochures, etc. can be found at many stock photo sites. For example, at iStockPhoto.com if I search for "business cards blank" I find numerous images similar to this: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023141056im_/http:/typophile.com/files/stock-illustration-22964126-business-cards-templates_3561.jpg) If you've got Photoshop you can easily take a business card mockup and apply it to the stock photo . Open the stock photo, then in a new layer add your card mockup, and then distort your card mockup to fit the photo's perspective by selecting the mockup and then Edit \> Transform \> Distort and drag the mockup's corners until they align with the card in the stock photo.
