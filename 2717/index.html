The identity for the 2000 "National Design Triennial: Design Culture Now" used tarzana quite well as the main signature typeface. It really capitalized on the 3 like form of the uppercase "E" The website for it can be found [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018222150/http:/ndm.si.edu/triennial/triennial.html). (it is also used it in the animated intro [page](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018222150/http:/ndm.si.edu/triennial/animation.html)) The book that accompanied the exhibition also used Tarzana. The book is called "Design Culture Now: National Design Triennial" you can have a closer look at the book on [amazon.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018222150/http:/www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/1568982151/ref=pd_sim_books_2/102-6103632-2921754?v=glance&s=books)
