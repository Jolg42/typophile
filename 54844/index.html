You can get this kind of book printing from an online service such as [Lulu.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021230217/http:/www.lulu.com/). [Acme Bookbinding](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021230217/http:/www.acmebook.com/) near Boston will give more personalized service. Both can ship internationally, but to a book printer, anything under 1000 copies is a very short run. If you plan to print several hundred copies, you may prefer to print them in Europe to avoid a very slow and/or expensive delivery. [IBT Global](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021230217/http:/www.integratedbook.com/index1.html) has a facility in London.
