I didn't spot this exact font -- it may be custom lettering -- but here are a couple of fonts with the same thick & thin inline effect Narcissus Open is a heavy typeface designed by Walter Tiemann in 1921 for the Klingspor Foundry in Germany based on ornamental inline capitals first cut Simon Pierre Fournier about 1745. [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/narcissus-sg/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140925113212/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/narcissus-sg/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/narcissus-sg/") and [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/narcissus/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140925113212/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/narcissus/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/narcissus/") Also Dominus [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/dominus-rr/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140925113212/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/dominus-rr/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/dominus-rr/") Don
