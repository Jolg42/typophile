Hobo, for everything except the |g|: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/hobo/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415095238/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/hobo/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/hobo/") For the |g|, Geometric 415 (Bitstream’s version of Metro No. 2) works nicely: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/geometric-415/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415095238/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/geometric-415/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/geometric-415/") And yes, the vibe is very 60s or 70s (Hobo saw a resurgence in use at that time), but the typeface actually dates from 1910.
