Dalton Maag Infoletter May out

Dalton Maag has just released its May infoletter. For an overview of the topics please read below.

Bruno Maag
Dalton Maag Ltd

  1. Your client needs a nice font

“Why do I need to buy a font? What is wrong with Arial!” If this is the
response you get when you try to convince your client to spend some money
on a good corporate font, Dalton Maag can help you…
More: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S1

  1. Ihr Kunde braucht eine schöne Schrift

“Weshalb brauche ich andere Schriften - ich hab doch Arial!” Diese Antwort
erhält man oft wenn man versucht, einen Kunden davon zu überzeugen, dass
er etwas ausgeben sollte für eine gute Schrift. Dalton Maag hilft Ihnen
mit Argumenten…
Mehr: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S1

  1. Flying is funky

When you fly you have the opportunity to collect Airmiles. Dalton Maag,
under art direction of London branding agency Lambie-Nairn, designed a
simple dot matrix style font as part of the new identity for Airmiles…
More: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S2

  1. Fliegen ist schöner…

Mit jedem Flug sammelt man Flugmeilen. Dalton Maag hat zusammen mit der
Branding Agentur Lambie-Narin ein einfaches Punktmatrix-Font für die neue
Identität von Airmiles gestaltet…
Mehr: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S2

  1. Linotype OpenType CD

OpenType is rapidly becoming the standard font format for the computer
industry. Linotype’s new OpenType CD contains all the classics from this
old type library in OpenType format.
More: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S3

  1. Linotype OpenType CD

OpenType ist sehr rasch zum Standard-Fontformat für die Computer-Industrie
geworden. Die neue Linotype OpenType CD enthält alle klassischen Fonts im
OpenType-Format.
Mehr: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S3

  1. Fargo renamed to Viato

Dalton Maag renames its Fargo font family to Viato after we were alerted
that some customers confused our typeface with FF Fago.
More: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S4

  1. Fargo wird Viato

Dalton Maag hat seine Schrift “Fargo” zu Viato umgetauft, nachdem wir
feststellen mussten dass es Verwechslungen mit der FF Fago gab…
Mehr: http://www.daltonmaag.com/news/38.html#S4

Metrics on italics: how to do it right?

Dear readers,

I am trying to do metrics for my italic serif-face Canapé, but I’m stuck right at the beginning…

For upright designs, I usually would space the “o” symmetrically, assigning (for example) 40em/1000 on each side of it. For “n” I would do similar.
When testing the settings with “nnnnn” and “ooooo”, this looks ok, and when typing “nonon”, it’s even as well. But not for italics obviously…

When doing the same thing here, the “nonon”-combination looks very uneven. It would only help to assign asymmetrical space on the “n” (i.e. 10 left, 50 right instead of 30 on both sides), shifting it into the right place, but this is not very practical. Even more, the “o” has to stay symmetrically spaced, otherwise the effect is cancelled. So this is very inconsistent and will lead to problems I assume.

Can anybody tell me how to do this right? At the moment I just don’t get where the problem is…

Thanks for your help
Sebilar

The best fonts you can't have...

Okay folks! Here are three of my favorites that you can’t have. These are unreleased- and most likely, will never be released- fonts that rock my little typographic socks.

Volvo Sans… used on all Volvo controls and switches. Its like Handel Gothic only more swedish-er.

Bloomindales… This swanky typeface has been strutting its stuff while the folks who made Chalet were not even born yet. [And no, Horatio doesn’t cut the mustard]

Lumene Script from Suomi… This is my favorite handwriting font ever! Perhaps when Lumene Cosmetics will change campaigns will then we have access to this genius face. Think of it as Emma Script with less caffeine. Plz Script is a significantly less refined alternative however the up-and-coming Suomi Script would be fascinating.

But good news in 2011 we get access to Dereon… a luscious scotch face and in 2008 Parisine Office from Porchez. :-)

Do you have exclusive fonts that you wish you could have… post images, names, and foundries.
