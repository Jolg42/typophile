ATypI conference promotion

Several individuals and companies who want to help promote this year’s ATypI conference have requested that we make web graphics available, so that these can be downloaded and used on websites as links to the conference site. These are now available and can be downloaded, with link code, from

http://www.atypi.org/40_conferences/28_Vancouver/48_promotion

There are four graphics: a banner ad, and three narrower ads of different sizes. If you want to help promote the conference in this way, please download the image or images that you want to use and serve them from your own web account, rather than linking directly to the images on the ATypI server.

If you would like to help promote the conference in other ways and are able to offer print media space or distribution, please contact Cynthia at the ATypI secretariat < secretariat@atypi.org>.

Many thanks to everyone who helps to make this conference a great success.

John Hudson
ATypI 2003 programme planning committee
www.atypi.org

A Font That Fights Cancer

Recently, Dan Bailey of Fontosaurus teamed up with Michael Jantze, creator of the nationally-syndicated (U.S.) comic strip, The Norm to create a font based on Michael’s comic lettering. Not unusual, in and of itself, but instead of profiting from this, we’re giving 100% of the proceeds to the Lance Armstrong Foundation. The LAF provides financial grants to research groups studying the disease and to non-profit organizations that assist people fighting the disease. If our wildest hopes came true, the proceeds from this font would fund the lab that found the cure, but we’d just be happy if we could help one person fighting the disease make it through one more day.

(That said, anyone else have any ideas of who I should contact to help spread the word?)
