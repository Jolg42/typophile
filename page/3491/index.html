Western New York Book Arts Collaborative

The Western New York Book Arts Collaborative exists to promote, encourage and develop the Printing Arts through education and access for individual artists and underserved communities. Through active programs such as lectures, workshops and exhibitions of all facets of the printed word and image — printmaking, papermaking, illustration, design, writing, binding — the long history of multiple editions from stone carving to the e-books can be explored and made accessible to the public as viewer and participant.

Cooper Old Style

Indices : Typefaces : Cooper Old Style

Cooper Old Style was designed by Oswald Bruce Cooper in 1919. It is the older, but lesser known sibling of Cooper Black. Cooper was a student of Frederic W. Goudy, and is evident in the very organic forms visible in Cooper’s types.

Complete Manual of Typography

Indices : Books : The Complete Manual of Typography

This book is a much needed contemporary guide to the ages old craft of typography. In the introduction, Felici explains that it is a guide to ’execution’ and not a style guide. Throughout it’s pages, he uses limited illustrations to explain such topics as the correct ‘color’ of a block of text, appropriate use of Small Caps, Tracking and Kerning, Managing Indentation and Alignment, Special Characters and Special Situations , Document Styles and Typopraphic Conventions, Tables and Language Specific Issues rounded off by a comprehensive glossary of typographic terms to make what is arguably the best technical reference manual on Typography to date.
