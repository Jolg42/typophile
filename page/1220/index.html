Help Stamp out Zero Response

Because we had a recent hiccup in Forum accessibility, I browsed the topic areas (I generally link to ’ tracker’) to note any topics that requested response and had “0” (zero, naught, nada) replies.

I was thinking back to a former member who became indignant and “ terminated his membership” from Typophile because no one replied to his request for critique. (Please, I don’t want to open old wounds, I’m just looking forward.)

I think members who frequent the Forum should try and be aware of any direct requests for assistance or critique older than two to three days. I know we can’t have the answers to everyone’s questions, but at least one member should make an attempt or at least acknowledge the post.

I think it’s a courtesy to those who are willing to become members and post topics or questions. Besides, it’s good for Karma.

I can’t ditch the family chosen for display type but I do have the option to choose a serif face to accompany it.

So I wondered if anyone would like to suggest directions to start in to help narrow down the choices? I guess the aim is to go for something with a contemporary feel — maybe with a generous x-height, extremely legible (for diversity purposes) and can tolerate tight fitting if necessary.
