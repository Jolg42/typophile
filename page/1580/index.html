Hey guys,

glad to see you’re still around :)

Some of you may remember the time I was posting messages about the evolution of the Simplease logo. It started more than 5 years ago and it had produced a fair amount of artefacts, which can be found inside the linked messages below:

  1. The original post introducing the company with it’s old name “Simplicious”
  2. Evolution of the Simplease logo – PART I
  3. Evolution of the Simplease logo – PART II
  4. Evolution of the Simplease logo – PART III

Over the last years we were relatively happy with our logo:

Nevertheless I was never really satisfied with how it had turned out and tried dozens of redesigns and spent hundreds of hours, working on concepts and ideas that all turned out to be mediocre at best.

Some days ago there was this idea of trying to set the word in a unicase type in the hope of ending up with a nicer and simpler overall appearance. I tried some different fonts and stumbled upon Designal from Félix Rufín.

I reworked the “L” to represent a lowercase “l” based on the underlying theme of DIN and worked on the overall rhythm by choosing a somewhat looser tracking. Finally I worked on the kerning and ended up with this:

It would be really great to get your feedback on the concept, choice of font, and especially the tracking and/or kerning.

Thank you very much :)

Request: Tempor sample

Greetings, fellow type enthusiasts!
A couple of years ago, I came across a thread on Typophile about the “Spice Girls font”, a typeface I’ve been intrigued by ever since I first saw it in 1998. This font has been searched for by many, many people many, many times, but has never been found. The Typophile thread I mentioned did, however, yield a possible lead: the pre-digital typeface called Tempor. (Thanks, Mike F!) According to my (admittedly limited) research, a specimen of sorts was published in The Solotype Catalog of 4,147 Display Typefaces (on page 157 according to Solopedia – though I might be wildly off there). Unfortunately I am unable to obtain a copy of this book at the moment, so I ask: if any of you could please be so kind to upload a scan of this specimen of Tempor, I would sincerely appreciate it. It’s a mystery I would love to help solve.
Oh, also, if anyone has any information on it, such as the name/s of its designer/s, foundry, known digitizations, I will love you forever!
Thanks for reading. :)
