Type and War

This past semester I had a graduate student taking my Digital Lettering class. Normally I have Sophmores and Juniors, but this guy was older and a lot more mature. After the first class we talked and I found out that he was a captain in the Marine Corps and he had been deployed to Iraq and was in Fallujah. I also found out that he was working on both a Masters in Design Technology, and an MBA at the same time. A very impressive guy.

Anyway he spent the semester designing a typeface that he wanted to use on a website he was creating. Not a bad first font. But it is the content and perspective that really recommends the site

http://2dinar.com/issues/pages/brief.php

TYPE BLOCK: photography + typography call for participants

TYPE BLOCK: photography + typography
an UPPERCASE project

International artists are invited to explore typography in their surroundings by documenting what they see within one city block of where they live or work.

Participants can post their images in a Flickr pool for the online exhibition. UPPERCASE gallery owner, Janine Vangool, and photographer Kirstie Tweed (Orange Girl Photographs) will curate from these images to create a physical exhibition and companion book. The exhibition will be part of Exposure, the Banff-Calgary Month of Photography (February 2008).
