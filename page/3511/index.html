New website of the Designiq studio

In April, the graphic design studio Designiq launched a new English version of its website at www.designiq.eu. As before, it provides all elementary information about the studio, while its portfolio section has been significantly extended, offering a number of new work examples accompanied by a commentary. It also presents a new News section.

From larger recent projects, the website represents examples of new graphic design of Respekt weekly, of the book Móda / Fashion presenting the work of fashion designer Liběna Rochová, posters for the theatre festival Příští vlna / Next Wave, and many others.

Filip Blažek’s graphic design studio was founded in 1997, in 2003, its name has been changed to Designiq. It focuses on two main fields of work — the design of logotypes and corporate identity, and the promotion of groups and institutions from cultural sphere. The studio employs degree-educated designers with extensive work experience. The website was designed by Filip Blažek and Radek Sidun, and it is powered by Vizus CMS system.

Letras Latinas Biennial 2006

Submitted by paul d hunt : 12.Apr.2006 11.22am

April 2006.

The Latin Letters Biennial 2006 is pleased in announcing the 70 works selected by the jury during days April 2-3 in the city of São Paulo, Brazil. The members of the jury wanted to emphasize the remarkable advance in the quality of the 427 received works. In this context the important growth is evident that has had our discipline in the region.

Thanks to the Judges of the Letras Latinas Bienial 2006.
Francisco Calles Trejo (*)
Luciano Cardinali
Juan Carlos Darias
Priscila Farias
Rubén Fontana
Vicente Lamónaca
Candelaria Moreno
César Puertas
Rodrigo Ramírez

(*) A Few days before the moment of the oath, Francisco Calles Trejo informed the organization of the Biennial that unfortunately he could not travel to be present at the moment of the oath. As opposed to this situation, the organization decided that Luciano Cardinali would occupy the place of the eighth juror, and Francisco Calles Trejo would be the ninth juror, sending their commentaries and decisions electronically.
