Veer Announces: New Jukebox Typefaces for May 2004

Veer Announces: New Jukebox Typefaces for May 2004

Veer is pleased to announce the addition of two new typefaces to our very own Jukebox collection. These typefaces can be found in the May edition of the Veer Visual Elements Catalog and on our web site.

…………………………………………………………………………………………………………………………..

Stephanie Marie by Jason Walcott { Jukebox } They say lower loops in handwriting indicate sensuality. No wonder new Stephanie Marie is so attractive. A lively, brush-style script font, named after a dear friend of the designer. Contains several alternate characters and ligatures. One weight.

…………………………………………………………………………………………………………………………..

Fanfare by Jason Walcott { Jukebox } Drum roll please… Fanfare marches out with jubilant style. A happy, shiny headline typeface reminiscent of game show and situation comedy titles from the late 60s and early 70s. One weight.
