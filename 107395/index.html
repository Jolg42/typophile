I suspect that your sample is most likely a PhotoLettering font from the 1960s or 1970s, but very little of their catalog is digitized. A competitor, Filmotype, has several digitized fonts that are somewhat similar, particularly Filmotype Hamlet and Filmotype Harmony: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/filmotype/hamlet/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422055018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/filmotype/hamlet/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/filmotype/hamlet/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/filmotype/harmony/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422055018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/filmotype/harmony/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/filmotype/harmony/") Also Filmotype Honey, as already mentioned.
