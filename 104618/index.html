Sorry, don't know the original name of the typeface this signage was made from. My first thought was " [Futura](http://web.archive.org/web/20140823045225/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/futura)" but after a closer inspection, too many details are off. That's clearly not solving this case and you're probably not after this kind of answer but could be easily redrawn from Futura: /C finials "à-la-/G", lowering /H crossbar, highering /A crossbar More alternatives: **[Erbar](http://web.archive.org/web/20140823045225/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/erbar)** (could work as it, probably the closest I could get), [Simplicita](http://web.archive.org/web/20140823045225/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/semplicita-pro) (quite same issues as Futura), [Simplo](http://web.archive.org/web/20140823045225/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/durotype/simplo) (same issues as Futura + /R off), [Journal Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20140823045225/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/journal-sans) (/K and /R off)
