That may not be a “unicase” font but rather could be a logo basedon an existing font. You can find other unicase fonts here [http://www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=unicase](http://web.archive.org/web/20070629151842/http:/www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=unicase "http://www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=unicase") Your font reminds me of Chalet Paris 1960 at House Industries but for the tail on the R and the m. Also shades of Avant Garde.

It’s likely they used [Kravitz Thermal](http://web.archive.org/web/20070629151842/http:/www.dafont.com/font.php?file=kravitz&page=1&nb_ppp_old=50&text=everbloom&nb_ppp=50&classt=alpha) by Ben Belvanz which I’ll bet is based on some custom lettering from a Kravtiz album. The font isn’t one of Ben’s better attempts. That m is a mess. An alternative is [Futura Maxi](http://web.archive.org/web/20070629151842/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontpackage&startrow=1&records=&currpage=1&totalrecords=4&displayfontid=C.106298.0.0&fontRendererText=everbloom) whose super high x-height should make the unicasing an easier task.
