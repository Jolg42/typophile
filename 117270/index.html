Sorry, it sounds probably helpless and lazy but to me, this logotype has nothing really distinctive. You could re-do the same starting with any standard/neutral/basic sans of your choice, simply editing it a bit. The only thing you need to keep in mind is /k design. You could start from [Helvetica Neue](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725021719/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/helvetica+neue) to [Myriad](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725021719/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/myriad), [Trebuchet](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725021719/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/trebuchet) or [Arial](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725021719/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/arial)... My choice here, Trebuchet Bold, 15 min reworked ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725021719im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Untitled-1_4691.png)
