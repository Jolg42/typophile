Well, I took this last one as a challenge, and I think it has defeated me. The counter on the P and R is too big for anything I can find. The J, K, C and S are also hard to find in combination anywhere; and of course the low Ä is really _not_ common at all. I pretty much hunted the condensed sansserifs at MyFonts, and the best I could come up with were Storm’s [’Ohrada’](http://web.archive.org/web/20081030030847/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/ohrada/), Berthold’s [’AG Condensed’](http://web.archive.org/web/20081030030847/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/akzidenz-grotesk-bq/medium-condensed/) and [’Silverscreen’](http://web.archive.org/web/20081030030847/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typeart/silverscreen/), from TypeArt. This is a good mystery, because condensed sans faces are used a lot, but I am coming to the conclusion that this is from an obscure source — maybe a small European foundry. - Mike Yanega
