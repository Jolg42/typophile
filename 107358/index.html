Quirky 1970's inline display face

I am looking to identify the original name/designer of the font used in the uppermost catch-line of this advertisment from 1977 (“It’s syn-ful!”) … and also in the company name seen on the cover of their 1977 catalogue.

A digital version of this face called “Upperville” was published by Bay Fonts in the early 90’s, but this was an absolute travesty of digitzation, mostly likely an autotrace and with many forms different to the few printed samples I’ve seen. I am looking for more original printed samples, preferably the full set of glyphs, and the only way I will be able to do this is to know the original typface name. Knowing the designer/foundry will also help me to track down the copyright holder and to understand if that is still current.

Font: 

ITC Uptight Regular

Note in "ARIES MUSIC" that S in MUSIC matches Uptight/Uptown while S in ARIES is different. This suggests to me that R, M and C may also be modifications. The font has the elements for the R. Merely mirror the 9 and do a little editing for the leg. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422054731im_/http:/typophile.com/files/UptightUptownRmodified_4613.jpg) M, C and S are even easier to modify. With the assistance of fvilanakis's list, and Don's identification of ITC Uptight I've managed to locate some possible print sources of the original. I also now see that this request has been posted here before: [http://typophile.com/node/47014](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422054731/http:/typophile.com/node/47014) I doubt whether a synthesizer company would be creating their own custom letters for a few print ads, and indeed the previous identifier, Mark Simonson, states that ITC Uptight Regular (as opposed to Neon) did indeed have several alternate letterforms which have been used in the samples I have posted here. Most modern sources such as Rookledge's Classic International Typefinder list only ITC Uptight Neon, not Uptight Regular. But at least now I have what I need. Thanks all!
