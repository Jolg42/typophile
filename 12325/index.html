Gabor Kothay

Indices : Designers : Gábor Kóthay

Gábor Kóthay was born July 19th, 1962. He works as a graphic designer and teaches second-form art students. Typeface design was a hobby for many years but it has become an everyday routine with Fontmunkások and Fontana Type Foundry. He lives with his wife and two daughters in a suburb of Szeged, a sunny southern Hungarian town that lies on the banks of the Tisza river.

Typefaces by Gábor Kóthay: Ambient, Incognito, La Danse, SchwartzKopf, Zanzibar, Zephyr
