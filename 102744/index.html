Commando Group website uses [P22 Underground](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108190121/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/p22/underground-basic). By switching to the [Pro version](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108190121/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/p22/underground-pro), you could get the [single storey /a](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108190121/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/p22/underground-pro/p22-underground-pro-book/glyphs.html#glyphs/385020/998) available as stylistic alternate through OpenType features. More in the same vein: [Brown](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108190121/http:/lineto.com/The%20Fonts/Font%20Categories/Text%20Fonts/Brown), [Fugue](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108190121/http:/www.radimpesko.com/fonts/fugue), [Relative](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108190121/http:/www.colophon-foundry.org/fonts/relative), [Value Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108190121/http:/www.colophon-foundry.org/fonts/value/sans)
