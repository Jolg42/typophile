I would say "correct" depends a lot on your budget! :-/ I say this because ideally you'd like to have a custom font (or a custom extension to a font you're using) that handles everything you need, but that can be very expensive... On the other extreme you could do a passable job by carefully choosing free fonts; you won't get a high level of visual harmony, but people will certainly be able to read it. Somewhere in the middle: choose a libre (free to modify) font that has as much of what you need as possible, and pay somebody to extend it. It all depends on how visually picky you are. hhp
