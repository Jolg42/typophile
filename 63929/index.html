Bravissima Script : : Bon appétit

Submitted by Ale Paul : 5.Nov.2009 1.53pm

SUDTIPOS :: NEW RELEASE :: 11.2009

Bravissima Script is the dynamic and spirited embodiment of the 1970s, when food was food and the wild brush ruled. It tells you to eat, and to do it right now. Another perfect blend of traditional Koziupa calligraphy and Paul art direction and tech, spiced up with OpenType features like the meal of your dreams. A personal favorite for food packaging design, especially hot stuff. Bon appétit.
