The Trajan column inscription was masterfully rendered thank to G.F. Cresci and Luca Pacioli’s drawings. Cresci arrived in Rome from Milan on 1550; six years later was nomined “Scriptor Latinus” of the Library, and on 1560, keeping his appointment, calligrapher for the Cappella Sistina, the time he publish is writing book “Il Perfetto Scrittore”.
