My inspiration for this typeface originated when I was walking around the Nelson museum looking at early Mayan pottery. I found the hieroglyphics to be very interesting in their shape and use of line. I thought this would make a great font. I have chosen to work with the earlier style of Mayan hieroglyphics instead of the later style which is more decorative. I am still continuing to work on this font so any suggestions would be greatly appreciated. Thanks, Megan ![Mayan Typeface](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017115711im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/29/7928.gif)
