_the magic of opentype_ The thing is, OpenType scripts have concentrated on capturing the beauty of formal calligraphy. If the same amount of genius that went into Zapfino Extra Pro or Caflisch Pro were to be put into mimicing "ordinary" writing, then we'd see some different results.
