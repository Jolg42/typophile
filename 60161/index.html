I don’t think a classical lead typeface (Garamond, Bembo …) suits best for engraving. It should be a typeface which has something engraving-like in the very nature of its style. Of the well-known this could be something like Didot or a classical script face. If you’re looking for something more fancy, look at [http://typophile.com/node/58746](http://web.archive.org/web/20100201041405/http:/typophile.com/node/58746 "http://typophile.com/node/58746") – its perfect for engraving (and all your other wedding stationary).
