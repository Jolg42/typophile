For some more specific ’chancery’ suggestions, you could look at Zapf Chancery Italic (the ’k’ is not the same in the regular version), or Bitstream Cataneo. All the other Chancery-ish styles I have found have a looped ’k’. (Corsiva, Poetica, Lucida Calligraphy and Le Griffe, to mention four.) - Mike Yanega
