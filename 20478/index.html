for some reason, I haven't been able to get on all day today (was typophile down or was it just me?) Anyway... WOW! I had no idea there were so many options out there. Here's the rundown (my opinion only) - Escrow - Not quite curly enough (old looking) - Precosia - Very nice, might be a bit hard to read at smaller sizes, lightbox material - American West - Close, but a bit too cliche western looking - Maverick's Luck - Close, add that one to the lightbox - Kilkenny - might be a bit too light - Missionary - I couldn't have asked for a more exact font for what I asked, but it's too thin, too - Calliope - getting really close here, add this one to the lightbox too - Eccentric - I didn't even think of something like that, could work - Berolina - Again, a direction I didn't even think of, I love this board! - Cafe Corina - VERY CLOSE! In fact, I plugged away in that site a bit, Billhead and Tyler are almost perfect. Added all three to the lightbox Okay, I just got some further direction.
