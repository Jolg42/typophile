The style is known as "prisma" which was particularly popular during the 60s and 70s. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140911043309im_/http:/typophile.com/files/churchward_4010.png) [Chrurchard Design-Lines](http://web.archive.org/web/20140911043309/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/churchward/design-lines/) is just one of many variations. ITC AKI Lines is another, popular as a phototype film font but I haven't seen digitized.
