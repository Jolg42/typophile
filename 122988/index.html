This lettering is a pastiche of styles. Note for example the median spurs -- found in many wild west fonts --and the foxtail terminals There are some fonts that catch some of the flavor of the lettering. An interesting substitute is Jonathan Barnbrook's Mason [http://www.emigre.com/EF.php?fid=104](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317111840/http:/www.emigre.com/EF.php?fid=104 "http://www.emigre.com/EF.php?fid=104") and Others include Midnight-Show [https://creativemarket.com/Alterdecofont/15306-Midnight-Show-typeface](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317111840/https:/creativemarket.com/Alterdecofont/15306-Midnight-Show-typeface "https://creativemarket.com/Alterdecofont/15306-Midnight-Show-typeface") Exocet by Jonathan Barnbrook [http://www.emigre.com/EF.php?fid=95](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317111840/http:/www.emigre.com/EF.php?fid=95 "http://www.emigre.com/EF.php?fid=95") and the Dogma movie font, a fan effort as Avqest [http://www.fontspace.com/gemfonts/avqest](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317111840/http:/www.fontspace.com/gemfonts/avqest "http://www.fontspace.com/gemfonts/avqest"). Jeremy Dooley's Newcomen [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/insigne/newcomen/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317111840/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/insigne/newcomen/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/insigne/newcomen/") Don
