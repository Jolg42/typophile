The font looks like Arabella designed by A. Drescher (1936) (see Jaspert’s ’Encyclopaedia of Type Faces’). The ’L’ in the book is like the sample, but the sample’s G has an added flourish on the tail, compared to the showing in the book. This might have been an added detail. - Mike Yanega
