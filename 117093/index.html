According to this [post](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724104521/http:/www.myfonts.com/WhatTheFont/forum/case/32999) on WTF Forum should be **Letraset English Tudor** , an open face blackletter with open areas filled in. > This sample is actually a typeface called English Tudor which was dewigned by Letraset in 1981 as dry-transfer lettering. > English Tudor was an open face black letter, the sample shown is someone filled in the open areas to make a solid version for the logo shown. > English Tudor has not been digitized as of yet, it could happen by having an open and a solid version. > You can find English Tudor in Letraset Graphics Manual (1981-1997). May be having a look to [Dieter Steffmann blackletter collection](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724104521/http:/moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann) might worth a shot.
