SG & RR digitizations both acceptable in my opinion. If you prefer a more hairline effect go for the RR. If you prefer less contrast go for SG. To compare with original check the [Ludlow Typefaces](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414065726/http:/archive.org/details/LudlowTypefacesEditionD1140) book at archive.org. Ultra Modern regular and bold is at pages 166 to 169. Note that Ludlow also produced an italic. Don
