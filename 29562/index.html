Anonymous @ 16 Nov 2006 — 10:42pm
It's ok to use Avenir for longer text matter, I think. At least, I have done that a few times ; ). Mind that you use a fair amount of leading and that you check the basic kerning (the lighter faces need more than is built–in…). BTW You can't go wrong if you combine it with a Frutigerdesigned serif. There is something very consistent in Mr F's designs. That may have to do with his exeptional drawing skills and his great knowledge of signs (he wrote about signs — a fascinating book: Der Mensch und seine Zeichen).
