Anonymous @ 30.Mar.2005 5.18am
"has the accessibility symbol" That's not an accessibility symbol. It's a 'handicapped' symbol or 'wheelchair accessible' symbol. accessibility \> physical disability. I'd seriously consider trying to come up with your own if you can. Check out what Apple use's for their icon. I co-opted it for a quick icon on our site: [http://www.courts.state.mn.us/](http://web.archive.org/web/20071103083931/http:/www.courts.state.mn.us/) Now, if you're trying to design a sign for handicapped parking, then it makes a lot of sense, but for accessibility in general, not so much.
