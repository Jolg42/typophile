I think you should get Pro Edition. If you are getting another PC for a member of your family who just use the PC for basic internet use or school work, then get Home. If you are getting a PC put together from components, think about getting the OEM version of whichever edition you choose. Its half price ... one caveat is that the license is tied to the hardware, it means you cannot move that version of windows to another PC after Activation (30 days). If you don’t activate during that 30 days, you can’t do Windows update (a simple workaround was published in slashdot regarding this). What happens if you don’t activate? Answered here ( [http://www.annoyances.org/exec/forum/winxp/t1010414167](http://web.archive.org/web/20080204005144/http:/www.annoyances.org/exec/forum/winxp/t1010414167 "http://www.annoyances.org/exec/forum/winxp/t1010414167")) So after activation you will not be allowed to install it to another PC even if your PC exploded! Anonymous @ 30.Jul.2005 12.13pm
The biggest difference for normal home use seems to be that the Home edition makes it harder for you to play around in the system files. This can normally be got around by using safe mode. I’ve had to do this once or twice to remove nasty viruses. Nearly all default settings treat you like you’re an idiot, but they can mostly be changed. If it’s for ’nothing special, really...’ I’d save your money and go with Home. Ed. (ps. yes, as Edwh said, the first things to get are a firewall\* and antivirus software\*\*, don’t trust the ones that come built in. \* [Zone Alarm](http://web.archive.org/web/20080204005144/http:/www.zonelabs.com/store/content/catalog/products/sku_list_za.jsp?lid=dbtopnav_zass) and \*\* [AVG](http://web.archive.org/web/20080204005144/http:/free.grisoft.com/doc/Get+AVG+FREE/lng/us/tpl/v5) keep my PC clean for free.)
