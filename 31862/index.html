FF Hydra all the way, Kenny. It has killer ink traps and comes in three widths -- [FF Hydra](http://web.archive.org/web/20110526211119/http:/www.fontshop.com/showfont.cfm?fid=FF.11346.0.0) (narrowest), [FF Hydra Extended](http://web.archive.org/web/20110526211119/http:/www.fontshop.com/showfont.cfm?fid=FF.11347.0.0) and [FF Hydra Text](http://web.archive.org/web/20110526211119/http:/www.fontshop.com/showfont.cfm?fid=FF.11463.0.0) (widest). Everyone I recommend it to falls head over heels for it.
