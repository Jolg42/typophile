You're scanning this guy in? I'd go for one of the thin sharpies and try again. Put more of your own natural touch to the letters. (I highly doubt you draw a lower case g like that.) A good thing to do is open up other fonts in your editing program, or in Illustrator, and look at the details of each letter-form. But don't be afraid of the Comic Sans comparison. If you're just getting into designing a font, you could do worse :)
