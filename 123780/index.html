One option would be to echo your Tuscan terminals in a restrained low contrast text typeface such as Zilvertype [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/zilvertype-pro/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317145207/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/zilvertype-pro/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/zilvertype-pro/") or Erasmus [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/erasmus-rr/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317145207/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/erasmus-rr/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/erasmus-rr/") More fonts with similar semi-Tuscan treatment [http://typophile.com/node/119951](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317145207/http:/typophile.com/node/119951 "http://typophile.com/node/119951") Don
