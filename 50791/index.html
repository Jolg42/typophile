Rich — I don’t know the story, but if you ever decide to try researching further, this info from the finder’s guide to the BPL Dwiggins Collection might help give you a head start. The relevant material is in Box #72. United States Postage Stamp Project: folder 24 — correspondence; ff. 25-26 — drawings & sketches; f. 27 — postal cards with sketches (annotated); ff. 28-29 — photographs of design by other artists; f. 30 — memos & clippings regarding project; ff. 31-34 — reference materials. BTW, folders just after this in the same box are related to the currency project. — Kent.
