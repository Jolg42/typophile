Typography term: Centered setting with decreasing line lengths

Is there a term in English for this kind of setting often seen in historical books? Centered setting with decreasing line lengths to make a roughly triangular shape.
See attached example of a Dutch book from the middle of the 18th century; I know the Hipnerotomachia Poliphili has beautiful ones too.

Florian Hardwig [pointed me](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415073820/https:/twitter.com/hardwig/status/425989840782766080) to [Haslam](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415073820/http:/books.google.nl/books?id=_Ri63jEKPfgC&lpg=PT47&dq=Haslam+%22Tapered+centring%22&pg=PT48&redir_esc=y#v=onepage&q=Haslam%20%22Tapered%20centring%22&f=false) who calls this “tapered centring”. (German: Spitzkolumne) Thanks!
