I haven't made it it Ire, yet, but there seems to be a lot of great [hand-lettered pub signs](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315165457/http:/www.flickr.com/photos/pauldhunt/sets/72157602552287340/) all around England. If you wanna beat the cliché, pick an [uncial](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315165457/http:/typophile.com/wiki/uncial): [FontShop](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315165457/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=uncial), [MyFonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315165457/http:/www.myfonts.com/browse/keyword/uncial/). Typophile Dan has compiled [a list of gems](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315165457/http:/www.typeoff.de/?p=183), and he has designed [a nice one himself](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315165457/http:/www.typeoff.de/?p=24), too.
