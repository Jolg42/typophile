Thanks for the feedback, all. There are a number of reasons why the upgrade was made, but I’d just like to point out that the effort is on-going. We could have very well left Typophile offline for a few more days to work out the kinks, but we wouldn’t want anyone to miss out on opportunities to discuss various typographic issues :) I will let Christian or Jared speak to details regarding code related and server based problems we are currently facing, but I can assure you that everyone is working hard to fix the bugs and create a better experience for everyone. We recognize the prolems and it is very frustrating for all. The slowness we are experiencing is related mostly to our server and again, we are exploring options for upgrading to a server package with enough muscle to support the thousands of users on the site, as well as the millions of pages that Typophile serves up. Here is a comment by Christian from a different, related thread: “During the upgrade millions of cached records were dumped from the database. It will take a little bit for these caches to be rebuilt. In the mean time the server is taking a beating.” Thanks for your patience.
