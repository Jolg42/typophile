Anonymous @ 16 Apr 2014 — 2:48pm
I am not certain what you mean by "packaging with a devilish slant." This could range from a cute Valentines' day devil to a heavy metal like Graveblade [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/graveblade/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417083110/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/graveblade/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/graveblade/") -- which is definitely an attention grabber and not too "decorative" in the swirly sense of Helena. Another option is a script like Excalibur [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/scholtz/excalibur-scf/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417083110/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/scholtz/excalibur-scf/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/scholtz/excalibur-scf/") -- pointy in a casual way. If you don't mind making letters, you could apply a motif like this [http://www.shutterstock.com/pic-56239237/stock-vector-devil-icon.html?sr...](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417083110/http:/www.shutterstock.com/pic-56239237/stock-vector-devil-icon.html?src=vVNCXtoJFAkQruigYqN8Dw-1-9 "http://www.shutterstock.com/pic-56239237/stock-vector-devil-icon.html?src=vVNCXtoJFAkQruigYqN8Dw-1-9") to the initial letter of your logo from an uncomplicated "friendly" font of your choice and leave the rest unchanged.
