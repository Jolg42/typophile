"Oregon" was set in [Handel Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150302015932/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/handel-gothic/handel-gothic/testdrive.html?s=OREGON&p=48) and customized\*. I really don't know what the big 'O' should be: it's very difficult to ID a font just by one character that carries so little specific traits. \*stretched horizontally, converted the upper left curve on the "E" to a corner, flipped the "U" for the "N" and also converted the upper left curve to a corner. Not too difficult to recreate.
