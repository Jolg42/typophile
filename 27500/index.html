The [Jukebox](http://web.archive.org/web/20080906232801/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Jukebox) collection is a Veer product, seeing as [Jason Walcott](http://web.archive.org/web/20080906232801/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Jason%252BWalcott) produces new typefaces under contract to us specifically for Jukebox.
