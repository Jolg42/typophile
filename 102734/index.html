Sorry, can't identify them, possibly even hand-drawn to me. Some alternatives though. CLARK & CO.'S: part of oblique cut terminals, reminds me of [Trade Gothic Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/trade-gothic)/ [Alternate Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/alternate-gothic). Some I like: [Noga](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontboutique/noga) (/C to be customized), [DIN Next Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/din-next) (/S off), [DIN Text Compressed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/parachute/pf-din-text-comp-std) (/S off), [Pragmata](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fsd/pragmata-pro) (/A, /R and /K off but not hard to customized), CA [Geheimagent](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-geheimagent) (too squared but nice vintage feeling), [BND](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-bnd), [No Dr.](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-no-dr), [Cula](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-cula), [Aires](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-airespro) SEWING COTTON: [Hamilton](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/scriptorium/hamilton), [Antinomy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/baselinefonts/antimony), [Ultramarina](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420085242/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/juan-jose-lopez/ultramarina)
