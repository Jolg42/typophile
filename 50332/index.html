You won’t likely find Bodoni in the body copy of any newspaper. But it’s still found as headletter in some papers. The Washington Post is still using a custom headline Bodoni that Matthew Carter designed for them, called Postoni. You can check it out at the Newseum. [http://www.newseum.org/todaysfrontpages/hr.asp?fpVname=DC\_WP&ref\_pge=lst](http://web.archive.org/web/20081009203335/http:/www.newseum.org/todaysfrontpages/hr.asp?fpVname=DC_WP&ref_pge=lst "http://www.newseum.org/todaysfrontpages/hr.asp?fpVname=DC\_WP&ref\_pge=lst") Can’t think of any others off the top of my head, but I’m sure others will chime in. — Kent.
