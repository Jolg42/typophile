I like my Garamonds paired with sans serifs that have a late 19th- or early 20th-century flavor: The Benton gothics: News Gothic Franklin Gothic Trade Gothic Benton Sans (Font Bureau) The unassuming grotesques: FF Bau (FontShop) Monotype Grotesque Super Grotesk (esp. the B weight) (FontShop) Maple (Process Type Foundry) The more-interesting-or-at-least-less-often-used-than-Futura geometrics: Metro Erbar Nobel (DTL or Font Bureau) Proxima Nova (Mark Simonson)

Futura is surprisingly nice paired with Galliard, which has some of the same genes as Garamond. Something about the simplicity and hardness of Galliard and the proportions of Futura keep them in harmony. But if you really want Adobe Garamond, which is much softer, you could look at Cronos, or one of OurType's sans designs might be right: Eva (new), Fresco Sans or Versa Sans.

I like my Baroque paired with a humanist. And two of them that has worked the best for me, believe it or not, are Scala and Quadraat Sans. For a less common alternative, try Fontfarm's [Kofired](http://web.archive.org/web/20130224074723/http:/fontfarm.de/themes/fonts/kofired/index.php) or John Sans. For an alternative to Futura, try an architectural sans (how do we come up with such term anyway?) like Verlag or Neutraface.
