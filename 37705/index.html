Nice, but I find that something isn’t in place. IMO, you have two options: 1. make the “pixel” G (nice) more like the one from the font you used (by “condensing” it, so to speak) 2. use a font with a G more like the pixel one you made (you used Helvetica Neue Condensed, but the pixel G does not look like a condensed font). Bye A.
Anonymous @ 2.Oct.2007 6.32am
The text should line up with the ’G’ inside the box. Also, I wouldn’t recommend combining raster graphics with vector graphics unless you need to incorporate a photo. It’s easy enough to redraw your ’G’ logo as a vector graphic, which will help eliminate the anti-aliasing around the edges. - Lex
