Your sample is a bit low-res for an accurate match. It closely resembles Optima: [http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/optima\_pro\_bold/ot\_ps/s...](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181035/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/optima_pro_bold/ot_ps/specimen/tester/ "http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/optima\_pro\_bold/ot\_ps/specimen/tester/") But I haven't been able to find an Optima-like typeface with a left-facing serif at the apex of the A. These two fonts (which are basically identical) by Steve Jackaman also come close: [http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/creative\_alliance/castle\_book/pc...](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181035/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/creative_alliance/castle_book/pc_tt/specimen/tester/ "http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/creative\_alliance/castle\_book/pc\_tt/specimen/tester/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/salzburg-rr/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181035/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/salzburg-rr/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/salzburg-rr/") Same for this font, which came bundled with Windows 7: [http://www.whatfontis.com/IrisUPC-Bold.font?text=DATACOM](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181035/http:/www.whatfontis.com/IrisUPC-Bold.font?text=DATACOM "http://www.whatfontis.com/IrisUPC-Bold.font?text=DATACOM") Also close: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/softmaker/columbia-serial/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181035/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/softmaker/columbia-serial/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/softmaker/columbia-serial/")
