Typography Books with their own websites

It seems that typography books lately come with the added bonus of a companion website. I only knew of two so far…

Thinking with Type (by Ellen Lupton)

Designing with Type (by James Craig, William Bevington, and Irene Korol Scala; now in its 5th edition)

…but now I just saw a link on Design Observer for the 4th edition of the book

Typographic Design: Form and Communication (by Rob Carter, Ben Day, and Philip B. Meggs).

It’s nice to have these resources online, no?
