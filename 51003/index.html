Anonymous @ 28 Oct 2008 — 9:32am
If upper and lower case, Futura Mini or Avant Garde. - large x-height (less ink used in capitals) -"missing" bits on letters, such as no tail on "t" and "j" -minimal, single-bowl forms of a and g. Alternatively, a face that works well with ample letterspacing, such as DIN or Interstate, because it can be used smaller. Also consider a good editor, for shorter words, and the use of understatement and "white space" to convey your message.
