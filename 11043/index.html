Anonymous @ 23 Apr 2003 — 9:12am
Sorry I haven't gotten back to all of you sooner. I was just reminded by a friend of mine that I had some responses. I guess I need to explain my process a little more. To start with I took a typeface, made some alterations, crumpled the paper, took digital pictures of each individual letterform in 3 different light settings. Then I took these images and manipulated them in Streamline. The high contrast and conversion I applied helped erase my letterforms to my goal. I hope this helps. Also, in response to Eduardo- I actually think the Frothy typeface looks very similar to a fellow student's design-Tetanus. I think it is also posted on here. Both Frothy and Tetnus remind me a lot of Baskerville. For legallity purposes, I'm not intending to sell this. Just for personal use and for my class project. Thank you!
