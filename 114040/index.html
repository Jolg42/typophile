Seems to be custom branding by lg2boutique [http://lg2boutique.com/en/work/464/hangar-29-branding](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829133000/http:/lg2boutique.com/en/work/464/hangar-29-branding "http://lg2boutique.com/en/work/464/hangar-29-branding") The word HANGAR is in the style of Sans octagonal/Sans angled. For a comparable base font see House Industries United Sans Semi Condensed Black [http://www.houseind.com/fonts/unitedcollection](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829133000/http:/www.houseind.com/fonts/unitedcollection "http://www.houseind.com/fonts/unitedcollection") and Ryan Clark's Liberator [http://www.losttype.com/font/?name=liberator](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829133000/http:/www.losttype.com/font/?name=liberator "http://www.losttype.com/font/?name=liberator") To make the word HANGAR the upper terminal of the G needs to be clipped and the chin squared. Also all corners should be rounded, in the style of Blender by Binnenland Nik Thoenen [http://www.binnenland.ch/font/about/Blender](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829133000/http:/www.binnenland.ch/font/about/Blender "http://www.binnenland.ch/font/about/Blender") Don
