Well, the only one I can answer offhand is #3. [X-height](http://web.archive.org/web/20090417174954/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/x-height) is a comparative measure of the height of the lowercase letters (e.g. a letter x) to the height of the capitals or ascenders. Impact has a high x-height because there is not a huge difference between the height of the lowercase and uppercase. Something like Nicolas Cochin has an unusually small x-height.
