Hi (from an amateur typographer, but take it as you wish). So here it goes. Your font has a **nice horizontal flowing feeling** to it yet you should **improve one or two things** : - The **lower stem+belly connection on the "b" and "d"** should get fixed, as in a simplified curve from the base to the stem instead of a complex stem to curve intersection (specially the "b"). It would prevent it to be a "brake" in the horizontal flow of the line. _On the other hand your font will get "rounder"..._ - The other thing is **the horizontal bar on the "a", the "e" and specially the "3"**. There are to many curves. It gives your characters a _blurry look_. Probaly if you eliminate the top or the bottom curve joint of the inner intersection it'll lighten up without loosing the design. I think you can develop a very nice font if you keep it up. **I specially like the "z".** Keep it up! Cheers. By the way, <cite>its still heavy WIP as you see</cite> what's WIP???
