The wildcard **^9** only matches one digit. So for numbers composed of more than one digit, you'll have to use the wildcard more than once. In your example, you'll have to do two Find/Change operations; one with the pattern you've used, and another with this pattern: **^9^9.(space)** For more tips and tricks, listen to [InDesignSecrets Episode 30: Find…Change…Format!](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414214700/http:/indesignsecrets.com/episode-30-findchangeformat.php)
