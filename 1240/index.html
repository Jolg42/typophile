Measure the vertical proportions: the x, capitals, ascenders and descenders height in relation to the eye (space between ascenders an descenders k - p ) and the capitals. (use Hkpl) Measure the contrast (relation between the thickest and thinnest parts. Also measure the uppercase and lower case alphabet length.
