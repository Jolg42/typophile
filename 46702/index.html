The more quirky fonts were probably hand drawn, as John suggests, but there are quite a few fonts out there that are quite similar. Here are a few suggestions from House Industries that I use ( [www.houseind.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20080906155610/http:/www.houseind.com/ "www.houseind.com")) Coop Condensed / Ed Interlock / Las Vegas Jackpot / Shag Exotica or Lounge You might also try Font Diner ( [www.fontdiner.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20080906155610/http:/www.fontdiner.com/ "www.fontdiner.com")) or Ray Larabie’s fonts ( [www.typodermic.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20080906155610/http:/www.typodermic.com/ "www.typodermic.com")) JH
