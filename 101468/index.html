3 Comments

Your vertical metrics values exceed the UPM (901+250=1151) and this behaviour would indicate that you have not balanced the metrics accordingly. If you read through [Karsten Luecke's guide to font metrics](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419200744/http:/www.kltf.de/downloads/FontMetrics-kltf.pdf) carefully and adjust your settings accordingly you should find that the vertical metrics start behaving as you would expect. At the very least you will have a greater understanding of what those settings change. Additionally, the Typophile threads listed below all deal with this issue in detail: [http://typophile.com/node/77906](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419200744/http:/typophile.com/node/77906 "http://typophile.com/node/77906") [http://typophile.com/node/13081](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419200744/http:/typophile.com/node/13081 "http://typophile.com/node/13081")
