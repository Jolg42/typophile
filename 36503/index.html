Anonymous @ 27.Aug.2007 7.57am
You state your objective is _not_ to challenge legibility, The letter shapes you have chosen do exactly that. It succeeds at what your objective is not, so your true objective, if actually achieved, is not readily apparent. This can be interpreted as failure. Your plan to break up the line of text on multiple planes is enough to make this difficult for the viewer. Don’t make it harder on them by using letter shapes that are not readily recognizable even when viewed from the angle that assembles the text. A simple geometric sans face such as a light weight of Futura should be enough if the letters are suspended in this multi-plane system.
