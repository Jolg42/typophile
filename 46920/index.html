Anonymous @ 30 Jun 2008 — 1:57am
This is most likely [lettering](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022104528/http:/typophile.com/wiki/lettering), not typography. It is sign painting by hand and not based on any single typeface. That said, [Neutraface](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022104528/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/house_industries/neutraface_no_2_collection/), which is quite similar as you observed, does indeed have this sort of G. See the [alternates](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022104528/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/house_industries/neutra_2_display_titling/?sample_text=See%20alternates%20below.&sample_size=24&acs_ss=2).
