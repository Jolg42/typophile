Should be [Marriott Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20141009103031/http:/www.myfonts.com/WhatTheFont/forum/case/304329), Marriott Hotels & Resorts [exclusive typeface](http://web.archive.org/web/20141009103031/http:/www.myfonts.com/WhatTheFont/forum/case/16926). Other alternatives: [Ocean Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20141009103031/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/ocean-sans-mt) (Extended Bold comes very close), [Cronos](http://web.archive.org/web/20141009103031/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/cronos) (Display Bold)
