Ludwig and Mayer

Indicies : Foundries : Ludwig and Mayer

Ludwig & Mayer was a large typefoundry in Frankfurt, Germany, which closed in 1984. When it closed its doors, its collection passed to Neufville.

Some of its many typefaces included Elegance (by Karlgeorg Hoefer), all the fonts of Jakob Erbar (including, obviously enough, Erbar), and Rhapsodie.
