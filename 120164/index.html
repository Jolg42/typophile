Q: "why it's NOT Helvetica." A: At least five letters, \_G\_ with no spur, \_R\_ with straight leg, and \_C\_, \_D\_ and \_O\_ with a vertical section in the curved strokes. The letter \_S\_ could pass for Helvetica, Arial etc. The letters have some similarities to DIN, except for the \_S\_. Might not be a standard font. Don
