Here’s some information (all via google): I know he has a mention in Meggs’ book. Wikipedia: [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur\_Mackmurdo](http://web.archive.org/web/20080704193044/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Mackmurdo "http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur\_Mackmurdo") (not much, but a few book listed for reference.) [Mr. Arthur H. Mackmurdo and the Century Guild](http://web.archive.org/web/20080704193044/http:/victorianweb.org/art/design/mackmurdo/vallance.html) [Britannica entry on Mackmurdo](http://web.archive.org/web/20080704193044/http:/www.britannica.com/EBchecked/topic/355008/Arthur-Heygate-Mackmurdo) Evolutionary Affinity in Arthur Mackmurdo’s Botanical Design Larry D. Lutchmansingh Design Issues, Vol. 6, No. 2 (Spring, 1990), pp. 51-57 (article consists of 7 pages) Published by: The MIT Press (Available [http://www.jstor.org/pss/1511437](http://web.archive.org/web/20080704193044/http:/www.jstor.org/pss/1511437 "http://www.jstor.org/pss/1511437") if you have access to JSTOR) A short listing, but has addresses to 2 of the homes he built: [http://eng.archinform.net/arch/7129.htm](http://web.archive.org/web/20080704193044/http:/eng.archinform.net/arch/7129.htm "http://eng.archinform.net/arch/7129.htm") [Symbolist Art Theories: A Critical Anthology](http://web.archive.org/web/20080704193044/http:/books.google.com/books?id=Vn_M6j7KejAC&pg=PA95&lpg=PA95&dq=%22arthur+mackmurdo%22&source=web&ots=iaedWDKr8v&sig=ESoa-ixlioGOjEp5bltSwAecmVI&hl=en) (Google Books) — Another research clue, look for more information on the “Century Guild”. [http://www.all-art.org/history424.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20080704193044/http:/www.all-art.org/history424.html "http://www.all-art.org/history424.html") — A strong statement that Mackmurdo “anticipated’ the style of Art Nouveau.
