“Old Friends” uses what looks to be a stenciled version of [Helvetica](http://web.archive.org/web/20060208041650/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontdetail&displayfontid=LH.120906.0.5&attributes.sampleSize=48&sampleText=SIMON%26GARFUNKEL&sampleSize=29).
