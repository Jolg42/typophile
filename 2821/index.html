It's a good idea...I'm not entirely sure it was as well executed as it might have been. I think for titling I'd have created a "Mo" ligature, more like "To". I work a lot with sign making software, and I'm used to the odd lumps and bumps that get created when you combine or weld shapes together. Like I said, I like the idea, I just think it would have benefited from a bit more polish.
