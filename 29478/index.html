For multi-script typography, the mix-and-match approach is the way to go in most cases. I've found a number of Japanese fonts will cover all of the scripts you require (the Japanese scripts, Chinese characters, Latin, and Arabic) and more, but they are often so typographically poor as to be unusable outside of the Japanese and Chinese scripts. A one-stop font that will cover all your needs would be convenient, but I don't think there is anyone with the resources and the typographic knowledge to create a single font that works well for all scripts. Ask yourself what languages you need to support and which ones will be the most important to you. If for example you will deal a lot with Arabic and other languages that use the Arabic script, then find out what the good typefaces for Arabic are. Ask typographically-enlightened native users of the Arabic script. There are very few people who can judge the typographical merits of fonts for all the different scripts, but for single scripts you will get a lot of responses.
