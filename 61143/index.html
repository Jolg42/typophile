I have been away since June or so, but expect to return actively in 2 weeks. I was shown a source in the famous commentary on the Code of Jewish Law, regarding the Magen Avrahom's views on contextual placement of the shva-na. There appears to be a grammatical view, based upon the Radak, with three levels of interpretation: 1. Minchas Shai - Shai L'Morah 2. Eliyahu achur - Gra - ArtScroll 3. R' Zalman Henna or Hanau - Chabad USA There appears to be a contextual view, based upon the Magen Avrahom (based upon the Radak, R' David Kimchi). Apparently, the view of the Magen Avraham is most accurate, with the other 3 views simply modifying or adding to the rules of the MA. Hence, automatic shva-na placement will be based upon the MA, with 3 alternative versions.
