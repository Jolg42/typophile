I suspect that Günter Gerhard Lange supervised the digitization of Standard for the PostScript version of Akzidenz Grotesk, as he was only born in 1921, while Standard/Akzidenz was introduced by Berthold in their 1896 catalog. I've found no information about the original designer. More information about Günter Gerhard Lange can be found at MyFonts.com: [http://www.myfonts.com/Person205.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20140803103614/http:/www.myfonts.com/Person205.html) Information about the H. Berthold Foundry: [http://www.myfonts.com/FontFoundry68.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20140803103614/http:/www.myfonts.com/FontFoundry68.html) and [http://www.bertholdtypes.com/home.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20140803103614/http:/www.bertholdtypes.com/home.html) David
