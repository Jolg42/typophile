Look at the height of the horizontal serifs versus the width of the vertical serifs. I'm guessing this was a font with very thin horizontal strokes, squooshed to make it appear taller. - Lex
Anonymous @ 13 Oct 2005 — 3:31pm
Lex, I think you're right. I tried it with a normal serif font at 300% the normal height, and got something very similar in shape. I wanted to try it with Perpetua, but it wasn't handy. (I think you need a typeface with a P whose bowl droops a bit after leaving the stem.) If you don't want to play such games, [Peplum](http://web.archive.org/web/20140117164141/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/peplum/peplum/testdrive.html?s=D+E+P+T&p=72) is similar 'off the shelf'. - Mike Yanega
