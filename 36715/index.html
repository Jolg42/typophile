Hercules/Herkules. The most readily available version is [Dieter Steffmann's Herkules](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422023927/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/samples/h/herkules.htm). The download link says T1, but there's an Opentype font in the download as well. It was also available from Opti as Huk Light.
