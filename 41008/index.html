It's close to Newhouse DT Super Condensed Bold [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/dtptypes/newhouse-dt/super-condensed-bold/](http://web.archive.org/web/20130225113431/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dtptypes/newhouse-dt/super-condensed-bold/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/dtptypes/newhouse-dt/super-condensed-bold/") But the shapes of the letters are actually more rounded, the a has an extending foot and the t has an angled top. It's possible this is a custom font for Towergate, but I don't actually know for sure. A larger image: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20130225113431im_/http:/typophile.com/files/towergate_5980.jpg)
