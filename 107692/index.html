Your work, especially your lowercase figures, looks reminiscent of the style of Braggadocio and Futura Black. Compare and check your typefaces with these: Braggadocio ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414031413im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Braggadocio_4989.gif) Futura Black ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414031413im_/http:/typophile.com/files/FuturaBlack_4596.gif) Paul Renner's original drawings for Futura ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414031413im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Renners-original-drawings_3729.jpg) Your typeface would be useful as a decorative typeface or something for movie titles. However, the usage will be limited to these.
