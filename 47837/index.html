Anonymous @ 1.Aug.2008 11.38am
I have the same problem Tiffany has: I can never remember who uses FF Meta, and I have very few examples. I suppose my laziness wins over my vanity. And Meta these days is everywhere, not quite like Arial, but certainly in the mainstream, so I don’t take photographs anymore. A few car brands use Meta: SEAT in Spain and Infinity in the US. Dodge has very fat Meta headlines in their campaigns in the US. Canon uses it for some products and campaigns, as does Bayer. MetaDesign San Francisco uses it (MetaDesign Berlin now uses FF Fago). The FontBook is set in FF Meta. The German Employment Agency (Arbeitsamt) used to but changed it for Daimler-Benz Corporate S after the last elections. You can still see quite few large letters above their buildings in Meta. Audi uses Univers, albeit a custom version (by Lucas de Groot at MetaDesign, 1996). So does Messe Frankfurt (by Erik Spiekermann and Erik van Blokland, 2006). Sorry – too late to check urls.
