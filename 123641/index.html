"TO SYDNEY" must be **[Grotesque MT (Extra Condensed)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415104058/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Linotype%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Grotesque%20MT%20Std%20-%20Extra%20Condensed&fid=bb6d9705f3fbef7f120dcceb93b48368&fsize=60&text=TO%20SYDNEY&fit=1)** "TEAL" looks modified and has no distinct letters to be sure, but it could be started as **[Benton Sans Extra Comp (Bold Italic)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415104058/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Font-Bureau%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=BentonSansExtraComp%20-%20Bold%20Italic&fid=0845128b327f05fb20b28a7db43b02fe&fsize=60&text=TEAL&fit=1)** or **[Antenna Comp (Bold Italic)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415104058/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Font-Bureau%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=AntennaComp%20-%20Bold%20Italic&fid=b1ec85aa618746f46bf400c62b34f729&fsize=60&text=TEAL&fit=1)**

The sans serif (TO SIDNEY and the smaller labels), could be various weights of Monotype Grotesque: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/grotesque-mt/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415104058/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/grotesque-mt/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/grotesque-mt/") |TEAL| could be replicated with Antenna Compressed Bold Italic: [http://www.fontbureau.com/fonts/Antenna/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415104058/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/Antenna/ "http://www.fontbureau.com/fonts/Antenna/") Hoefler & Co.’s Numbers (Indicia) could help you replicate the sequential numbering: [http://www.typography.com/fonts/numbers/styles/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415104058/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/numbers/styles/ "http://www.typography.com/fonts/numbers/styles/") PsyOps Crash Numbering is also an option: [http://www.psyops.com/shop/typefaces/crash-numbering/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415104058/http:/www.psyops.com/shop/typefaces/crash-numbering/ "http://www.psyops.com/shop/typefaces/crash-numbering/")
