\>July 7-11. Baselschoolofdesign.ch I’ve always been intrigued by Basel, but really, you are going to need a few semesters or a few years to learn how to do type, not a few days. Travel to TypeCon is expensive from Europe, even given the weak dollar. Europe has a lot of great conferences, too, like TYPO Berlin and most of the annual ATypI conferences. But actually, given a lot of the travel and accommodation costs, TypeCon may not end up being more expensive… Regarding language, both the University of Reading (UK) and the Dutch Royal Academy of the Arts in the Hague teach their Masters courses in English.
