Anonymous @ 16 Jun 2009 — 10:53am
The main problem is that the trigger glyphs were in the classes you were switching. Try this: feature CALT { @set1 = [A B D F G H I K L M N O P Q R T U V W X Y]; @set2 = [a b d f g h i k l m n o p q r t u v w x y]; @trip1 = [C E J S Z]; @trip2 = [c e j s z]; sub @trip1 @set1' by @set2; sub @trip1 @set2 @set1' by @set2; sub @set2 @set2 @set1' by @set2; sub @set2 @set2 @trip1' by @trip2; sub @trip2 @set2' by @set1; sub @trip2 @set1 @set2' by @set1; sub @set1 @set1 @set2' by @set1; sub @set1 @set1 @trip2' by @trip1; } CALT;
