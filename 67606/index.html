Anonymous @ 22 Feb 2010 — 10:52pm
Gentium covers a huge range of Latin-based alphabets and also covers basic Cyrillic and Greek. But it doesn't include Chinese characters. You must have seen it used in combination with another font that supports Chinese. Code2000, on the other hand, covers just about every writing system you can think of; take a look at the list at [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code2000](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123093631/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code2000). There are not many other fonts with even comparable coverage of the world's writing systems. A quick search reveals Code2000 includes 53,068 characters (63,546 glyphs) in version 1.171. [TITUS Cyberbit Unicode](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123093631/http:/titus.fkidg1.uni-frankfurt.de/indexe.htm) by comparison has 36,161 characters in version 4.0, and Gentium has around 1,500 glyphs.
