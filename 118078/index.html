Other names and versions of Ionic include Aurora BT (also called News 706 BT by Jackson Burke, Bitstream), Imperial BT, Corona LT, News 705 BT (which is Bitstream's Corona), Nimrod MT, Clarion MT. Source: Luc Devroye's Ionic (newspaper) typefaces page. [http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-50774.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415214132/http:/luc.devroye.org/fonts-50774.html "http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-50774.html") which is based on a comparison by Uli Stiehl.
