Swash and diacritics

I’m adding some fairly elaborate swash characters to the font I’m working on. These work contextually. One version of the ll combination overhangs the character before and after. Not a problem for “jello” but it is for “millipede.” What I’ve done is to sub i before and after with dotlessi, and the terminals of the swashes indicate the dots. But now I’m wondering what to do with îllém and so forth. It seems a bit culturally insensitive and possibly confusing meaning to convert these to “dotlessi” and “e”. Obviously I can ignore sub these, but the swashitude of the font would be severely reduced in say czech. Any advice, or examples of how this has been handled well?
