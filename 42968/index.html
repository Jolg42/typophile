Uppercase kra?

The kra has only a lowercase form in old Greenlandic, and is deprecated in modern Greenlandic in favor of the q, but, if the kra had an uppercase form, what would it look like, and how would it be distinguished from the K?

The uppercase is K’ (K + U+02BC modifier letter apostrophe). Take a look at [this SIL page](http://web.archive.org/web/20080906212130/http:/scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&item_id=UnicodeNames) and [this PDF file](http://web.archive.org/web/20080906212130/http:/www.evertype.com/alphabets/greenlandic.pdf).
