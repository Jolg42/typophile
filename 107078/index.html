I would recommend an old style sans serif. Sans, because the consistency of line weight and lack of serifs connotes mechanical efﬁciency, and old-style (a.k.a. humanist) to connote custom attention to detail. My effort in this genre is [Sensibility](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417161207/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/shinn/sensibility/). You can use “old style”, “humanist” and “sans” as keyword searches at font sites to ﬁnd others.
