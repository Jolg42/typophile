It must be **[Plantin Condensed (Bold)](http://web.archive.org/web/20130817033852/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Adobe&ffam=Plantin%20Condensed%20-%20Bold&fid=27a3ccec4540910d75fcecf41ccef110&fsize=60&text=Tillamook&fit=1)** by Frank Hinman Pierpont (1991, available from Adobe, Monotype, Linotype) It looks manually emboldened and the /k is probably a bit customized. ------------------ I found it using "Find my Font": [http://www.findmyfont.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20130817033852/http:/www.findmyfont.com/ "http://www.findmyfont.com")
