Anonymous @ 20 Nov 2013 — 1:40am
Actually, the more I look at it the more I think it's a customization of existing typefaces. Nothing is really consistent: - "WTAPS": /S should be "slab-serifed" too but it's not, making me thinking they could have started with some " [United Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150302160520/http:/typophile.com/node/99935#comment-539470)-like", manually adding slab-serifs - "LIMA" VS. "WTAPS": nothing matches (different weight, different /A...). "LIMA" is actually matching many "NBA" font from Eriq Jaffe's collection I've already pointed to. - /76 counter: also inconsistent with both "WTAPS" and "LIMA" designs. Counter can't be square but hexagonal/faceted if had to match "WTAPS" or /7 would have been bottom serifed to match "LIMA".
