This is almost definetely a custom designed logo which may have started life as something like [compacta](http://web.archive.org/web/20150321005252/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/compacta/ef-light/testdrive.html?s=weber&p=72) or swiss 911 with modifications to the w and b. You may also want to check out [aurora condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150321005252/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/aurora/condensed/testdrive.html?s=weber&p=72) for something similar. Or they may simply have drawn it from scratch. Either way, I think they've modified their earlier logo and simply added little serifs. Here's their original and modified logos. These larger images may also help someone else suggest something.
