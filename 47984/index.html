Univers CE fonts are available in PostScript Type 1 or TrueType formats, and they have the "Central European" character set. [http://www.linotype.com/137563/universcentraleuropean55roman-font.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20130707113320/http:/www.linotype.com/137563/universcentraleuropean55roman-font.html "http://www.linotype.com/137563/universcentraleuropean55roman-font.html") [http://www.linotype.com/137566/universcentraleuropean55roman-characterma...](http://web.archive.org/web/20130707113320/http:/www.linotype.com/137566/universcentraleuropean55roman-charactermap.html "http://www.linotype.com/137566/universcentraleuropean55roman-charactermap.html") Univers LT is the Western European character set (with a Euro symbol). If you license the OpenType Com fonts (see [http://www.linotype.com/12641/univers55roman-font.html#format](http://web.archive.org/web/20130707113320/http:/www.linotype.com/12641/univers55roman-font.html#format "http://www.linotype.com/12641/univers55roman-font.html#format")), or the Pro fonts, you get both the Central European and the Western European glyph coverage! But if you need a non-OpenType font, you have to pick one or the other :( My guess is that most people use OpenType now anyway. But I could be wrong!
