If you have InDesign and Illustrator CS, you’re bound to have Minion Pro and other large Adobe type families. Open the OpenType files in FontLab and follow the same naming conventions — just substitute the name of your face where appropriate. Works like a charm.
Anonymous @ 10.Aug.2006 9.38am
Also, take out spaces in the names between Medium and Italic; some of these programs see those names as identical, and if the style name is MediumItalic, it shows up as unique.
