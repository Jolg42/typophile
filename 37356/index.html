Anonymous @ 21 Sep 2007 — 9:20pm
Haley, I've had many friends that need a business card - fast. Our solution: I buy cover stock #110 from Staples. I run it through 10 up to a sheet on a 4/c laser printer (Okidata) and cut (well, I have a person who does superb, accurate xactoknife slits....) If there is a bleed - then we use an 8-up format, so we can give (till it) bleed(s)... They go home happy - and we are cheap, cheap, cheap. And... yes, they look professionally printed and we receive many-a-smile. Or... you could go to Popular Printing in NY - fast, reliable, not expensive. Here are their business card prices: [http://popularprinting.com/BC.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623044025/http:/popularprinting.com/BC.html "http://popularprinting.com/BC.html") P.S.
