They show the direction of the curve, clockwise or counter clockwise. In FontLab you can optimize the curves automatically, to get rid of some stuff. But be careful if you have delicate shapes – they can be distorted or become something you didnt expect. Under the meny “Contour” you have different choices that has to do with the curves and to clean them up, correct etc. Try them a little and you’ll see what they do.
