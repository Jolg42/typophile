Anonymous @ 9 Mar 2006 — 7:24am
Oddly enough, about a year ago, I was doing some informal researching into "Pirate" looking typefaces and scripts for a children's poster mock-up. David Nalle's site does have several candidates besides Captain Kidd (he has a [great web page](http://web.archive.org/web/20150413024540/http:/www.fontcraft.com/scriptorium/kidd/index.html) on the inspiration and development of the face). You can also find many "historic" styled faces available at [The Walden Font Co.](http://web.archive.org/web/20150413024540/http:/www.waldenfont.com/). By mixing a titling face and "handwritten" lines here and there, you can emulate an "old treasure map" look. Look at their "Divers Handes" collection for scripts. FYI- you might contact them direct since they seem to be working on a new "Nautical Themes" collection. Maybe you can be one of the first users and be truly unique with your project. Did you see Type Oasis [Pirate & Fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20150413024540/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/pirates/index.htm) yet?
