I like this. Just a couple of comments: The '1' seems just a little too thick. Also, I'd put another copy of "Two Twelve" on top of the mark, rotated 180 degrees to mirror the rotation of the last '2'. As for the colours, I think the options with higher contrast work best (1,2,5,6), but I do dig the blue-grey in option 3. - Lex
Anonymous @ 14 Nov 2006 — 11:52am
i agree with Lex that the 1 seems too thick. i also think that'd be really cool to put TWO TWELVE above the mark, upside down. very neat idea, Lex. as for me though, i don't think the type choice works with the mark. to me they each speak a different style, and are not harmonizing. i'd rather see either a condensed serif or a thin sans-serif. and i'm leaning towards the sans-serif in my mind. or even a lowercase hand-drawn script could be nice. the 212 has an up-scale elegant look to it, and hand-drawn scripts usually represent that because it takes time to do. it's easy and cheap to just type out the words in Didot. for color, i prefer the top left and bottom right. it's simple and the color works well. for multiple colors, i like the top right one; the low-contrast tan on tan and gray blue are very nice. i'm not digging the yellow/maroon combo at all, but i do like where it's trying to go - it seems different, unique, and that's just what bars want.
