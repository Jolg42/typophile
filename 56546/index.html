I'm not a developer, but as a UI designer I have had a chance to play with Android in action on the G1. Despite the poor visual design (and interaction for that matter), Android itself has a lot of potential and can be a very powerful system if the right people get their hands on it. Again, I don't know a lot about the development side of things, but it is my understanding Android has a unique rendering engine for type, though apparently has some difficulty with many TrueType fonts (if you were to install your own) - other than Droid, which is currently the default used on the G1.(It is also important to note that processing speeds of the hardware can be an obstacle for the OS) Here are a couple of resources: [http://androidguys.com/?p=656](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014142029/http:/androidguys.com/?p=656 "http://androidguys.com/?p=656") [http://understandinglimited.com/2009/03/07/android-complex-script/](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014142029/http:/understandinglimited.com/2009/03/07/android-complex-script/ "http://understandinglimited.com/2009/03/07/android-complex-script/")
