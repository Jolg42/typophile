Hi Michelle, that’s Forelle, a script face by Erich Mollowitz from 1936. There’s a [commercial version by Mecanorma](http://web.archive.org/web/20071231134030/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/samples/f/forelle.htm) and a [free one by Dieter Steffmann](http://web.archive.org/web/20071231134030/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/samples/f/forelle.htm).
