That's it Bob! The rubber stamps Harold Lohner used for Artistamp Medium [http://www.identifont.com/similar?KHJ](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725005323/http:/www.identifont.com/similar?KHJ "http://www.identifont.com/similar?KHJ") look like they were based on a condensed version of the circa 1900 De Vinne design, with the R and Q edited to be less floppy. For a digital variation of condensed De Vinne with smooth letters see Hawthorne: [http://www.identifont.com/similar?6EF](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725005323/http:/www.identifont.com/similar?6EF "http://www.identifont.com/similar?6EF") A regular width version of DeVinne was recently digitized by Jeff Levine as Publication: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/publication/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725005323/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/publication/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/publication/") Also De Vinne from wooden type fonts: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/de-vinne/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725005323/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/de-vinne/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/de-vinne/") For a rubber stamp treatment of the wider width of De Vinne see Harold Lohner's Hand Stamped JNL: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/hand-stamped/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725005323/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/hand-stamped/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/hand-stamped/") The De Vinne typeface has been discussed many times before at typophile, most recently at: [http://typophile.com/node/104584](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725005323/http:/typophile.com/node/104584 "http://typophile.com/node/104584") Don
