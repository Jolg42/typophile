There are a few typefaces I would like for this mark... If taking the serif route I think a Hoefler Text Roman is always a good choice. If considering a san serif, Sudtipos makes some great faces to match the angularity of this mark. Mr. Giacco and Politica are great faces, check them out. [http://sudtipos.com/fonts/36](http://web.archive.org/web/20080905125730/http:/sudtipos.com/fonts/36 "http://sudtipos.com/fonts/36") [http://sudtipos.com/fonts/42](http://web.archive.org/web/20080905125730/http:/sudtipos.com/fonts/42 "http://sudtipos.com/fonts/42") There is also an interesting serif from adobe called Belwe [http://store1.adobe.com/cfusion/store/html/index.cfm?store=OLS-US&event=...](http://web.archive.org/web/20080905125730/http:/store1.adobe.com/cfusion/store/html/index.cfm?store=OLS-US&event=displayFont&code=BELQ10006000 "http://store1.adobe.com/cfusion/store/html/index.cfm?store=OLS-US&event=displayFont&code=BELQ10006000") Good luck!
