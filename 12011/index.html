Education

Indices: Education

Type design (strictly)

  • MA Typeface Design
  • Type & Media (1-year postgraduate course) at KABK, Den Haag, The Netherlands
    Teachers: Jan Willem Stas, Erik van Blokland, Peter Verheul, Frank Blokland, Petr van Blokland, Françoise Berserik, Peter Bilak, Robin Kinross, Paul van der Laan, Frans van Mourik, Christoph Noordzij, Just van Rossum, Fred Smeijers
    Language: English
  • Carrera de Diseño de Tipografía (CDT-UBA) (1 and a half-year postgraduate course) at Universidad de Buenos Aires (UBA), Buenos Aires, Argentina
    Director: Rubén Fontana. Academic coordinator: Marcela Romero. Teachers: Carlos Carpintero, Miguel Catopodis, Pablo Cosgaya, Aldo De Losa, Griselda Flesler, Ruben Fontana, Tomás García Ferrari, Víctor García, Patricio Gatti, Horacio Gorodischer, Alejandro Lo Celso, Darío Muhafara, Betina Naab, Alejandro Paul, Mariana Podetti, María Eugenia Roballos, Eduardo Tunni, Marcela Romero, José Scaglione, Andrés Torresi.
    Language: Spanish
  • The Type@Cooper certificate programs at the Cooper Union in New York City. Language: English
  • Certificate of Advanced Studies (CAS) Type Design at Hochschule der Künste, Zürich, Switzerland. 1-year part-time program.
  • Master of Advanced Studies (MAS) in Type Design and Typography at Hochschule der Künste, Zürich, Switzerland. 2-year part-time program.
    Teachers: Hans-Jürg Hunziker, André Baldinger, Rudolf Barmettler; guest lecturers have included Hans Eduard Meier, Robin Kinross, Fred Smeijers, Gerard Unger, Erik van Blokland, Jeremy Tankard, Bruno Maag, Luc(as) de Groot, Ole Schäfer, Peter Bilak
    Language: German (plus some French & English)
  • ‘DSAA Création Typographique’ (2-year program) at Ecole Estienne, Paris, France
    Language: French
  • 3-year Bachelor program in Type Design & 1-year ‘Meisterschüler’ program at Hochschule für Grafik und Buchkunst/Academy of Visual Arts in Leipzig, Germany
    Supervised by Fred Smeijers
    Language: German (English?)
  • Master’s degree at Centro de Estudios Gestalt, Veracruz, Mexico
    Language: Spanish
  • Non-academic: Font Bureau Operated by Berlow and others in Boston, USA, with design collaborators including Roger Black, Cyrus Highsmith, Jill Pichotta, and others, as suggested by Paul
