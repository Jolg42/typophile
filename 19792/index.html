The main title is set in some predigital version of [Koch’s sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20080924105923/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontdetail&displayfontid=LP.9493.0.0&attributes.sampleSize=48&sampleText=NOSFERATU&sampleSize=48). The more recent [Kabels](http://web.archive.org/web/20080924105923/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&startrow=1&records=20&currpage=1&totalrecords=12&searchby=&searchID=&searchletter=&searchstr=kabel&font_categories_checkbox=font_name_search&fontRendererText=NOSFERATU) from Linotype and ITC are similar, but more regularized, lacking idiosyncrasies like the “upside-down” cap S. Credits could be in any predigital Futura or Futura copy such as [Twentieth Century](http://web.archive.org/web/20080924105923/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&startrow=1&records=20&currpage=1&totalrecords=6&searchby=&searchID=&searchletter=&searchstr=twentieth&font_categories_checkbox=font_name_search&fontRendererText=HENRIK+GALEEN). Lanston’s [No. 206](http://web.archive.org/web/20080924105923/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontpackage&startrow=1&records=&currpage=1&totalrecords=2&displayfontid=G.10964.0.0&fontRendererText=Art+Direction+ALBIN) version is the closest match I find. [Here](http://web.archive.org/web/20080924105923/http:/www.p22.com/lanston/products/twentiethcen.html) is the most recent digitization of this version by the folks at P22, but it is missing the italics.
