Anonymous @ 21.Sep.2007 9.30am
Alignment Zones: Font Info -\> Hinting Settings -\> Primary Zones (right panel). For example, if you want/need Alignment Zones of 15 units, you add two Primary Zones, one from -15 to 0, one from 1000 to 1015. I don’t know the technical usefulness of it, but I believe they are mainly as space for overshooting round and/or pointed shapes below and above the x-hight. And I guess that helps hinting.
