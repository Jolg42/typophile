TypeCon2005: Who? When? What?

Alright! Now the site is live and registration* available, let’s starting a running list of who’s going, when they are arriving/leaving, and what you hope to do/eat/see in the big apple!

Who: Me, myself and I, aka Miss Tiffany, Tiff, Tif, and to close friends and family, T.

When: I’m hitting the east coast the Friday before it all starts to hang out with an old friend and will arrive at the official conference hotel, The Roosevelt Hotel late Sunday night or Monday morning. I’ll be leaving, thoroughly satiated and happy, the following Monday.

What: I really hope to visit: The Chisholm Poster Gallery — The Metropolitan Museum of Art to see two of my favorite paintings by Pierre-Auguste Cot - The Storm and Springtime. But I can’t say no to the Lehman collection or anything from the PreRaphaelite era or the Dutch, hmm, or Kandinsky, or Caravaggio. Yikes. — MoMA to see the new changes in architecture. — But most of all, to see my friends at the conference whom are all just my type.

* The only browser experience problems at this time is Internet Explorer and only on a PC. However, these people can see some of the content on the site and can still register and see most of the content if they skip directly to http://www.sotalink.com/register.html — We are aware of the problem and are working toward a solution.
