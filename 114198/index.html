Similar: [Director's Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317131827/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/lettering-inc/directors-gothic/?refby=bowfin). The A could be made with inverted V, but the numerals are not exactly the same. Old Image Club font [Pacifica Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317131827/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/imageclub/pacifica-condensed/?refby=bowfin) has the A, but is more squared. Could be custom design for the phone app. - Mike Yanega
