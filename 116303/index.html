'LES MALLES MOYNAT' has been reborn in December 2011 by LVMH group. It was a well-known high-end trunk (malle in french) manufacturer. A point to consider is almost all companies in the fashion industry business use bespoke typefaces and custom lettering (which sounds obvious). So, you're right, that's neither Futura or Neutraface but this /S is definitely to close to Futura to be a coincidence (or a customization). A point for Neutraface, from ' [Popular Logos](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812/http:/popularlogos.blogspot.fr/2011/12/le-dieu-du-luxe-ressuscite-une-marque.html)' blog > After some search, type has been customized but still based on Neutraface. Another point for Futura. Baseline of their main logo is set in Futura. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812im_/http:/typophile.com/files/moynat_5238.png) ... But 'PARIS' baseline from their website logo looks like Neutraface No.2 ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812im_/http:/typophile.com/files/logo-moynat_6707.jpg) Actually, that could easily be a mash-up of these 2 (or a customization of any of these 2): - /L, /E, /O, /N and /T from Futura - (alternate)/M and /A from Neutraface No.2 Alternatives but all /S are off: [Milano](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mtt/milano), [Noir](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/m-b-creative/mb-noir), [Arquitecta](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/latinotype/arquitecta), [Celias](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typedynamic/celias), [Radikal](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/nootype/radikal), [Ano](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317064812/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/alias/ano)
