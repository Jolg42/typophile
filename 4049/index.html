Anonymous @ 31.Mar.2004 12.27pm
Most likely it's custom, but the lower-case is not extremely distinctive -- many formal scripts have similar forms. Check out [Mike Yanega's site](http://web.archive.org/web/20080603080659/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/Script01Pg1.html) for a selection. There's nothing there, though, that remotely looks like the Budweiser B. If all you need is a clean version of the logo, though, go to [Brands of the World](http://web.archive.org/web/20080603080659/http:/web.brandsoftheworld.com/?id=112&query_id=3139212&page=1&brand_id=26990). You can also do a search there for Budweiser and find a lot of Bud logos. (And Miller ones, too!)
