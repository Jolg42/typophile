Holiday presents for Typophiles?

Handcrafted Typophile Photo Albums on eBay

For the past several months, my wife (of Tami Jo Studios, http://tamijo.com) has been transforming the Typophile Film Fest 4 posters into some amazing lotus-fold photo albums. She’s just put them on to eBay, and is donating 10% of proceeds toward Typophile.

Original poster design can be seen here: http://typophile.com/node/37243

The albums open out from a 4.5” square to allow photos, drawings, or other images to be tipped-in inside.
