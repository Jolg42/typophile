Anonymous @ 13 Jun 2007 — 11:14am
Hex isn't a color model, merely a way to write the RGB values using hexadecimal numbers. There's really no point in trying to find some set-in-stone numerical conversion of Pantone to RGB for on-screen media. Just hold your swatch up to the monitor and take a best guess at it. Nearly all monitors are going to be different from each other, so you're really just aiming for a ballpark approximation anyways.
