[STEPInside Design](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417094959/http:/www.stepinsidedesign.com/) If you can find old issues of Critique magazine, I always thought the writing was very good. [Graphis](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417094959/http:/www.graphis.com/journals/index.php) isn't what it used to be, but still worth a poke through at the bookstore. You should also consider the online forums/blogs that are populated and written by well-published authors. Specifically I'm thinking about [Design Observer](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417094959/http:/www.designobserver.com/) and [Speak Up](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417094959/http:/www.underconsideration.com/speakup/).
