Jaquizaca – a jaunty typeface with a serious attitude

I created Jaquizaca in 2001 to make inter-titles for a TV programme. After twelve years it was revised and updated with ligatures, contextual alternates and other OpenType features. It supports all the European languages using Latin alphabet.
In 2004 I draw a snakes’n’ladders board to illustrate a magazine article on graphic design process. Now, with a little help of my friends, I translated it into English, Spanish and German.
