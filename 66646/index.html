Anonymous @
_Is there a way to do this direclty in Fontlab?_ One way would be to 1. Select a radius for your rounded corners--say, 12 units); 2. In the EFFECTS menu, select BOLD, uncheck "Keep glyph dimensions," then select -12 for H weight and V weight; 3. In the same menu, check "Make round corners" and change the weight to +12. You can fiddle with the values to get the effects you're looking for...
