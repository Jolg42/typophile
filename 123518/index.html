Lettering on buildings of the era was custom designed by the architect or builder rather than being copied direct from a font. As you note it's similar to Lombardic. The medial spurs and Tuscan terminals make it a true Victorian Frankenfont. For digital fonts with some similarity to the Lombardic design you could also check for fonts tagged uncial, medieval, illuminated, Irish, medieval and Celtic. I posted a fairly comprehensive set of links to this genre of fonts in [http://typophile.com/node/123144](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424032727/http:/typophile.com/node/123144 "http://typophile.com/node/123144") Don
