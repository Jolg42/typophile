Anonymous @ 13 Mar 2014 — 3:36pm
The letters are in an old style called stovepipe. FAIK They are not a font as such. I can offer several fonts that generally match the letterforms shown in the image --\_A\_ with apex top, \_R\_ with vertical leg, \_S\_ with horizontal center stroke. Valuco [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/aesthetic-apparatus/valuco/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140319231548/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/aesthetic-apparatus/valuco/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/aesthetic-apparatus/valuco/") Gaz [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/gaz/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140319231548/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/gaz/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/gaz/") Liquor Store [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/chank/liquorstore/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140319231548/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/chank/liquorstore/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/chank/liquorstore/") Iwan Reschniev [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fdi/iwan-reschniev/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140319231548/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fdi/iwan-reschniev/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fdi/iwan-reschniev/") Lobby Card JNL [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/lobby-card/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140319231548/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/lobby-card/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/lobby-card/") There are quite a few that that match the \_R\_ but not the \_S\_ and vice versa. Run through the stovepipe tag at Myfonts [http://www.myfonts.com/search/tag%3Agaspipe/fonts/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140319231548/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/tag%3Agaspipe/fonts/ "http://www.myfonts.com/search/tag%3Agaspipe/fonts/") and you will see what I mean. All are wider than your letters, although Iwan Reschnie is closer in width than the others. Don
