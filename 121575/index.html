Updated: Martina, which dates from the early 1990s, is apparently based on the photo-lettering era Filmotype Hudson. Some letters in Martina like \_E\_ and perhaps \_A\_ were alt letters in the Filmotype Hudson filmstrips. The alt \_E\_ is found in the digital Filmotype Hudson as an alt \_E\_, but it does not include an alt \_A\_ like the one in Martina. The Martina \_A\_ is either an alt \_A\_ that was in the filmstrips and was missed in the Filmotype digitization, or was created by editing \_a\_ when the digital Martina was made. Don

The Digital outlines, node positions etc. are virtually identical so one is a clone of the other. Identifont states a 1992 copyright for Hudson by The Font Company. Internal information on the Hudson font now being sold by FontSite claims a copyright of 1996. Internal information on Martina claims copyright 1990-1993 FontBank, Inc. I have no idea which dates reflect reality and who copied whom. Don
