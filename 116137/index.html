"Happy" is pretty subjective but I suppose it has about the same effect as cute, or rounded, and avoiding a sterile mechanical style like Helvetica. For a sans try Yummo [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/yummo/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092806/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/yummo/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/yummo/") But why restrict your choices to a sans? For example check out Laura Worthington's fat hybrid \_Ganache \_ [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/laura-worthington/ganache/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092806/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/laura-worthington/ganache/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/laura-worthington/ganache/") Or fairy tales handwritten roman letters like Besley Hand [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/juraj-chrastina/besley-hand/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092806/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/juraj-chrastina/besley-hand/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/juraj-chrastina/besley-hand/") Think outside the box. BTW "happy" and "serious" seem to be incompatible as font criteria. But I can see "happy" and well designed as a great combination. Don
