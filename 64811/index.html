And the two main subcategories seem to be Egyptian Fats and Geometric Fats. Non Format seems to have based its Egyptian Fats off of Giza Nine Five. And there is a decent selection of other Egyptian Fats like Sabbath Black, Akimoto, etc. As for the Geometric Fats, the selection seems not so great imo. Probably best to make your own for this style.
