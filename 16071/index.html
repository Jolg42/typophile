Six degrees of separation - Le Monde redesign

• Le Monde newspaper was the brainchild of Gen Charles De Gaulle
• The Billy Joel hit “We didn’t start the fire” contains the line “Charles De Gaulle, California baseball"
• The California Angels baseball team, renamed the Anaheim Angels, renamed the Los Angeles Angels of Anaheim were swept aside by the Boston Red Sox in the 2004 post-season on their way to their curse breaking World Series win.
• The Boston Red Sox play at Fenway Park.
• Matthew Carter designed “Fenway” for Sports Illustrated.
• On Monday Le Monde’s redesign hits the streets and uses, you guessed it, Fenway.

The Grauniad is a (now largely affection) nickname for The Guardian, which used to be notorious for its misspellings. I don't think they still have the reputation, but the name has stuck. The point of Kevin's presentation in Helsinki was about the relationship between the pleasurable aesthetic experience of typography and measurable increase in cognitive performance, based on related work in which people are found to perform problem solving tasks more easily and quickly after an enjoyable mental experience such as watching an amusing video rather than a dry lecture. So not directly related to the crapshoot of trying to increase readership in a competetive newspaper market.
