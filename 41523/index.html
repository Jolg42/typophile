I think it will be difficult to find someone who has Dorian. DTL license holders are rare, and Dorian isn’t often discussed (nor purchased, I assume). [Elmo van Slingerland](http://web.archive.org/web/20081203221431/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Elmo%252Bvan%252BSlingerland) may be the best type designer name of all time. You might also dig [FF Quadraat](http://web.archive.org/web/20081203221431/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_quadraat_ot/), which has a similar Dutch pedigree but is perhaps less delicate.
