It’s Pistilli Roman, which hasn’t yet been digitized. Phil Martin’s [Didoni](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009062244/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontdetail&displayfontid=UR.109805.0.0) is a close copy, but the digital version’s hairlines are not nearly as thin. Phil’s version was not a straight knock-off. He added a bunch of swash and alternate characters. Ironically, Phil’s contributions are missing from the URW digitization, so what you get is essentially just a knock-off of Pistilli Roman.
