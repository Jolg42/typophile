Anonymous @ 7 Sep 2014 — 4:05pm
\_Eau Cologne\_ and \_Saigon-Hanoi\_ are not the same design. Note for example the different \_N\_. For a striped font with the \_N\_ in \_Eau Cologne\_, you can use Archive Copperplate Head ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150219143801im_/http:/typophile.com/files/ArchiveCopperplateHeadInUse_5607.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/archivetype/archive-copperplate-head/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150219143801/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/archivetype/archive-copperplate-head/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/archivetype/archive-copperplate-head/") which is an accurate, but indifferent quality, digital rendition of Hermann Ihlenburg's Copperplate from the 19th century. Change the convex inner curve on the terminal serifs for \_E\_, \_L\_, \_C\_, and \_G\_ to a concave inner curve by blanking out the unwanted part, and you have a fair match to \_Eau Cologne\_ . Don
