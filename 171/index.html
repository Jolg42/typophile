Anonymous @ 19 May 2003 — 10:47am
Some brief, but recent, historical context: underlines came about because typewriters didn't have italics or bold for emphasis. Now that we have italics and bold readily available, underlines just remain a leftover convention; an unnecessary homage to a time without an alternative. And it kills me that the web relies so heavily on these. Down with underlines! (I'm sure someone can point out a good use for them, but at the moment I can't think of one.)
