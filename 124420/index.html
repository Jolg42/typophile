Reminds me of Lectura, a design by Dick Dooijes, Amsterdam Foundry, circa 1966. Letraset sold a version as a contact transfer from Tetterode. Character set is in the old Letraset product catalog. A much thicker and more conventional version by Paul Hickson is available in digital format as Leighton from Red Rooster [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/leighton-rr/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150413075147/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/leighton-rr/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/leighton-rr/") Don
