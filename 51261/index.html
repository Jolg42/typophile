[Avenir](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Avenir) – [Frutiger](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Adrian%20Frutiger)’s [Futura](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Futura) – would be an obvious answer, no? [Avenir Next](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/typophile.com/node/27007) comes with old-style figures. Have a look at [Thomas Phinney](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Thomas%20Phinney)’s [Hypatia Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/www.adobe.com/type/browser/landing/hypatia/hypatia.html): 6 weights from Extralight to Black (though no Italics [yet](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/www.typophile.com/node/41338)), and all kind of figures you can imagine. Once you found your way throught the various OpenType Stylistic Subsets, Hypatia Sans will turn out to be a very [versatile system](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106223646/http:/typophile.com/node/33619).
