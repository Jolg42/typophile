It must be **[Charter](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106065909/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=ITC&ffam=Charter%20ITC%20-%20Regular&fstyle=&fsize=60&fid=88a88e9058a64bcf4a5467a3df784f64&wrap=2&text=The%20BC%20Provincial%20www.gov.bc.ca)** by Matthew Carter (1993). It's available as Charter ITC (ITC), Charter BT (Bitstream), Charter BT Pro (Bitstream) and once upon a time there was also an Charter EF version from Elsner+Flake. I think the original design owner was URW. ----------------------------------------------- I found it using Find my Font - [http://www.findmyfont.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106065909/http:/www.findmyfont.com/ "http://www.findmyfont.com")
