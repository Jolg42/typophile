Gill Sans actually isn’t free. However, some weights of it ship with OSX. Which isn’t free. For the serif: Check out the [FAQ Free](http://web.archive.org/web/20070908122048/http:/typophile.com/node/14652). [Charis SIL](http://web.archive.org/web/20070908122048/http:/scripts.sil.org/CharisSILfont) is _somewhat_ similar to Scala Serif—if you don’t take account of much more than the angled serifs. [Bitstream Vera](http://web.archive.org/web/20070908122048/http:/www.bitstream.com/font_rendering/products/dev_fonts/vera.html) comes in matching Sans, Serif & Mono. Then of course, Scala is rather unique— [and it starts from $40](http://web.archive.org/web/20070908122048/http:/www.fontfont.com/shop/index.ep?clist=PS,12486&cview=P12486) …
