I can't find this exact font but I found one very similar to it. It was published in 1999 by Bright Ideas (a defunct foundry), is named **Stacker** , and is now apparently freeware available on the net. Do a search for "Bright Ideas Stacker font", in quotes, to find a download link.

Although Bowfin has it listed from a Spanish Letraset page scan, and Luc Devroye seems to have the same pic. Not sure about a digital version though. Hope this helps.
