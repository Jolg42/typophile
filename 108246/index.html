HUNGER THIRST: most probably custom and possibly hand-drawn from scratch as [Derek Hollister](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/dribbble.com/shots/608462-Hunger-N-Thirst-Identity), one of the designers in charge of this identity at [The Infantree](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.theinfantree.com/work/hunger-n-thirst) has a type work on his dribbble called ' [Absolute Finest Type](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/dribbble.com/shots/986127-Absolute-Finest-Type) which looks pretty close to the one for 'HUNGER N THIRST'. For what it's worth, if I have to recreate it, I'd probably start with some font like [Diamante EF](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/diamante) adding those latin (triangle) serifs. More 'Diamante': [Five Star Final](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/solotype/five-star-final), [Herald Gothic FB](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/herald-gothic), [Chamfer Gothic RR](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/chamfer-gothic-rr), [Uncle Sam Slim NF](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/uncle-sam-slim-nf), [Pillar](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/loshaj-foundry/pillar), [Dayta](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/elibrumbaugh.com/Dayta-Typeface) "Out-of-the-box" alternatives but still some customization needed: [Supremaganda BTN](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/btn/supremaganda-btn), [Bourbon](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/hold-fast-foundry/bourbon), [Sheepman](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/sheepman), [Yeoman Gothic RR](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/yeoman-gothic-rr) Starting with a slab and (mainly) reworking serifs to latin ones: [Player](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829175301/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/player)
