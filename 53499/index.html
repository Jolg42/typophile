This was embossed in Photoshop. Check the way the lines merge sloppily on the crossbar of the E. Look at the curves on the rest of the E. When hunting for an embossed font, keep in mind that TrueType, Postscript and OpenType are limited to pure black and white - no gray except dots, lines etc.
