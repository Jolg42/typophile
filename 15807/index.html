FontShop carries [Avant Garde Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20140821015703/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&searchstr=avant%20garde) from three vendors. Note that the E+F and ITC versions are identical in size, weight and spacing, whereas the Monotype version differs quite a bit.
