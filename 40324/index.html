If you can figure out how to get the Word Options dialog to show up, for Display you want to Show Measurements in units of millimeters. And sure enuff, when you open the Paragraph dialog, Indentation is now set in millimeters, but Spacing \> Line spacing: “exactly” \> the measurements are still in points! So the only way is to calculate how many points to set. Not too difficult; 72 points per inch divided by 25.4 millimeters per inch comes to 2.8 points per millimeter. Multiply by 6 equals 16.8 points. I think. Try it. See if it works. j a m e s
