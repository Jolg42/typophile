Hi Tommy, I’m afraid you won’t be able to find this as a font. These numbers were designed specifically for their intended use and are as such unavailable to the general public. This most probably isn’t even a digital “font” as you and I know it. H&FJ’s [Greenback](http://web.archive.org/web/20081013084206/http:/www.typography.com/catalog/numbers/more7.html) is the absolute closest you’ll get.
