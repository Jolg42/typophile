Anonymous @ 21 Feb 2010 — 10:37pm
[Hubert Jocham](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414232925/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Hubert%20Jocham)’s [Safran](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414232925/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/hubertjocham/safran/?refby=mfde) is another, slightly more broad-pen-flavoured option. The family spans 9 weights, and also the italics have a double-storey ‘g’. There’s a [Pro version](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414232925/http:/www.hubertjocham.de/item.php/type/sans/SafranPro/) with small caps and extended language support. But Stephen’s right: without knowing why FF Kievit is out of the question, it’s impossible to come up with suitable alternatives for you.
