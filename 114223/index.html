Probably by an actual typewriter. Faux typewriter sans fonts are fairly rare. Earmarks for this one are i with a lower serif, two story a, and single story g. Very short descenders & ascenders. Wide lowercase letter forms. Not a perfect match, but a very well executed font. Atlas Typewriter, [http://typographica.org/typeface-reviews/atlas-typewriter/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725015910/http:/typographica.org/typeface-reviews/atlas-typewriter/ "http://typographica.org/typeface-reviews/atlas-typewriter/") and [https://commercialtype.com/typefaces/atlas/typewriter](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725015910/https:/commercialtype.com/typefaces/atlas/typewriter "https://commercialtype.com/typefaces/atlas/typewriter") If you want a distressed match then some digging of "free" fonts might be your best bet. Myfonts did not have a match, clean or dirty. Don
