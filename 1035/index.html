The [Adobe version](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017231122/http:/www.adobe.com/type/browser/F/FUTQ/F_FUTQ-10016000.jhtml) is my least favorite. It was one of their earliest releases and you can kinda tell. The [Bitstream version](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017231122/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/futura/) is pretty good, especially for text. There is also [the new one from Neufville Digital](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017231122/http:/www.neufville.com/uk/startfuk.htm) which looks very good and has lots of extras like small caps and old style numerals. Also, it has CE, Turkish and Baltic language support. One thing that I miss from the pre-digital days are the display cuts of Futura. All the available digital versions seem to be based on the text sizes (except, I remember Image Club did display versions, I don't know if those are still available). The text cuts--even good ones--look a bit clumsy at display sizes.
