Hand lettering in the style of the era. Note the variations in repeating letters. The beaked terminals on \_T\_ and \_S\_ are distinctive, but are found in some fonts of the 1900s. Also with \_E\_ similar to the title. For example check the fonts at Greater Albion Typefounders [http://www.myfonts.com/foundry/Greater\_Albion\_Typefounders/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140527155623/http:/www.myfonts.com/foundry/Greater_Albion_Typefounders/ "http://www.myfonts.com/foundry/Greater\_Albion\_Typefounders/") and you might find something you like. Note the similarities with Bonning, Bonaventure and Vertrina. Other similars include Canada Type's Ronaldson [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/ronaldson/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140527155623/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/ronaldson/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/ronaldson/") and Intellecta's Benjamin Franklin [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/benjamin-franklin/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140527155623/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/benjamin-franklin/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/intellecta/benjamin-franklin/") Don
