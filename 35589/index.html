Anonymous @ 31 Jul 2007 — 7:15pm
Your problems may also come from the options when you generate the font (but I would remove all kerning pairs anyway). For instance I just downloaded the [public domain]\* icon font [marvosym.ttf](http://web.archive.org/web/20141018210214/http:/www.marvosym.de/download.html) (Für Puristen) and installed it on my mac. TextEdit would'nt even see the font. So I uninstaled it, opened marvosym.ttf with fontforge, clicked "File \> Generate fonts", then clicked "Options" and unchecked "Apple", "Opentype" and "Old style kern" and then generated a new ttf font that I installed; everything seems to work nicely (always better to check the file with "Font Book" before installing, of course). [edit] \* I meant Freeware; here is the copyright: _Martin Vogel's Symbols are freeware - look for information and new versions at [http://www.marvosym.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20141018210214/http:/www.marvosym.com/ "http://www.marvosym.com") - Neue Versionen und Informationen finden Sie auf [http://www.marvosym.de](http://web.archive.org/web/20141018210214/http:/www.marvosym.de/ "http://www.marvosym.de")_
