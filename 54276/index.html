[metro DF](http://web.archive.org/web/20110616144643/http:/www.haroldsfonts.com/metro.html) by Harold Lohner was inspired by this look: _»METRO-DF was inspired by a trip to Mexico City. The official signage of the Mexico City subway uses a peculiar adaptation of the classic font Eurostile, designed by Aldo Novarese. The capital E was most unusual. The overall feel was of a past vision of the future, with a distinctively Mexican touch. (The squared circle, or rounded square, is very common in both old and new Mexican design.«_
