The copyright of a Booker font says: "1995 Solotech Design. All Rights Reserved." According to Luc Devroye info on Solotech: [http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-29677.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150302015304/http:/luc.devroye.org/fonts-29677.html "http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-29677.html") > "[...] some of their fonts (if not all) are exact unaltered copies of other people's fonts." Therefore I don't think the original font name was "Booker". This is one of these fonts copied/sold by many forgery companies under different names and although I doubt any of them is the original design, I'll list them here to help discovering the original: **Booker** - 1995 Solotech Design. All Rights Reserved. **Dishrow** - Converted to PC format for distribution by Reaso **Eliza** - Generated by Fontographer 3.5 **Enterprise** - (C) 1992-1993. ATTITUDE, INC.
