What I can say is I was not able to identify it either. It also remains unsolved on [WhatTheFont Forum](http://web.archive.org/web/20130819020910/http:/www.myfonts.com/WhatTheFont/forum/?q=lantern). So, 2 possibilities: totally hand-drawn from scratch or customization from an existing typeface (what I prefer, less frustrating ;). Sweet Square is also the closest I've found but some details you've already spotted remain off (but not hard to reproduce). It also shares many similarities with [United](http://web.archive.org/web/20130819020910/http:/typophile.com/wiki/www.houseind.com/fonts/unitedcollection), [KP Duty](http://web.archive.org/web/20130819020910/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/kp-duty) and [Full Block](http://web.archive.org/web/20130819020910/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/fonts/fullblock.php) but none of these ones are matching either.
