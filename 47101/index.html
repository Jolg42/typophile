Hi Mike, This is a very subjective question, since it’s hard to guess how much ’tweaking’ you want to do. I looked through hand-drawn lettering fonts for each of the three styles on the cover. (1) = HOW GAMBLERS WIN; (2) = OR THE SECRETS OF; and (3) = ADVANTAGE PLAYING. I looked for fonts without layering that you would use for (1) and (3). These would be fonts I might try for these (just ideas based on a quick look): (1): [Ad Hoc](http://web.archive.org/web/20081026074328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/ad-hoc/), [Rekord](http://web.archive.org/web/20081026074328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/ff-rekord/), [Repent](http://web.archive.org/web/20081026074328/http:/www.dafont.com/repent.font), [Sinclair](http://web.archive.org/web/20081026074328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/sinclair-script-rr/) (2): [Escher Hand](http://web.archive.org/web/20081026074328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/p22/escher-hand/), [Kooky](http://web.archive.org/web/20081026074328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/kooky-bt/) (3): [Moulin Rouge Solide](http://web.archive.org/web/20081026074328/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/t26/toulouse-lautrec-moulin-rouge/) - Mike Yanega
