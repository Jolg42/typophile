Anonymous @ 20 Dec 2009 — 3:47am
Maths don't help with optical margin adjustment tho (and neither does InDesign, really). If full justification makes optically bad L/R margins (especially when you have massive differences in point size), it can be a good idea to make the text box slightly too wide, _centering_ the type, and finetuning the sizing. (Don't forget to put guidelines where the optical margin should be.) And if you need to finetune the left margin, insert a thin space before the first character and kern against that. Takes time! :)
Anonymous @ 21 Dec 2009 — 9:03am
No need to convert to outlines for real-time scaling. Set up your lines in centered and separate text frames (comes in handy for optically adjusted line spacing aswell). InDesign lets you scale text frames with their content, just press cmd and shift, and grab one of the center handles.
