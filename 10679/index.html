Two possibilities here, both freebies, that might get you "close enough" to fool the casual observer or provide something that can be modified without too much trouble: Campanile (PC Postscript only) by Dieter Steffmann [http://www.steffmann.de/english/fonts1.htm](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310071635/http:/www.steffmann.de/english/fonts1.htm) Ringlet Black by George White.
