It reminds me of the Hopi tribal symbol, or World Symbol. [Here](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009211634/http:/www.mc.maricopa.edu/~ladelia/Music%20files/native_american/images/Hopi%20tribal%20symbol.jpg), and [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009211634/http:/www.whats-your-sign.com/images/NAWorldHopi.jpg).
