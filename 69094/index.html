I think that it is someone's version of Devinne. Not the ornamental Devinne, but the boldface of Romana that ATF created and called Devinne - and other foundries mimicked. Opti, for one, offered a version years later with the pointed top to the 'A' and downward vee of the 'M' - as well as that 'R' as an alternate. Jeff Levine's [Hand Stamped JNL Regular](http://web.archive.org/web/20100415211836/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/hand-stamped/) is a deliberately crude and inexact version of this.
