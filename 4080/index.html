![howl](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516143031im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/30/32855.jpg) Allen Ginsberg's "Howl" set in 10/11 Baskerville, 1956. Every line of verse is treated as a justified paragraph, with 3 pica hanging indent. No hyphenation. Title, Cloister Bold. (Sample is 30% larger than actual size, to show sufficient type detail.)
