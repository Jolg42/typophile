There seems to be some resemblances between the initials in this script and Poppl Exquisit. The H is quite similar, and one of the A versions is closer to Exquisit. However, only certain letters are similar, and the lower case is quite different. Unfortunately my books show little in the European scripts, so I have no idea whether Friedrich Poppl designed many other script typefaces, besides this and Residenz. This is what I would look for, as a starting place. - Mike Yanega
