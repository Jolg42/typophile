There are some fonts that imitate Arabic, but so far I haven't found an exact match. [Catharsis Bedouin](http://web.archive.org/web/20141008164158/http:/www.dafont.com/catharsis-bedouin.font?nb_ppp=20&text=Lupe+Fiasco) is one that's free. Linotype has one called [Pide Nashi](http://web.archive.org/web/20141008164158/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/pide-nashi/) that also has a similar feel, and I think Scriptorium also has several faux Arabic fonts, with [Serendib](http://web.archive.org/web/20141008164158/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/scriptorium/serendib/) as the closest style in their collection to your sample. - Mike Yanega
