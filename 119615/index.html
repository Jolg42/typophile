Brothers is definitely an excellent alternative (some more after). I'm pretty convinced it's a custom, after having a look to their weibiste, from [Freshman](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/www.dafont.com/freshman.font) (used for th rest of the online identity). Serifs has been edited (or chopped off) and turned into flared latin ones. /R leg has been transformed (/P + oblique leg) Some more in the same vein, not matching either: [Dealers](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fcreativemarket.com/Gumpita/49869-Dealers), usual LHF's [Roebuck](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/fonts/roebuck.php), [Antique Half Block](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/fonts/antiquehalfblock.php) and [Old Stock](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/letterheadfonts.com/fonts/oldstock.php), [Reidfork](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/creativemarket.com/swistblnk/75323-Reidfork), [Charcuterie](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/laura-worthington/charcuterie) Block, [Gin](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fort-foundry/gin) Quick'n'dirty job starting from Freshman ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317065733im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Untitled-1_3846.png)
