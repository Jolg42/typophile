Hello urfan.alimov, The logotype "iddaa" looks to be [Helvetica Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419080559/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica/) or a variant of it such [Helvetica Neue Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419080559/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/neue-helvetica/). It's not perfectly printed, but that's the most probable typeface chosen. The body text is a tough one. I ran it through [Identifont](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419080559/http:/www.identifont.com/similar?2LA) and that is the best result from my findings on there. It's a proportional, san-serif typeface typical of a receipt that's all I can see from it something similar to this imitation called [Merchant Copy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419080559/http:/www.fontspace.com/cumberland-fontworks/merchant-copy). I have also done some good, old Googling and encounter this [thread](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419080559/http:/graphicdesign.stackexchange.com/questions/20027/identify-these-fonts-used-on-receipts) on the graphics forum of the Stack Exchange. Have a read, it appears to suggest a font called [Hypermarket](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419080559/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/green-type/hypermarket/), which looks close to your receipt. -Inferno986return-
