I'm not sure if it was ever a commercially available font since it was a promotional ad for Suddler & Hennessey (Lubalin was once their VP/Creative Director) and may have been one of their unique headline film fonts. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424023529im_/http:/typophile.com/files/herb_lubalin_116_4154.JPG) The closest re-appearance of the face I found was Lubalin's "oh! ah!" letterhead design (featured in U&lc) which was actually handlettered by [Tony Di Spigna](http://web.archive.org/web/20150424023529/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Tony%20Di%20Spigna).
