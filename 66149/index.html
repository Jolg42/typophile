One fantastic reason to get a tattoo is that (most) people harbor delusions that at least some of the things that make them who they are are immutable. I’d be surprised if anything about a person is necessarily safe from change. I know a lot of self-serving middle-aged types who could benefit from a daily reminder that they were once myopic 20-year-olds. And that said, the illusion of the durability of identity is often a very complete one, and the pleasure (short-term) of a tattoo can be great.
