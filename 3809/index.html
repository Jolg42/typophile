Well, you probably don't care any more, Rob, but I know that the forum participants are gnashing their teeth, desirous of knowing the identity of that grungy script. Or maybe not ... It seems to have begun its life as a version of a typeface that appears in the Solotype Catalog as Chopin. Dieter Steffmann digitized it as [Chopin Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20050826014149/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/samples/c/chopin.htm). Here's [another](http://web.archive.org/web/20050826014149/http:/www.dafont.com/en/search.php?nb_ppp=40&texte=News&nq=1&q=chopin) Chopin Script by a 'Diogenes'. And you can also find it as [Chase Callas](http://web.archive.org/web/20050826014149/http:/members.dca.net/pheanix/msie/c5.htm) by the defunct Soft Horizons. One of these, or yet another version, seems to have begat [Radagund](http://web.archive.org/web/20050826014149/http:/www.dafont.com/en/search.php?nb_ppp=40&texte=News&nq=1&q=radagund), which I believe to be the font used.
