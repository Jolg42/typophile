This is one of a series of posters done by artist [John Gilroy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320042544/http:/www.flickr.com/photos/vip223/galleries/72157622949213930/) for Guinness in the 1930s and 40s. Like many color posters of that era, this one was probably hand-lettered. My eyes aren't as keen as they used to be, but I believe the two lowercase **a**'s are slightly different. The artist was clearly using Gill Sans (1926) as a model. (This poster predates Candara by nearly half a century, and Humanist 521 is Bitstream's brand name for Gill Sans.)
