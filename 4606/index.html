There is no rule for doing the optical compensations, they are always done by eye. Just thicken them. Check this out: [http://briem.ismennt.is/2/2.3.2a/2.3.2.05.verticals.htm](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017131942/http:/briem.ismennt.is/2/2.3.2a/2.3.2.05.verticals.htm) [http://briem.ismennt.is/2/2.3.2a/2.3.2.06.illusion.htm](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017131942/http:/briem.ismennt.is/2/2.3.2a/2.3.2.06.illusion.htm)
