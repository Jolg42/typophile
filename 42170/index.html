I would argue that you need to do whatever makes reading the text easier for the reader(s)... Also, you are making two changes at the same time: going from ragged right to justified _and_ increasing the width of the gutter... With justified text there is the risk of having rivers of white space (due to unequal wordspaces), but it will give you less lines of text overall.
