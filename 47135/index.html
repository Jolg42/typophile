Baker Signet - forward

I’ve recently taken on a fairly big brand job and inherited a logotype which utilizes Baker Signet with the addition of some slight changes. I’m looking to streamline this logo and presented Fedra Sans along with some of the existing nuances. Still client response was “too far out.” I’m hoping someone may have a suggestion of a typeface that embodies some of Baker’s qualities but be a bit more contemporary if you will. A foundation for me to move this logotype forward. I appreciate any input.

Thank you,

Anonymous @ 7.Jul.2008 8.45am
JC. Arthur is probably one of the few typographic/lettering artists whose work is readily identifiable and individualistic. The word idiosyncratic comes to mind.It is borne out of his personality, the tools he uses and his studies. If you are looking for something with his “trademark style” you’ve only to search the myriad faces he has designed over the decades. Look at Hiroshige(Sans) as a possible alternative or just google his typefaces. Most are calligraphic but there are others to choose from. Michael
