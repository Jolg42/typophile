[Freshman](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317063054/http:/www.dafont.com/freshman.font?fpp=100&text=DICK+THE+BIRTHDAY+BOY) could do the job. I like also [National Champion](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317063054/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/kyle-wayne-benson/national-champion), better designed but some earmarks of your sample are absent and it doesn't have this vintage/amateur (inconsistent x-width + absence of kerning/spacing). [Player](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317063054/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/player) Bold (to get serifed /E crossbar) is another alternative but less convincing.

Thank you for your help. I decided to go with Freshman, as it looked the closest and was free. To compensate for the character spacing, I simply formatted it to have 8 pt. expanded spacing, and it looks darn near perfect. Thanks again!
