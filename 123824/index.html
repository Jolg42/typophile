The intertitle lettering artist seems to have been influenced by the style used by lettering artist Elizabeth Colwell. A version of her lettering from 1916 is the basis for a font by Fleisch & Apostrophic called Colwell ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416032247im_/http:/typophile.com/files/ColwellAsCycle_3950.jpg) [http://www.dafont.com/colwell.font](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416032247/http:/www.dafont.com/colwell.font "http://www.dafont.com/colwell.font") A toned down interpretation was published as a font by American Type Founders before 1923. Nick Curtis produced a digital version as McKenna Handletter NF ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416032247im_/http:/typophile.com/files/McKennaHandletterNFAsCycle_3532.gif) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416032247im_/http:/typophile.com/files/McKennaHandletterNFAscomes_6633.gif) Luc Devroye has an informative page on Elizabeth Colwell's lettering and various digital productions [http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-51178.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416032247/http:/luc.devroye.org/fonts-51178.html "http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-51178.html") Don
