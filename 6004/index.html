A formal analysis of the character shapes makes me believe the logo is custom designed, or at least customized. The "fr" ligature is very uncommon (only one typeface I know of has it), the left side of the "u" fits snugly against the "r", the top left serif of the peculiar "v" follows perfectly the movement of the tail of the "f" into the foot serifs of the "r", while its swash fills the gap between the "r" and the "u" perfectly, and both "e"s are designed in such a way that they "perform" flawlessly.
