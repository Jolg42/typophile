The construction of lower case ‘s’ is better than that of capital ‘S’. ‘Some concerns I have are about width (D, J, M, S, W, m), and the balance of upper and lower parts (g, S).’ D: looks relatively OK. J: It could either drop below the baseline for better spacing or it could have the lower part of ‘C’ reflected but shoter. M: A little wider and with ink traps added. S: C, G, S, a, c, J, j and y they would better have similar ‘beaks’ – ‘stroke endings’. Either like those of ’C’ or ‘G’, or even just straight horizontal. W: Make it a little wider and add ink traps. m: In relation to ‘n’ it looks relatively OK. S: The diagonal stroke should move a little downwards and very slightly the diagonal of ‘s’. g: Its ear on the x-height should be a little longer. j, y: the end-stroke below the baseline could become a little horizintalized. The metrics need fine tuning. I hope all the above will be of some use. By the way I just posted some more things to Critique/San Serif/ Doric, you are welcome to tell me what you think about it.
