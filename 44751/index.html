Keep in mind that much of Lubalin Graph's character comes from the fact that it's a geometric (the rounds are "perfect" circles). Once you go wide, you get ovals. You might want something more square like [Dispatch Extended](http://web.archive.org/web/20110506142514/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/font_bureau/dispatch_extended/), which is less country than Hellenic. As for sans serifs, there's nothing more mod/scifi/60s than [Eurostile](http://web.archive.org/web/20110506142514/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/eurostile_std_ot_virtual/). See also [ITC Blair](http://web.archive.org/web/20110506142514/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/itc/itc_blair/), [ITC Flatiron](http://web.archive.org/web/20110506142514/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/itc/itc_flatiron/), and the newish [FP Head](http://web.archive.org/web/20110506142514/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontpartners/fp_head/?sample_text=SCI-FI%20MOD%201960).
