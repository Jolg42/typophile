I also agree about about the ITC styled ligature, but the logo is not one single typeface, but a blending of several with a little custom work. IMO, it has the “TH” ligature of ITC Avant Garde Gothic, and “I” It uses either the [Kabel](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202063624/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/kabel/heavy/testdrive.html?s=THING&p=48+) or ITC Kabel cap “N” or [ITC Serif Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202063624/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/itc-serif-gothic/bold/testdrive.html?s=THING&p=48) cap “N” and squared off the terminals. The cap “G” is treated similar to the cap “G” in [Kable DT Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202063624/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dtptypes/kabel-dtcondensed/kabel-dt-condensed/testdrive.html?s=THING&p=48) and could have be modified by using the “G” from either Serif Gothic or Kabel. But then again, we may be missing a single typeface that has all of the above.
