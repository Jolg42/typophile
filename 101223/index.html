These numerals were [created by Erik Marinovich for the opening feature of Cincinnati Magazine annual ‘Where to Eat’ issue](http://web.archive.org/web/20130820054238/http:/www.erikmarinovich.com/work/cincinnati-magazine). I can remember the [Pompadour Numerals](http://web.archive.org/web/20130820054238/http:/www.losttype.com/font/?name=pompadour) by Andy Mangold (which have been turned into font since) and the [Christmas Numerals](http://web.archive.org/web/20130820054238/http:/www.behance.net/gallery/Christmas-Numerals-(Free-Font)/6278933) by Stereotypes Sasha Timplan.
