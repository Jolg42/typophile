Steve, The name of the original typeface is Glenmoy. I haven't heard of any other digital version except for Mousse script by Alejandro Paul. His interpritation is to be found at Veer, [http://www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=UMT0000162#specimen](http://web.archive.org/web/20110419190222/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=UMT0000162#specimen "http://www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=UMT0000162#specimen")
