What you're looking for is one of the FHWA Standard Alphabets for Traffic Control Devices. Popular immitators include: [Interstate](http://web.archive.org/web/20150305002137/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/interstate/) [Blue Highway](http://web.archive.org/web/20150305002137/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/blue-highway-deluxe/) [Expressway](http://web.archive.org/web/20150305002137/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/expressway/) There is also the approved alternate: [ClearviewHwy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150305002137/http:/www.clearviewhwy.com/)
