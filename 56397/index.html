Command G Name your glyphs "paragraph.alt" and Q.alt" They will now be available to users in the glyph palette. Also: Create a Stylistic Set. sub paragraph by paragraph.alt; sub Q by Q.alt; This will work if you generate ".otf" fonts, and be available to users in the OT features palette.
