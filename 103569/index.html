"THE BEST OF BLONDIE": confirming [Quest](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014194646/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/quest-rr/) First sample song titles and text: possibly [Optima](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014194646/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/optima) Second sample song titles: [Adelon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014194646/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/softmaker/adelon-serial) is Softmaker version of [Albertus](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014194646/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/albertus) (see [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014194646/http:/typophile.com/node/93218#comment-510520) for more implementors of it). Bitstream version [Flareserif 821](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014194646/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/flareserif-821) is another option to get such boldness. There are also [some extra-bold versions of Albertus](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014194646/http:/typophile.com/node/27712) (Alaska) shipped by HP and Brother with their laser printers.
