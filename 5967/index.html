Anonymous @ Fri, 2004-08-06 21:18
The lower case looks exactly like Spumoni, and the upper case looks like Spumoni with added/modified serifs. I agree with Mark. For comparison, there are three other commercial fonts that have a strong stylistic resemblance to Spumoni (none of them match the samples): [Elroy](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518125540/http:/www.fonthaus.com/products/fonts/view.cfm?sku=SD10101) by Christian Schwartz; [Heatwave](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518125540/http:/www.fonthaus.com/products/fonts/view.cfm?sku=PN10131) by Panache Graphics; and Sniff by Joan Barjau of Type-O-Tones (sample follows). Sniff has the most 'embellishment', but has the same sort of casual feel that I get from Spumoni. The Nick Curtis font is all caps and is based (apparently) on Goudy Stout, which it resembles, with added bounce. ![Sniff sample](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518125540im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/83/42310.gif)
