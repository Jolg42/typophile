The Buzz on the street

When Adobe announced the acquisition of Buzzword, the Flash based word processor, I couldn’t wait to check it out especially with a quote like this…

“Because it was built with Adobe Flex™ software and runs in the Adobe Flash™ Player, Buzzword enables greater document quality, outstanding typography, page layout controls, and robust support for integrated graphics, regardless of the browser or device.”

It’s pretty cool, but I’m going to have to fire up the Phinnsignal on the outstanding typography claim ;-)
