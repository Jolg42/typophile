Anonymous @ 31.Jul.2007 10.32am
Well, I was in a similar situation, and the gallery was in a museum district. I decided to try and use that larger context. I also looked at the range of shows—which included everything from contemporary international art to period artifacts like clothing and furniture. I decided to pick a contemporary serif and combine that with more traditional sans so the publications etc. could shift in feel and remain within the identity. I think Pentagram’s work for the DMA is very solid, using Fedra Sans is very nice. [http://a.velluminous.org/dma.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20071230035014/http:/a.velluminous.org/dma.html "http://a.velluminous.org/dma.html") Maybe a family like OurType’s Fresco and Fresco Sans would work. It would probably function well for wayfinding and identity, and have a serif option for extended text in a catalogue.
