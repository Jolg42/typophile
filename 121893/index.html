1971 is near the apex of the photolettering era when fonts were commonly squeezed, stretched and otherwise distorted on demand. so the nameplate \_THE BLACK PANTHER\_ may be somewhat different from the font used to derive it. In no particular order, here are some fonts based on narrow linears of the era. Permanent Headline URW ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322135631im_/http:/typophile.com/files/PermanentHeadlineURW-AsBLACKPANTHER_5780.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/permanent/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322135631/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/permanent/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/permanent/") Plakette TS Light ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322135631im_/http:/typophile.com/files/PlaketteTSLight-AsBLACKPANTHER_5068.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef-typeshop/plakette/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322135631/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef-typeshop/plakette/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef-typeshop/plakette/") British Inserat MN Cond ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322135631im_/http:/typophile.com/files/BritishInseratMNCond-AsBLACKPANTHER_5343.gif) [https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itfmecanorma/british-inserat/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322135631/https:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itfmecanorma/british-inserat/ "https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itfmecanorma/british-inserat/") Don
