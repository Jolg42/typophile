No Mike, I'm pretty sure, because I recognize the methodics in constructing the letters, which struck me when I first saw the face. While Entropy\* and Osprey\*\* are hybrids constructed from two or more fonts\*\*\*, this one is assembled by fusing parts of the next letter in the alphabet of the same typeface (Trajan by the looks of it). \* **Entropy** is uppercase _Missive_ fused to lowercase _Memphis_. **Missive** itself looks like a mix of _Garamond_ and _Trade Gothic_. \*\* **Osprey** is basically _Entropy_ with _Park Avenue_ lower- case thrown in for added flavour. How far can you go? \*\*\* a popular practice in the deconstructivist heydays of the early nineties; see also P. Scott Makela's [Dead History](http://web.archive.org/web/20130914174437/http:/www.emigre.com/EF.php?fid=88) (Linotype Centennial and VAG Rounded) and Max Kisman's [Fudoni](http://web.archive.org/web/20130914174437/http:/www.identifont.com/show?M0) (you can guess which faces).
