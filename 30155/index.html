This looks to me like a customized version of Trade Gothic, News Gothic, Benton Sans, Franklin Gothic, etc. But if you're looking for a face that has some of the distinctive characters already included... Akkurat has the curved l but not the one-story g. [http://lineto.com/](http://web.archive.org/web/20130811154925/http:/lineto.com/ "http://lineto.com/") Flama has the curved l and one-story g, but DIN-like construction. [http://vllg.com/Feliciano/Flama/mudTyper+Weights/](http://web.archive.org/web/20130811154925/http:/vllg.com/Feliciano/Flama/mudTyper+Weights/ "http://vllg.com/Feliciano/Flama/mudTyper+Weights/") Foco has the desired features but adds a hint of calligraphic construction. [http://www.daltonmaag.com/browse/fonts/dama/foco](http://web.archive.org/web/20130811154925/http:/www.daltonmaag.com/browse/fonts/dama/foco "http://www.daltonmaag.com/browse/fonts/dama/foco")
