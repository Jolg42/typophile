It is described in the OpenType ["name" table](http://web.archive.org/web/20130216011217/http:/www.microsoft.com/typography/otspec/name.htm) specification. Scroll down to the "Name IDs" section and read the description for ID 18: Compatible Full (Macintosh only). This is the entry that the "Mac Name" field represents. Regards, Adam
