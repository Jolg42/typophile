This is actually Monotype Script, available at the source given by Ryuk. It's a German Script designed by F. H. Steltzer, 1931 based on Pragefest, aka Samson Script, designed by E.Lautenbach, Ludwig & Mayer , 1928. Resembling British Script and Signal. Lighter weight version was Monoscript 351.
