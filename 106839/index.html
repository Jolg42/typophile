Only confirming your title... Looks definitely handwritten to me, all repeating letters are different (sample [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/eventjubilee.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/WELL_WED_C10_-1477.jpg). According to [this post](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/annabriggsphotography.com/?p=2083), " [Coral Pheasant](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.coralpheasant.com/) purveyed of unique custom paper goods and stationary for weddings". Try asking them, they may answer. Some alternatives now: [Plumero Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/marketplace.veer.com/font/Plumero-UMT0000018), [Saissant](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/magpiepaperworks/saissant/), [Vermandois](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/magpiepaperworks/vermandois/), [Jacques and Gilles](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/emily-lime/jacques-and-gilles/), [Carolyna](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/emily-lime/carolyna-pro-black/), [Peoni](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/emily-lime/peoni-pro/), [Bombshell](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/emily-lime/bombshell-pro/), [Cursive CK](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.creatingkeepsakes.com/articles/CK_Cursive), [Anna Clara](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/trial-by-cupcakes/anna-clara), [Cantoni](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/debi-sementelli/cantoni), [Asterism](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/great-lakes-lettering/asterism), [Key Notes](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/the-lilypad.com/store/Key-Notes.html), [Ondise](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417092640/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/magpiepaperworks/ondise)
