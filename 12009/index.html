Veer

Indices : Font Retailers : Veer

Veer provides visual elements for use in professional creative work, such as graphic design, motion design, advertising and filmmaking. Products include stock photography, illustration, maps, typefaces, and unique design-related merchandise. Products are showcased on the website veer.com and in regular direct-mail catalogs.

As part of the company’s ongoing involvement in the design community, the creative folks at Veer also maintain The Skinny, a lively design and pop-culture influenced weblog. Veer also hosts the type-related animated shorts “Behind the Typeface: Cooper Black” and “Etched in Stone” – both produced by Typophile regular Cheshire Dave.

Veer sells fonts from the following foundries: Adobe, Alias, Atomic Media, Cabinet, Device, Elsner+Flake, Font Bureau, Fountain, G-Type, IHOF, Jukebox, Lanston Type Co., Letraset Fontek, Mean Tangerine, P22, Red Rooster, Rimmer, Sherwood, ShinnType, Test Pilot Collective, Type Trust, Umbrella, and Virus.
