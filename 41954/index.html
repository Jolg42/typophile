Opti created nothing whatsoever original fontwise. Your desired font was originally Filmotype's (predigital) Lariat Script. The only other digitization I'm aware of is David Rakowski's [Anderson Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20131016234906/http:/www.faces.co.uk/fonts/Intecsas/Anderson-Script-IN20151/).
