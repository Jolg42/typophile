Anonymous @ 4.Jan.2006 9.47am
A digital version of Andrich Minerva was released by [Image Club](http://web.archive.org/web/20090925023630/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Image%252BClub) in 1989 and subsequently revised several times during the early 90s. The face was discontinued for quality and consistency reasons in 1998 when Image Club was still part of [Adobe](http://web.archive.org/web/20090925023630/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Adobe). Since it was discontinued and Agfa not longer distributes the family as part of [the current Image Club library](http://web.archive.org/web/20090925023630/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/searchresults.htm?st=5&cid=Image+Club), the only place that Andrich Minerva is still available is on an old copy of the Image Club LetterPress disc. I believe I still have a couple of lightly-used copies of this disc, if you are interested in obtaining one. Here are a couple of samples set in Image Club’s Andrich Minerva using the text from your image. As you can see, the cap ’R’ is different and the kerning is fairly ugly. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20090925023630im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/andrich1.gif) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20090925023630im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/andrich2.gif)
