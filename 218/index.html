Well, I wouldn't say it looks so legible. There is a lot of odd stuff, for instance: -t is too high, and its crossbar too; -it's too tight for this size. try 2 pixels spacing, "stem-to-stem"; -vwyz are wide (or are the other letters too narrow?); -VW are really wide; -bdpq looks strange: the bowls should be similar to o; -x is narrow; -A's counter is too closed; -the numbers seems to differ in style from other characters... I think you still have a lot of work to do here. Good luck.
