[Ascender’s study](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.ascendercorp.com/pr/pr2004_11_09.html) found that the 10 most popular typeface families are: [Poynter](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/fontbureau.com/fonts/PoynterOSText), [Franklin Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/itc/itc_franklin_gothic_std_complete_vp/), [Helvetica](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/neue_helvetica_std_complete_vp/), [Utopia](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/creative_alliance/utopia_std_complete_vp/), [Times](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/times_std_complete_vp/), [Nimrod](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/monotype/nimrod_std_vp/), [Century Old Style](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/monotype/monotype_century_old_style_pro_vp/), [Interstate](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Interstate), [Bureau Grotesque](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Bureau%20Grot) and [Miller](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202022852/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Miller). Further, 35 of the top 100 daily newspapers use a custom typeface or modified font in addition to retail, off-the-shelf fonts.
