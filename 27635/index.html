I've gone through the fonts on [Comicraft](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/www.comicbookfonts.com/) and [Blambot](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/www.blambot.com/), but I can't find an exact match. Oddly enough, the font I've found that has the most in common with your mystery font (in the basic letterforms) is [GFYJeanna](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/chank/gfy-handwriting-fontpak/gfyjeanna-regular/), a ["Go Font Yourself"](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/www.chank.com/font_detail.php?sku=5456) font from [Chank](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/www.chank.com/). I suppose there's a chance that your mystery font is a customized -- and much-improved -- version of GFYJeanna. If you don't need an exact match (and no one else is able to find one for you), I suggest that you go through the offerings of Comicraft and Blambot. They both have many fonts that are much nicer than an unmodified GFYJeanna.

I've been hunting for this in [Part 11 of the Script Font ID Guide](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/Script11Pg1.html), which is all hand-printed fonts. I used the 'G' and the 'y' as my initial filter, and I couldn't find a match for this lettering. The problem, as our [FAQ](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/typophile.com/forums/typeid/faq) says, is that there are just too many hand-printed fonts, because they are cheap and easy to make. It's very possible that this is a custom font done for this company. My suggestion is similar to Spire's, although I suggest looking for a similar font in the Script Guide, and then looking up the supplier in the Reference List. Many of them can be found as free downloads, or part of a rather inexpensive font collection. - Mike Yanega

Michael, Thanks for your comments about my Guides. I agree that your suggestion would make things easier for the users, but there are two main reasons that I have not made links to suppliers from the samples: 1) There are multiple suppliers for most of the fonts, since the designing foundry is usually only one source, and I am not trying to play favorites, even though I could benefit from doing so, with sales referral fees. 2) Links have a way of getting out of date, and the maintenance task would be horrendous. I have been lucky enough to have wonderfully generous volunteers to help make samples for the Guides, and even to take on the design of the Serif Font ID Guide for me, but I'm spread too thin to take on the addition and maintenance of about 6,000 links. I am thinking about the idea of putting links into the [Supplier Codes List](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/ListPages/SupplierCodes.html), and I already do have them on the [Type Providers page](http://web.archive.org/web/20130916185126/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/providers.html). - Mike Yanega
