Goudy's "I am Type!"

I am trying to find a reference to the first publication of Goudy’s The Type Speaks. I wonder if anybody knows. Was it originally published by Lanston Monotype Co. in Philadelphia? If so, when? One good reference I have here is, The Type Speaks, by Frederic W. Goudy (Chicago/Utica, NY: Black Cat Press, 1937; 93 copies, hand-set by Howard Coggeshall and Norman W Forgue and printed at HC’s press. 5 x 7 1/2 in., 8-page string-tied booklet). Also, The Type Speaks, by Frederic W. Goudy (Washington: The Press of the Three Bears, 1938). But that’s about it…
