I think this was an old Letrasetfont called Herkules, based on an old Art Nouveau era typeface called Hercules. I think Dieter Steffmann at typOasis (Designer section) has a [digital version](http://web.archive.org/web/20110315095456/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/samples/h/herkules.htm) of it. - Mike Yanega
