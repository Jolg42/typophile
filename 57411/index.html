The font that immediately sprang to mind seeing your sketch was Parsons, available in digital form as [Parsnip NF](http://web.archive.org/web/20131216050706/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/parsnip-nf/). This will give you a very old-school flavor, perhaps not what you're looking for. In the original, there are lots of alternate characters and long extenders, but none for the caps P, B, and S specifically. In general, that kind of thing would make it easier to weave. Probably just a wacky idea, but maybe it leads you somewhere.
