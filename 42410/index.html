LUXE shows signs of [squooshing](http://web.archive.org/web/20080228132852/http:/typophile.com/wiki/squoosh). Could be [Trade Gothic Extended](http://web.archive.org/web/20080228132852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/linotype/trade_gothic_lt_std_extended_ot/?sample_text=LUXE&sample_size=60) with slabs attached. MAGAZINE is [FF DIN](http://web.archive.org/web/20080228132852/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontfont/ff_din_ot_regular/?sample_text=MAGAZINE)
