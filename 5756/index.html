And cross stitching... Speaking on "first pixel fonts, I own two embroidery books, containing more of 200 full square flavored alphabets, most of them are very "enlightened" with more of 30 pixels x-height, but the small sizes, 7, 9, 10, 12, are very similar to our contemporanean screen fonts (Silkscreen and others) They all came from the "Sajou", "Rouyer" and "Alexandre" embroidery booklets, from 1940/50 I think. By the way, my favorite classic pixel font is Georgia in small sizes.
