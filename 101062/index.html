The monospaced letters (but unfortunately not the numerals) are very similar to **[ProFontWindows](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/ProFontWindows)** free font designed by Andrew Welch (SQ Software, 1987) The grotesque is very close to **[ITC Franklin Gothic Book](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=ITC&ffam=ITC%20Franklin%20Gothic%20Book%20-%20Regular&fid=f0fa0fd2fd18567fc887a33456871365&fsize=48&text=SIMPLIFIED%20CONTROL%20AND%20FLIGHT%20MANAGEMENT%20FOR%20THE%201980's&wrap=2)** with small differences in /G, /1 (you can use /I) and /8

The grotesque from 70's NASA technical report reminds me of letterings done with [Leroy Lettering Machine](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/typophile.com/node/79271) (including some suggestions for similars such as Leroy Lettering Light, Planscribe, Engineering Plot) Adding [Gordon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/gordon/) and [Register Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/btn/register-sans-btn). Unfortunately, I don't have a match for the monospaced either but I can point point to a direction that you may find helpful: [NB55](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/neubauladen/nb55set) by [Neubauladen Stefan Gandl](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.neubauladen.com/), [NE Saber and Saber Hund](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.hypefortype.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=Neutura), suprb [ASCA and ASCA-D](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.youworkforthem.com/designer/48/suprb/), Identikal [Bennu Sans A and B](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.hypefortype.com/bennu-sans-a.html), [Gerusa](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/scannerlicker/gerusa/), [ARAME](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dimitrelima/0arame/), [Roundabout](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011232016/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/roundabout-nf)
