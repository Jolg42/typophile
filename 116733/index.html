This is Favorit/Teuton Text. German from 19th century. Wilhelm Woellmer foundry in Berlin. Specimen Peltzendorfer \_Schriften Atlas (1898)\_ ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317075427im_/http:/typophile.com/files/FavoritAkaTeutonTextSM@pleltzendorfer1898_4009.jpg) Nearly identical, Kirchengotische Schrift. Excellent quality digital version, Aeronaut by Georg Herold-Wildfellner for Face Type [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/facetype/aeronaut/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317075427/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/facetype/aeronaut/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/facetype/aeronaut/") Don
