Anonymous @ 15.Sep.2007 2.00am
I find it incredible that a design and PR agency would sacrifice its own identity for the sake of speed. Two weeks is more than adequate time to develop a good logo. Sometimes the hardest decision to make for a designer (or anyone designing something) is to kill what they are working on. However, all the time spent on noodling this particular direction could be much better spent on finding something better. And by better, I’m not suggesting you need to invent a new typeface or re-invent the graphic wheel. Many design firms both huge and tiny use a very well set typeface with little modification. If you need a crisp, professional appearance, and you need it quick, that is the best way to go. Like Litera, I hate to sound harsh and overly critical, but sometimes we need a creative director to straighten us out. I’ve had it done to me, and have had to do similar to others before. Please realize it’s not about you personally, but an honest attempt to help you get the best results for your agency.
