This some variation of Clarendon, which resembles the Egyptian [once known as Antique] but has bracketed rather than rectangular [slab] serifs. Not an abundance of this style with height to width proportions similar to your image. Consider: Thorowgood ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418223757im_/http:/typophile.com/files/ThorowgoodAsGOTIA_4129.gif) [https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/thorowgood/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418223757/https:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/thorowgood/ "https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/thorowgood/") Consort ExtraBold CondensedRR ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418223757im_/http:/typophile.com/files/ConsortRRExtraBoldcondAsGOTIA_6248.gif) [https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/consort-rr/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418223757/https:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/consort-rr/ "https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/consort-rr/") CA Normal Serif Extra Bold ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418223757im_/http:/typophile.com/files/CANormalSerifExtraBoldAsGOTIA_5108.gif) [https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-normal-serif/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418223757/https:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-normal-serif/ "https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/capearcona/ca-normal-serif/") Don
