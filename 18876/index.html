Happy Day to Grant Hutchinson

Grant Hutchinson, part-time Splorper, and full-time Veerster celebrates his 30-something birthday today. Grant is the quintessential Typophile, dipping his toes in every aspect of typography - fontographing, selling, writing, and all the tech stuff. He’s been around the block and knows what’s what and I’m proud to call him a pal.
