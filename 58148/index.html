I actually prefer the lefty2.jpg It is traditionally 'correct' in respect to the laws of logo design. It scales well, its simple but eye catching. Its truly beautiful. Less is more and just because gradients and three dimensional bells and whistles are sexy it doesn't mean they're practical. Imagine the 3d version at the top of a letter head photocopied three times over in black and white and then faxed across the country. The lefty2 would retain a true to form image. As it should. It's wonderful.
