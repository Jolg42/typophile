Decided to treat the 2 fonts separately, look too different for me. Milano: close are [(DIN) Neuzeit Grotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20140828221148/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/neuzeit-grotesk)/ [Geometric 706](http://web.archive.org/web/20140828221148/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/geometric-706), [Proxima Nova](http://web.archive.org/web/20140828221148/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/marksimonson/proxima-nova) Extra Condensed (playing with versions to get the single storey /a) PIANTA \* MAP \* PLAN (and the rest except MILANO): can't find a font that could match both lower and uppercases. Here are some you may find interesting though: [Planeta](http://web.archive.org/web/20140828221148/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/gestalten/planeta_ot/ot_ps) (Plakat, /M and /I off),
