Hi Tom, _Legible from a distance?_ yep _Even colour?_ N, Y and W too heavy, A slightly heavy, M functional but lacks stylistic integration _Any strokes look too fat or thin compared to the rest?_ N verticals and joints, joints at baseline of W _Do any curves look strange?_ nothing overtly strange, but "wierd is good" in the sense that a lot more can be done with the curves you've got.
