This is an old wood type called Painters Roman. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701130457im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Painters-Roman_6613.jpg) It was first produced in the late 1870s by Vanderburgh and Wells and by the William Page Wood Type Co. Here is the character set of a condensed version, in the form of a mirrored photograph of the actual wood type. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701130457im_/http:/typophile.com/files/PaintersRomanWoodTypeMirrorSM_6728.jpg) I don't recall a digital version, but will check further.
