This is hand-lettered for sure. As Mike would say, there are ton of fonts that closely mimic handwriting. You can search any of the font sites like Myfonts or Dafont and find tons of them. Or go to Mike's site: www.bowfinprintworks.com and search his archive.

It is handwriting. None of the letters are exactly the same. But you can get a pretty similar feeling with [Sketchley](http://web.archive.org/web/20050515203706/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/sketchley-bt/).
