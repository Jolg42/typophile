FIFA World Cup US Bid 2018/2022 Custom Type

I was really excited to see that one of the first projects I’ve gotten to work on while working at Pentagram has been posted on their blog. I’ve been designing this custom typeface under the creative direction of Michael Gericke and Luke Hayman.

It’s currently being used on the GoUsaBid.com site. I’ve been incredibly excited to see what the talented designers at Pentagram are doing with the typeface. Look forward to a future Pentagram blog post with all the applications of the bid message and ’Game’ typeface.
