The art of using type. Colloquially used to describe the range of activities incorporating [graphic design](http://web.archive.org/web/20140112120646/http:/typophile.com/wiki/graphic%20design), [typesetting](http://web.archive.org/web/20140112120646/http:/typophile.com/wiki/typesetting), and [type design](http://web.archive.org/web/20140112120646/http:/typophile.com/wiki/type%20design). Font nerding, the favorite activity of Typophiles, is something else entirely.
