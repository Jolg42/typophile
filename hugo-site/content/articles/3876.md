From the talented artist and typographic designer, Delve Withrington:   
   
"   
 I'd like to invite you all to some upcoming gallery   
 exhibits that I'm participating in. They're all around   
 the bay area so hopefully everyone will have a chance   
 to see my work at a convenient location. The dates are   
 listed below (beginning with one tomorrow night!) with   
 the details on each following. For those of you not in   
 the bay area, I'm looking for shows all over the country   
 and I'll be sure let you know if something happens near   
 you. In the meantime, you can get a sneak peek at some   
 of my new work at my site: [http://www.withrington.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014142235/http:/www.withrington.com/)   
   
   
 Calendar at-a-glance:   
   
 1. Tomorrow, 6-8:30pm, Pro Arts Gallery, Oakland   
   
 2. 12/6/02, 7-10pm, City Art Gallery, SF   
   
 3. 1/3/03, 7-10pm, City Art Gallery, SF   
   
 4. 1/12/03, 2-4pm, Berkeley Art Center, Berkeley   
   
 5. 1/29/03, 6:30-8:30pm, Los Medanos College Art Gallery,   
 Pittsburg, CA   
   
   
 The Details:   
   
 The first event on the calendar is the closing party   
 and final bidding opportunity in the silent auction at   
 Pro Arts Gallery in downtown Oakland. I've a new piece   
 called "Axial Discord" in this show and it could be   
 yours for very cheap if you place the winning bid. The   
 show (which is fantastic, by the way) is up right now   
 but closes this Saturday. Pro Arts Gallery is located   
 at: 461 9th St., in Oakland near Broadway (two blocks   
 from the 12th St BART). Refreshments served. More info   
 available at: [http://www.proartsgallery.org](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014142235/http:/www.proartsgallery.org/)   
   
 The next two exhibitions are show openings at the City   
 Art Gallery in the Mission district of SF. During the   
 December show, I'll have a few pieces in the back room.   
 However, for the January show, I'll have several works   
 in the front room, so you city folk shouldn't miss it.   
 And if you just can't wait for either of these shows,   
 I've actually got a few pieces of art in the back room   
 for the show currently running at City Art through the   
 month of November. The City Art Gallery is located at:   
 828 Valencia St. between 19th and 20th Streets. Refresh-   
 ments served. More info is available at their website:   
 [http://www.cityartgallery.org](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014142235/http:/www.cityartgallery.org/)   
   
 Fourth on the list is a show at the Berkeley Art Center,   
 which is just a bit North of downtown Berkeley. This is   
 a fabulous members show that I'll have a piece in. It'll   
 be on exhibit from 1/12/03 to 2/15/03. The BAC is located   
 at: 1275 Walnut St. Refreshments served. Find more info   
 about BAC online at: [http://www.berkeleyartcenter.org/](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014142235/http:/www.berkeleyartcenter.org/)   
   
 Finally, I'm in a two-person show at the Los Medanos   
 College Art Gallery in Pittsburg, CA. I do admit, this   
 is a bit of a trek for some, but it will be well worth   
 it for those of you who can make it. I will arrange a   
 carpool so get in touch with me if you need a ride.   
   
   
 That's it for now. I'll be sure to keep you all updated   
 on when and where my new work can be seen. Otherwise, I   
 frequently update my website regarding my exhibitions.   
   
 I hope to see you at one (or two) of them soon.   
   
 Yours,   
 Delve Withrington   
"
