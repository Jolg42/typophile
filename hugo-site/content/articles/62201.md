There will be regional differences when it comes to these sorts of things.  <cite>The Chicago Manual of Style,</cite> which is a widely accepted guide in the US, addresses your first question in two sections: 6.127 and 6.129. Basically, if the list is introduced by a complete sentence and a colon and the list items are short, then the list is not punctuated and does not end with a period. If the introduction to the list is not a grammatically complete sentence and the list elements consist of phrases or sentences themselves, then usually the list is punctuated with a semicolon between items and concluded with a period.  But there's room in there for plenty of nuance and exception.  Your second question is addressed by the CMS in §6.64. Again summarizing: When the colon is used within a sentence, then it is followed by lowercase (unless a proper name). When the colon introduces several separate sentences, then the following word is capitalized.
