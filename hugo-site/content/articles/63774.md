Hey all,

I'm doing my thesis on the role of the serif in Latin and non-Latin writing systems.

I would like to start with the Latin writing system.  
1. Origin of the Serif  
2. Different shapes of Serifs  
3. It's role in readability/legibility  
4. It's role as a decorative element

Then it was my plan to apply that scheme to other writing systems, such as Japanese, Chinese, Hebrew, Arabic, Devenagari, Cyrillic, Greek...(These are the possible candidates. I won't discuss them all. )

I would work as following:  
Is there a serif/equivalent to a serif?  
Does it have a name? What's its origin?  
Does it improve readability/legibility?  
Or does it merely exist for esthetical reasons?

I have some questions I would like to ask you:  
1. Is this a good idea all together? Should I just stick with Latin?  
2. Do you know any good books on any of these writing systems?
