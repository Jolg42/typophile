Elbowruminations is a graphic design blog for non-designers, businesspeople and designers, maintained by [Elbowroom Design](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023060508/http:/www.elbowroomdesign.com/). Based in Calgary, Alberta, this blog [critiques good and bad graphic design](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023060508/http:/www.elbowroomdesign.com/musings/category/aroundcalgary/%E2%80%9D), spotlights [new technology](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023060508/http:/www.elbowroomdesign.com/musings/category/technology) and [great blogs](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023060508/http:/www.elbowroomdesign.com/musings/category/blogs/), and encourages [creativity in business](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023060508/http:/www.elbowroomdesign.com/musings/category/features/). Elbowruminations aims to be a thorough and thoughtful resource for designers and non-designers alike.
