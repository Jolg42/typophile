**1 April, 2006 - U.S. Government Announces Font Anti-Piracy Initiative**

_Washington, D.C._ — Today the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco & Firearms (ATF) announced a major offensive to stop font piracy. Its team of specially trained investigators apprehended a syndicate of online font software pirates and is investigating reports of counterfeit fonts. For complete information visit: [http://www.fontpolice.net/](http://web.archive.org/web/20070815193646/http:/www.fontpolice.net/ "http://www.fontpolice.net/")
