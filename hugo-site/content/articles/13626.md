On a designer friend’s coffee table the other day, I came across a big gorgeous book that was nothing but a collection of the best of [U&lc](http://web.archive.org/wiki/Uandlc) magazine! (For anyone too young to remember, U&lc, Upper & lower case, was this brilliant oversized typography and design magazine that was around from the 70’s until 1999.) In any case, it was a “must read” for graphic designers and I’m baffled that I hadn’t heard of this book before. You can order it straight from the publisher [http://www.markbattypublisher.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20061123004134/http:/www.markbattypublisher.com/) who has lots of other interesting graphic-design books. (Check out the book about [graffiti](http://web.archive.org/wiki/graffiti) in the Paris Underground, it’s fascinating.)
