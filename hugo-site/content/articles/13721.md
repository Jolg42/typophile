The Pratt Institute is a university-level art school in New York city. Both undergraduate and graduate degrees are available. Graphic Design is part of the school's curricular offerings.

The school's website is [Pratt.edu](http://web.archive.org/web/20130922092440/http:/www.pratt.edu/).
