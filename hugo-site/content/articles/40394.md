A [decorative](http://web.archive.org/web/20140718172358/http:/typophile.com/wiki/decorative) typeface with a second [stroke](http://web.archive.org/web/20140718172358/http:/typophile.com/wiki/stroke) inset within the main [outline](http://web.archive.org/web/20140718172358/http:/typophile.com/wiki/outlines). The **inline** stroke is typically much thinner than the main type outline. Sub-types of inline include centered stroke and offset stroke.
