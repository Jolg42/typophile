Digital Type Design   
Summer Institute of Graphic Design   
Rhode Island School of Design   
   
Mondays-Fridays, July 8-19   
9am-5pm / 10 Sessions   
   
This course is for students and professionals alike who are interested in learning how to create typefaces. Participants learn about the principles of spacing, legibility and letter drawing by creating their own typefaces. The course is taught by Cyrus Highsmith, senior designer at Font Bureau.   
   
For more info go to:
