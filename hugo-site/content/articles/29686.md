Hello everyone,

i am currently working on a logo for a cell phone/web-based dating application. After some client feedback, I am now looking for some alternatives to the current font (Linear by E + F). I’ve tried various light italic sans serifs (with Neutraface being the best match i’ve got so far), but i think that i need something with a lower x-height and shorter extender length (like Linear).
