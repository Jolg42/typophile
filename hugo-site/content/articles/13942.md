[Device Fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20150205000845/http:/www.devicefonts.co.uk/) is the label of type designer [Rian Hughes](http://web.archive.org/web/20150205000845/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Rian%20Hughes). Hughes is also know for his distinctive illustration and animation style.
