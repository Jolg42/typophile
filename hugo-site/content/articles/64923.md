During the _Type[&]Design 2009_ conference session at the Royal Academy of Art on the 19th of November, David Lemon gave a presentation on his proposal for ‘glyph completeness’ in OpenType fonts via Acrobat Connect. Although the audience could see him, David had to do his presentation without any visible contact with the other side. Also he had to talk slowly, because of the slightly delayed sound. Although both speaker and audience considered the session to be somewhat surreal, the presentation was highly appreciated.  
&nbsp;
