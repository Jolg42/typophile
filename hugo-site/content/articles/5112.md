Hi,   
   
Archura: arched textura.   
   
I started off the design of my first typeface with a post to Typophile ( [http://www.typophile.com/forums/messages/4100/29010.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417131841/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/4100/29010.html)), and so I thought I would submit the final design as well. Archura is the result of my typeface design class with Cyrus Highsmith at the Rhode Island School of Design.
