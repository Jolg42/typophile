This summer Dan Morris is going to teach letterpress printing at the Cooper Union. The classes start next week. Dan is a very cool guy and he has helped bringing our printshop up to speed. I think that this class could be great for the type obsessed here in the New York area. I am certainly going to try to get into this class and thought I should advertise it here.
