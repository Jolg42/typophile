Years ago, I created [Kosal Says hy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123042507/http:/www.1001fonts.com/font_details.html?font_id=2243) purely as an my first font endeavor. I uploaded to as many sites possible hoping to see it used everywhere (however bad it may be.) Lo and behold, I do a search for graffiti fonts on myfonts and see a font that is extremely close to my original drawings. So close that it can't be a coincidence: [Shallow.](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123042507/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/wilton/shallow/)

 ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123042507im_/http:/typophile.com/files/comparison_3976.gif)

Looks like the designer softened up the letters, added whatever ornament necessary to distinguish it, thickened the strokes, and made some much needed improvements. Is this legal? Probably so, because I labeled it as freeware, right?

Not that it bothers me, my original was crappy anyway. I'm just surprised.  
Slightly flattered.
