Designed by [William Addison Dwiggins](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904160201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/William%20Addison%20Dwiggins) for [Megenthaler Linotype](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904160201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Megenthaler%20Linotype) under the direction of [C. H. Griffith](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904160201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/C.%20H.%20Griffith) starting 7/19/39 through 2/6/41. These typeface [fleurons](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904160201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/fleurons) were inspired by Dwiggins' work with stencils and stencil artwork. One can clearly see some of the same shapes and curves in the design of his marionettes.
