Small circular character used as a universal typographic element, principally in the role of a [period](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019005803/http:/typophile.com/wiki/period), but also as part of an [i](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019005803/http:/typophile.com/wiki/i) or [j](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019005803/http:/typophile.com/wiki/j). [commas](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019005803/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Comma) are often extensions of the period dot.
