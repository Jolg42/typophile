It has 3 inherent problems that militate against legibility. 1, It has no serifs, therefore nothing to 'sacrifice' against the erosive effect of high contrast (as in black type against a white page). Hold your index finger up against a strong light source and you will see that the light erodes the outline of the finger, 2, Its lines have no 'finish,' no fixed terminal point, so that they bleed off into nothingness on the page while serifs provide a definite ending to each stroke, so that each letter is 'closed' and self-contained. 3, There is little or no variation in line weight (thickness), therefore none of the 'rhythm' that seems to somehow naturally lead from one letter to the next. All of these failings make sans serif less comfortable to read than Roman.
