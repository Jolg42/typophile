From schoolchildren to senators, every refined 19th-century American learned Spencerian penmanship. **[Burgues Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20081208191414/http:/www.sudtipos.com/fonts/97)** is based on the pinnacle of this style, the ornate lettering of calligraphy teacher Louis Madarasz (1859–1910). More than 500 characters, including swashes, titling, and alternates. A Veer Exclusive by Alejandro Paul
