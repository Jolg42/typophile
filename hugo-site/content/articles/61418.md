The only advantages that Arial acually might have over Helvetica – a huge character set and good screen rendering – is probably irrelevant for your project. More information in [this thread](http://web.archive.org/web/20100201055847/http:/typophile.com/node/42524).  Courier might be the hardest nut. The digital versions of both [Courier](http://web.archive.org/web/20100201055847/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Courier) and [Courier New](http://web.archive.org/web/20100201055847/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Courier%2520New) don’t perform very well in print (nor on screen), to say the least.
