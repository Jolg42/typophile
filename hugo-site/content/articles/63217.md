One Typophile, on seeing a preview of Greater Albion's Cherritt described it as a “Victorian Courier” equivalent. We rather like that idea, courier being an eminently practical family of faces, but Cherritt can do so much more. We've just posted a .pdf sampler with specimens of all twenty faces in the soon to be released Cherritt family on our [blog](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014211234/http:/gatf.wordpress.com/).
