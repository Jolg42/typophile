The stroke of [capital](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018043201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/capital) [R](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018043201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/R) projecting downwards from the [bowl](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018043201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/bowl). Also found on capital K, lower case k. Legs vary in form, from straight lines to [spine](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018043201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/spine)-like structures with curled tips.
