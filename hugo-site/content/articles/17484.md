Anyone can tell me _why_ the diaeresis/tréma/umlaut in the lower-case **ï** most of the time sits lower than the dot in the **i**? This puzzles many designers of Cyrillic typefaces. The thing is that in Ukrainian the combination of the **і** and the **ї** is quite common (e.g., _історії_, 'of the history'). The doubling of the **ї** is not unusual either (e.g., _її_, 'her/hers'). Any help would be much appreciated.
