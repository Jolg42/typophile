 [Simple](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317213318/http:/vimeo.com/9682948) is a video created by students in [Advanced Media](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317213318/http:/tannerwoodford.com/syllabus/2010s), a course in the [School of Design Innovation](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317213318/http:/design.asu.edu/sdi/) at [Arizona State University](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317213318/http:/www.asu.edu/). For more context on this work-in-progress, [see other videos from this class](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317213318/http:/typophile.com/node/68269) or [read the official project description on the course syllabus](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317213318/http:/tannerwoodford.com/syllabus/2010s/1.html).
