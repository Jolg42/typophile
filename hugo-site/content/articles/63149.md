[CAPTCHA](http://web.archive.org/web/20130820033339/http:/www.captcha.net/) and the [word superiority effect](http://web.archive.org/web/20130820033339/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Word_superiority_effect) (WSE).

I have noticed facebook and other sites are starting to use real words more often (as opposed to [pseudowords](http://web.archive.org/web/20130820033339/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoword) &c.) thus making them significantly easier to recognize. Does anyone know if this is on purpose?
