I shall arrogantly tell you how my friends found out about my acceptance to college.

I posted this as my Facebook status:

<cite>Thomas Levine is appalled at Cornell University’s use of Arial in the “Congratulations” banner.</cite>

Then I posted [this image](http://web.archive.org/web/20080706171526/https:/selfservice.admissions.cornell.edu/images/Accepts.jpg) with the following comment.

<cite>I think they normally use Frutiger everywhere else. Why did they have to use Arial here? If they have Frutiger, they must also have Helvetica.</cite>

That really was the first thing I noticed after I realized that I had been accepted.
