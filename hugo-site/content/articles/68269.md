Hello! For my first post, I'd like to introduce you to the work of a group of designers I teach.

The first project this semester in [Advanced Media](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422210723/http:/tannerwoodford.com/syllabus/2010s), a course in the [School of Design Innovation](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422210723/http:/design.asu.edu/sdi/) at [Arizona State University](http://web.archive.org/web/20150422210723/http:/www.asu.edu/), was to create a 30-60 second typographic film, with copyright-free audio, based on a quote received on the first day of class. The first half of the four-week project was spent collaborating with a group of 7-8 peers to make and refine typography-inspired concepts. After this process, the students chose the best of three concepts, then spent two weeks executing it as a group. A well-rounded concept was emphasized.
