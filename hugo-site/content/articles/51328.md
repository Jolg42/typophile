**About the font:** I suggested to Haley she could make a more commercial and appealing version of my [Jellybrush font](http://web.archive.org/web/20150223135958/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/sentinel/jellybrush/). Haley loved the idea because that one is right up her street. Nobody does cute better than Miss Fiege can. She did a great job on it, then passed it back to me for refinement and product finishing.

So for the curious, all glyphs in the lower case of Soft Serve are Haley's designs except the new **x** by yours truly, and most of the cappies are Haley-tweaked versions of Jellybrush capitals.

It was the kind of beautiful, smooth-running collaboration you dream about.
