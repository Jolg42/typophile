Designed by [William Addison Dwiggins](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904153932/http:/typophile.com/wiki/William%20Addison%20Dwiggins) for [Mergenthaler Linotype](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904153932/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Mergenthaler%20Linotype) under the direction of [C. H. Griffith](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904153932/http:/typophile.com/wiki/C.%20H.%20Griffith) starting 12/1/30 through 5/25/39. Known as Experimental 55 within the drawing offices at [Linotype](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904153932/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Linotype).
