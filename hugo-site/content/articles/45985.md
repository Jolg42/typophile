However, if your family had the typographic styles Light, Regular, Medium, Bold, Black, then you might consider making the Black font act as the style-linked bold of the Medium font, and leave the Light without a style-linked bold, or you could make the Medium act as the style-linked bold of the Light font, and leave the Black on its own, or you could make the Light, Medium and Black three independent fonts without any style linking.
