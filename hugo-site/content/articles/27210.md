 WORKSHOP 04. \>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>\>   
   
Arabic Calligraphy by Ali Assi, calligrapher and Dr. Abdallah Kahil, lecturer   
   
This 2-day workshop consists of four sessions in two days. The demonstrations and supervision of participants exercises will be given and conducted by the eminent Lebanese calligrapher Ali Assi.   
   
Day One Morning Session   
   
- An illustrated lecture about the history and various functions of Arabic calligraphy - A demonstration about the tools and materials of the calligrapher - Presentation of the Arabic Alphabet, its formal categories and subdivisions, such as vertical and horizontal letters, and connectable and uncontestable letters   
   
Afternoon Session -Rules of the naskh script, and its writing methods Visual presentation and participants exercises   
   
Day Two Morning session - Writing various Arabic scripts, including thuluth, diwani, ruqa`, kufic etc - Continuation of participants exercises in writing individual and connected letters, forming words   
   
Afternoon session - Function of calligraphy as an independent art, and as an element in graphic design. - Demonstration and exercises   
   
20 students \*students level: preferably Arabic speaking   
   
date April 14-15, 2005 time April 14, 2005 9:00 - 12:00 and 2:00 - 6:00 April 15, 2005 9:00 - 12:00 and 2:00 - 6:00 place TBA
