A Japanese designer has released a font, called ‘ [Readymade](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110509/http:/www.flopdesign.com/font3/readymade.html)’.  
It is similar to [Giorgio](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110509/http:/www.christianschwartz.com/giorgio.shtml) by [Christian Schwartz](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110509/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Christian%20Schwartz).

 ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110509im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Readymade_4110.jpg)
  
Of course, the x-height of Readymade is shorter than the one of Giorgio, and the characters are not the very same as characters of Giorgio. But Readymade seems to be inspired by Giorgio and Corvinus.

The designer is selling Readymade for commercial use, but it is downloadable freely only in this November for personal use.
