[MADType](http://web.archive.org/web/20150204235942/http:/www.madtype.com/) is [Matthew Aaron Desmond](http://web.archive.org/web/20150204235942/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Matthew%20Desmond) Typeface Design. Matt specializes in designing unique original retail fonts for both text and display. MAD also provides custom typeface design services and logo design. Located in Minneapolis, MN.
