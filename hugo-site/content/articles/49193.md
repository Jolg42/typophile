A german-speaking conference on “Typography and Literature” will be held in Berlin from Sept 25-27. The scope is mostly scholarly, but there is one typographer/designer mentioned in the programme: Friedrich Forssmann. Typophiles from the area might still be interested in the conference. For more see [the conference website](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106210400/http:/www.sfb626.de/veranstaltungen/aktuelle_veranstaltungen/tagung_typographie_und_literatur.html) (in German only).
