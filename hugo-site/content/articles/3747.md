I'm not so sure I agree with Baskerville. The 'e' has a slightly slanted bar, and the 'f' has a shallower 'crook' than any Baskerville I am seeing. The ampersand could have come from a collection of ampersands like [these two fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518124335/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/castletype/ampersands/) from Castle Type.
