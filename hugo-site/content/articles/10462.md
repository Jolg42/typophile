I'm the art director for a small theater company whose unofficial motto is "theater for people who hate theater; theater for people who love theater."   
   
Our audience, for the most part, ranges from 18-35, not your typical theatergoing crowd. Here is the logo, plus some recent poster images, to give you an idea of the kind of work we do:   
   
 ![impact](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019065435im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/30/7036.gif)   
   
 ![scab](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019065435im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/30/7037.jpg)   
   
 ![henry iv](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019065435im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/30/7038.jpg)   
   
 ![mouse](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019065435im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/30/7039.jpg)   
   
Now, the request: I want to select a typeface (with both display and text weights) that reflects the company's attitude. Show logos will always vary, but I want to keep the information font the same from poster to poster (other design elements will also help create a consistent look among posters).   
   
In addition, I want the typeface to be appropriate for text usage, such as for the program. Two faces I've considered are [HTF Gotham](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019065435/http:/www.typography.com/catalog/gotham/index.html) and [PsyOps Faceplate Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019065435/http:/www.psyops.com/html/spec_faceplate.html), but neither seems to really fit the bill.   
   
Any suggestions will be quite helpful.
