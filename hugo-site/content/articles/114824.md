The style known as _Scotch Roman_ is considered as a modern face.  
But if you look closely at the design of a _Scotch Roman_, it appears it has the bracketed serifs of the transitional faces.  
Modern faces in the Vox-AtypI definition are called _Didones_ meaning Didot and Bodoni. These two types have hairline serifs.  
Punchcutter Richard Austin who previously worked for John Bell and gave him transitional types was the creator of _Scotch Roman_.  
I observed some specimen of _Scotch Roman_ that have the high contrast of the _Didones_.

What is your view on _Scotch Roman_ inside the Vox-AtypI category system?
