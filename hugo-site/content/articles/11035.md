A glyph is a particular representation of a character, multiple characters (ligature), or even part of a character. A font is a collection of glyphs, some or all of which may be directly mapped to characters. It is possible for a font to include 'unencoded glyphs', _i.e._ glyphs that are not directly mapped to characters, but only indirectly mapped via glyph lookups. So, for example, a regular uppercase A, a smallcap A, and a swash A are all glyph representations of the character 'Latin uppercase A', but only one of them will be directly mapped to the character codepoint U+0041.
