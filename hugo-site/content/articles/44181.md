If you dig calligraphy, head to the history museum at Chapultepec. There see a wonderful 18th-c calligraphic painting of a gentleman on horseback. I saw it 25 years ago, and last month it was just as wonderful as the first time. When at Chapultepec, also see the Juan O’Gorman murals of Mexican history. Then go to the Zocalo and get a guided tour of the Diego Rivera murals in the Palacio Nacional. Same story, same characters, wonderfully different painting styles.
