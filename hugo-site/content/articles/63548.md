Like Greater Albion, kernest.com has an ambition to restore visual character and astetic design principles to the world. Kernest.com offers a range of fonts for live on-line use on your web pages, offering the chance to go beyond ubiqitous standbies such as Arial, Times and Verdana.

Greater Albion has taken the step into Web Typography through a partnership with kernest.com. We have made a range of our typefaces available for live web use through kernest’s service at an annual subscription of $9. You can view our page on Kernest [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20131016211519/http:/greater-albion-typefounders.kernest.com/fonts).

Intiall we've made twelve faces available. More will follow later:

- Bamberforth
