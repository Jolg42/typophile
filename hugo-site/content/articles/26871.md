Recently the latest issue of Pts. magazine was presented.   
   
Pts. is an infrequently published magazine on type design, typography and other areas of interest.   
   
5 Pts. is the do-it-yourself issue, and approaches type design and typography from an unexpected, maybe questionable but feasible perspective. This new issue contains contributions of Elliott Earls, Chris Vermaas & Cyrus Highsmith, Ties Alfrink & Marc de Bruijn, Hugo de Pagter, Stoffel den Drijver, Jan Willem Stas & Rick Vermeulen, Paul Mijksenaar, Zack Ostrowski, Jocelyn Fuller, Kristen Borchardt, Christine Niemeier, Doug Kisor, Evert Bloemsma & Jan Middendorp, Anka Kresse, Pekka Lähde and type-basics by Underware.   
   
5 Pts. is world-wide available in specialized bookshops. Pts. is published by Underware, Den Haag, the Netherlands.
