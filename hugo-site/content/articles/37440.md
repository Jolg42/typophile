Visually harmonizing Hebrew and roman is difficult, because Hebrew has thick horizontals and thin verticals, and roman is the other way around.  The most usual way to harmonize is by putting them in mirrored columns, so to speak.  One way is to use 'sans' style in both scripts, as these have less contrast.
