In 1976, [Victor Caruso](http://web.archive.org/web/20121117122856/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Victor%20Caruso) revived [Rudolf Koch](http://web.archive.org/web/20121117122856/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Rudolf%20Koch)'s 1927 [Kabel](http://web.archive.org/web/20121117122856/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Kabel) for [ITC](http://web.archive.org/web/20121117122856/http:/typophile.com/wiki/ITC). The design was a significant departure from the original, with a massive increase in x-height. See the Typowiki [Kabel](http://web.archive.org/web/20121117122856/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Kabel) entry for more information.
