I'm curious to find out what other typophiles think of [Bembo Schoolbook](http://web.archive.org/web/20131106065656/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/bembo-mt/schlbk/) with it's altered a, g & y. I know that some people believe that these letterforms are easier for children to read. I am not among that number. But is there any good reason to make these alternates available? are there better-looking versions that what we see in Bembo Schoolbook?
