It's very [Melior](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516143613/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/melior/)-like, but it's not Melior. [Renault](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516143613/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/renault/) is also similar. I suspect they were custom designed for the ratings board when they were introduced in the late 1960s. [This poster](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516143613/http:/www.filmratings.com/images/poster5.jpg), which shows all of them together, seems to support this. The ratings that were added later--NC-17 and the 13 in PG-13--don't match the style. In the early years of the system, there was also X, and, for a short time, M and GP.
