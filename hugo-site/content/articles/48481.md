I'm an intern for [WilDCOAST](http://web.archive.org/web/20150425045337/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WiLDCOAST) and one of our areas of focus is promoting ecotourism in Latin America. As you would expect many of these companies don't have the money or knowhow to create a snazzy logo (or website) so we're designing it for them.

Right now I'm working on [Baja by Kayak](http://web.archive.org/web/20150425045337/http:/bajabykayak.com/)
