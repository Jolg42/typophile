Hi,

I my tests I've found that if you try to generate a non 'style-linked' family and therefore you do not check 'Font is Bold' checkboxs, then in Corel 12 (running in a PC) no Bold, SemiBold or Black version will show up in the font menu.

The only way to see them in Corel's menu is by marking them as 'bold' in the checkbox (which is indeed not recommended by Karsten Lücke in his paper ( [http://www.kltf.de/downloads/FontNaming-kltf.pdf](http://web.archive.org/web/20100203123456/http:/www.kltf.de/downloads/FontNaming-kltf.pdf "http://www.kltf.de/downloads/FontNaming-kltf.pdf"))

Would I do any harm if I use the checkbox 'Font is Bold' while making a non 'style-linked' family?

Any advice?

Ramiro E.
