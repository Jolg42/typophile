Somehow I have a feeling that of the European scripts, the Latin and Armenian scripts can't be the only one with a typographical history of [ligatures](http://web.archive.org/web/20141031100941/http:/typophile.com/wiki/ligature). Are there any common ligatures in Cyrillic/Georgian/Greek scripts? Or any common ligatures in Latin/Armenian other than the standard ones defined in the [Unicode](http://web.archive.org/web/20141031100941/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Unicode) spec (in addition to other semi-common ligatures like fj, ct, ck, it, ip)?
