As Dr Karow and his colleagues at URW recall\\\\* most of the fonts that were made in this way later underwent a lot of manual cleanup, or were redigitised manually, but in the hurry to make fonts available in new formats some customers shipped autotraced outlines with various kinds of problems. I know nothing about engraving machine fonts, but I am curious to know whether the URW fonts supplied with Coreldraw X4 are technically faulty. Whosoever is familiar with engraving machine fonts may download the PDF file

[http://www.sanskritweb.net/forgers/corel14.pdf](http://web.archive.org/web/20100103105457/http:/www.sanskritweb.net/forgers/corel14.pdf "http://www.sanskritweb.net/forgers/corel14.pdf")

and may zoom to 1000 percent and more a few typical letters which in my opinion are technically defective, e.g. on page 4 of font “G4L S14 Fette Din Mittel” these letters:

6 (left downstroke at bowl intersection)  
9 (right downstroke at bowl intersection)  
^ (top roof)  
a (downstroke at bowl intersection)  
b, d, g, h, m etc. (downstrokes at bowl intersections)

etc. etc. etc.
