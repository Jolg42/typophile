But you can also create a new frame on any position you want by click-and-dragging the outline of the frame. If you release the mouse button and there is still more text to be placed, you will see a red \\\"out box\\\" at the bottom right of the last frame. Click on that to re-load the cursor and drag a new frame, where the rest of the text will flow into.  On the PC, you can hold Alt down to keep the cursor loaded with text after drawing a frame -- on your Mac it's probably the Option key. The cursor will change to reflect the new state.\"}]"
