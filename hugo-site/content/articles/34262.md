The TypeCon2007 conference hotel and headquarters is the Crowne Plaza in downtown Seattle. The center of activity for TypeCon2007, the Crowne Plaza is a sweet hotel in a fantastic location. The Crowne Plaza offers wireless or wired high-speed internet access, a spacious but intimate lounge area and restaurant, our own gorgeous outdoor patio, and many other amenities.
