Australian radio [confuses <cite>I</cite> and <cite>l</cite>](http://web.archive.org/web/20091023130036/http:/www.abc.net.au/mediawatch/transcripts/s2711819.htm "‘MediaWatch’ segment") – [something Jesse Helms used to do](http://web.archive.org/web/20091023130036/http:/www.slate.com/toolbar.aspx?action=print&id=2194921 "Coverage by – wait for it – Christopher Hitchens").

“The problem lies in ABC News’s adoption of the Arial font for its news scripts [a bad idea right there]. Arial makes no distinction between a capital <cite>I</cite> and a small <cite>l</cite>. So Kim Jong-Il does look exactly like Kim Jong the Second.”
