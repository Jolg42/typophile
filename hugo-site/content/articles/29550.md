The font used on Air Force One is usually said to be Palatino (for example, [http://typophile.com/node/19363](http://web.archive.org/web/20111228174021/http:/typophile.com/node/19363 "http://typophile.com/node/19363")), but it doesn't really look like Palatino to me. For instance, the serifs in Palatino are flat vertical or horizontal lines, while the serifs in the AF1 font are at a 45 degree angle (except in the arms of the E and F) and look like arrowheads. Is it a custom US government version of Palatino, or is it some other font completely?
