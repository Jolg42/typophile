For instance, many people don't like [Futura](http://web.archive.org/web/20141015225122/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Futura) for body copy because of the long ascenders/low x-height (aside from the sans/serif debate). They say it breaks up the color too much. Same thing for other typefaces with low x-heights like Venetians.

But that doesn't make sense to me. Presumably, the larger x-height makes the letters appear larger. But that means that you need to increase the leading proportionately to compensate. If you used a typeface with a low x-height at a larger point size, wouldn't you basically have the same results?
