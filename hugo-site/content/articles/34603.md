**[Indices](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415053939/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Indices) : [Designers](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415053939/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Designers) : Todor Vardjiev (Todor Vardzhiev)**

Prof. Todor Vardjiev (born in 1943 at Blagoevgrad, [Bulgaria](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415053939/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Bulgaria)) is a bulgarian graphic and type designer. He was a student of [Albert Kapr](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415053939/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Albert%20Kapr) and he is teaching at National Academy of Arts, Sofia.
