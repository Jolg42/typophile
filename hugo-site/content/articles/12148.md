Canadian specialty publishers, headquartered in Vancouver. Among other areas of expertise, Hartley & Marks have published many books on typography, including [Robert Bringhurst](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Robert%20Bringhurst)'s <cite><a href="http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Elements%20of%20Typographic%20Style" class="freelinking" rel="nofollow">Elements of Typographic Style</a></cite>, [Gerrit Noordzij](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Gerrit%20Noordzij)'s <cite><a href="http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Letterletter" class="freelinking" rel="nofollow">Letterletter</a></cite>, [Stanley Morison](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Stanley%20Morison)'s <cite><a href="http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Letter%20Forms" class="freelinking" rel="nofollow">Letter Forms</a></cite> and [Robert Bringhurst](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Robert%20Bringhurst) and [Warren Chappell](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Warren%20Chappell)'s <cite><a href="http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Brief%20History%20of%20the%20Printed%20Word" class="freelinking" rel="nofollow">Brief History of the Printed Word</a></cite>. Along with [Hyphen Press](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829233955/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Hyphen%20Press), one of the most significant publishers in English of typographic materials.
