We would like to announce that [Terminal Design](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/) has launched its new website featuring a number of new font releases and updates to old favorites.

[Tangent](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/sets/?CID=10) A new geometric sans in eight weights with obliques and small caps.

[Enclave](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/sets/?CID=6) A contemporary condensed slab serif also in eight weights with small caps and lovely italics.

[Now Playing](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/sets/?CID=9) A marquee font designed for use on the new plasma screen marquee for the reopening of the Apollo Theater in Harlem.

[ClearviewADA](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/sets/?CID=4) A family of Clearview fonts that conform to the letterform specifications for signage outlined in the Americans with Disabilities Act legislation

[ClearviewText](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/sets/?CID=5) Is the OpenType update of our popular ClearviewOne family.

[Giacomo 2.0](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/sets/?CID=7) Is updated to include small caps and Cyrillic.

[Rawlinson 2.0](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014105459/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/sets/?CID=8) Also updated to include small caps.
