Greetings Bay Area type friends.

It's been a while since this group gathered in earnest. Let's fix it. No time better than Saturday, May 20. Why? Because type designer [Alejandro Paul](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181000/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Alejandro%20Paul) will grace our city this weekend - all the way from Argentina. Let's get together and show him a good time north of the border(s).

For those unfamiliar with Alejandro's work, have a look at [Sudtipos](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181000/http:/sudtipos.com.ar/fonts2.htm) and [Veer](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317181000/http:/www.veer.com/partner/link.aspx?i=1048&c=4&p=search/results.aspx?keyword=alejandro+paul). Ale is a new master of the script font, with hundreds of releases over the last few years, and a lovely jovial fellow to boot.
