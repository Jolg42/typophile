Greater Albion Typefounders has released another of it's 'Evolutionary' faces at MyFonts.com. [Coronard](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014232204/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/coronard/) uses calligraphic detail toelaborate an underlying Roman serif form, and is a great way to combine ancient looks wih modern legibility.
