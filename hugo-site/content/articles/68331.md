Readers may enjoy my article “ [Web Standards for E-Books](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019211713/http:/www.alistapart.com/articles/ebookstandards/ "Web Standards for E-Books")” at A List Apart, particularly the sections on exactly which typographic features E-book files should and must not contain. (Of the latter: Ligatures, hung punct, hyphens.) I’m sure that Bringhurst apologists, who are a lot like Ayn Rand apologists, will fulminate with rage at what I have to say about the em dash.
