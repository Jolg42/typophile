Anyone inspired by Typecon2007 to start a new typeface? There was so much on display to take in and enjoy.

Perhaps after seeing the Veer presentation, you are inspired to do a massive display script project ALA [Alejandro Paul](http://web.archive.org/web/20081007160643/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Alejandro%252BPaul)? Or was it the next gen text faces coming out of Mexico that really rocked your boat?

I do know one thing, for some reason I don’t think I’m too interested in doing a “grunge” typeface anytime soon...

After speaking with [Eben Sorkin](http://web.archive.org/web/20081007160643/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Eben%252BSorkin), [Nick Shinn](http://web.archive.org/web/20081007160643/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Nick%252BShinn), [Kent Lew](http://web.archive.org/web/20081007160643/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Kent%252BLew) and others, I finally decided to sit down last night to start fleshing out the drawings I’ve had about an upright semi-italic text face I’ve been doodling about for the past two years.

What’s your inspiration and/or new project?
