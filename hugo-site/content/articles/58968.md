Greater Albion has just released our latest new family of typefaces, Keynsia.

The Keynsia family revives the spirit of the 1950s-in fact it’s our first venture into the design ethos of that decade. Its simple and elegant lines make for an eye-catching set of display faces.

A range of different faces make up the family, all having an extensive character set. Keynsia is available on [Myfonts.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014060310/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/keynsia/overview.html) and we've put some examples of it in use on our [blog](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014060310/http:/gatf.wordpress.com/).
