So far, ( [http://www.cjstones.com/prod\_images/lettering/HebrewScript.jpg](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317102254/http:/www.cjstones.com/prod_images/lettering/HebrewScript.jpg "http://www.cjstones.com/prod\_images/lettering/HebrewScript.jpg")) is the closest thing I have found.
