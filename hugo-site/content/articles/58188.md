Now, I'm normally rather against typefaces that try to emulate the writing style of other languages or seek to imbue a "culture" into a font (for example: [Chinese & Japanese Style](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022104554/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=201 "Chinese & Japanese Style Type")), but the work done on Abu Dhabi's official font is rather impressive:

[Abu Dhabi's New Font](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022104554/http:/brand.abudhabi.ae/Sites/OBAD/Navigation/EN/SupportingElements/Typography/primary-font,did=88374.html)

Most impressively, it manages to avoid the cheesy look [most Arabic-styled fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022104554/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=202&page=1 "Other Arabic fonts") managed to trudge into and fits the brand rather well. I like the loops on the 'g' and 'y,' though I have to wonder what would happen if they tried to write any word that had a 'gy' together, like 'clingy.'

What do you all think?
