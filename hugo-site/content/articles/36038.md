[T-26](http://web.archive.org/web/20140809065939/http:/www.t26.com/fonts) is sort of a hybrid of a digital type foundry and font distributor started by [Carlos Segura](http://web.archive.org/web/20140809065939/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Carlos%20Segura) in 1994. T-26 publishes its own exclusives designs, but they also distribute fonts from many well know independent type foundries.
