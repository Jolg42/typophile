Spike is showing Dr. No tonight. I stopped everything just to watch the titles. Why oh why can’t we have more inspiration like that today? The Dr. No titles were designed by [Maurice Binder](http://web.archive.org/web/20060504205127/http:/www.artofjamesbond.com/binder.htm) and they make me smile. Down with shadows and blends and all that is 3D … go back to analog and 2D design!
