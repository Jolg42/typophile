I think it looks like a faux stencil font made by overlaying a white strip over parts of the letters. The M is what looks wrong to me as a stencil pattern. Except for that M I would say FF DIN could have been used. Maybe Linotype's [Rundfunk Grotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518124147/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/rundfunk-grotesk/rundfunk-grotesk/testdrive.html?s=RAMPAGE&p=48) would fit it.
