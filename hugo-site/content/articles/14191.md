15th century [punchcutter](http://web.archive.org/web/20141002112827/http:/typophile.com/wiki/punchcutter) who worked under [Aldus Manutius](http://web.archive.org/web/20141002112827/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Aldus%20Manutius) at the [Aldine Press](http://web.archive.org/web/20141002112827/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Aldine%20Press) in Venice. [Bembo](http://web.archive.org/web/20141002112827/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Bembo) is an interpretation of the alphabet he cut for an edition of Pietro Bembo's "De Aetna."
