[Fontever](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317123759/http:/www.fontever.com/) is the world's first private font marketplace launched in 2014. Fontever sell limited and rare font submited by typographers. It is also the first free typeface selling site that do not receive commission and fee from typographers.
