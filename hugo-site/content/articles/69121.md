The “ [On Language](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011202342/http:/topics.nytimes.com/topics/features/magazine/columns/on_language/ "Column archives")” column of the <cite>New York Times</cite> has used custom-illustrated logotypes each week for years, which the Society of Publication Designers blog helpfully collected in two posts ( [first](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011202342/http:/www.spd.org/2010/03/post-3.php "Celebrating ‘On Language’"), [second](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011202342/http:/www.spd.org/2010/04/celebrating-on-languagepart-2.php "Celebrating ‘On Language,’ Part 2")). Many Usual Suspects are represented among the letterers.
