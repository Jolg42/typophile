Designed by [William Addison Dwiggins](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/William%20Addison%20Dwiggins) for [Mergenthaler Linotype](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Mergenthaler%20Linotype) under the direction of [C.H. Griffith](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/C.H.%20Griffith) starting 2/3/42 through 7/2/45. Also known as Experimental 269 in the drawing offices at [Linotype](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Linotype). Re-drawn by [David Berlow](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/David%20Berlow), [Tobias Frere-Jones](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Tobias%20Frere-Jones), and [Thomas Rickner](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Thomas%20Rickner) at [The Font Bureau, Inc](http://web.archive.org/web/20140904163241/http:/typophile.com/wiki/The%20Font%20Bureau%2C%20Inc) for Premiere magazine in 1994.
