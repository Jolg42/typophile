Here is an interesting find.  
I was leafing through an old Russian font book from 1930, and came across a peculiar attempt at merging [cyrillic](http://web.archive.org/web/20140803141546/http:/typophile.com/wiki/cyrillic) with a [blackletter](http://web.archive.org/web/20140803141546/http:/typophile.com/wiki/blackletter) aesthetic. I don't think the idea ever really caught on, as I can't remember any examples prior to the 90's (and no successful ones since)

 ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140803141546im_/http:/typophile.com/files/cyrgoth_5450.png)

Still, I wonder if this might be useful to someone attempting one.
