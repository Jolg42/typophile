A couple of years ago, some interesting found lettering in a headline was posted [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416055311/http:/typophile.com/cms/node/31959) in the type identification forum. The general consensus reached was that the lettering was hand lettered, though a couple of faces (including one of mine) were suggested as the basis of approximating the 'look and feel' of the lettering.

This is the specimen that was posted in 2007:

 ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416055311im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Svengalis_3729_3937.jpg)

Here is my progress to date at trying to recreate it as a font. So far I've worked on the lower case, or 'small capitals':

 ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416055311im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Svengali_5482.png)

I'd be pleased to hear suggestions and feedback on this project so far,

Paul.
