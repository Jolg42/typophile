Dear Typophile community.

This logo is for a small [architecture firm](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416044135/http:/omkrets.se/) in Stockholm:

 ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416044135im_/http:/typophile.com/files/omkrets-20dpc-black_4719.png)
  
 [(high resolution here)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416044135/http:/omkrets.se/common/omkrets-logo2-hires.png)

It is based on the typeface [Orgasmia Outline](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416044135/http:/t26.com/fonts/Orgasmia) by Brito Renato (t26.com) but I have altered the "o" and the "t" to make every letter consist of (only) one self-intersecting line.

I like how the letters are both pixel-based and drawn with a line.  
The name " o m k r e t s " means "circumference" or "orbit" (sort of) in Swedish.

Please tell me what you think!

Thanks!  
Mårten
