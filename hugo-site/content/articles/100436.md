I am happy to finally announce the release of " **Lestatic**", my largest typeface project to date, and one with a slightly more serious take than usual from !Exclamachine. It is available exclusively through [myfonts.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415055336/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/Lestatic/fonts/). The collection of 9 families and 27 styles includes a free mashup font to give a taste of the grungier sides of Lestatic. Also, for a limited time, Lestatic Lashed, a more stroked variant, is 75% off!
