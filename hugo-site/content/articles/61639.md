Greater Albion Typefounders have just posted the first preview of their next release, Brosse. Brosse is intended to be a lively slabserif face that emphasises simple geometric curves. The sampler can be viewed [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416090455/http:/gatf.wordpress.com/files/2009/09/brosse.pdf). All constructive feedback greatfully received.
