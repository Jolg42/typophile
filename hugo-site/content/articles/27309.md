In the interests of fairness, isn't it about time [Impact](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174658/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Impact) got the beats?  
After all, other types made ubiquitous by [bundling](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174658/http:/typophile.com/wiki/bundling), such as [Comic Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174658/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Comic%20Sans), [Papyrus](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174658/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Papyrus), [Trajan](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174658/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Trajan) and [Arialvetica](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174658/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Arialvetica) have been roundly dissed for overexposure.  
Only trouble is, it could prove difficult to find an alternative with suitable, er, impact, especially considering Impact's barely-protruding [descenders](http://web.archive.org/web/20150123174658/http:/typophile.com/wiki/descender).  
Suggestions, anyone?
