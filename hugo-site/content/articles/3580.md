The whole thing appears to be a custom job. The A and R are typical modifications, but rare (non-existent, as far as I know) in fonts with these proportions. There are extended sans serif fonts you could start from such as [ITC Blair](http://web.archive.org/web/20050518124129/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/blair-itc/medium/testdrive.html?s=flatron%a0lcd&p=48).
