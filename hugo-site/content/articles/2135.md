The    Germans were pretty much ahead by that time, probably because the fascists    were already thinking of streamlining everything for mass-production of    anything needed for the war that was planned. The idea was that most sign    makers would construct the characters by ruler and compass, and scale the    measurements from a grid. This was thought to be easy.
