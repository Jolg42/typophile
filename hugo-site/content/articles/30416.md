So, the example says: The outlines of glyph 'Adieresis' are moved to the right at 5 units, and then the advance width is increased by ten.   This looks a bit complicated at first, but has to do with the fact that a font does not know about a rightside bearing; rather, the rightside bearing is advance width -- the glyph's total width including leftside and rightside bearings -- minus leftside bearing.   What the example does is: it increases both leftside and rightside bearing at 5 units each.  The second and forth number remain zero because you do not want to move the glyph up or down.
