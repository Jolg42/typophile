Type designer Xavier Dupré, whose new FF Yoga was recently the featured face on Typophile, has a new website, [http://www.xavierdupre.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019132249/http:/www.xavierdupre.com/).

There are some examples of early versions of his designs in a section called [Design Steps](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019132249/http:/www.xavierdupre.com/html/design.php?p_lang=en), which offer some glipses into his creative process.

Even more fascinating, for me, are the samples of two of his typefaces for the Khmer script used in Cambodia, [SIPAR Apsara](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019132249/http:/www.xavierdupre.com/html/sipar_apsara.php?p_lang=en) and [CKS Chrieng](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019132249/http:/www.xavierdupre.com/html/cks.php?p_lang=en). I don't read Khmer and can't judge them as a native reader would, but they appear beautiful. I knew Xavier had designed some non-Latin typefaces while living in Southeast Asia, but I had not seen any samples until now.
