The feature will initially be called \\\"xxxx\\\".        In the right pane of the panel, you'll see the initial template of the feature code appear. Change the two occurrences of \\\"xxxx\\\" in the right pane \\\" to the feature you want. Let's say lowercase to small caps ('smcp').
