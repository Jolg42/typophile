With the overuse of [Trajan](http://web.archive.org/web/20080403235831/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=ADT0001724 "See Trajan Pro on Veer.com") in movie titles, I found this use especially ironic given the name of the movie: _[Requiem](http://web.archive.org/web/20080403235831/http:/www.requiemthemovie.com/ "View the movie site")_. I guess they tried to set the title apart by customizing the R and the E, although I think they butchered the latter.
