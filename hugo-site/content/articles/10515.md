I don't know who letters Sin City, perhaps one of comic book aficiandos will chime in, but Dark Horse comics has used computer fonts before from [Comicraft](http://web.archive.org/web/20050528000747/http:/www.comicbookfonts.com/). Even if Frank Miller does the lettering himself, I'm sure you can find something very similar over at Comicraft in their lettering section. For less expensive comic book fonts, I'd suggest [Blambot](http://web.archive.org/web/20050528000747/http:/www.blambot.com/). I hope this helps.
