I'm trying to draw a 'neo-grotesque' typeface. I have drawn the letters (I've already posted some images on my [flickr gallery](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233951/http:/www.flickr.com/photos/danielecapo/4311310654/)), no numbers, no punctuation, etc.  
The first version of G and R (in brown) was too Univers-esque so I decided for a different shape.  
Basically I'm trying to draw this font for my use to replace Helvetica (in posters and short texts).
