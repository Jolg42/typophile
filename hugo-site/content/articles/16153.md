Hi

I am graduate student in Communications Design Program at Pratt Institute, NYC.  
As a part of my studies I have started the research process for my thesis, and clearly convinced that I wanted this to deal with Type I have come to a point were OpenType has crossed by.

For this matter I’ve tried to read as much information on the web and in all the blogs about this subject, (typophile, typographica, typotheque and typblography) to get to a point where I would like to ask for opinions and advice from all of you (typographers, type designers and graphic designers) about your opinions and expectations regarding OpenType.

Some generic questions that come to my mind are:  
- Is OpenType going to become the standard format in the near future putting apart TrueType and PostScript fonts?  
- Will OpenType allow for better typographical usage from general users?  
- Will the internet benefit from the use of OpenType?  
- Will most common applications become OpenType savvy?

A more precise question that I have is about the possibilities that OpenType encoding can allow.  
Taking Amy Papaelias great thesis project as starting point, ( [http://www.typetalkfonts.com/typetalk\_demo.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20071221093056/http:/www.typetalkfonts.com/typetalk_demo.html "http://www.typetalkfonts.com/typetalk\_demo.html") & [http://typographi.com/001007.php](http://web.archive.org/web/20071221093056/http:/typographi.com/001007.php "http://typographi.com/001007.php") ) I have been wondering how far can we take the encoding of OpenType fonts. Any thoughts?

I’d kindly appreciate anyone’s opinions, comments or suggestions for future readings, directing me to other posting or to any other related materials.

Thanks.

Giovanni Jubert
