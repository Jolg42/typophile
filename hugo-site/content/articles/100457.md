Which font is this Italic:

[http://www.sanskritweb.net/fontdocs/zigabonus-italic.jpg](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015063948/http:/www.sanskritweb.net/fontdocs/zigabonus-italic.jpg "http://www.sanskritweb.net/fontdocs/zigabonus-italic.jpg")

Which font is this Roman:

[http://www.sanskritweb.net/fontdocs/zigabonus-roman.jpg](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015063948/http:/www.sanskritweb.net/fontdocs/zigabonus-roman.jpg "http://www.sanskritweb.net/fontdocs/zigabonus-roman.jpg")

These 2 fonts were used for typesetting a commentary to the epistles.

For full bibliography see [http://library.brown.edu/find/Record/b1875141](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015063948/http:/library.brown.edu/find/Record/b1875141 "http://library.brown.edu/find/Record/b1875141")

The commentary was printed in 1544 in Lovanium (Leuven, Netherlands).

I own a copy of this 603-page huge commentary book bound in parchment.
