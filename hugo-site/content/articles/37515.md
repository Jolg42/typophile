This was from an ID question I was stumped on. The typeface is a serif that reminds me of ITC Usherwood, but is different in a number of ways. It is used for navigation and titling on the site for [15 Central Park West](http://web.archive.org/web/20071017181543/http:/www.15cpw.com/). It shows up on the covers of their Brochures, and inside some of them as well. It was from a brochure that I took the attached screenshot.
