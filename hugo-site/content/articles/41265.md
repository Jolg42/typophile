It needs to be professional enough to look like I didn’t slap Arial on there, but cute / playful enough to fit the style of my site. Check out [the site](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821152435/http:/tinycrumb.com/home) for a better idea of the style I’m going for.

I am a noob though so please be nice :)
