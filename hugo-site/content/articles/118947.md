I stumbled over a [link](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421062323/http:/collections.bm-lyon.fr/mil/search?query[]=title_s:%22Apollo%22) to some specimens of this typeface called Apollo. I like the quirks that sets it apart from similar faces, the C in particular, with the top terminal cut horizontally and the bottom terminal cut at an angle. Has this typeface ever been digitized or revived under another name? I hadn’t heard of it before.
