After reading this post explaining the concept of using a separate stylistic set for each stylistic alternate:

[http://typophile.com/crss/node/55586](http://web.archive.org/web/20150217005401/http:/typophile.com/crss/node/55586 "http://typophile.com/crss/node/55586")
