This design started out from imagining that the dotless _ı_ was the basic _i_, and the dots on _i_ and _j_ were diacritics. I was traveling in Turkey and Hungary at the time, and the other diacritics flowed naturally.

Please let me know what you think, good or bad. I'm also looking for a name, if anyone has any suggestions.
