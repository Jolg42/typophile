What started out as a set of nerdy, cryptic notes to myself is gradually evolving into a full-blown blog of its own. I called it "typography/typology", which was lame and boring and NOT AT ALL NECRO. Since I'm all about the darkness, you can now find it under its new name, [Typomancy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420132523/http:/www.typomancy.org/). I also gave it its own domain (it used to be stashed away as a mostly unpublicized subdirectory of my [personal homepage](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420132523/http:/www.pushby.com/forrest/) (despite which Stephen and Dan and Luc Devroye, among others, managed to find it).

Why have my own blog when there's already [Typographica](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420132523/http:/www.typographi.com/), [Typographer](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420132523/http:/www.typographer.org/), and this fine blog? There isn't an ironclad reason, but part of it is that I'm still going to use [Typomancy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420132523/http:/www.typomancy.org/) as a place to stash my private notes to myself. Another, better reason is that I'm more interested in the lower-level aspects of type design, specifically dealing with font formats and the politics thereof. Also, it's fun to have a place to do my own experimentation with nameplates and site designs and the like.

The three of you who bookmarked the old version of the blog will probably want to update your links; the old blog isn't being updated, even though it'll redirect you to the new one. Check it out, and thanks for reading!
