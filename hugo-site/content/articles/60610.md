Greater Albion Typefounders have just announced a surprise new release on their [blog](http://web.archive.org/web/20131016205525/http:/gatf.wordpress.com/). The surprise reldease is Cullion, which we're calling a modern unicase blackletter. If you want to see what one of those looks like you can try it out on [Myfonts.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131016205525/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/cullion/) or see it in action on our [blog](http://web.archive.org/web/20131016205525/http:/gatf.wordpress.com/).
