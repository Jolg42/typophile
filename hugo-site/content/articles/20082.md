David Pankow has been for many years the curator of the Melbert B. Cary, Jr. Graphic Arts Collection, also known as the [Cary Library](http://web.archive.org/web/20130919055228/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Cary%20Library), at the Rochester (New York) Institute of Technology. Pankow also teaches a class at RIT on "The History of the Book," and was for some years the editor of the American Printing History Association's journal, "Printing History."
