A question: I have been fiddling around with the Tyopwiki page for [Garamond](http://web.archive.org/web/20081014051136/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Garamond). One thing I tried to do was to divide “Garamonds” into “based on Claude Garamond”, “based on Jean Jannon”. On the authority of Bringhurst and Morison I had [LTC Garamont](http://web.archive.org/web/20081014051136/http:/www.p22.com/lanston/products/garamont.html) as Goudy out of Jannon, and have so classified it. But P22’s Lanston website (linked above) says it was based on Garamond’s types of 1540 ... which would make it a Claude. I don’t have a decent sample, but my inclination would be to say that the Jannon attribution looks right, as well as being historically plausible.

Have I got it wrong? Or have P22/Lanston? I don’t want to “correct” the Wiki and make it worse.
