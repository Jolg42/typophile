Examples:  In many cases Monotype might have no feet, in others it may have, depending upon the type of Monotype caster. On the other hand, foundry, which often has distinct feet, as this is actually how the jet is removed, doesn’t always have them either. European metal foundry that has been ground to Anglo-American type height is easily identifiable as the feet have been ground off and the grinding pattern is usually discernible.
