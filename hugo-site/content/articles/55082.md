Just noticed [this](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124051720/http:/www.tiro.nu/Articles/Cambria/Cambria_Math_Basic_Spec_V1.pdf) on the Tiro site. TWO weights of a new Fraktur, TWO new script weights, a double-struck weight with a black weight left over when the incisions are removed... remarkable stuff.

Oh yeah, and two weights of a Cambria Sans as well, plus their italics. I would be surprised if they stop here and don't expand it further in the future.
