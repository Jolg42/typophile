[066.FONT](http://web.archive.org/web/20150204224733/http:/www.066.pl/ "Go to the website") is [Piotr Wozniak](http://web.archive.org/web/20150204224733/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Piotr%20Wozniak)’s small studio in Poland designing alternative fonts. Its history began in 1997 after signing an agreement with [Linotype Library](http://web.archive.org/web/20150204224733/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Linotype%20Library) for his Kropki font.
