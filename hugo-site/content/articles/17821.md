Twiggy, vibrant colors and patterns, etc. Part of the packaging features Op Art patterns. I’m looking for a typeface that reinforces this theme. I’d like something mod and idealy feminine/girly and fun, energetic, that evokes the era and its spirit. Maybe a script. Any ideas? Thanks much for any help you can offer.
