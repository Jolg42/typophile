Recently, Dan Bailey of [Fontosaurus](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015040131/http:/www.fontosaurus.com/) teamed up with Michael Jantze, creator of the nationally-syndicated (U.S.) comic strip, [The Norm](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015040131/http:/www.thenorm.com/) to create a font based on Michael's comic lettering. Not unusual, in and of itself, but instead of profiting from this, we're giving 100% of the proceeds to the [Lance Armstrong Foundation](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015040131/http:/www.laf.org/). The LAF provides financial grants to research groups studying the disease and to non-profit organizations that assist people fighting the disease. If our wildest hopes came true, the proceeds from [this font](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015040131/http:/www.fontosaurus.com/fonts.php?target=136&display=single) would fund the lab that found the cure, but we'd just be happy if we could help one person fighting the disease make it through one more day.
