Just FYI, registered members of typophile are allowed to submit news stories. There is a link on [the news page](http://web.archive.org/news) in the right-hand column that allows you do this. However, once you post an item, it is stuck in moderation until approved by a moderator. So, after posting news items, please [contact a moderator](http://web.archive.org/readme#help) to approve your posting for you. Thanks for reading!
