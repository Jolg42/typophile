Let's say you are a type designer with fonts being sold at three different distributors at royalty rates of 15%, 50%, and 65%. Let's say you would like to earn $30,000 per year from font sales. A high price will bring in more money per font, but may result in lower sales. A lower price may increase sales but bring in less money. It's not easy to determine the best price for a font.
