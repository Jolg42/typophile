I followed a post on Fontfeed to this [video](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724164930/http:/vimeo.com/3830490) of a guy in Japan doing what you might call a psychogeographical font, and noticed he was using some tool I'd never encountered before... [OTEdit](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724164930/http:/musashi.or.tv/oteditmac.htm). The site, and most of the interface in the app itself is all in Japanese, but I was able to start a font and draw a glyph. It seems like a good, simple tool for somebody just starting out in type design. Does anyone know much about this app or its developer/s? An English localization would be nice.
