**From [dotsies.org](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415054905/http:/www.dotsies.org/)**  
“_Since latin letters (a, b, c, etc.) are optimized to be written by hand, they take up a lot of unnecessary space. Your eyes have to move at a frantic pace from left to right to read. The letters in Dotsies words smoosh together, so words look like shapes! Get more screen space! Save paper. Have a more comfortable reading experience_ (Muth, C. 2012).”
