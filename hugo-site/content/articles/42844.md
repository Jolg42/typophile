Hello typo nuts!

I've made this poster for a public debate society, called [Hvadharvigangi](http://web.archive.org/web/20130310025924/http:/www.hvadharvigangi.dk/) ("What are we doing?"). It's meant to hang around in universities, libraries and the like to draw attention to the monthly debates. I've tried to convey a both fun and serious mood, because that defines the society: serious topics (this particular one: "Genetics and ethics"), presented and debated in a friendly and "fun" way.

I recognize that you won't understand much of the text, because it's set in Danish. But I hope I can get some feedback on the overall layout/composition anyway. I'll be happy to explain anything in the text that might be important.

My main concern is the potential over-use of different sizes in type. It might appear a bit too messy. What do you think? The font is [Knockout](http://web.archive.org/web/20130310025924/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/font_overview.php?productLineID=100013) + a wee [Auto](http://web.archive.org/web/20130310025924/http:/www.underware.nl/site2/index.php3?id1=auto&id2=overview)

Also, the header is my additional proposal for a _new logo_. So this element is important in it self. [Their old logo](http://web.archive.org/web/20130310025924/http:/www.hvadharvigangi.dk/) appears at the top of their webpage.

I've attached 3 similar variations of the poster.

Thank you so much!

p.s. The picture is just an example of how I want the future ones to be placed and cut. This one is completely irrelevant to genetics obvisouly
