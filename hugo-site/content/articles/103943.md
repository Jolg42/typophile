[Ink] Trapping is something very particular, which I don't think applies in this case. The purpose of an ink trap is, as the term suggests, to capture ink during printing. It is a special treatment of an internal angle designed to compensate for the phenomenon of inkspread that would otherwise distort the letter shape at this point. As such, ink traps are properly designed relative to output technologies.
