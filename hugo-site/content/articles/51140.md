Microsoft Groups, [http://groups.msn.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20130319070409/http:/groups.msn.com/ "http://groups.msn.com") which has been hosting a VOLT Users Community group and another for Fontlab has announced that it is closing all groups as of February 2009. It recommends that groups move to Microsoft's Multiply.

In view of the large number of typographers who are members of these groups, and the extensive messages containing valuable advice exchanged over the years, I wonder whether Typophile could or would want to offer to host these groups, members, messages and all?

Or at least invite members to join Typophile?
