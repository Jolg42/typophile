Anonymous @ 25 Jan 2003 — 2:01am
Hey! You could poke someone's eye out with that! I see where the blackletter influences fit in. The undecorated letters remind me of Diotima, Koch Antiqua, and Nicholas Cochin. The letters are very thin, though. Have you started bumping up against the limitations of em-square granularity? Speaking of which, also be careful of sharp, acute angles in your contours. They're usually not a problem, but can sometimes cause strange rendering artifacts on certain output devices. Some of the decorated caps can be confusing, since the stem widths are so close to the hairline strokes. I'll have to look at it some more before I can offer more useful advice. But you should definitely continue with it... you've got something there.
