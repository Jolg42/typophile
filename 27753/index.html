I like it a lot. It has a classical elegance with some nice subtle contemporary (transitional?) qualities. Now for the critique bit... I think the bent stems on the d, m, n, r and u add an inappropriate quirky element that i'm not liking too much. Maybe lose the skews on both axis on the i dot, and there's a few flat spots that could do with some rounding off, like the tail of the a and g, and the top of the f. I'm not sure about the end of the arm? (sorry, not up to scratch on my type anatomy) on the r. It seems a bit vague. I'm certainly no expert on serif text fonts but maybe the bowls of the a, e and g need to be a little bigger for clarity at small print sizes. But if you change everything i've mentioned it may lose all the qualities you were aiming for. Anyway, i could see it becoming a very nice and useful font. Now show us your x ;)
