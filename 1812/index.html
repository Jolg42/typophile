It is almost certainly not a font, but hand lettered. The o and v link together as a ligature in a unique way, and the e is clearly designed to only work at the end of a word, and the L is one that could only work at the beginning of a word. It would be possible to do something like this in an OpenType font, but only a couple of OpenType script typefaces have been done that are so elaborate, and this isn't one of them. Regards, T
