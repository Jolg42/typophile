'You cannot not communicate' - Paul Watzlawik 'You are what you are seen to be' - Erik Spiekermann 'Appearances are not false' (Nur der Schein trügt nicht) - Paul Klee 'Form is the outside of content, and content is the inside of form' - Jean-Luc Godard Of course, one implication of all these quotes is that identity design won't do anything for you if you suck.
