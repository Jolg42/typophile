Perhaps the designer used Optima Bold Italic and applied a horizontal expansion of about 120%. This would produce a general appearance similar to "Sleep." He could then have done a series of edits: straightened the vertical and cross strokes, converted some corners to arcs, and sharpened the tips of the terminals. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018213015im_/http:/typophile.com/files/OptimaBoldItalExpHoriz120Percent_4186.jpg) Don
