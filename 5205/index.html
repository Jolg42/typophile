That ain't no font - it's hand painted. You could try [Interstate](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224225/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/interstate/bold/testdrive.html?s=PETERSEN&p=72) or [Din](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224225/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/ff-din/bold/testdrive.html?s=PETERSEN&p=72) to get a similar look. But you won't match this with a single font, the Rs don't match.
