I hate to be a downer, but you will need to read the EULA of any dingbat/image font very closely before you use it for t-shirt designs. Many foundaries do not allow their dingbat/image fonts to be used as a sole element in a design such a t-shirt of logo.
Anonymous @ 24.Jul.2006 11.47am
I once had a designer use a glyph, capital D, from one of my fonts, as a corporate logo. I was fine with that, but pointed out that the client wouldn’t be able to copyright or trademark their own logo. The same holds true for ornaments/dinbats, I would imagine.
