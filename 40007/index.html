The beliefs of the "shareware" boards is if no one has put a copyright or designer's name, and they have tried to locate it -- then it is up for anyone to use. Since you are a profession and want the proper permission, then maybe you can find something "legal" to substitute. There are many great handwriting fonts these days. In my searching I came across this conversation on the very same topic [http://ask.metafilter.com/52509/IS-Angelinattf-really-a-freeware-font](http://web.archive.org/web/20140718064940/http:/ask.metafilter.com/52509/IS-Angelinattf-really-a-freeware-font "http://ask.metafilter.com/52509/IS-Angelinattf-really-a-freeware-font") If we believe what is written then this font is really named Angie and was used exclusively for a greeting card company -- and you know, there is no reason to doubt that - happens all the time.
