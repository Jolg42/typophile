What is the double crown club?
Anonymous @ 26 Nov 2006 — 10:41am
the [Double crown club](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Double%20crown%20club) was a club formed around 1932 by [Stanley Morison](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Stanley%20Morison), alongside [oliver simon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/oliver%20simon)( [curwen press](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/curwen%20press)) [Bernard Newdigate](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Bernard%20Newdigate) ( [shakespear head press](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/shakespear%20head%20press)) , [gerard Maynell](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/gerard%20Maynell) ( [westminister press](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/westminister%20press)) [Francis Maynell](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Francis%20Maynell) ( [nonesuch press](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/nonesuch%20press)) , [Wren Howard](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Wren%20Howard), [Jonathan cape](http://web.archive.org/web/20131013052417/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Jonathan%20cape), and more. came accross this info in the Thames and hudson typography manual, havent found much more info about the group lou
