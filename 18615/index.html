I don’t know of any universal symbol. The most common symbols used in Western newspapers, at least, are the [dagger](http://web.archive.org/web/20081011042151/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagger_(typography)) and the [star of David](http://web.archive.org/web/20081011042151/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_of_David), to indicate, respectively, Christian and Jewish deaths.
