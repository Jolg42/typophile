Locating and licensing Chelmsford

I’m trying to track down and license a font called Chelmsford. It’s influenced by/derived/ripped-off from Optima, but comes in a very light weight and has a few added touches – an Optima Titling Light, if you will. Check out the A, for example.

My guess is that this dates back to the early days of DTP, and is a digitization of someone’s film/CRT version of Optima. MyFonts, Fontshop and Fonts.com (along with a general Google search) didn’t turn up anything.

In addition to Michael’s comment, a few links: [Optima Nova](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Optima%20Nova) offers a [Light weight](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/www.linotype.com/54144/optimanovalight-font.html) aswell as a [Titling version](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/www.linotype.com/54345/optimanovatitling-font.html) (an all-caps font with a couple of ligatures and alternates). If you’re looking for a lighter titling version of Optima, check out [Young Finesse](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/doyaldyoung.com/113_YF_Roman/1131_Spec_YF_Roman/1131_Spec_YF_Roman.html) by [Doyald Young](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Doyald%20Young). That typeface started out as Fino Titling Light and is described by its author as “an ultra-thin, elegant drawing of Zapf’s Optima”. Beorcana by [Carl Crossgrove](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Carl%20Crossgrove) has a wide range of [Display weights](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/www.beorcana.com/specs2.html). If it may be less calligraphic, have a look at [Viceroy](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/www.typecuts.com/fonts.php?kid=8) by [Andrea Tinnes](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Andrea%20Tinnes) and [Verena Gerlach](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Verena%20Gerlach) which should be available soon: “ReVerb holds an exclusive license till June 2009” [!] Furthermore: [Accent Graphic](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=GTT0000001#characters) by [Nick Cooke](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Nick%20Cooke) has a Light, [TF Arrow](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/www.treacyfaces.com/050806_TFArrow_2c5a.html) by [Joseph Treacy](http://web.archive.org/web/20120717144440/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Joseph%20Treacy) even comes with an Extralight.
