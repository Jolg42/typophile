Ahh, that is vital info. Cochin and Mrs. Eaves aren't what you're looking for. Engravers is fairly benign so really any serif will sit well with it. We recently designed a [rug shop identity](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151823/http:/www.therugmerchants.com/) with Engravers and [Warnock](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151823/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Warnock). Other contemporary serif faces: [Fresco](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151823/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Fresco%20OT) [Swift](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151823/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Swift) [ITC Charter](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151823/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/paratype/itc_charter_ot/) [FF Nexus](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151823/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_nexus_serif_ot/)
