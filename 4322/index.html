Actually, Richard, Alt + 0165 in a properly made Windows font ought to get you the Yen symbol. While the infinity glyph is part of the MacRoman character set, it is not present in the 255 glyphs of the Basic Latin and Latin 1 Supplement [character sets](http://web.archive.org/web/20091230141831/http:/www.alanwood.net/demos/ansi.html) used in Windows fonts. [This page](http://web.archive.org/web/20091230141831/http:/www.alanwood.net/demos/charsetdiffs.html) shows other characters that differ between the two platforms.
