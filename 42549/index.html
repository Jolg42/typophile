Hi Lisa, <cite>I like that one small but when large it’s too heavy.</cite> What you’re experiencing is the problem/the absent of [Optical Scaling](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Optical%20Scaling). Over the last years, several foundries have published good text font families with optical sizes. Check out [Adobe’s line-up](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/www.adobe.com/type/topics/opticalsize.html). Or [Sumner Stone](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Sumner%20Stone)’s [ITC Bodoni](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/www.stonetypefoundry.com/itcbodoni.html) and [Cycles](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/www.stonetypefoundry.com/cyclesoverview.html). Or [Hoefler & Frere-Jones’ faces](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/www.typography.com/collections/index.php?collectionID=700001). Or … For Caslon: [ITC Founder’s Caslon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/founders-caslon/) has optical sizes. You can also simply combine matching fonts and, for example, utilize [Big Caslon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017055311/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/carter_cone/big_caslon/) by Carter & Cone for large sizes.
