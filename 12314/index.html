Arthur Baker

Indices : Designers : Arthur Baker

Arthur Baker has been drawing letters for decades and has written over 20 books on the subject. Along with his knowledge of letter forms and historical syles of calligraphy and his own custom designed pens and brushes, his style is admired and imitated by many calligraphers and type designers. He currently lives in Andover, Massachusetts.
