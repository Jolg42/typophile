Anonymous @ 24 Feb 2006 — 3:16pm
I actually think it's sufficiently distant from Frutiger, certainly more distant than some other fonts out there. For one thing, it has a nice warmth, due probably to the soft finish, but also the variances in the terminals, etc. In fact the main "problem" that jumps out -the pervasive wobbly outlines\*- might actually be something to keep in the end! It might add just the fauve feeling that a sans like this needs so badly to be: attractive; and different. \* Like see the right side of the counter of the "b". But really almost everywhere. :-/ A macro thing: I would make the descenders a bit shorter. And lc micro stuff: - "c": a bit too narrow. - The eye of the "e" is a hair too big. - The "f" and "t" are imbalanced - but again here this might be a good thing! - "k": maybe a bit too conservative. - "r": beak terminal a bit too out-of-character; try it like in the "s". - "v": too wide. - "z": too sharp. I might look at the UC and nums later. -- I think you should think about what this needs to be, and I have a feeling you'll actually uncover something very useful. hhp
