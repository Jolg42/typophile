Anonymous @ 14.Jul.2007 8.46am
- [PTL Skopex Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20081120064307/http:/www.primetype.com/faces_info.php?fid=37) by Andrea Tinnes (5 weights & italics and small caps each — ‘contemporary, vertical’) - [FF Karbid](http://web.archive.org/web/20081120064307/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_karbid/) by Verena Gerlach (3 weights & Display cuts, small caps etc. — ‘Berlinesque, readable’) - [Linotype Authentic Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20081120064307/http:/www.linotype.com/970/linotypeauthenticsans-family.html) by Karin Huschka (4 weights & italics each — ‘post-modern’) - [Ambigue](http://web.archive.org/web/20081120064307/http:/www.linotype.com/56036/ambigue-family.html) by Carla Schweyer (7 weights — ‘low x-height’) - [Ronnia](http://web.archive.org/web/20081120064307/http:/type-together.com/font_ronnia_home.php) by Veronika Burian (5 weights & italics each — ‘humanist’) I’ll leave it to you to judge whether they’re <cite>chunky</cite> or not …
