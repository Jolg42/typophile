Hello Jonathan, where are you coming from? Do you know Briar Press? [http://www.briarpress.org/](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907155135/http:/www.briarpress.org/ "http://www.briarpress.org/") There you can find some letterpress printers ans their adds. If you are from Europe, I’ld recommand: [www.druckerey.de](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907155135/http:/www.druckerey.de/ "www.druckerey.de") Martin Z. Schroeder is a fine letterpress printer. Have a look into his photos with references: [http://www.druckerey.de/02angebot.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907155135/http:/www.druckerey.de/02angebot.html "http://www.druckerey.de/02angebot.html") Yes 2 up to 3 weeks is realistic. No, sorry, correction: its fast. Its another time dimension, not comparable with Mac’s World. Lots of steps of working includes manual human ressources and its not just a click on your mouse pad. Difficult to understand for a digital designer, but believe me: its just another dimension, not a question of “faster” or “slower”, or more or less hectic. Yes, difficult to understand.
