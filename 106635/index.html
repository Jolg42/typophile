Signet, New York, 1973 lettering probably from Photolettering but I don't know the name off hand. [BTW the sexist cover illustration is by Frank Frazetta.] F and underscore are probably custom artwork. Sansita One by Pablo Cosgaya at Omnibus-Type could be edited to produce the lettering: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831130124im_/http:/typophile.com/files/SansitaOneInUse_5116.jpg) [http://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/sansita-one](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831130124/http:/www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/sansita-one "http://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/sansita-one") [http://www.omnibus-type.com/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831130124/http:/www.omnibus-type.com/ "http://www.omnibus-type.com/") It has an Open Font License which permits this.
