Anonymous @ 20 Aug 2009 — 2:13pm
Base your decision on how the face looks in an online type tester, for reasons such as the fact that sifr does not support kerning or tracking. So the display styles of [Brown Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150308183624/http:/www.shinntype.com/brown.html) might be good for a sans headline, as they are tightly fitted. Other Shinntype news faces with display styles: Nicholas, Pratt, Walburn, Worldwide. And I would say, make your headlines big, using display faces, because it will create a more sophisticated quality than what people are used to when they see default web faces blown up.
