Perhaps someone else recognizes an exact match. This is in a style called "Tuscan" which includes a large number of fonts with variations in the split ends shown in your sample. The style often includes the spurs sticking out at mid height. Your sample is a logo so it could be modified from its source. If you would be satisfied with something similar a good place to check for commercial fonts is [MyFonts with tag Tuscan](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014090712/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/tag%3Atuscan/fonts/). Identifont has some [Tuscan fonts not shown at MyFonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014090712/http:/www.identifont.com/list?2+tuscan+22+G7I+1+K0X+1+99E+2+GI3+4+JV6+5+EOB+11+324M+12+3284+14+JVU+14+C4Y+15+FWW+15+JV4+16+JVP+16+JV0+18+C4X+20+JVN+20+K2T+21+JVC+28+JVQ+29+M7R+32+L8J+35+2GCK+36+2A76+37). Letterhead fonts has a [Western category](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014090712/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/fonts/styles/western.php) which includes Tuscan fonts. For free fonts, of varying provenance and credibility, you could check [Dafont category Western](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014090712/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=106) which includes Tuscan fonts.
