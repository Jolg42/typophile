SUP, like the superscript option in most word processors will raise (and sometimes scale) the regular numerals. I'd imagine Wikipedia uses it because most system fonts don't contain a full set of the real thing\* and because it's machine parse-able? Looking at the fonts on my machine, Calibri seems to have them all (Georgia does not), and the design and alignment seems consistent.
