Caslon 540 is closest, with less contrast than Big Caslon. Adobe Caslon has less than 540. My Williams Caslon Text [http://www.fontbureau.com/fonts/WilliamsCaslonText/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317153812/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/WilliamsCaslonText/ "http://www.fontbureau.com/fonts/WilliamsCaslonText/") has still less. The bold on mine, which has higher contrast than the regular, will work at bigger than text sizes.
