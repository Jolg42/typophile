Back in the day, there was a magazine called Factsheet Five that served as a reviewer/chronicler/informal clearinghouse for hundreds of zines. It was founded by Mike Gunderloy and ran for several years before foundering in the 90s after a couple of changes in owner/editorship. (The publication's ghost site is still afloat at [http://www.factsheet5.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022124536/http:/www.factsheet5.com/)) Here's an eBay auction of old issues: [http://cgi.ebay.com/ws/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&item=1560075436](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022124536/http:/cgi.ebay.com/ws/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&item=1560075436) Check out [http://www.zinebook.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022124536/http:/www.zinebook.com/) for lots of zine info, too.
