[Excelsis](http://web.archive.org/web/20150323000442/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/solotype/excelsis/) is similar but not quite identical to Rubens. I remember seeing Rubens on one of the Solotype CD's, probably the Victorian one. [Castcraft/Optifont](http://web.archive.org/web/20150323000442/http:/www.castcraft-software.com/) also sells [a version](http://web.archive.org/web/20150323000442/http:/www.castcraft-software.com/designer3/designer3.11.html). There is a "freeware" version of dubious origin under the name [Ruben](http://web.archive.org/web/20150323000442/http:/www.grimghosts.com/down/fonts/fonts.html). I worked on a new digitization of a condensed Rubens-style typeface, based on typography from Disney's Haunted Mansion attractions. It's called [Ravenscroft](http://web.archive.org/web/20150323000442/http:/www.grimghosts.com/down/fonts/fonts.html) and is freeware. [Treehouse](http://web.archive.org/web/20150323000442/http:/www.frontiernet.net/~treehous/Typefaces.html) also has a Haunted Mansion inspired font called [Victorian Mansion](http://web.archive.org/web/20150323000442/http:/www.frontiernet.net/~treehous/Mansion.html).
