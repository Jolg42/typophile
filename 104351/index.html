Zapf's book _About Alphabets_ is in Optima, published in 1970, original Typophiles edition in 1960. [Here's an edition](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416064006/http:/oakknoll.com/detail.php?d_booknr=116327&d_currency=) for sale. And [here's a scan of a page.](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416064006/http:/pingmag.jp/images/article/kobayashi_zapf_book2.jpg) I'm guessing [the original German version](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416064006/http:/www.abebooks.com/servlet/BookDetailsPL?bi=10315668942&searchurl=kn%3Duber%2Balphabete%2Bzapf%26x%3D35%26y%3D17) is also in Optima. It seems the design was completed in 1958, so the German edition may be the first full book.
