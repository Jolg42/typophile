[Pizzadude.dk](http://web.archive.org/web/20080605060228/http:/www.pizzadude.dk/home.php) is willfully freeform, but it is in Indie Fonts 3, so maybe it’s not out there enough for you. As the creator, Jakob Fischer, says on the About page, “Don’t expect me do re-do Times New Roman or Futura!” That’s a far cry from a lot of type designers, who are always looking to create book types.
