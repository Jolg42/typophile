On the first sample you can create a brush in photoshop that gives you that finish; In Photoshop CS4 which is the one I have now I found brush 59px or 39px that could give you that effect. You can also draw directly in a piece of acetate with a sponge and create the effect to then burn it in a silkscreen and come up with a result like it. The second one could also be a big brush in photoshop, but I could also think that if you don't ink a silkscreen evenly you would have this result. Hope this helps. Patty
