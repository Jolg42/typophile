"Titanic" movie titles compact sans w/ tiny serifs

Hello all,

This is a deceptively simple font to identify – for me at least. I’ve searched the web extensively, gone to www.whatthefont.com, and have obtained no concrete results. This font looks so familiar…but subtly different.

The font I’m talking about is the font (or, what I’m assuming is a font) used in the logo for the movie “Titanic” : not Trajan, but the Impact-esque font used in the logo (with the rivets in the text). First glances make this seem like Impact, but the font doesn’t quite match up to the logo. (For instance, the “T” is different…it has slight points on each end of the horizontal line.)

Any suggestions as to what this font might be would be greatly appreciated. Examples are included below. (Some are taken from promotional material for the film; others are taken from the film’s movie trailers. These fonts seem to be roughly the same.)

The closest "Helvetica" I could find was "Helvetica Inserat," which is an almost exact duplicate of "Impact." :( The thing to notice is that there is a slight sharpness on the bottom and top edges of the letters, most noticeably in the bottom example (one with the rivets). Notice how the T and I have a sort of spread-out at the bottom ... it's subtle, but noticeable. Helvetica Inserat (and Impact) don't have this. Also, for some reason, I think that the bar in the "A" character is a bit too high in Helvetica Inserat (and Impact)...I'm not sure why I say this, but it doesn't look right to me. One of the closest fonts I've found thusfar is Agency FB Black Compressed; however, the C doesn't match up on this, and it's still very not close. Any other suggestions?
