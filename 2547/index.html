I found a few other faces that Glaser had done, they are all from his book ["Art is Work"](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044430/http:/overlookpress.com/design/glaser.shtml). I don't know if any of these were for PhotoLettering either. Most were custom jobs, but interesting nontheless. This one was done for Bruegger's, a company that makes bagels. ![Brueggers](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044430im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/83/23497.gif) This one was a proprietary face for the Rainbow Room in NYC; It had two styles: wide and narrow. ![rainbow](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044430im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/83/23498.gif) Einstein Bold was done for the album cover of [Einstein on the Beach](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044430/http:/www.epinions.com/musc_mu-11672) by Philip Glass. ![einstein](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044430im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/83/23499.gif) This one was called Kitchen. ![kitchen](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044430im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/83/23500.gif) Another typeface called The Sesame Place, that was based on an earlier typeface of his called Houdini. ![sesame](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044430im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/messages/83/23501.gif)
