Try [CrossFont](http://web.archive.org/web/20050525000700/http:/www.asy.com/scrcf.htm). Every now & again I have to use a font on a Mac (I'm a die-hard PC user - mostly because I'll die with hardly any money) and CrossFont usually does a good job of making them usable on a Mac platform. IIRC, the trial version is fully functional except that it doesn't do batch conversions. If you have a bunch of fonts, it might be worth the $45 for the license. Hope that works for you.
