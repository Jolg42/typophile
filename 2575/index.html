Anonymous @ Mon, 2004-01-05 11:15
I think it's fairly clear that this was a custom lettering job. The letterforms have typical modern characteristics, but I doubt you will find an exact match among existing fonts. The distortions you're seeing have to do with the fact that it was drawn as an italic, not simply slanted from upright artwork. When you slant ovals, you get an unnatural looking south-west to north-east diagonal stress. Correctly drawn italics are designed to compensate for this distortion. (You can see the compenstaion if you back-slant them to upright.) In this case, there appears to be a bit of over- compensation.
