According to the copyright note in the font, this is "Sans Poster Bold JL" reworked with a starry effect. My initial impression is that "Sans Poster Bold JL" is not a rip of another font-maker's work. So here is a link to "Starlight Sans JL" [http://www.fonts2u.com/starlight-sans-jl.font](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418123859/http:/www.fonts2u.com/starlight-sans-jl.font "http://www.fonts2u.com/starlight-sans-jl.font") Note that Jeff Levine's website, "Jeff's Fonts & Dingbats" has expired.
