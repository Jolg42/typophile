Is it possible that the font you remember was just similar to Zubilo? The bouncy, irregular, blocky shape style is not really a new thing, if you go back to [Artistik](http://web.archive.org/web/20140707110156/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/artistik/), and Harquil (which has been digitized as "Harlequin", and is shown in Solotype and Petzendorfer books, and very similar to [Gaiety Girl](http://web.archive.org/web/20140707110156/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/hihretrofonts/gaiety-girl/)), which are both Art Nouveau era (over 110 years old). There are others, including [Softie](http://web.archive.org/web/20140707110156/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/tailspinstudio/softie/), that MyFonts Uses all over its site. - Mike Yanega
