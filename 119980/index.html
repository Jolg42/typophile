Discussed here a few years ago, albeit about more current packaging: [http://www.typophile.com/node/93388](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.typophile.com/node/93388 "http://www.typophile.com/node/93388") Consensus seems to be that a proprietary face called TK Super Duper-BG2 is used, which was based on historical precedents like your label sample. Your sample is pretty small, but even when I sought out bigger samples from the same era, I didn't get any exact digital matches. In addition to LHF Hensler, mentioned in the post above, here are a few others that might work (and, yes, I've included some Copperplates): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/ideal-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/ideal-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/ideal-gothic/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/parkinson/modesto/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/parkinson/modesto/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/parkinson/modesto/") [http://www.linotype.com/44502/CopperplateGothic30BC-product.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.linotype.com/44502/CopperplateGothic30BC-product.html "http://www.linotype.com/44502/CopperplateGothic30BC-product.html") [http://www.linotype.com/44498/copperplategothic30ab-font.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.linotype.com/44498/copperplategothic30ab-font.html "http://www.linotype.com/44498/copperplategothic30ab-font.html") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/memo/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/memo/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/memo/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/american-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/american-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/american-gothic/") [http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/elsnerflake/itc\_symbol\_medium\_ot...](http://web.archive.org/web/20141026060042/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/elsnerflake/itc_symbol_medium_ot/?affId=98220 "http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/elsnerflake/itc\_symbol\_medium\_ot/?affId=98220")
