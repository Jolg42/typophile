Anonymous @ 6 Feb 2007 — 1:36pm
Look up Alphonse Mucha, the king of Art Nouveau, for colors, styles, etc. Lots of flowery stuff and colors that came back into vogue in the 1970s. Also look up Vienna Secession and Jugendstil. A couple of "art nouveau" fonts I can think of: Auriol, Desdemona, etc... Don't confuse Art Nouveau with Art Deco.

Anonymous @ 7 Feb 2007 — 4:38pm
I think KOLO is more Arts-and-Crafts than Art Nouveau. To me, it's not organic enough to be Art Nouveau. It feels more C.R. Mackintosh/ craftsman than Mucha/Studio/Paris. ELPHINSTONE is too ... too ... calligraphy. BELWE has potential for content, and **[arnold-boecklin](http://web.archive.org/web/20120506075914/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/arnold-boecklin/)** may work for display? But -- I don't think anyone does it better than **[P22 Art Nouveau](http://web.archive.org/web/20120506075914/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/p22/art-nouveau/)**
