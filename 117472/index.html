Angled terminals like your \_V\_ are found in the lowercase of Jenson Oldstyle designed by J. W. Phinney of the Dickinson Type Foundry in 1893, ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701141004im_/http:/typophile.com/files/JensonOSLC_5304.jpg) but not in the uppercase. Now available as LTC Jenson [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/lanston/ltc-jenson/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701141004/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/lanston/ltc-jenson/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/lanston/ltc-jenson/") The terminal style was popular circa 1900 and it was used in the uppercase of some lettering designs. Some digging might turn up a digital font with the \_V\_. don
