I GAVE A MAN! The scripts we have suggested so far lack the tone of the anguished and explosive cry for help of this lettering. Note the sharp corners. As a substitute I would use BiffBamBoom by Comicraft [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/comicraft/biff-bam-boom/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141006194437/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/comicraft/biff-bam-boom/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/comicraft/biff-bam-boom/") The lettering is very successful because it expresses the vigor shown in BiffBamBoom within the frame work of a less staccato treatment of the tops and bottoms of letters. A good project for a font maker. Somewha similar idea with Piekos FX [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/blambot/piekos-fx-bb/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141006194437/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/blambot/piekos-fx-bb/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/blambot/piekos-fx-bb/") Don
