- <cite>Brasilia is the name of the font, I probably should have explained.</cite> ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414193344im_/http:/typophile.com/files/brasilia_1_4448.png) …And that name is taken. A typeface called [Brasilia](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414193344/http:/www.typogabor.com/hollenstein/pages/25-hollenstein.html) was designed by Albert Hollenstein and Albert Boton in 1960. It is now available from a number of distributors—as part of the Monotype’s _Creative Alliance_ font library—under the name of [Brazilia](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414193344/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/creative_alliance/pl_brazilia_vp/). Apparently, the design of Brasilia/Brazilia was inspired by the architecture of the new capital city of Brazil, which was the hottest thing going, in 1960.
