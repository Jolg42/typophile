I have many, but these are my current favorites: - [FF Balance](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&searchstr=balance) - This has so much character merely because the stress is horizontal rather than vertical. Looks great every time I see it (which is sadly too rare). - [FF Strada](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&searchstr=strada) - Simple forms mixed with some organic terminals and a thoughtful italic. Mmm. [Another sample of FF Strada.](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/static.flickr.com/14/20098053_1f82de0bbc_o.jpg) - [FF Quadraat Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/showfont.cfm?fid=FF.10989.0.0) - There is experienced method behind the lack of uniformity. - [FF Profile](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/showfont.cfm?fid=FF.11100.0.0) - Has a friendly air for less serious texts. - [FF Legato](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&searchstr=legato) - This groundbreaking design requires some reading to fully appreciate. [Start&nbsp;here.](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/typographi.com/000969.php) - [Versa Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&searchstr=versa%20sans) - More brilliance from a Dutch master. - [Freight Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/showfont.cfm?fid=GF.118889.0.0) - Excellent [complimentary styles](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.joshuadarden.com/) available too. I recommend loosening the spacing a tad. - [Amplitude](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&searchstr=amplitude) - The Normal and Wide are really great for small print. - [Lisboa Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322223818/http:/www.veer.com/partner/link.aspx?i=1048&c=4&p=products/typedetail.aspx?image=FOT0000081) - Veer mailed a sans poster a few weeks ago and this new release was the most impressive on the sheet.

This is the sort of context in which a sans serif can work quite well.
