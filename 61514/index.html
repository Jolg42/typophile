Graffiti &c

About as far from science as you can get (not that there’s anything wrong with that).

A famous graffiti artist, Blek Le Rat, somehow ended up on my facebook and he joined a group that linked to this site called Maquis Art. Better brush up on your Français.

Despite the fact I’m a bit of a digit head, I have to admit I’ve always been fascinated by graffiti art, due to the fact that it’s largely type-based. There’s a fantastic documentary on the history of graffiti called Bomb It. I highly recommend.
