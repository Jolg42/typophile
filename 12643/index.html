Very nice. It does bother me that the spline control points in the _FontLab Studio_ icon are wrong. I think those could be fixed without detracting too much from the design of the icon. It’s also a little disconcerting to see that the split-up pieces of the _BitFonter_ logo contain partial pixels of the _B_ glyph. The pieces should be split on exact pixel boundaries. Finally, I’m confused as to the purpose of the _r_ that exists in the negative space of the _TransType_ logo. It seems to just clutter up the logo — probably more so in the smaller versions of the icon, such as the Windows-typical 32×32. It also doesn’t work at all with the upside-down _T_. I’d probably keep the two _T_s and eliminate the _r_ entirely.
