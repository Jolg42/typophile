Anonymous @ 31 Jan 2014 — 2:06pm
Definitely not a font. Simon Walker's lettering. Looks like stiff old Times Roman poured into a vortex to end up as a charming toilet paper lettering. To duplicate it, apply the buttery vertical strokes found in fonts like FF Tartine Script [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/tartine-script/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090744/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/tartine-script/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/tartine-script/") or Aspic [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/alias/aspic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090744/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/alias/aspic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/alias/aspic/") to the letters of your choice add ball terminals, do swooshing and you are there. Don
