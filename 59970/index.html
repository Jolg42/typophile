(1) [Kozuka Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/store1.adobe.com/type/browser/P/P_1801.html) (小塚ゴシック) and [Kozuka Mincho](http://web.archive.org/) (小塚明朝) from Adobe Adobe’s Kozuka Gothic and Kozuka Mincho are easy to get and have as a modern and warm feeling as Cronos has. (2) [Hiragino Kaku Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.screen.co.jp/ga_product/sento/sample/hirg.html) (ヒラギノ角ゴシック) and [Hiragino Mincho](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.screen.co.jp/ga_product/sento/sample/hirm.html) (ヒラギノ明朝) Hiragino Kaku Gothic and Hiragino Mincho are loaded on Mac OSX. They were designed by [Jiyukobo](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.jiyu-kobo.co.jp/home.html) (字游工房), which is one of the most respected foundries in Japan. (3) [Yu Gothic-tai](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.jiyu-kobo.co.jp/ytl/ygf.html)(游ゴシック体) and [Yu Mincho-tai](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.jiyu-kobo.co.jp/ytl/ymf.html) (游明朝体) They are designed by Jiyukobo as well. (4) [TypeBank Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.typebank.co.jp/english/fonts/gothic/) (タイプバンクゴシック) and [TypeBank Mincho](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.typebank.co.jp/english/fonts/mincho/) (タイプバンク明朝) TypeBank Gothic and TypeBank Mincho are well designed fonts. (5) [Gothic MB101](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.morisawa.co.jp/font/fontlist/details/fontfamily008.html) (ゴシックMB101) and [Ryumin](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.morisawa.co.jp/font/fontlist/details/fontfamily001.html) (リュウミン) They are ones of the most frequently used font in Japan. Type Project’s [AXIS](http://web.archive.org/web/20150113011528/http:/www.typeproject.com/product/axis_basic/) is also a nice sans serif font. AXIS doesn’t has the serif companion but Kozuka Gothic might be a good companion.

Anonymous @ 17 Jul 2009 — 12:44am
Also consider the Ryo "PlusN" fonts, available as two serif families (Ryo Text PlusN and Ryo Display PlusN) and one sans serif (Ryo Gothic PlusN). These combine the kana and punctuation of the Ryo "Std" fonts, which were specifically tuned for readability, with the kanji and other glyphs from the Kozuka Mincho and Kozuka Gothic families. Roughly 70% of Japanese text is comprised of kana, so making the kana glyphs more readable increases overall readability. I believe that the Ryo PlusN fonts are bundled with the Japanese versions of the Creative Suite.
