Anonymous @ 15 Dec 2005 — 9:00am
Not all languages use the same quotation marks. The opening quotes for some languages, such as German, are "low-9" quotes. They are paired with the raised 6-shaped quotes on the right (which are the same as the English-style opening quotes). See the [Wikipedia article](http://web.archive.org/web/20140910084802/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotation_mark). I know they use these in Icelandic, so I'm guessing this is the convention in Norwegian as well.
