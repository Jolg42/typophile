Your best bet would be to contact [FontShop International](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202052627/http:/www.fontfont.com/contact.html) as FF Isonorm is a type family from their FontFont collection. I checked the "designers" section on their [website](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202052627/http:/www.fontfont.com/shop/index.ep), but the information on Kirchner is unusually sparse: "Robert Kirchner, an Austrian FontFont designer." I know he passed away a couple of years ago due to illness.
