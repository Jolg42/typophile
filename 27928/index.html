Anonymous @ 26.Aug.2006 12.18pm
Linotext’s name has NOT been changed. We have two typefaces based off of Wedding Text, Mariage and Linotext. Here is the link to Linotext: [http://www.linotype.com/948/linotext-family.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20080103050612/http:/www.linotype.com/948/linotext-family.html "http://www.linotype.com/948/linotext-family.html") Linotext is a slightly lighter font than Mariage, which feels like a slightly darker digitization to me. I recommeded Mariage in this case only because its slightly greater dense-ness seems closer to the Syndey Morning Herald’s nameplate than Linotext does.
Anonymous @ 26.Aug.2006 12.23pm
As a side note, Linotype has been distributing “Linotext” since about 1926. I don’t know where Mariage came from, or in what year or format it first entered the Linotype Library.
