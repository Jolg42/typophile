That sure is an interesting project! The problem is twofold: for one, if all you want is to mimic the typographic choices of a french publication from the early sixties, you risk to end up with something bland (as in the example shown in the page you linked)\*. The most important side, though, is that in so doing you would betray the very spirit of situationism. Situationism is about what works _now_. It’s about subverting conventions and conventional wisdom. And using them to attain a political goal. Art is the primary political weapon of the situationists. A trivial suggestion would be, for example, setting an article about animal rights/anti-fur activism using the poshest didoni\*\* available. \* Which, to tell the truth, is italian, but you got the point. \*\* [http://typophile.com/search/?keys=fashion%20magazine](http://web.archive.org/web/20091129143937/http:/typophile.com/search/?keys=fashion%20magazine "http://typophile.com/search/?keys=fashion%20magazine")
