Brian: An acronym is commonly used for a group, such as a "Council", but DWFC is not an acronym friendly title, meaning that it doesn't flow. "W" rarely lends itself to a nice sounding acronym (exception, A&W Rootbeer). DFC is not bad, but unfortunately, it isn't consistent with their URL. RE: "Drywall Finishing doesn't conjure a lot of graphic images in my mind" Have you considered doing a strictly typographical logo? This would be an appropriate approach for a "Council". Maybe a very simple shape such as a trapazoid (representing an angled sheet of drywall) could be incorporated
