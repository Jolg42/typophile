\>\> _Do you think the new set is an improvement over the old logo?_ Yes, I do prefer the new set over the first one. While the effect of the diamond shape going both over and behind the G is cool, it would be lost somewhat when reduced to the size needed for a business card or sent via fax. This is why I always design logos first in just black and white. That way I know it will work on a photocopy or over a fax. With your second set, the lighter blue thin cross hairs could be converted to black for things like fax cover sheets and still work fine. -- Nancy Foster Fontzilla's Alter Ego [http://www.fontzilla.org](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023075750/http:/www.fontzilla.org/ "http://www.fontzilla.org")
