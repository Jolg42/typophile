The copyright of a digital font usually denotes it's debut year in digital form (for a specific publisher). If you go to this page: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/ag-old-face-bq/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140714071637/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/ag-old-face-bq/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/ag-old-face-bq/") you can see this is a redraw of an older face: > In 1984 H. Berthold released this redraw of Akzidenz-Grotesk by Günter Gerhard Lange. > This design emphasizes the 19th Century hand-setting roots of the original Akzidenz-Grotesk. > Designers: Günter Gerhard Lange > Design date: 1984
