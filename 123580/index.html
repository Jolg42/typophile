'Life During Wartime': probably hand-done as all repeating letters are different (3 /i and 3 /e); loosely similar: [Brannboll](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mawns/brannboll-ny), [Harman](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ahmet-altun/harman) Script, [Spumante](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/laura-worthington/spumante), **[Rosarian](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/laura-worthington/rosarian)**, [Superb](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/resistenza/superb), [Lavinia](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/eurotypo/lavinia), [LBPie](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/creativemarket.com/lbolter/205051-LBPie) 'a film by Todd Solondz': again, hand-done (3 different /d); alternatives: [Learning Curve](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/Learning-Curve-Pro), [School Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/school-script), [Propisi](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/propisi) 'DURING': reminds me a lot of [Spire](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415082018/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/spire) with a reduced /R leg of course
