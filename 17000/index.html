I am not so sure that this even started out as DIN, although I admit it would be a pretty good way to fudge it, if you just wanted a resemblance. I looked at ITC Conduit, based on Yves' principle about DIN 'not-quites', but it is even less like the sample. You also realize that the 'a' and 'e' are the same letter flipped over.
