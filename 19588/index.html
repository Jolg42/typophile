Ouch, a hand written script... See [section 14](http://web.archive.org/web/20070809000417/http:/typophile.com/forums/typeid/faq/#Q_15) of the FAQ. Those are the hardest. Because they are so easy and cheap to produce there are literally thousands of them floating around on the web. Have you ever seen this script in another context, with different characters, especially repeating ones? It could even be a custom lettered logo for all we know, and only these 6 characters exist.
