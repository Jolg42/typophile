Anonymous @ 24 August, 2006 - 10:46am
If you simply want — or can use — and outlined bullet, this is a perfect opportunity for nested styles. You could create a “character style” of the black outline and “nest” it in your “paragraph style” which assigns proper indentations. [A few useful links on creating nested styles.](http://web.archive.org/web/20070125220739/http:/www.google.com/search?hl=en&lr=&client=safari&rls=en&q=indesign+%2B+nested+styles&btnG=Search)
