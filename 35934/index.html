There may be something you have that is close. What weights do you need. Are you using PC or Mac? A very nice freeware serif is Tallys: [http://www.josbuivenga.demon.nl/tallys.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20141110021158/http:/www.josbuivenga.demon.nl/tallys.html "http://www.josbuivenga.demon.nl/tallys.html") Also look into a font called Gentium: [http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site\_id=nrsi&item\_id=Gentium...](http://web.archive.org/web/20141110021158/http:/scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&item_id=Gentium_design "http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site\_id=nrsi&item\_id=Gentium\_design") Otherwise, I can't believe you have a business and don't have some kind of choices that came bundled with the programs you own. (always check the CDs) If you do a lot of non-profit work, consider the Softmaker XXL cd. It has a ton of fairly decent fonts for a relatively small sum.
