My Classification System

I think You might get a laugh out of this. I have decided to rearrange the fonts at my new place of employment. At present there is a load of free “fonts”, conflicts, broken families and to make matters worse, most of them are catagorised alphabetically! Urrgh. So I have devised a new method of catagorisation for the mess. Bear in mind that the others using the fonts aren’t really typographically aware and “real” classifications would probably make it worse.

sans�geometric
sans�rubbish
serif�slab
serif�traditional
