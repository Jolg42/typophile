The image seems to be from one of the original posters for the movie, which was released in 1984. I suspect it is lettering adapted from a monoline slab serif font such as Rockford. The oversize bowl on the R and the ski jump leg could have been added to evoke a nostalgic retro/deco mood. At least I have not seen the combination in a digital or pre-digital monoline slab serif font. There is at least one digital font inspired by Rockford that has a ski jump leg on the R similar to the lettering: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20141123045257im_/http:/typophile.com/files/1984LetteringAndSanchezSlabWithAltR_3715.jpg) The lettering is in red and the font in black. It's [Sanchez Slab](http://web.archive.org/web/20141123045257/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/latinotype/sanchez-slab/), using the alt R. Don
