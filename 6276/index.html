Ooooh, many, but I'll restrict myself to a couple of lesser known options. [Oxtail](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/www.psyops.com/html/spec_oxtail.html) is subtly different, a contemporary take on Clarendon. [Fantabular](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/www.mvbfonts.com/html/fonts/fantabular/pk_fantabular.htm) is monospaced, which opens up a lot of design possibilities. [Silica](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/www.stonetypefoundry.com/html_pages/SilicaOverview.html) is an often overlooked little gem by Sumner Stone. [ApexSerif](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/thirstype.com/fonts.php?font=ApexSerif) is so contemporary it hurts. ![:-)](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/clipart/happy.gif) [Charifa](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=255) is very geometric, inspired by Bauhaus. [Aminta](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=789) is a re-interpretation of the typewriter style. [Barbera](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=570) is nice'n'quirky. [Aurea Ultra](http://web.archive.org/web/20100220210827/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=452&dw=237) has only one weight, but with a killer italic. Tell me if you need more options.
