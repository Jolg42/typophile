The first non-Hebrew/Greek/Latin Psalter was indeed the _Psalterium David et Cantica aliqua_, printed by Marcellus Silber from Regensburg for Johannes Potken in 1513. I also asked in what _language_ the edition was printed: the script is generally known as Ethiopic, but this term is not really accurate for the language.
