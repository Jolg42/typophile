Note that an ultrasound machine could use a custom OEM font which is not necessary available as a general purpose digital font. That said, you may like one of the following similar fonts: **[Fox Grotesque (Alt Black)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417102504/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Various%20%2F%20TipografiaRamis%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Fox%20Grotesque%20-%20Alt%20Black&fid=f0c1787a254682249396fd45b555176d&fsize=48&text=DERFO%2019%2F06%2F2%2011%3A42%3A07&wrap=2)** [TipografiaRamis, 2012] **[Merge Pro (Bold)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417102504/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Various%20%2F%20Philatype%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Merge%20Pro%20-%20Bold&fid=f2046ef44b09bb963ab97ebe1ede4b6e&fsize=48&text=DERFO%2019%2F06%2F2%2011%3A42%3A07&wrap=2)** [Philatype, 2012]
