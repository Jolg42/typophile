Anonymous @ 10 Jul 2005 — 5:09pm
Most of that, including the first R, matches Kanzlei Bold, stretched vertically a bit. The two digital versions I'm aware of are Dieter Steffmanns [Fette Kanzlei](http://web.archive.org/web/20141112181352/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/samples/f/fettekanz.htm) and Paul Lloyd's [Plain Germanica](http://web.archive.org/web/20141112181352/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/lloyd/blackjewels/index.htm) (listed as Germanica). The top of the k and l, as well as the tail swashes on the C and second R will require custom work.
