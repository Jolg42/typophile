Well, "Senator" was said to have been based on her "Emigre 15" (now renamed "Lo-Res Twenty-Two Serif"); "Matrix" was the outline version of "Emigre 14" (now renamed "Lo-Res Twenty-One Serif"); "Modula" was based on "Emperor 15" (now renamed "Lo-Res Twenty-Two Narrow"); The entire "Base" family was designed with screen-resolution bitmap grids in mind; etc. She seems to use the bitmap grid to its full advantage from the very beginning of her design process.
