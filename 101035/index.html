I would guess this was hand lettered. Compare the cap S's. Something that could fill the bill as a starting point would be Albertus Black or Flange Medium Italic. You would still have to oblique it and try to condense to match. Or just hire a guy with a big red nose to redraw it.
Anonymous @ 2 Mar 2013 — 9:26pm
Agree with previous comment, definitely hand-lettered. Some I like for this kind of job: [Highway](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/youngjerks.com/Highway-Typeface) by Dan Cassaro, [Hensler 2](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/fonts/hensler2.php), [Chipping](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/chipping/), [Mexborough](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/mexborough/), [Chipperly](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/chipperly/), [Liberty](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/liberty/), [Symbol](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/symbol/), [Agora](http://web.archive.org/web/20131130062403/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/agora-bq/)
