Anonymous @ 24 Oct 2009 — 6:46am
The list from Ryuk was pretty good for angular, but maybe some of the heavy German styles should also be considered? They are not all angular, exactly, but the feel is similar in my opinion. See Tobias Frere-Jones' version of Imre Reiner's [Reiner Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/reiner-script/) (1951), Reiner's [Mercurius Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/mercurius-bold-script/) (1957); Reiner's [Matura](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/matura-mt/) (1938), Reiner's 'Mustang' (revived by Patrick Griffin as [Hunter](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/hunter/) (1956), W. Wege's [Signal](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/signal-bq/) (1931), Georg Trump's [Time Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/time-script-lt/) (1956), Heinz Schumann's [Stentor](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/stentor/) (1964); Herbert Thannhaeuser's 1954 'Kurier' (revived as [Puma](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/puma/) by Rebecca Alaccari, Thannhaeuser's [Lotto](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/lotto/) (1955), George Hauser's [Hauser Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/hauser-script-rr/) (1937), Rudolf Koch's 1927 'Kurrent' revived by Sudtipos as [Divina](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/sudtipos/divina/), Paul Zimmermann's [Impuls](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/impuls/) (1945), Peter Schneidler's [Maxim](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/profonts/maxim/) (1956), F.H.E.Scheidler's [Graphis](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/graphis/) (1934) and [Reporter](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414233133/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/reporter/) by Carlos Winkow (1938). This list was compiled mainly by scanning through Jaspert's book and using my notes about existing fonts of the heavy German styles. - Mike Yanega
