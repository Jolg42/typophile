Anonymous @ 17 Jan 2007 — 8:16pm
The type of museum might play a role in your design decisions. Legibility is probably first and foremost though. Consider that you will have people of all diferent heights (and eye strengths) trying to read at a fixed level. Larger than usual leading and point sizes over 16 help out in these areas. Museums are often surprisingly darker than you think of them to be also. The Museum may have a style guide that they adhere to. The copy editor might be a good person to inquire about that with. Or inquire with the installation manager. They are often aware of what works and what doesn't in their particular galleries.
