Anonymous @ 12 Feb 2008 — 1:43pm
There is little correlation between the appearance of a type in print and online, especially at text size. If one is familiar with a face in print, it would be foolish to assume that knowledge is worth anything on screen. You have to see it. There are also many ways to "process" a type for screen display. So "use Arial/Georgia" is safe, but I'd say, try your idea and see if it works for your readership on their preferred browsers/OS. [update] OK, there is some correlation! Looks fine to me.
