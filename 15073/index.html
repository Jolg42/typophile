Anonymous @ 13.Sep.2005 3.19pm
I could not find any current faces that look like your sample, but I did find more information on the face used in Frankenstein. Dan Solo’s sample of “Othello” was indeed correct. Though another typeface named “Othello” from Monotype was released in 1928, Morris Fuller Benton’s “Othello” was released in 1934 by American Type Founders. Here is the only sample and availability (in hot metal) I could find: [Don Black hot metal faces](http://web.archive.org/web/20081007114606/http:/www.donblack.ca/fonts/o.htm) (scroll down to page bottom) List of faces by [Morris Fuller Benton](http://web.archive.org/web/20081007114606/http:/www.typolexikon.de/b/benton-morris.html) (scroll down to “O”). It would make a great revival face since it is still very unique for display. If someone digitized it already, it hasn’t shown up in any sans serif display cuts I’ve searched through (plus it probably isn’t named “Othello” anymore).
