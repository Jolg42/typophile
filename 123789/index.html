I have been asked to re-create these three metal serial number plates to be used on a restored classic Ferrari.
Hopefully the good people of the forum can help me with a few pointers as to the fonts used.

I’m counting 7 different fonts being used - not including the old style Ferrari logo typography (a vector of which is also proving difficult at the moment)

|motore contiene| is Eurostile: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121945/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile/") |SHELL SUPER MOTOR OIL| could be replicated using Tablet Gothic (substitute a lowercase |l| for the |I|): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/type-together/tablet-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121945/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/type-together/tablet-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/type-together/tablet-gothic/") The center panel appears to be a mix of Univers (numbers) and Helvetica (letters): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/univers/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121945/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/univers/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/univers/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121945/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica/") |FERRARI 365 GTB/4-A| appears to be News Gothic Bold: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/news-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121945/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/news-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/news-gothic/") |MOTORE ENGINE|, |AUTOTELAIO CHASSIS|, and |MADE IN ITALY| appear to be Futura: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/futura/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121945/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/futura/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/futura/") |365 GTB/4-A| appears to be Reforma: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/reforma-grotesk-bold-e/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317121945/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/reforma-grotesk-bold-e/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/reforma-grotesk-bold-e/")
