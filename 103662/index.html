the Hundreds: a frankestein... "the" looks like to come from [Brush Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/brush+script), "H" from [Rage Italic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/rage+italic) and "undreds" close to [Spoleto/Aktuelle/Athletic Script/Opti Sport Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/typophile.com/node/70274), [Fenway Park](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jukebox/fenway-park-jf), [Cut Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/artistofdesign/az-cut-script) or [Vitrina](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/cubanica/vitrina) Undftd: also close to [Spoleto/Aktuelle/Athletic Script/Opti Sport Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/typophile.com/node/70274). More in the same vein: [Trattoria](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/ht-trattoria), [Team Spirit](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/www.dafont.com/team-spirit.font), [Mascot](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/mvbfonts.com/mvb_mascot/mvb_mascot), [Varsity](http://web.archive.org/web/20150131192527/http:/marketplace.veer.com/font/Varsity-Script-JBT0000052)
