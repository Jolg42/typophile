Your teal/yellow logo is definitely the strongest, but the letter forms look a little live-trace-esque. especially for a technology company, i think a less hand-lettered look is appropriate. The direction that the mark is going in is nice. A little freedom from ipod/itunes shapes and icons seems like a good idea to me.
