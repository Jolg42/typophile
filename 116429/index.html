A 19th century serif style called Detroit or mansard. The lowercase of Outstanding by "bobistheowl" is in the ballpark for CRUZAN ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322133346im_/http:/typophile.com/files/OutstandingLCAsCRUZAN_3633.jpg) [http://www.dafont.com/outstanding.font?text=cruzan&psize=l](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322133346/http:/www.dafont.com/outstanding.font?text=cruzan&psize=l "http://www.dafont.com/outstanding.font?text=cruzan&psize=l") More info by Luc Devroye [http://luc.devroye.org/bobistheowl.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322133346/http:/luc.devroye.org/bobistheowl.html "http://luc.devroye.org/bobistheowl.html") Outstanding is based on Big Vintage Letters by Big Letters Org [http://bigletters.org/big-decorative-vintage-letters.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322133346/http:/bigletters.org/big-decorative-vintage-letters.html "http://bigletters.org/big-decorative-vintage-letters.html") With some digging it might be possible to find the original 19th century source. Note that the Outstanding font has awkward sizing. Resize the glyphs to about 40% and they work much better. Relatively minor editing of Outstanding could produce a pretty close facsimile of CRUZAN. Don

Found the 19th century source of the letters used to make Outstanding. It's \_Cameo\_, patented in 1874 and sold by Mackellar Simiths & Jordan. There is also a digital version by "Character" at abfonts [http://abfonts.freehostia.com/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150322133346/http:/abfonts.freehostia.com/ "http://abfonts.freehostia.com/") Open the page, follow the link in the third paragraph to \_list of all fonts\_, page down to \_CameoAntique\_.
