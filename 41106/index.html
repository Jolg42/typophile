There are literally hundreds of text faces that would work. The most important question to ask is: what is the subject, tone and audience of your magazine?

If your goal is to find a "text" version of Stag, here are a few slabs that work well in running copy: [Amasis](http://web.archive.org/web/20100820030143/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=Amasis) [PMN Caecilia](http://web.archive.org/web/20100820030143/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/pmn_caecilia_std_ot_virtual/) [Serifa](http://web.archive.org/web/20100820030143/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/elsner_flake/serifa_ot/) [Egyptienne F](http://web.archive.org/web/20100820030143/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/linotype/egyptienne_f_std_ot_virtual/)
