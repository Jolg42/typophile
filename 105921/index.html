Can't find a perfect match and really doubt there's one actually, commercially available speaking. As almost all the time for the fashion industry, they probably here too use bespoke typefaces which makes perfectly sense when your business tries to evoke luxury, uniqueness and fashion. This one is somehow similar to [Engravers Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/engravers+gothic/)/ [Blair](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/blair/)/ [Sackers Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/sackers+gothic/), slightly wider. With some little alterations, you could do pretty much the same with [Proxima Nova MS](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/marksimonson/proxima-nova), [Gotham HTF](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/gotham/overview), [Maax 205](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/gautier/maax), [Nexa FFF](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/font-fabric/nexa), [Room GJ](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/glen-jan/room) or even [Avenir](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044321/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/avenir) (more work here).

Anonymous @ 30 Aug 2013 — 6:15pm
Hello, If I may clarify this a little bit: The logotype is a bespoke design created by Julien Gallico, the art director responsible for the new Balenciaga identity. We had no hand in it at all. He commissioned us to create a new typeface for this identity. While they certainly share a similar esthetic (that was the goal!), the logo and the typeface are not directly derived from each other. The typeface is a sort of spin-off (I'm not going to say custom since it often means quickly changing a few letters) of our typeface U8, which we transformed under his art direction. As far as we know, the typeface has not been used very much yet. We expected it to be used for little prints on labels and things like that, the webfont was not planned (hence the rather poor rendering in your screenshot), but we are glad they embrace the design even for their website.
