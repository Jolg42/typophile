If you want something in that kind of plain oldstyle vein, but not quite as soft as Garamond No.3, then take a look at James Montalbano’s [Rawlinson](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044233/http:/www.terminaldesign.com/fonts/characters/?ID=56) — an underappreciated family, IMO. I like its sturdy, plain-spoken qualities. It’s not as sharply cut as Christian’s DB News, but it shares some DNA. — Kent.
