Anonymous @ 7.Mar.2007 8.30am
It looks like 구름 M (_Gureum_, meaning “cloud”, and M standing for medium weight) offered by the Korean font outfit [윤디자인 (YoonDesign)](http://web.archive.org/web/20071229080056/http:/yoonfont.co.kr/shop/han_view.asp?itemidx=723). In fact, you can see the Cyworld logo on the [front page](http://web.archive.org/web/20071229080056/http:/yoonfont.co.kr/) for their website. I can’t find any further information on the design, though. Korean fonts include latin alphabet glyphs by default in addition to the hangul (Korean alphabet). It is pretty common, I imagine, for Korean designers to use the latin alphabet component of their Korean fonts for latin-alphabet logos.
