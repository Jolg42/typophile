Recently, URW++ has been offering all of their fonts in OpenType PS format in various levels. “Level 1” OpenType fonts from URW++ contain Western & Central European characters, “Level 2” OpenType fonts from URW++ also contain Cyrillic and Greek. Bodoni Antiqua and Corporate A fonts are offered with the WGL4 character set (West, CE, Cyrillic, Greek): [http://www.urw.de/cgi-bin1/dalcgi/english/wgl4liste0.htd](http://web.archive.org/web/20071126054756/http:/www.urw.de/cgi-bin1/dalcgi/english/wgl4liste0.htd "http://www.urw.de/cgi-bin1/dalcgi/english/wgl4liste0.htd") More URW++ Didone fonts with just Western and CE can be found at: [http://www.urw.de/cgi-bin1/dalcgi/english/Kliste0.htd?kgruppe1=1&kgruppe...](http://web.archive.org/web/20071126054756/http:/www.urw.de/cgi-bin1/dalcgi/english/Kliste0.htd?kgruppe1=1&kgruppe2=1&kgruppe3=3 "http://www.urw.de/cgi-bin1/dalcgi/english/Kliste0.htd?kgruppe1=1&kgruppe2=1&kgruppe3=3") ParaType also offers a version of PT Bodoni that includes Western, CE and Cyrillic characters: [http://www.paratype.com/pstore/default.asp?search=Bodoni&fcode=PT\_BDN](http://web.archive.org/web/20071126054756/http:/www.paratype.com/pstore/default.asp?search=Bodoni&fcode=PT_BDN "http://www.paratype.com/pstore/default.asp?search=Bodoni&fcode=PT\_BDN") Note that ParaType’s Cyrillic version are design-wise usually much better than URW++’s.
