Microsoft Word for Windows has a nice built-in conversion routine labeled "Fix Broken Text". It deals with formatted text, RTF etc. as well as with plain text. For Mac OS, there is the excellent [Cyclone](http://web.archive.org/web/20100204115444/http:/www.alanwood.net/unicode/utilities_fonts_macosx.html#cyclone). For Windows, I often use [SC UniPad](http://web.archive.org/web/20100204115444/http:/www.unipad.org/) for this kind of work. Also, [Alan Wood’s Unicode resource page](http://web.archive.org/web/20100204115444/http:/www.alanwood.net/unicode/) in general is worth recommending as a source of such information. Adam
