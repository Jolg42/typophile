There's some free fonts in that style: [Cape-Arcona](http://web.archive.org/web/20110902121103/http:/www.cape-arcona.com/)'s [Address Unknown](http://web.archive.org/web/20110902121103/http:/www.cape-arcona.com/sites/fonts/font_address.html) might do. If you want a rough, quick-printed dot-matrix or inkjet look for the receipt font, you could look into something like the [Spikee](http://web.archive.org/web/20110902121103/http:/www.veer.com/results.asp?Image=TPT0000075), [Tickee](http://web.archive.org/web/20110902121103/http:/www.veer.com/results.asp?Image=TPT0000076) or [Yeti](http://web.archive.org/web/20110902121103/http:/www.veer.com/results.asp?Image=TPT0000063) families from Test Pilot Collective.
