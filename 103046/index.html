Old Serif Italic from 1950's

Hi Folks,

This old movie title type is eluding me. The closest I found was Nick Curtis’ version of the Saturday Evening Post type that he called “Saturday Morning Toast”. It’s at least from a typeface older than the movie, but it’s not a great match. The rounded tops of the R and B should be good earmarks, but this may never have been digitized. I looked in the McGrew book, skimmed the Solotype Catalog, and even looked in a Photo-Lettering catalog.

Anyone have any ideas?

Thanks,

Anonymous @ 15 May 2013 — 2:29pm
It’s LSC Book, which used to be part of the ITC library. There’s no authorized digital version as far as I know. Now, LSC Book wasn’t released until around 1970, so you might think that would rule it out. However, the film was originally titled _Cuban Rebel Girls_ when it was released. In a later reissue of the film, the title was changed to _Assault of the Rebel Girls_. I don’t have a date for that, but judging by the type it was after 1970. (More info [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701102751/http:/www.imdb.com/title/tt0052717/releaseinfo?ref_=tt_dt_dt#akas) and [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701102751/http:/www.scoopy.com/cubanrebelgirls.htm).)
