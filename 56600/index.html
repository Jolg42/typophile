hi KC, Some of your design should go in the bleed space - that's what bleed space is for. PMS colors are spot colors - e.g. if you want some words in a certain orange, you can specify a particular Pantone orange for those. With regard to how you should save the file (as well as color questions), you should communicate with the printer who will print the panel. hope that helps a bit.
