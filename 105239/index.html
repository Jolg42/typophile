“Energizer” is probably custom, reminds of Impact, though. The rest is [Interstate](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417210703/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/interstate/).

Thank you SO much! The Energizer is definitely some form of Helvetica. I was able to use LT Condensed Black Oblique with just a little tracking and kerning the sides of the "i." It matches almost perfectly. (Blue is my version over the black original). Thanks again! ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417210703im_/http:/typophile.com/files/FoundFont_3559.jpg)
