Anonymous @ 6 Jul 2004 — 6:30pm
This has come up before (or maybe somebody asked me about this via email), but I can't find the thread. The large capital G, PG, and R date back to when the ratings were introduced in 1969 and were custom designed. [Melior](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315014941/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/melior/bold/testdrive.html?s=RPG&p=48) is similar as is the [Renault](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315014941/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/renault/bold/testdrive.html?s=RPG&p=48) font, but there are no off-the-shelf fonts that will match these. The 13 is [Korinna Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315014941/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/itc-korinna/bold/testdrive.html?s=13&p=48) and the NC-17 is [Stone Informal](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315014941/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/itc-stone-informal/stone-inf-itc-bold/testdrive.html?s=NC-17&p=48).

Anonymous @ 6 Jul 2004 — 8:58pm
I believe these characters are different (see Mark's post above). NC-17 was added much later than the original ratings (in the 90s, I think) and intended or not, became the defacto replacement for the dreadful-sounding but seldom-used 'X' rating. Also PG-13 was created in the mid 1980s after Spielberg campaigned the MPAA and argued there was too much of a gap in between PG and R. _(Many, including Spielberg, thought Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom was too dark for the PG rating it received)_. As mentioned in one of those earlier threads, I don't think they redrew the seemingly-custom ratings logos, but simply patched them. ![:-)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150315014941im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/clipart/happy.gif) James is fine, Yves, but I don't really care either way.
