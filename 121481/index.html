A logo is similar to a trademark. It's a visual symbol for the brand, and may appear anywhere the company wants to identify itself: On a website, product packaging, business cards and letterhead, advertising (digital, print, or TV), vehicles, promotional items, etc. A logo may be composed of type, custom lettering, or images (abstract like the Nike swoosh or pictorial like the Starbucks mermaid), or some combination of the above. A logo has strict specifications that must be followed regarding color, aspect ratio, orientation, etc. A banner, as I understand it, is a header for a web page, or an advertisement embedded in a web page. A banner may include a logo, but it may also contain other words or images, and unlike a logo, its design may vary depending on its specific purpose.
