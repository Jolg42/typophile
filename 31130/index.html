Typophile anti-pixel badge

I’m assembling a few badges to put on my blog, and I wanted a Typophile badge. Couldn’t find one, so I made one.

This uses the silkscreen typeface available at Kottke.org, a widely used typeface for this use. I had to recreate the logo for 15 pixels because the favicon is 16 pixels and looks like poop when squooshed.

Anyone else have a badge variation? Also, feel free to use this one if you want to represent.

Hmmm I don’t know. Do you mean the text or the shading? One way to suss out what happeing in a micro pixel thing you like is to zoom in close & check out what happening on a pixel by pixel basis. The edge is just a sigle pixel line on the top & left 100% white dialed back to 45% or so and the bottom is 100% black dialed back to 20% or so. #6 has a trick hard pixel shadow. It’s on a dark red so it doesn’t read as what it is which is just hard black pixels. The last 3 are just 2 color... Having a pixel friendly logo helps as well of course.
