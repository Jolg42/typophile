The _Moonraker_ bit is clearly inspired by early [Emigre](http://web.archive.org/web/20080106133259/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Emigre) typography, while _Apartments_ is set in [Frutiger](http://web.archive.org/web/20080106133259/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontdetail&displayfontid=LH.107984.0.1&attributes.sampleSize=48&sampleText=A+P+A+R+T+M+E+N+T+S&sampleSize=24).
Anonymous @ 11.Oct.2005 7.33am
I think Moonraker is [Emigré Modula Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20080106133259/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontpackage&startrow=1&records=&currpage=1&totalrecords=3&displayfontid=E.78.0.0&fontRendererText=WOOVRAKER), with the W and V inverted to become the M and N, and a tweak to the stem of the R. (An old font called Institution had the same R as Moonraker, but would also have had to invert the W & V). - Mike Yanega
