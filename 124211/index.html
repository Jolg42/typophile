\_FILSON\_ looks widened and perhaps boldened and perhaps has other changes. \_SINCE 1897\_ might be close to a font. Closest match I found so far is Goudy Catalog ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942im_/http:/typophile.com/files/GoudyCatalogAsSince1897_4331.gif) [https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/goudy-catalogue/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942/https:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/goudy-catalogue/ "https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/goudy-catalogue/") An also ran is Stratford ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942im_/http:/typophile.com/files/StratfordAsSince1897_6347.gif) [https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/stratford/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942/https:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/stratford/ "https://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/stratford/") Using Stratford Medium might work as a base for making \_FILSON\_ ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942im_/http:/typophile.com/files/StratfordMediumAsFILSON_5708.gif) if it is widened 130% ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942im_/http:/typophile.com/files/StratfordMediumWidth130pctAsFILSON_4235.jpg) Don

Hoefler Text Bold, stretched and squashed, would also make a good basis for recreating the look of |FILSON| (you’d also have to refine the serifs): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/apple/hoefler-text/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/apple/hoefler-text/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/apple/hoefler-text/") Or use Hoefler Titling Bold, which has finer serifs: [http://www.typography.com/fonts/hoefler-titling/styles/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/hoefler-titling/styles/ "http://www.typography.com/fonts/hoefler-titling/styles/") ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150402034942im_/http:/typophile.com/files/HoeflerTitling_4664.png)
