Can't find a match and really doubt there's one actually. Gatorade owns Propel and use [custom-made fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/answers.yahoo.com/question/index?qid=20081101164312AABRpUF). As the 2 logos look blatantly connected, we could easily imagine Propel is also a custom job. Previous logo version could give some track though. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919im_/http:/typophile.com/files/PropelZeroLogo_5970.png) For what it's worth, if I had to reproduce it, I'd probably start with one among [Helvetica](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/helvetica) (Black Condensed or any of the Compressed)/ [Inserat](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/inserat)/ [Impact](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/impact)/ [Smart Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/smart-sans)/ [Plakette](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef-typeshop/plakette)/ [Folio](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/folio) Condensed Bold/ [Loft](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.fonts.com/font/monotype-imaging/loft), shear it and rework all counters "à-la- [Inverserif](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/typophile.com/node/102242)". And if you only need this logo as vector, it is freely available to download [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150312164919/http:/www.brandsoftheworld.com/logo/propel-fitness-water).
