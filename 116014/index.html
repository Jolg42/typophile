[Scotch Modern](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416152653/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/shinn/scotch-modern/) is a 19th century revival with Latin, Cyrillic and Polytonic Greek, and small caps and other expert features throughout. Note the handling of roman/italic in the Greek, for present day functionality amongst the three scripts. There are also stylistic options for characters such as rho, theta, phi etc.
