Nope. That's an old typeface called Tasmin (Bold, I believe). Unfortunately, the only digital version I'm aware of is called Tango Regular by WSI - a company that made its living selling batches of knockoff fonts to software companies who then bundled them with its software. Edit: Mike Yanega offers a [showing of Tasmin](http://web.archive.org/web/20150202004057/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/BauhausFaces6.html) including numerals.
