Norbert Florendo

Indices : Designers : Norbert Florendo

Norbert Florendo (b. 19–) is a graphic designer and typographer.

He served as Art Director, Ad Manager, Creative Director, Manager of Multimedia at Compugraphic and Agfa, and Typographer in Residence during the years between Allan Haley and Cynthia Hollandsworth.

Contributed to the addition of over 400 new and licensed typefaces to the Agfa-Compugraphic library. Principally involved with the acquisition and marketing of Otl Aicher’s Rotis Typeface System for Agfa.

Past Director-At-Large, Type Directors Club
Past TDC Traveling Exhibit Chairperson
Former member of ATypI

Graduate of The Cooper Union and a calligraphy student of Paul Standard.
