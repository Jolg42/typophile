Evetts’ book is interesting but it may give the mistaken impression that the Trajan letters were planned out geometrically. There isn’t any evidence of this. You should try to find a copy of Fr Edward M. Catich’s _The Origin of the Serif,_ which a good university library should have, and the rarer _Letters redrawn from The Trajan inscription in Rome._ The latter is likely to be kept in special collections, although there are six copies for sale on ABE, beginning at $138.
