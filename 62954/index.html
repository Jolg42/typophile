58Rodeo is closer. It’s based on an old typeface called Hellenic Wide which has come up often here. One old FontBank version is called Hefty, and Jason Walcott has made a newer, up-to-date font for Veer, called [Hellenic Wide Complete](http://web.archive.org/web/20091112224306/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=JBT0000096). - Mike Yanega
