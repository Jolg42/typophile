As for the logotype for \_laura mercier\_, it looks like a modified version of neue Helvetica® Std 27 Ultra Light Condensed [http://www.fonts.com/font/adobe/neue-helvetica/27-ultra-light-condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415203702/http:/www.fonts.com/font/adobe/neue-helvetica/27-ultra-light-condensed "http://www.fonts.com/font/adobe/neue-helvetica/27-ultra-light-condensed"). Main difference is that \_a\_ has had is tail clipped. A classic example of logo-making 101 -- take a retail font, clip something from a letter and cash a big cheque. Don
