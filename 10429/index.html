Oh. Wasn't sure what you meant. As far as the marks ... hmmm, well you mentioned the Gutterball thread, in which our friend Jay W. pointed out that a mark ought to be more distinctive than simply typing a company name in a specific font. Sometimes this is difficult b/c of time constraints. But it sounds like you have some time before your classes start, yeah? I would humbly suggest that you select a name from your group and play around with some sketches, just paper and pencil. That is always a good starting point. You might try both uppercase and lowercase, or mixed; you might try different forms of a specific letter; you might try different type classifications. bj
