Anonymous @ 25 May 2009 — 9:40pm
I did something similar to what James did... I set up a document in InDesign and opened the Glyphs palette... That will show you every character in your typeface, allowing you to double-click on the ones that haven't been showing up and pasting them into a textbox. You can also choose different Unicode sets, like Basic Latin and Latin 1, Extended Latin A, etc. It's time-consuming the first time, then you just add to it or tweak it, as James says. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018212433im_/http:/typophile.com/files/glyphs_6436.png)
