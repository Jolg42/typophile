If you're going to talk ambigrams you have to reference the master 'John Langdon' who is credited with inventing them. His site is a great resource of great examples. ----- snip ------ "In the early 70s, trying to do with words what Dali and Escher did with images, I invented (or discovered) what came to be known as "ambigrams." In 1980, I discovered that Scott Kim had invented them, too, almost at the same time." ----- snip ------ [John Langdon Website](http://web.archive.org/web/20130818060603/http:/www.johnlangdon.net/background)
