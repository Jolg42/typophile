Grapes, minus the initial G, is Adobe Caslon Italic: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/adobe-caslon/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140824142955/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/adobe-caslon/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/adobe-caslon/") Didn't find an exact match for the initial G, but Notera and Abuelito are close: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mawns/notera/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140824142955/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mawns/notera/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mawns/notera/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/corradine/abuelito/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140824142955/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/corradine/abuelito/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/corradine/abuelito/") Pressed appears to be a distressed version of Copperplate Gothic, or possibly Copperplate Gothic Hand: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/copperplate-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140824142955/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/copperplate-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/copperplate-gothic/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/wiescherdesign/copperplate-gothic-hand/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140824142955/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/wiescherdesign/copperplate-gothic-hand/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/wiescherdesign/copperplate-gothic-hand/") As for Magical, SimpleLife is very close. I didn't find anything as close in my search.
