Since this DTP Types, "started in 1989", surely doesn't own the rights to this old typeface, I thought I'd mention two other, reasonably priced sources. The Solotype Catalog shows this typeface under the name Pretoria (no n on the end). Brendel Informatik digitized it by that name and offers it via the [Quick Brown Fox](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516225128/http:/www.qbf.de/tceng.htm) website. Dieter Steffmann digitized it as [Vivian](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516225128/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/steffmann/samples/v/vivian.htm).
