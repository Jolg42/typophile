I am not clear as to whether you are looking for a rough or smooth base font to uses as a basis for redrawing. For a rough font consider Chancellor ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317094949im_/http:/typophile.com/files/ChancellorAsSGWREHNMAK_5624.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/pintassilgo/chancellor/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317094949/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/pintassilgo/chancellor/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/pintassilgo/chancellor/") Also Berliner Grotesk BQ Med ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317094949im_/http:/typophile.com/files/BerlinerGroteskBQMedAsSGWREHNMAK_6301.gif) [http://www.linotype.com/261314/BerlinerGrotesk-family.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317094949/http:/www.linotype.com/261314/BerlinerGrotesk-family.html "http://www.linotype.com/261314/BerlinerGrotesk-family.html") [http://www.fonts.com/font/berthold/berliner-grotesk-bq/volume?SiteId=15](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317094949/http:/www.fonts.com/font/berthold/berliner-grotesk-bq/volume?SiteId=15 "http://www.fonts.com/font/berthold/berliner-grotesk-bq/volume?SiteId=15") would work. EDITED ADDITIONAL INFO: This style of lettering seems have originated with Lucian Bernhard's Sachplakat --object poster or Plakatsil -- lettering. From about 1908 onwards versions were produced by German type makers such as Heinz Hoffmann, who designed the Block fonts ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317094949im_/http:/typophile.com/files/BlockBQAsSGWREHNMAK_6507.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/block-bq/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317094949/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/block-bq/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/block-bq/") Don
