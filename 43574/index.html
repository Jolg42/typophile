I think the idea of obliquing the Roman ’s’ is a key. If you do that with a heavy weight of [ITC Century](http://web.archive.org/web/20080828111044/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/itc-century/) (or adding stroke thickness might be even better), and use the Italic for the rest of the letters I think it’s very similar. Century has the flat top serifs you need. - Mike Yanega
