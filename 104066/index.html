Anonymous @ 23 Jun 2013 — 8:44pm
Vega is the Scangraphic clone of VAG Rundschrift / VAG Rounded. If we may believe [the english Wikipedia](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015113953/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedley_Place) on this, VAG Rundschrift / Rounded was designed by Gerry Barney at the agency Sedley Place in 1979. The [german Wikipedia](http://web.archive.org/web/20131015113953/http:/de.wikipedia.org/wiki/VAG_Rundschrift) tells a different story though. They date the design 1978 and attribute the design to Wolf Rogosky and Gerd Hiepler with later adaptations by Bertel Schmitt and Manfred Schwarzer. The apostrophe in the Bitstream version used by Michel Boyer and in the MacGregor’s Market logotype differs from the version available from URW, E+F, Adobe and Linotype. In my eyes and because older Bitstream data usually has origins at Linotype, the design of the Bitstream apostrophe is probably what the Germans call a ‘Verschlimmbesserung’, which roughly might translate into something like ‘inappropriavement’.
