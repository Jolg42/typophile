Blackletter 1955 book Enzian und Edelweiss By Verlag Hans Henning Podzun

This blackletter is on the cover of a book _Enzian und Edelweiss_ [two alpine plants] published by Verlag Hans Henning Podzun in 1955.

Larger scale image attached.
I don’t know of a digital font that matches, but perhaps there is something from metal type or the photo-type/contact-letter sheet era?
Failing that, does anyone know of any more letters in this style? Perhaps the letters are modeled on something from a lettering book? Any more book covers by this publisher or others?
Thanks.

Two modern reinventions of the blackletter that, in my opinion, share some aspects with this nice kind of square wide fat lettering: [http://www.type-together.com/Eskapade](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317115939/http:/www.type-together.com/Eskapade "http://www.type-together.com/Eskapade") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/moyenage/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317115939/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/moyenage/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/moyenage/") There are others but, for what I can see, they tend to resolve contrast eliminating thin strokes and replacing them with the pointed terminals of wide strokes. Also, most of them go for an hexagonal grid, which prevents horizontal strokes of some substance.
