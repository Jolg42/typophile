I think a bolded [Shelley Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20141118212252/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontpackage&startrow=1&records=&currpage=1&totalrecords=3&displayfontid=LH.457.0.0&fontRendererText=Kiehl%27s) would be pretty close, if you used two of them to get the right mix of ascenders. Notice the non-looping 'h' ascender in Andante, and looping 'l' in Volante, and similar K in Andante. The mismatched ascenders were the hardest feature to find. - Mike Yanega
