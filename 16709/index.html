Linotype’s LinoLetter for December 2005 – What to give a Type-Lover this Christmas

Check out the full version of this month’s newsletter at the Linotype website: http://www.linotype.com/8-38-8-19120/issue200512.html.

Content Preview: Giving gifts is easy and fun! Presenting a special edition of our newsletter, just in time for Christmas. Inside this month’s issue, you’ll find a list of fonts, t-shirts, and other design-oriented gems that are sure to fit under any Christmas tree. Additionally, this is the season to get acquainted with two typographic treats: the all-new Palatino nova, a long awaited 21st Century version of Prof. Hermann Zapf’s Palatino, and Candice, a “groovy” font in the party style of the 70s.

Just Completed: The Linotype OpenType Library! Equip yourself and your computer with over 2500 top-of-the-line OpenType fonts, and be prepared for any design task the
future holds.
