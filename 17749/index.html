That’s [Stephen Farrell](http://web.archive.org/web/20060918142729/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/browsebydesigner2.php?u_id=622)’s [Missive](http://web.archive.org/web/20060918142729/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=713), one of the original hybrid faces that made everyone notice [Carlos Segura](http://web.archive.org/wiki/Carlos%20Segura)’s [T-26](http://web.archive.org/web/20060918142729/http:/www.t26.com/fonts.php) when it emerged on the type market in the early nineties, the heydays of [grunge](http://web.archive.org/wiki/grunge) typography.
Anonymous @ 6 February, 2006 - 3:39pm
It’s funny how Stephen made Missive — a mash-up of what looks like Sabon and News Gothic — evolve into [Entropy](http://web.archive.org/web/20060918142729/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=710) by welding the Missive caps with Stymie, and then eventually end up with [Osprey](http://web.archive.org/web/20060918142729/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=714) by smashing it all into Park Avenue.
