Consider the work of [Alan Fletcher](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821130131/http:/www.typophile.com/wiki/Alan%252BFletcher). Although, perhaps, I’d say his was more wit than comedy. [The Art of Looking Sideways](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821130131/http:/www.amazon.com/Art-Looking-Sideways-Alan-Fletcher/dp/0714834491/ref=pd_bbs_sr_1?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1207678867&sr=8-1) by Alan Fletcher [Alan Fletcher: Picturing and Poeting](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821130131/http:/www.amazon.com/Alan-Fletcher-Picturing-Poeting/dp/0714847127/ref=pd_bbs_sr_2?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1207678867&sr=8-2) by Alan Fletcher [Beware Wet Paint](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821130131/http:/www.amazon.com/Beware-Wet-Paint-Alan-Fletcher/dp/0714843784/ref=pd_bbs_sr_3?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1207678867&sr=8-3) by Alan Fletcher Also consider [A Smile in the Mind](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821130131/http:/www.amazon.com/Smile-Mind-Beryl-McAlhone/dp/0714838128/ref=pd_bbs_4?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1207678867&sr=8-4) by Beryl McAlhone. This book is all about wit and humor in design. It does contain some work by Mr. Fletcher.
