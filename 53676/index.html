Anonymous @ 19 Jan 2009 — 4:51am
It would be worth checking out [Collinge & Clark](http://web.archive.org/web/20141027182035/http:/www.allinlondon.co.uk/directory/1277/3340.php) in Bloomsbury. They have an excellent selection of old specimens and type books. They're usually closed on weekends, but their schedule is a bit erratic, so you never know. And it's a shame you won't be in town the following weekend, when the [Ephemera Society](http://web.archive.org/web/20141027182035/http:/ephemera-society.org.uk/) has one of their big fairs. Those are an absolute treasure trove!

Anonymous @ 19 Jan 2009 — 6:50am
Foyles are good for current publications and often have recently out-of-print titles but won't have anything especially rare. They are a very big store with well-stocked departments on most subjects, and a very nice neon sign which I think dates from the 1930s. [Maggs](http://web.archive.org/web/20141027182035/http:/www.maggs.com/) are a specialist antiquarian dealer. Sadly I don't think Zwemmers have any stores left in London.
