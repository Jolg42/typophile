The CS suite has several backdoors to get fonts to work. Put the files in C:\Program Files\Common Files\Adobe\Fonts and they should be visible in all CS programs. Put them in C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe InDesign CS3\Fonts (or whereever your InDesign resides) and they'll only be visible in CS3. Similar for Illustrator and Photoshop. <cite>I’m student, so i ain’t got any software licences.</cite> You are mistaken. A student license is also perfectly valid: [http://www.adobe.com/aboutadobe/openoptions/student.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20111208015645/http:/www.adobe.com/aboutadobe/openoptions/student.html)
