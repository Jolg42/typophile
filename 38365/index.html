Anonymous @ 25 Oct 2007 — 8:54am
Couple of points: • The pen angles on the _d_ and _q_ are different from the rest of the font; did you just mirror the _p_? • The _k_ is just odd but almost works.&nbsp; (What does it look like in the source text?) • The _s_ is too light in the main stroke of the letter. • The _t_ is very awkward; note the height of the cross-bar and the rounding-off of the bottom of the stem in the original. • Where the original _o_ has full-width stroke at either end of the stroke, yours just looks like somebody bit into the letter. • The _v_ is a bit too wide, the _y_ and the right half of the _w_ much more so.&nbsp; (Yes, I see that they're all the same width; they shouldn't be.) On the other hand… • Good job on replacing the teardrop dot on the _i_, though the height and shape could be improved.&nbsp; (Maybe a slightly curve-y teardrop, almost a comma, a bit higher up? • Love the _m_.&nbsp; This font needs more such flourishes—why'd you drop the dangling bit on the _r_? —Joel
