The concave style in \_MAY THE BIRD...\_ may not have made it into the digital era. The closest digital for the bottom three quarters could be Fredericksburg NF, an interpretation of the 19th century design called "Teutonic" ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150326195451im_/http:/typophile.com/files/FredericksburgNFAsMaySM_5548.jpg) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/fredericksburg-wbw/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150326195451/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/fredericksburg-wbw/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/fredericksburg-wbw/") The top quarter is a "wanted poster" reverse contrast style as shown in many "wild west fonts" such as Buffalo Western ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150326195451im_/http:/typophile.com/files/BuffaloWesternasMAY_4327.jpg) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/kustomtype/buffalo-western/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150326195451/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/kustomtype/buffalo-western/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/kustomtype/buffalo-western/") Don

'MAY THE BIRD...': completely new to me this one; a cross-genre French Clarendon/Italienne x Tuscan Concave; couldn't find anything similar; best option would be what Don said: start with 'Teutonic' (another version [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150326195451/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/teutonic)) and add large fat top serifs; you could also do it the other way round and starting from a Tuscan Concave Slab like [Number 154](http://web.archive.org/web/20150326195451/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/woodentypefonts/number-154) reworking all bottoms; 'LITTLE JIMMY DICKENS': interesting digital alternative with Sergiy Tkachenko's [Sports World](http://web.archive.org/web/20150326195451/http:/www.behance.net/gallery/3373761/Sports-World-(Typeface)) of 4th February;
