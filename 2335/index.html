35 New Fonts from Mark Simonson Studio

Mark Simonson Studio has just released 35 new retail fonts:

 Blakely Bold and Heavy

 Goldenbook Light, Regular, and Heavy

 Metallophile Sp 8 Light and Light Italic

 Refrigerator Light and Heavy

 Changeling Light, Regular, Bold, Stencil, and Inline

 Sanctuary Regular and Bold

 Sharktooth Bold and Heavy

 Felt Tip Roman Bold and Heavy

 Felt Tip Woman Regular and Bold

 Raster Gothic Condensed Regular and Bold (12 fonts total)

 Raster Bank

I have been working on these fonts for the last two years and am excited (and relieved!) to finally be able to make them available to the public.

All of these fonts are available immediately from MyFonts.com:

http://www.myfonts.com/foundry/marksimonson/

Sample showings and more information can be found at my site:

http://www.ms-studio.com
