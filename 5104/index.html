[Kaufmann Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224136/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/kaufmann/) comes to mind immediately. As does Nick Shinn's [Handsome Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224136/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=SHT0000008) Letraset's [Santa Fe](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224136/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=LFT1058400) is very clean. And Typodelic's [Natural Script Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224136/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typadelic/natural-script/bold/) isn't too bad. Neil Summerour's [Luce](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224136/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=UMT0000004) probably isn't as bold as you need, but it's definitely monolinear. [Gilles Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516224136/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/gillies-gothic/) is probably a bit too displayish.
