!Disc Inferno BASIC Crit

I’ve finally released the free (as in beer) version of !Disc Inferno, despite Luc crediting me for it months ago. This features the first 200 of the 1300 glyphs or so, meaning no international scripts, swatches or other such goodies. I’ve incorporated the feed back from mentioning this previously, but now you can look at a fuller map, or download and play with it.

Particularly, I’m interested in people’s impressions on the relationship of x-height to overall size. However, I look forwards to your thoughts and comments in general, as I work on to release the very expanded version in 1Q07.

DL available @:

Anonymous @ 28 Nov 2006 — 3:43pm
Hmmm, I suppose I wouldn't be posting it here after release if I wasn't dissatisfied in some way, would I? I think it is this: I enjoy not only the connotation, but I also enjoy the details, when I look at an entire word. However, any more than one word, and the legibility seems to drop. A lot. Why, if we read by word shape? Should the leading or the size of the space be altered? Is there a way to get a flow between words that is as fun as that between letters? I guess what I am looking for is a critique of metrics, not glyphs. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417130550im_/http:/typophile.com/files/hownowbrowncow_6389.jpg) [Choz Cunningham](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417130550/http:/www.chozcunningham.com/) [!Exclamachine Type Foundry](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417130550/http:/www.exclamchine.com/) [The Snark](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417130550/http:/www.exclamachine.com/snark)

I don't know about getting rid of the diagonals, the only letters that don't have to have them are V/W. The X and the D needed them for legibility.
