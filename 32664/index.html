Thanks for linking to that article, Vincent. That should clarify the MetaPlus vs. Meta. A correction: Lining and Tabular figures are not the same thing. [Lining Figures](http://web.archive.org/web/20100504081729/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Lining%2520Figures) actually means the numbers line up horizontally. You could call them "all caps" or "titling" figures. Lining figs can be proportionally spaced. TF stands for [Tabular Figures](http://web.archive.org/web/20100504081729/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Tabular%2520Figures) which means they all have same width for tabluar setting. I have never heard of Meta LL.
