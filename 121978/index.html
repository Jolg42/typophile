Anonymous @ 13 Dec 2014 — 8:17am
Too many points is the problem with making any of these elaborate ornamented fonts. About half a decade ago I took a stab at making a well engineered version of Lettres Ornées with the minimum number of points. I got as far as doing part of the letter \_E\_ ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150413234109im_/http:/typophile.com/files/LettresOrn%C3%A9es1_6253.jpg) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150413234109im_/http:/typophile.com/files/LettresOrn%C3%A9es3_6382.jpg) At the rate I was going it would have taken an incredible amount of time to do the full alphabet! So I threw in the towel. A project for somebody with lots of time on their hands! Which probably explains why we are stuck with mega-point digital versions that sometimes seize up during viewing and fail in printing. Don
