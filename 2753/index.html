When i was student i've find a cool method. You print your text in horizontal mirror on a rhodoid paper. As the ink haven't time to dry, you report it on a sheet. Use an ink jet printer and the result will be great.
Anonymous @ Mon, 2004-01-19 15:21
Bender, I use a similar technique a lot at work: I print the mirror image with the inkjet on the wrong (glossy) side of projection film and transfer while still wet to a blank piece of paper. Experiment a little with size and paper quality and you'll get very interesting stamp effects.
