One way of strengthening PDF security is to use Type-\>Create Outlines or similar. A cracker can still blow away time re-importing them into a font editor and adding the metrics on his own, but the amount of time needed to reconstruct such a font adequately will usually be worth more in an adequately skilled person's dollars than the cost of simply buying a license. The downside of Create Outlines is that there is no hinting. Supposedly the newer versions of Acrobat will anti-alias line art. I'll try making some comparisons this weekend or so.
