Something between [Kabel](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/kabel) and [Geometric 415](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/geometric-415/)/ [Metro #2](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/metro-2/)/ [DH Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dsgnhaus/dhsans/)/ [Grosse Pointe Metro](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/grouptype/grosse-pointe-metro/)/ [Examiner](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/nicksfonts/examiner-nf/). For the rest, [Kaleidoscope Magazine](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/typophile.com/node/94337#comment-514276) is using a font called [AMB Kabel Ten](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/kaleidoscope-press.com/) possibly by [OK-RM](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.ok-rm.co.uk/#/project/kaleidoscope-13/), [Snowdon Blue](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/typophile.com/node/97891#comment-529470) [Snowdon](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.snowdon-review.com/#/review-overview/) by [Matthew Fenton for OK-RM](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.ok-rm.co.uk/#/project/snowdon-review-2/) and [Verities Magazine](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/WhatTheFont/forum/case/513425) Scotland by [Haakon Spencer](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.haakonspencer.com/). You may like also [Euclid](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/swisstypefaces.com/fonts/euclid-bp/all-styles), [Circe](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/circe), [TJ Evolette](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.youworkforthem.com/product.php?sku=T1795) and [Diamonds](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416005713/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/hvdfonts/diamonds)
