Interesting idea, a swirly font that combines a swirl with a ball terminal at one end and a plain swirl at the other end. Here are two fonts that give you your choice of these two effects. Janda Hide And Seek [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/kimberly-geswein/janda-hide-and-seek/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701153728/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/kimberly-geswein/janda-hide-and-seek/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/kimberly-geswein/janda-hide-and-seek/") and Emploi [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/emploi/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701153728/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/emploi/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/paratype/emploi/") Emploi might be more to your taste than Janda Hide And Seek. If you can tinker with fonts Emploi could be edited to give it balls where you want them. Don
