David, I think you have to decide between making all the glyphs the same width or the same stem width. The E look very wide and the V and W look tiny and lost. I am just trying to figure a way for glyphs to differentiate more. You have tried a very tough problem but when you solve it, the result will look quite simple. Keep at it.
