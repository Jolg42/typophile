It looks like all text (big text, small text, numbers) are set in **[DIN Schablonierschrift](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317114833/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Dafont-1&ffam=DIN%20Schablonierschrift%20-%20Regular&fid=e1489c73438fc46f0c3ddbca3e7a4905&fsize=48&text=SKOOL%20LUV%20AFFAIR%20COMEBACK%20TRAILER%20182%3A32%3A2&wrap=2)** free font by Marian Steinbach If you look closely at the small text you can easily see the stencil cuts on letters /A & /M
