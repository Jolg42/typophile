Caslon Antique is a heavily used old 'veteran' . There are plenty of less frequently used distressed old typefaces. [Sherwood Types at P22](http://web.archive.org/web/20110124001529/http:/www.p22.com/sherwoodtype/index.html) has a very nice collection of fonts based on old types that might give you a less familiar, but authentic look. You can also search on 'Antique' at sites that allow keyword searches, and find some other ideas. - Mike Yanega
