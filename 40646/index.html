James Moseley was kind enough to set me straight on both of these questions this evening. He explained that you can see the Van Dijck type by looking in “Type specimen facsimiles” edited by John Dreyfus and published in London in 1963. Also, he explained that the small and famous early Elzevir books contain Garamond and Granjon made fonts. Thanks James!
