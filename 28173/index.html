!the troubles

!Exclamachine Type Foundry releases a new disply font today. A horror/display typeface, !the troubles includes the basic ASCII character set, as well a proposed Irony Mark as a standard OpenType ligature. The narrow, deformed, broadly spaced letters adds an eerie vibe to any text. http://www.exclamachine.com

To include the Irony Mark at the end of a sentence, simply type a tilde immediately after a period, before a space, in any OpenType aware application. (This mimics the I.M. convention of affixing a tilde to facetious statements.)
