Should be their own corporate typeface called 'MSC Angular', a bespoke typeface designed by [Manuel Schibli](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/manuelschibli.com/). Alternatives: [VUCA Grotesque](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/www.hugohoppmann.com/vuca-grotesque), [Fox Grotesque](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/tipografiaramis/fox-grotesque), [Novelty](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/arstype.angusrshamal.com/a-new-free-font-ars-novelty), [Futura Classic](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/wiescherdesign/futura-classic), [Dessau Zukunft](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fountain/dessau_pro/ot_ps), [Siberian](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/omtype/siberian), [Programme](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/www.optimo.ch/typefaces_Programme.html), [Euclid Flex](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420/http:/www.swisstypefaces.com/fonts/euclid-flex) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140829193420im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Untitled-1_4951.png)
