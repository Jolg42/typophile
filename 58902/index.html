Sounds like you are looking for a “single storey” a. [Century Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20090614065601/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/century-gothic/%E2%80%9D) is very similar to avant garde. [Futura](http://web.archive.org/web/20090614065601/http:/www.linotype.com/472/futura-family.html%E2%80%9D) is a good font to invest in, in my opinion. I have found it very useful over the years. You could also try something with a slab serif, like [Geometric Slab 703](http://web.archive.org/web/20090614065601/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/geometric-slabserif-703/%E2%80%9D) [Chalet](http://web.archive.org/web/20090614065601/http:/www.houseind.com/fonts/collections/chalet%E2%80%9D) from House Industries has a retro-modern feel to it.
