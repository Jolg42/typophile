I’d go with a humanist sans, which combines the suggestion of modernity/efficiency of a sans with the readable proportions of a roman. There’s been an explosion in the last 5-10 years, as you may know. A few that I’ve used or have caught my eye recently Freight Sans [http://www.fontshop.com/search/?q=freight+sans&x=0&y=0](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202192340/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=freight+sans&x=0&y=0 "http://www.fontshop.com/search/?q=freight+sans&x=0&y=0") Sans Priva Pro [http://typetrust.com/fonts/font.php?id=MTk0](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202192340/http:/typetrust.com/fonts/font.php?id=MTk0 "http://typetrust.com/fonts/font.php?id=MTk0") FF Milo [http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontfont/ff\_milo\_text/](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202192340/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontfont/ff_milo_text/ "http://www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontfont/ff\_milo\_text/")
