Chauncey H. Griffith

Indices : Designers : Chauncey H. Griffith

1879-1956

Vice-President of Typographic Development for the Mergenthaler Linotype Company of New York.

William Addison Dwiggins and Rudolph Ruzicka worked for him.

Associated Fonts:
Ionic (1925)
Poster Bodoni (1929)
Excelsior (1931)
Paragon (1935)
Opticon (1935-6)
Janson (1937)
Bell Gothic (1938)
Corona (1941)
Monticello (1946)
