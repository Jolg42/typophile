Anonymous @ 9 Dec 2007 — 10:32pm
If you're making a numbered list, tabular figures are necessary--whether oldstyle or lining. If you have a column of phone numbers (say on a business card--even if only two numbers), tabular figures make it so much neater. Price lists, dates, contents pages, recipes, etc. Wherever one or more numbers appears flush above another, then you should use tabular figures. And in those situations, why not have the option of old style figures?
