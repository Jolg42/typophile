Can't recognize this one and doubt actually it could a font, more a Frankenstein with letters borrowed to some classic geometric sans: /o and /v from [Futura](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804171333/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/futura), /s "à-la- [Avant-Garde Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804171333/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/avant+garde+gothic)", /a as stated to Bauhaus. Many freebies (an endless list actually) are in the same vein too. Having a look to [Techno \> Sci-fi](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804171333/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=303), [Techno \> Various](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804171333/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=304) and [Basic \> Sans serif](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804171333/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=501) sections at DaFont could be helpful to spot either an alternative or a match. Some alternatives to play with: [Sofia](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804171333/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mostardesign/sofia-pro), [Dessau](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804171333/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fountain/dessau_pro)
