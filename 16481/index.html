Fontlab will let you import Illustrator files. There are very specific steps to take. You might do a search on google to find them. Alternatively, yes, you could get somebody to convert your font for you. Also, Fontlab will support multiple kinds of grids - but is it a bitmap font you want to make? Maybe you should post some images of what you have so you can get better advice.

Mads, when working in Illustrator and FontLab, it’s better work in points rather than mm because 1 pt in Illustrator = 1 font unit in FontLab. Please read [http://groups.msn.com/fontlab/tipsandtricks.msnw?action=get\_message&mvie...](http://web.archive.org/web/20081009105816/http:/groups.msn.com/fontlab/tipsandtricks.msnw?action=get_message&mview=0&ID_Message=2675 "http://groups.msn.com/fontlab/tipsandtricks.msnw?action=get\_message&mview=0&ID\_Message=2675") You can set up any grid subdivisions in FontLab you want.
