Then it _is_ [Akzidenz Grotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20080130014248/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Akzidenz%252BGrotesk). Possibly a pre-digital version. Take into account that this family used to be quite patchworky, and still – even after GGL’s recut(s) and digitization – is far from being as systematical as Univers etc. From the Wiki: <cite>The original series remained quite divers, individual weights showing not much resemblance but in name. […] That only changed when they cut Series 57, and then Series 58, named for the years of release. These had some sizes (but not all) recut under the direction of <a href="http://web.archive.org/web/20080130014248/http:/typophile.com/wiki/G%2525C3%2525BCnter%252BGerhard%252BLange" class="wiki-create">Günter Gerhard Lange</a>, who was their (freelance) artistic director at the time.</cite>
