I think the most important thing -at least concerning Latin caps- is that there are two styles of proportions/widths: 1) The classical style, which has a lot of divergence; like look at the “B” versus the “D” in Trajan. 2) The modern style, where the widths converge a lot; see Spiekermann’s stuff. As always though there are exceptions, such as Nick Shinn’s Richler. hhp
