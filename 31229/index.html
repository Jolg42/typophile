I’d go for something with proper [small capitals](http://web.archive.org/web/20070911200620/http:/typophile.com/wiki/small%252Bcapitals)… faking it is never pretty. It seems to be a [Trajan](http://web.archive.org/web/20070911200620/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/trajan/) style you’re after. [Trajan Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20070911200620/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=ADT0001724#characters) seems to have small caps. You might also like [Minerva Display _Antiqua_](http://web.archive.org/web/20070911200620/http:/www.t26.com/fonts/display.php?f_id=218&dw=1421) by [Gabor Kothay](http://web.archive.org/web/20070911200620/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Gabor%252BKothay) (which doesn’t seem to have small caps unfortunately).
