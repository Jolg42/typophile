Up until now the policy has been that documents are upward compatible (eg you can open a CS3-doc in CS4), but NOT downwards (eg you can not directly open a CS4-doc in CS3, but have to export a file in the IDX-format to open it in the lower version, one step at a time — from CS4 to CS3 works, but not to CS2 — you have to re-export in CS3). Chances are that the new version works likewise. It has to do with new features being introduced in new versions that will not translate well to the older ones. I may be not correct, but this was what I understood from the demo I attended. . . . Bert Vanderveen BNO
