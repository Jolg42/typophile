It’s possible it’s custom, it’s also possible it’s a script with alternatives. I think it’s also possible it’s a few fonts and some modifications thrown together. “Buck” and “tate” look very similar to [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itfmecanorma/script-bold/](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907044655/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itfmecanorma/script-bold/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itfmecanorma/script-bold/") Script Bold. The S might be free hand, or another brush or marker script all together (it does look familiar). then if you look at dafont, there’s Marcelle which could have been the inspiration (it’s clearly a modified Script Bold.) [http://www.dafont.com/marcelle.font?nb\_ppp=50&psize=l&text=Buck+State](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907044655/http:/www.dafont.com/marcelle.font?nb_ppp=50&psize=l&text=Buck+State "http://www.dafont.com/marcelle.font?nb\_ppp=50&psize=l&text=Buck+State")
