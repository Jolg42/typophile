By Wayne J. Stettler, 1965 for VGC. There is a font out there called GT-Stetler (yes, one t) that is only the caps of Stettler. Not much help, but that is the only digitization I'm aware of.
Anonymous @ 16 Jun 2014 — 5:09am
If you are looking for substitutes you could use Identifont for Optima [http://www.identifont.com/find?font=optima&q=Go](http://web.archive.org/web/20140725021438/http:/www.identifont.com/find?font=optima&q=Go "http://www.identifont.com/find?font=optima&q=Go") and go through the similar fonts. Intersting ones include Cyrus Highsmith's Amira, Thierry Puyfoulhoux's Alinea Incise, José Mendoza y Almeida's Pascal, Gábor Kóthay's Minerva Modern, and Hermann Zapf's Optima aka Classico or Zapf Humanist 601.
