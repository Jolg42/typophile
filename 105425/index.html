Chanel: [already discussed many times](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fwww.google.com/search%3Fhl%3Den%2526source%3Dhp%2526q%3Dsite%253Atypophile.com%252Fnode%2Bchanel%2Blogo) and considered as "custom". Similar: [Engravers Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/engravers+gothic/)/ [Blair](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/blair/)/ [Sackers Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/sackers+gothic/) Tom Ford: [Helvetica Neue](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/neue-helvetica) Bold but this is not the [official logo](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/www.tomford.com/) which is also custom, somehow similar to [Proxima Nova](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/marksimonson/proxima-nova) and [Avenir](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/avenir). You'll find more suggestions for alternatives in the [previous discussions](http://web.archive.org/web/20140724111140/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fwww.google.com/search%3Fhl%3Den%2526source%3Dhp%2526q%3Dsite%253Atypophile.com%2B%22tom%2Bford%22%2Blogo).
