One of the best-known superfamilies is of course Luc(as) de Groot’s <cite>Thesis,</cite> currently including the following members: – [TheSans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesans/thesans/) ( [Hair](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesans/thesans-hair/)/ [Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesans/thesans-condensed/)) – [TheSansMono](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesansmono/thesansmono/) ( [SemiCondensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesansmono/thesansmono-semicondensed/)/ [Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesansmono/thesansmono-condensed/)/ [Typewriter](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesansmono/thesans-typewriter/)) – [TheSansArabic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/thesansarabic/thesansarabic/overview/) – [TheMix](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/themix/themix/) ( [Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/themix/themix-condensed/)) – [TheMixMono](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/themixmono/themixmono/) – [TheMixArabic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/themixarabic/themixarabic/overview/) – [TheSerif](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/theserif/theserif/) – [TheAntiquaB](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/theantiqua/theantiquab/)/ [TheAntiquaSun](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415050143/http:/www.lucasfonts.com/fonts/theantiqua/theantiqua-sun/) There’s a lot of potential for expansion, obviously. Can’t wait to see the release of TheAntiquaSun Mono SemiCondensed Hair.
