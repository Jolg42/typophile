The [head](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011235646/http:/www.microsoft.com/typography/otspec/head.htm) table. xMax is the topmost extremum found in all glyphs, usually calculated automatically e.g. by FLS5. As to the more general question: Like every testing tool, FontValidator tests what its programmers told it to test. Regard results as hints -- in the end it is _you_ who decides if this or that is an error or not.
