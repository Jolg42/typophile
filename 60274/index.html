In general you're looking for a Venetian font: Adobe's Jenson Pro is one, Nick Shinn's Goodchild is another. The field is wide... do you want "authentic look" or "inspired by"? Centaur is a great one--a redesign of Nicolas Jenson's work, and is considered a Venetian Renaissance font. Designed (if I remember correctly) in the 1920s by Bruce Rogers. Does great in display (as the header for the Metropolitan Museum of Modern Art) as well as in print (for a long time Rogers used it exclusively himself on small, fine art books).
Anonymous @
If you want a really distinctive look check out P22’s revival of Benton’s [Cloister Oldstyle](http://web.archive.org/web/20120103163014/http:/p22.com/lanston/cloister.html).
