You can find it as: **Eremitage** by Brendel Informatik (1994) **Zapotec** by NovelFonts (1994) **Watford DB** or **Watford** by SoftMaker (1995) but you should be extra careful on obtaining a license to use it in a project, as all of the above companies are well known for their font clones/forgeries. I suspect that all above are clones of the original, if somebody knows more about it please enlighten us. ------------------ I found them using "Find my Font": [http://www.findmyfont.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023203640/http:/www.findmyfont.com/ "http://www.findmyfont.com")
