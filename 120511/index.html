Anonymous @ 21 Oct 2014 — 10:29am

Anonymous @ 21 Oct 2014 — 12:45pm
That makes sense. I can see the solution for /m, but for the other characters I don't think such a solution would work. M/N/W/AE/OE/&/@/Ø are particularly too dark. For OE I would simply increase the counter a bit. For Ø perhaps the slash within the counter should be lighter than outside it. I would prefer to remove the slash from the counter completely, but given the point size at which the typeface seems optimal, I don't think the slash will be obvious enough if you remove the part within the counter. It seems quite impossible to fix all the dark spots using a pixel-based solution. But I guess since the design is inspired by pixel typefaces, it may not be necessary to remove all dark spots.

Anonymous @ 22 Oct 2014 — 1:35pm
If you widen all letters enough, indeed the dark spots should mostly disappear, but I think you will have to wide the characters too much to remove the dark spots completely. If you widen the characters just a little, characters like /m will still look relatively too dark even though on an individual basis you've lightened the /m. It's worth a try though. Your characters are quite condensed, so you do have some space to work with.
