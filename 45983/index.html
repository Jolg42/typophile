Type ID: exhibit signage @ Art Institute of Chicago

This beautiful typeface was used in the signage & accompanying brochure for the exhibition “Figuration in Contemporary Design” at the Art Institute of Chicago. Photography was sternly discouraged, but an understanding security guard allowed me a couple furtive shots of the typography. Any help identifying this lovely font? Thanks very much!

This is a new one to me. The ’s’ is very odd, with two kinds of serifs. Swash Romans are pretty rare too, and none that I know of match this. The closest I can think of is Thierry Puyfoulhoux’s large and varied [Classica](http://web.archive.org/web/20080609231630/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/presencetypo/classica/) family which includes a Gallic version with the Venetian style e, and the Prestige subfamily that has all kinds of swashes and ligatures. There is a swash W like the one shown, only it is reversed. This might be another time when writing to the Museum might get an answer. - Mike Yanega
