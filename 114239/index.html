The 1923 _Manual of Linotype Typography_ featured early 20th century historicist design alluding to speciﬁc earlier eras, demonstrating how typeface, ornament and layout work together to create a cohesive style. Download the PDF: [http://vitor.io/its-a-gift-never-lend-books#mergenthaler](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416032000/http:/vitor.io/its-a-gift-never-lend-books#mergenthaler "http://vitor.io/its-a-gift-never-lend-books#mergenthaler") Better yet, buy your own copy for less than $100.
