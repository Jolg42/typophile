[FutureBrand](http://web.archive.org/web/20150130165201/http:/typophile.com/wiki/FutureBrand) did the UPS rebranding a couple of years ago. Featured on their site is the casestudy claiming that " a custom typeface was develloped", when in fact, even here on Typophile, a lot of us noticed that it is merely, if at all, an adaptation of the ubiquous FF Dax. So I guess it is safe to suggest you use this typeface if you can't get the "UPS Sans" corporate typeface.
