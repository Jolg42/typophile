This is what is often referred to as psychadelic type. It's going to be hand-drawn in order to be wrapping in this style. [Arnold Boecklin](http://web.archive.org/web/20141016154548/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/arnold-boecklin/) is probably the most well known psychadelic style typeface.
