Anonymous @ 14 Aug 2008 — 12:00am
\> subtle curves into the slabs These are called "brackets". All the typefaces you list are unbracketed slabs. The flat bottomed 'a' is pretty unusual so I don't think you'll find a match for that. Best to start with something close and modify it. The closest thing I can summon is [Amasis](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023164420/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/monotype/amasis_std_bold/?sample_text=Langes&sample_size=96).
Anonymous @ 14 Aug 2008 — 12:08am
Thanks, I knew there was a proper term for the slabs, but it just escaped me. But, yes, brackets are exactly what I'm looking for.
