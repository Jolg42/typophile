We get this with our Sunday paper. Looks like most of the heads are set in various weights of [Knockout](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414124256/http:/www.typography.com/catalog/knockout/series_c.html). The text is mostly [Farnham](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414124256/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/Farnham) and (I think) News Gothic. Could be Trade Gothic, or even [Benton Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414124256/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/BentonSans). In fact, the latter seems more likely given the other fonts they use. I'm not sure if this is always true, but the latest issue features [Mrs. Eaves](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414124256/http:/emigre.com/EF.php?fid=109) for display type on the cover and the features. Oh, and I see a bit of [Gotham](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414124256/http:/www.typography.com/catalog/gotham/index.html) thrown in here and there.
