Indie Fonts 3

Fresh from the printers, we have the brand new Indie Fonts 3 books. This is the exclusive hardcover version. This volume features 20 foundries with over 400 pages of type specimens, plus a bonus CD with 53 fonts!

Like the previous two volumes, Indie Fonts 3 showcases some of the best independent type designers and foundries from around the world. There isn’t a better way to preview type designs than in print and this book has over 400 pages of fonts from digital foundries.

Indie Fonts 3 includes specimens and fonts from:
Baseline Fonts, Blue Vinyl Fonts, Device Fonts, E-phemera Fonts, Font Brothers, Handselecta, Jeremy Tankard Typography, Kenn Munk, Letterhead Fonts, Pizzadude, Positype, Process Type Foundry, Rimmer Type Foundry, Shinntype, SparkyType, Suitcase Type Foundry, Suomi Type Foundry, Village, Virus Fonts & Wilton Foundry.

This book was assembled by the original editors of the first two Indie Fonts, but published by Rockport Publishers in a flex-bound cover. The flexbound version will be available everywhere design books are sold. For those completists who prefer all 3 volumes in hardcover, we are the exclusive outlet for the limited hardcover version. Both editions contain the same pages, but the hardcover is…well, hardcover with a dust jacket. As an introductory special, we are offering Indie Fonts 3 at $35 plus shipping (list price $39.95) until February 15th.

As a special offer we are bundling Indie Fonts 2 & 3 together for the very special price of $60.00 (prices do not include shipping).
