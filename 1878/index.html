The typeface (I wont say font, because to most of us that means a digital typeface) has some interesting features, and particularly the 'g' (which again looks a lot like Hollandse Medieval). Hollandse Medieval is available as a single-font display from Monotype (Red Rooster's Erasmus is very similar), but for text, the closest to what you want is Dutch Medieval, a four-font text family from Canada Type Fonts available at MyFonts in OpenType (with oldstyle figures and keystroke-linked fonts) for a good price.
