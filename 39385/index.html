For a 1930s sans that's more art deco than modernist, consider Mark Simonson's Mostra, [http://www.ms-studio.com/FontSales/mostra.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20111217035824/http:/www.ms-studio.com/FontSales/mostra.html "http://www.ms-studio.com/FontSales/mostra.html") or House Industries' Neutra. For a "serif movie title lettering" font, maybe something from Letterhead Fonts or Nick's Fonts. You may be interested in Ephemera foundry--in Indie Fonts 3.
