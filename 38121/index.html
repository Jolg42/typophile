Seeking Castaway complement

Hello, I am searching out ideas for a print project. I am looking for a text font to use with the (House) font Castaway (which I am using for headers). This is a fun eatery guide. Target is vegetarians. Needs to complement whimsical illustrations and be easily read at 10 or 11 pts. Something on the lines of Cafeteria, but not Cafeteria as it is too condensed. Something playful, but no crazy hand lettering. Suggestions greatly appreciated.

Thanks in advance!

Not for long text, but [MVB Grenadine](http://web.archive.org/web/20071017203254/http:/www.fontshop.com/search/?q=grenadine) or [MVB Pedestria](http://web.archive.org/web/20071017203254/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/mvb_fonts/mvb_pedestria/) might be just the thing. Otherwise, [FF Sari](http://web.archive.org/web/20071017203254/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_sari_1/) would complement Castaway well and can be set in long paragraphs.
