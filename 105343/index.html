I suspect this is sign lettering in a style sometimes called flareserif rather than a specific font. For similar fonts take a look at [Flange BQ](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317100337/http:/www.identifont.com/list?3+Flange%20BQ+0+HCJ+1+HF9+2+K2+2+GIA+2+EPZ+2+4CZ+2+CAX+2+3DV+2+CMN+2+41C+2+43T+2+EJ+2+26V+2+8GG+4+1W81+4+2VA+4+HE5+4+5GU+4+6CF+4+6DH+4+LX+4+N1U+5+OX3+5+32I+5+U5+5+226+5+F8+5+23M+5+22VP+5+GUO+5) and similar fonts in the link.
