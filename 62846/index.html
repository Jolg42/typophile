Greater Albion Releases Paveline

We have just released Paveline on Myfonts.com.

Paveline is a punctuated script; by which we mean it has the look and character of handwriting, but the glyphs are discrete entities to aid legibility. It is actually based on a moderately stylized adaptation of our chief designer’s handwriting. Use it to give a handwritten touch to your work. Paveline works well as small handwriting or large scale poster captions. You can see some more samples of Paveline in action on our blog.
