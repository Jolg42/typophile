Complementary fonts for a Science Fiction/Western novel

 I’m looking for fonts to go with the project mentioned in this thread: a Firefly-related book. The two genres for the book are science fiction and Western, and I’m wondering if there are fonts with a ‘feel’ in both directions.
 Don’t need old-style figures, but I do need book and titling weights, an italic suitable for long runs of text, and a complementary alternate form—a matching sans-serif, perhaps, or at least with a similar x-height and color, but readily distinguishable from the main text. I can fake small capitals if need be. The Gentium Book/Fontin combo I’ve tried here doesn’t work too well.

 Also, is the Chinese font I’ve used (MS MingLiU) the best choice, or can someone recommend a better Chinese titling font/weight? (I.e., a more calligraphic font, still in Song style.)

I've always liked Western & Sci-Fi fonts, but trying to find both was certainly a challenge! I don't know if it's too late, but here are some ideas. Some sans-serif faces: [MVB Solano Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421204433/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mvbfonts/solano-gothic/bold) [Social Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421204433/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/social-gothic) And some slab-serif: [Dispatch from Font Bureau](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421204433/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/dispatch) [Anubis by DSType](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421204433/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dstype/anubis) [BF Corpa Serif](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421204433/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/brassfonts/corpa-serif) (or Gothic) [LTC Squareface](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421204433/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/lanston/ltc-square-face)
