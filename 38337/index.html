Love that town. Definitely hit the [Art Institute](http://web.archive.org/web/20080706223216/http:/www.artic.edu/aic/index.php). Tremendous Impressionist/Post-Impressionist collection. There’s some great architecture all around too. You may want to check out a walking tour with the [Chicago Architecture Foundation](http://web.archive.org/web/20080706223216/http:/www.architecture.org/). Oh and if you’re into funny, gotta catch a mainstage show at [Second City](http://web.archive.org/web/20080706223216/http:/www.secondcity.com/?id=theatres/chicago/mainstage).
