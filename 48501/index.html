FF Unit is a fine face for signage — compact but very open and legible. [FF Unit Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20081122111730/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_unit_complete_collection_pro/) contains all Central European character sets and should support the languages you need, including Bosnian, Croatian, Slovenian, and Sorbian. See the [specimen PDF](http://web.archive.org/web/20081122111730/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/download.php?docid=289).
