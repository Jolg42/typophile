I suppose you mean the "Infinity" script at bottom right? It is published as **[Carpenter Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20131101122336/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/grouptype/carpenter/)** by James West (GroupType, 1993-06), also published as **[Carpenter ICG](http://web.archive.org/web/20131101122336/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Image-Club-Graphics&ffam=Carpenter%20ICG%20-%20Regular&fid=ed5f15f3d21a6471339c40baed679c07&fsize=60&text=Infinity&fit=1)** by Image Club Graphics and digitized as **Carpenter Script** by Dan X. Solo. (1999, Dover Publications CD) It's also available as **OPTICarmella HandScript** (Castcraft Software, Inc. 1990-1991) and cloned under different names by several well-known font forgery companies like NovelFonts, FontBank & SoftMaker. I don't know what "sweet paper" is, but some contrast & perspective correction might help somebody else to find out: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20131101122336im_/http:/typophile.com/files/sweet-paper-01_5594.jpg)
