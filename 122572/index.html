Agree with Don, custom lettering. 2 options for me: - Don's approach: mash-up fonts, a basic sans and a script; for instance, the sans could be [Buttacup](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.dafont.com/buttacup.font) and the script [Elan](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/elan) (Don's suggestion), [Lightnin](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/lightnin), [Fontella](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/fontella), [Gillies Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/gillies-gothic), [Kaufmann](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/kaufmann), [Penmanshift](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/penmanshift) (nice this one), [Coquette](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/marksimonson/coquette), [Futura Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/futura-script), [Maison](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/ht-maison), [Varga](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/varga) (nice too), [Airstream](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.dafont.com/airstream.font) - choose an appropriate sans ( [Buttacup](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.dafont.com/buttacup.font)) and customize letters. I like and even if it's different (flare serif) [Highway](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/youngjerks.com/#/highway-typeface) by Dan Cassaro. Some ready-to-go options: [Selaive](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/latinotype/selaive), [Merced](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/latinotype/merced), [Brownstone Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/sudtipos/brownstone-sans), [Myra Caps](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fwww.behance.net/gallery/6367355/Myra-Caps-%28Typeface%29), [Kylie](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fwww.behance.net/gallery/2721389/Kylie-%28Typeface%29), [Solomon](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/font-fabric/solomon), [Gist](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/yellow-design/gist) (slab and inline... ouch... but same vein), [Peacock](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/justanotherfoundry/peacock) Maybe a dead-end but you could have a look to [Nexa Rust](http://web.archive.org/web/20150320090943/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/font-fabric/nexa-rust) and try to mix Nexa Sans with its Script... Not sure it works though...
