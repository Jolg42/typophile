Type Aid

Submitted by JamesKing : 15.May.2007 3.04pm

The St Bride printing library is holding a fundraiser and after party for its 6th annual conference: Great British Design?. Everyone is welcome and all money raised will help to keep the library running. Details are below and you can keep informed about the event here. Please come along and spread the word!

When:
Friday, June 1, 2007,
from 6:00 PM

Where:
St Bride Institute
Bride Lane, Fleet Street
London, England EC4Y 8EQ

Type Aid

Raising money for St Bride’s – The world’s foremost printing and graphic arts library

Featuring:
Dave Farey’s typographic pub quiz (starts at 7pm)
(Team and individual prizes including unlocked Monotype and Linotype CDs)

Visuals by the guest speakers and YOU
(to display your work email type.aid@gmail.com for info)

£6 entry includes 3 free drinks and quiz entry (then drinks for donations)
Free entry for conference ticket-holders
