FontFont fonts are expensive. IF you're looking for a sample of a \*typeface\* to use as a model for hand stitching, and IF a chancery is to be that model, here is a place where you can get them legitimately, for free. [http://www.fontspace.com/category/chancery](http://web.archive.org/web/20131020193300/http:/www.fontspace.com/category/chancery "http://www.fontspace.com/category/chancery") particularly -- though the W is different -- [http://www.fontspace.com/gust-e-foundry/texgyrechorus](http://web.archive.org/web/20131020193300/http:/www.fontspace.com/gust-e-foundry/texgyrechorus "http://www.fontspace.com/gust-e-foundry/texgyrechorus") A generally more useful site for free fonts is font squirrel [http://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/list/category/Calligraphic](http://web.archive.org/web/20131020193300/http:/www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/list/category/Calligraphic "http://www.fontsquirrel.com/fonts/list/category/Calligraphic") If you're really after a \*font\*, that is, some software that will be used (more or less automatically) to drive a printing press, laser printer, sign-painting machine, or in your case, a sewing machine, you may need to keep looking
