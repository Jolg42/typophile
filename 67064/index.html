Swift was designed by Gerard Unger in 1985, and is more than capable of performing the task to which you have assigned it. For the header, another design by Unger would certainly recommend itself. You may want to consult [a list of all his typefaces](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418185554/http:/www.gerardunger.com/allmytypedesigns/allmytypedesigns01.html). If for some reason you desire a Modern face, both [Coranto](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418185554/http:/www.gerardunger.com/fontstore/store-coranto.html) and the earlier [Paradox](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418185554/http:/www.dutchtypelibrary.nl/Paradox_rdrct.html) are drawn in this style. Caranto as well as [Capitolium News](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418185554/http:/www.gerardunger.com/fontstore/store-capitolium-news.html) both have specific Headline variants. For a sans serif that is meant to be used for headlines, there is always [Big Vesta](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418185554/http:/www.gerardunger.com/fontstore/store-bigvesta.html). These fonts and others can be [ordered directly from Gerard Unger](http://web.archive.org/web/20150418185554/http:/www.gerardunger.com/fontstore/store-howto.html).
