Well, that is an interesting question to ask here. Most often fonts downloaded from the internet are illegal copies or of obviously bad quality. Both are frowned upon here. But if you want to know, as always, JFGI (just f\*\*\*ing google it). [http://simplythebest.net/fonts/how\_to\_install\_fonts.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20130819040733/http:/simplythebest.net/fonts/how_to_install_fonts.html "http://simplythebest.net/fonts/how\_to\_install\_fonts.html") or [http://support.microsoft.com/kb/314960/](http://web.archive.org/web/20130819040733/http:/support.microsoft.com/kb/314960/ "http://support.microsoft.com/kb/314960/") or just copy and paste the file into C:\Windows\Fonts. It will automatically appear in any windows program, after you restart the program. I am assuming you are using a windows OS.
