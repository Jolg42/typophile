It's very clean. I only noticed a couple of things I would change. -The arm on the r needs a little more weight. -You may want to make the bowl smaller on the g to give more room between it and the lower arm. -The spine on the s could be smoother. -On the base curve of the t and top curve of the f, I would make them a bit more subtle to work more with the rest of the font. -Maybe try something different with the z. I think it has potential, it's very simple and neat. Bring on the uppercase.
