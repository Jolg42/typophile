The condensed sans looks like Arizona, published by Brendel & Pabst. This company was sued for forging lots of fonts but also selling some unique ones. I don’t know if this is an original. Read more in [this PDF](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317162727/http:/www.sanskritweb.net/forgers/greenstreet.pdf). More about Brendel at [Luc Devroyes site](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317162727/http:/luc.devroye.org/fonts-23628.html).
