I didn't spot a font that matched these Spanish wine shop letters. Here is a somewhat similar font of about the right stroke thickness based on lettering found on buildings constructed in Spain's Andalucian region in the 1930's and 1940's. John Chippindale's Avenida ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417034510im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Avenida_A-M_5050.gif) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417034510im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Avenida_N-Z_5276.gif) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417034510im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Avenida_lc_a-m_6592.gif) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417034510im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Avenida_lc_n-z_5436.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/letraset/avenida/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417034510/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/letraset/avenida/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/letraset/avenida/") Many other Art Deco fonts, but none that I saw get much closer.
