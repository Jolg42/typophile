If you're looking for something like Futura, but with a curved lowercase "j", you may like Futura's cousin from behind the Iron Curtain, [Super Grotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317145405/http:/www.fontfont.com/fonts/super-grotesk). I think [Brandon Text](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317145405/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/hvdfonts/brandon-text) or [Brandon Grotesque](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317145405/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/hvdfonts/brandon-grotesque) could be fine choices as well, maybe a bit overused as webfonts lately.
