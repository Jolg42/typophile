Hi Doug, looks like they sent you some PC application (executable) files, perhaps further compression? Tell them you need .ttf or .pfb font files to be of any use on a mac. I use PC fonts from time to time and they work fine under tiger.

They might be PC executable ZIP files. If so, 'squeeze' them with [File Juicer](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023030501/http:/echoone.com/filejuicer/). You'll get normal ZIP files on the other end, which are easily openable on Mac OS X.
