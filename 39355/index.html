_Also, this typeface so far has been made entirely in Illustrator, and i have no idea how to turn it into an actual font. Does anyone have any links or info i could read up on how to combine my characters in Illustrator into a font?_ Hi. You need to get a font creation program ( [Fontographer and FontLab](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416170016/http:/www.fontlab.com/) are two, but there are others) and bring your Illustrator drawings into it. Check out the [How-To](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416170016/http:/typophile.com/node/12369) section of the TypoWiki for more info on the practical aspects. Check out the [Books](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416170016/http:/typophile.com/node/12157) section to find literature on the subject of designing or creating a typeface. Also, a Google search will probably get you some online tutorials on the basics.
