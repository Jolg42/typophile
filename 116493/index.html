Nothing free that I can find that is even in the ballpark. About as close as I can get is Jeff Levine's Bayview JNL [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/bayview/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317110048/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/bayview/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/bayview/") based on the condensed version of Inland's typeface Studley from circa 1900. At US$25 it's not a budget breaker. It can be used as is for a rough approximation. But relatively minor editing can make it very close. Don
