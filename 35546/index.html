corporate font for bank

Hello!

I wonder what the expertes suggestions might be:
Let’s say a bank gets a a new owner and plans to have a new corporate design: more friendly, more contemporary. They used a customized Futura so far and everything was quite the same for more than 15 years. As there will be a lot of different publications, a big Type-Family like Corpid or Corporate would propably be a good choice because of flexibility. But is there another suggestion? Especially concerning friendliness and contemporary feeling? Or maybe it is better to have a face that conveys those values for the display-sizes (headlines, advertisments, posters), and have a more neutral sans-companion for all the rest …
Any suggestions /font-names are welcome.
Thank you for your ideas, I really appreciate this forum.

Speaking to whether to go with a friendly display face or friendlier display and body types, [here's](http://web.archive.org/web/20150409035321/http:/underware.nl/site2/index.php3?id1=underware&id2=case-studies&id3=01&idpic=05) an example of a bank choosing to use a typeface in every application -- and I think it was a fantastic solution. I believe that Citibank uses Interstate in this manner, too. One wide-ranging type family I can think of is [Fedra](http://web.archive.org/web/20150409035321/http:/www.typotheque.com/fonts), which comes in sans, serif, and display varieties _and_ a monospaced version, which means that it could be used on automatically generated papers, too. Also, take a look at the suggestions [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150409035321/http:/www.typophile.com/node/17684).
