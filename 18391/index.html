Anonymous @ 8.Mar.2006 7.47am
Eric Gill’s work; he started as a stone cutter. Most of his typefaces derive from the alphabets he learned from stonecarving teachers. Perpetua, Joanna, Aries, Golden Cockerel Michael Harvey also has done a lot of carved lettering, as well as a bunch of very nice lapidary typefaces. One of Michael’s other major influences is his prolific work as a letterer for book covers, so his typefaces reflect this as much as they reflect his stonecarving work. Strayhorn, Mezz, Ellington, Andreas
