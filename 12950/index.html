Laurie McCanna

Indices: Designers: Laurie McCanna

Designed several faces for the Nimx foundry in the early 1990s. Laurie has been the proprietor of Laurie McCanna’s Free Art Site since 1995 - providing inspiration, tools, tutorials. and recommended reading for designers and publishers. (Actually, the site has quite a bit more than free art.) Some of the typefaces designed by Laurie include Nimx Jitterbug and Nimx Holiday Mix, a collection of festive icons.
