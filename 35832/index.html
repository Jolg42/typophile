Stephen, [Type Tours](http://web.archive.org/web/20140406172439/http:/www.typevents.com/index.php?id=81,0,0,1,0,0) of the City of London, organised by Type Events are very good. See also: [ ](http://web.archive.org/) [http://typophile.com/node/33877](http://web.archive.org/web/20140406172439/http:/typophile.com/node/33877 "http://typophile.com/node/33877") [http://typophile.com/node/29958](http://web.archive.org/web/20140406172439/http:/typophile.com/node/29958 "http://typophile.com/node/29958") [http://www.flickr.com/photos/timbones/sets/72157594392158650/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140406172439/http:/www.flickr.com/photos/timbones/sets/72157594392158650/ "http://www.flickr.com/photos/timbones/sets/72157594392158650/") BTW, the St. Bride's reading room will be closed for a while. For further information: [http://stbride.org/events\_education/events/temporaryclosure](http://web.archive.org/web/20140406172439/http:/stbride.org/events_education/events/temporaryclosure "http://stbride.org/events\_education/events/temporaryclosure") You don't have to sign in at the library. However, in my experience it is best to contact the library first if you have a specific request. Most of the collection is tucked away, as apparently the reading room contains just 7% of their collection.
