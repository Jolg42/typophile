Looks very quirky. Perhaps old wood type? You might like [Rhode](http://web.archive.org/web/20081011010731/http:/www.fontbureau.com/fonts/Rhode)from Font Bureau. You might also like [Knockout No. 94 Ultimate Sumo](http://web.archive.org/web/20081011010731/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/font_overview.php?productLineID=100013) or [Leviathan Black](http://web.archive.org/web/20081011010731/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/font_overview.php?productLineID=100015) or [Champion Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20081011010731/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/font_overview.php?productLineID=100002) all from Hoefler & Frere-Jones. Oh and [Maple](http://web.archive.org/web/20081011010731/http:/www.processtypefoundry.com/typefaces/maple/inuse.html) from Process Type Foundry has a very nice black weight and is a little quirky too.
