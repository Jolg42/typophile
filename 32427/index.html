Anonymous @ 21 Mar 2007 — 9:15am
MyFonts currently lists over 1370 families with CE support. To look for Central European fonts on MyFonts, go to: [http://www.myfonts.com/International#ce](http://web.archive.org/web/20120423004828/http:/www.font.org/mf/International#ce) There, click on "Central Europe" and then on "Search for fonts containing Central Europe characters". To fine-tune the search go to [http://www.myfonts.com/search](http://web.archive.org/web/20120423004828/http:/www.font.org/mf/search) There, click on "Fonts" and choose "Central Europe" as character set. Optionally, you can filter the results by entering some keyword (e.g. "sans") or selecting a particular foundry (e.g. URW++, ParaType, Elsner+Flake, Storm, Linotype, Ascender, DSType, Flat-it, P22, to name a few that have larger offerings of CE fonts). Regards, Adam
