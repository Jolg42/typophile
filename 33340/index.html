[Gretel](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009143437/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fountain/gretel/) and [FF Nelio](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009143437/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_nelio/) might not be what you’re looking for, but they are others in this elegant pixel genre. You might also like [FF Screenstar](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009143437/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_screenstar/) which is a simple pixel sans, but a tad more style than FF Screen Matrix. Nick Shinn’s [Panoptica](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009143437/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/shinntype/panoptica_pixel/) is also very interesting and [Unibody](http://web.archive.org/web/20071009143437/http:/www.underware.nl/site2/index.php3?id1=unibody&id2=overview) is without doubt the finest free pixel family.
