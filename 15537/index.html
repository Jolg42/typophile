Looks like the two definitions are acceptable: From WordNet (r) 2.0 : **masthead** 1. a listing printed in all issues of a newspaper or magazine (usually on the editorial page) that gives the name of the publication and the names of the editorial staff, etc. [syn: flag] 2. the title of a newspaper or magazine; usually printed on the front page and on the editorial page

(BTW -- hi Kathleen, long time, no talk) Masthead has mistakenly been used so often instead of the correct terms of _nameplate_ or _flag_ that it has taken over through common misuse.
