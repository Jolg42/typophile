That |G| is highly unusual for a gothic. Does your sample date from the 1920s? I’m not sure what the original metal typeface was, but here are a few digital typefaces (with a bit of surgery on the crossbar of the |G|) that could help you replicate the look: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/alternate-gothic-lt/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317090935/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/alternate-gothic-lt/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/alternate-gothic-lt/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/rama-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317090935/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/rama-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/flat-it/rama-gothic/") [http://www.felicianotypefoundry.com/cms/fonts/grotzec-condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317090935/http:/www.felicianotypefoundry.com/cms/fonts/grotzec-condensed "http://www.felicianotypefoundry.com/cms/fonts/grotzec-condensed") [https://klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/founders-grotesk-condensed/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317090935/https:/klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/founders-grotesk-condensed/ "https://klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/founders-grotesk-condensed/")

After considering various options I would suggest as the closest substitute, KLIM's Founders Grotesk Extra Condensed Medium, which is based on a 1912 Miller & Richard specimen of No.7 Grotesque all-caps. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317090935im_/http:/typophile.com/files/FoundersGrotXCondMedAsSong_5036.jpg) For a closer match clip the bar on \_G\_ Also compare to the Semibold weight ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317090935im_/http:/typophile.com/files/FoundersGrotXCondSemiBoldAsSong_5562.jpg) Don
