tools may not be the limiting factor to creativity, but to a professional logo tools do make the difference. Vector is needed, no matter what vector, but that’s how logos need to be constructed. Logos must be scalable. Pixel-designs don’t allow for lossless scaling. Before you say tools don’t matter, I think you would have a hard time typesetting a PDF magazine of all you had to work with a a ziploc bag of cut-out construction paper fonts from the foundry.
