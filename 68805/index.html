French early 19th century typefaces?

Hello everyone,

I’m an illustration major working on my graduation project which is an illustrated biography of Jean-Francois Champollions (1790 – 1832), the decipherer of the Egyptian hieroglyphs.

Being an illustrator I’m sadly not very typography savvy so I could really use some help.

The text I’m working with is a mix of narrative and original letters so I’m looking for two fonts: a handwritten one I can use for the letters and a normal seriph-font for the narrative text, both with a sort of French early 19th century feel.

Suggestions would be much appreciated. Thank you.

Honestly, I couldn't think of a digital Didot as a good choice as a body text typeface, given the strong contrast of thick/thin serifs and the inherent weakness of early digital Didot type translations. But a little search gave some interesting results: [http://www.fontshop.com/fontlist/subclasses/serif\_didone/](http://web.archive.org/web/20141114053127/http:/www.fontshop.com/fontlist/subclasses/serif_didone/ "http://www.fontshop.com/fontlist/subclasses/serif\_didone/") For example, Didot LP by Letter Perfect and FF Cellini seem to be very appropriate.
