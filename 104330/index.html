1970, the era of photo-composition lettering. The closest available design is from Letraset, Compacta Bold Outline, designed by Fred Lambert for Letraset in 1963-5: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420132357im_/http:/typophile.com/files/CompactaBoldOutline_5902.jpg) It has been published by Eisner + Flake as [Compacta Bold Outline OT](http://web.archive.org/web/20150420132357/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/elsnerflake/compacta_bold_outline_ot/). There was also a Compacta Outline which was slightly narrower & with lighter lines. AFAIK no digital version, but I stand to be informed. Don
