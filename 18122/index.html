I'm pretty convinced that this is not a font, although it's hard to prove when the only duplicated letter is an 'i'. Some of House's painter scripts, and their 'Fabulous', as well as a couple of Jason Walcott's scripts (Jukebox Foundry at Veer.com) might also be close enough substitutes. - Mike Yanega
