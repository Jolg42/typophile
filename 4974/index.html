Found it! It's a font called [Good Girl](http://web.archive.org/web/20050819074844/http:/www.dafont.com/en/search.php?nb_ppp=40&texte=canoe%2Bsurfing&nq=1&q=goodgirl) by Cathy Davies, who states in the copyright area, "GoodGirl was inspired by a font in 1960 Modern Teen magazine." and "No copyright; use as you wish". Ms. Davies also created several [other retro fonts](http://web.archive.org/web/20050819074844/http:/moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/davies/cathy.htm). Oh, and a bit from her bio: "In 1997, Cathy Davies casually put some fonts she designed on a website as a free download. Within two years, these fonts had been used in graphic designs from TV commercials to mainstream rap albums to riot girl zines."
