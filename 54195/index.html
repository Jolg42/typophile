Try stock image sources. Like Stockxpert, iStockPhoto, Jupiterimages etc. Royalty-free images are available from very low prices actually, and such vendors tend to have large selections. There are also some free stock sources (of course pix are of varying quality there), such as Stockxchange (sxc.hu).
