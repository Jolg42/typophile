In glyph window, VOLT display glyphs ordered by glyph id. May be you display glyphs in FontLab ordered by Unicode? VOLT always order glyphs in group by glyph id, so if your glyphs are not ordered consistently you will get in trouble. For example, if you a has glyph id 3, b is 4, but a.sups is 11 and b.sups is 10, then `{a,b} -> {a.sups, b.sups}` will map `{3,4}` to `{10,11}`, so a will become b.sups, and b will become a.sups. Only way is to avoid this is to reorder glyphs or list them in lookup separately. Thanks, Sergey
