Anonymous @ 29.Jul.2007 6.17am
Kay, are you aware of [Blaktur](http://web.archive.org/web/20090421160729/http:/www.houseind.com/index.php?page=showfont&id=581) by House Industries or [Fakir](http://web.archive.org/web/20090421160729/http:/www.underware.nl/site2/index.php3?id1=fonts_in_use&&findme=fakir) by Underware? Of course they’re not Tannenbergs but rather much more contemporary approaches to (fat, unembellished) blackletter. Then, they come with technical advantages you might appreciate when using it for an art exhibition design, like extensive character maps, well-mapped alternatives and ligatures and all the other OpenType bells and whistles. Just an idea. I don’t know what DS-Tannenberg has to offer, unfortunately there is not a lot of information provided. Concerning characters: if this [chart](http://web.archive.org/web/20090421160729/http:/www.fraktur.com/Zeichenbestand.gif) applies to DS-Tannenberg, you’ll maybe miss ‘Å’, ‘@’?
