"Eatopia": [Alpine Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/charlesborges/alpine-script/) "youthathon": a [Tektrron](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.dafont.com/tektrron.font), slightly customized, could work "quipio": hard to say, customized (finials curved), very generic (non distinctive, even boring to me) and too few letters. From hand-drawn to any geometric sans, including freebie. Some: [Futura](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/futura/), [Avant-Garde Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/avant+garde/), [Helvetica](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/helvetica/), [Avenir](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/typophile.com/wiki/%5B%5Bhttp%3A/%252Fwww.myfonts.com/search/avenir/), [Raleway](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.google.com/webfonts/specimen/Raleway), [Nexa](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/fontfabric.com/nexa-free-font/)... Your best chance is probably asking to [Sumesh](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.behance.net/gallery/Quipio/6058507) "ONSBAY": [Lithos](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017100638/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/lithos/)
