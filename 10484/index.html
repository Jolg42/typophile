I disagree with the philosophy that one should have a stable of fonts, ready to use on whatever job comes along. As an art director, I was always looking for the new font for the new job. As a type designer, the one(s) I'm working on at the moment are always the most essential. And bear in mind, the right font for the job in hand may not have been designed yet, and may require commissioning.
