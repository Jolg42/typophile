imagine a stretched 8-gon, 12-gon, or 16-gon or any n-gon. now try to round that polygon in illustrator to create a perfectly rounded “O”, similar to a condensed font “O”. the resulting “O” won’t look like a stretched circle (ellipse), it will look like a rectangled circle - that vintage TV screen shape. I couldn’t find a way to do this neither in Illustrator nor in FontLab. I could pull the handles but the resulting rounded shape has to be regular (symetrical). that means we need some kind of automated function to create a rounded shape out of a an angled one, or at least appropriate grid snapping. After rounding, the corner points should remain at their original coordinates, as well.

Anonymous @ 22.Oct.2007 5.06am
If you want the TV shape I would suggest smooth points at the sides and top/bottom, and corner points in the corners. If the latter are too sharp, change to smooth points with the control points very close to the point. I doubt you will find a filter for this, but you should be able to do a decent job with a lot of guides to align your control points correctly.
