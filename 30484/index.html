“Goth Punk” is a neat idea. That would entail taking a gothic typeface (not sans!) and giving it a punk setting — mixing style, and perhaps size and baseline shift. Here’s how it could look in FF Merlin, which has a variety of styles. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20071224115743im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/goth_4917.png) Or you could mix blackletter “Gothic” with sans serif “Gothic” in the same word. And then add distress...
Anonymous @ 9.Jan.2007 8.25pm
I think getting the pub his “gorey, perverse” without looking like a horror flick face will be tricky. What about something snappy and clean from one face, with “eerie” rounded distressed for the rest? A Jeckyll & Hyde sorta thing: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20071224115743im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/horrortext_5650.jpg) top: Chiller and !the troubles, bottom Perpetua and !MISQOT (warning: two of those are mine, but that’s my bag.) &nbsp; Consider, as well, the pulpy posters of 50’s “My zombie stepmother was a giant teen-aged were-space alien squid”-type films. Brushed strokes, black weights, tilted baselines. And non-dripping heavy vertical perspectives. Or anything shattered, though thats more just dated than camp. Or look at anything here (pay and free): [http://www.astigmatic.com/index.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20071224115743/http:/www.astigmatic.com/index.html "http://www.astigmatic.com/index.html") —- Nick: What I wouldn’t give to do the [macabre restyling of this](http://web.archive.org/web/20071224115743/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typeco/gothic-gothic/) as a luridly distressed face. Gothic Gothic Gothic.
