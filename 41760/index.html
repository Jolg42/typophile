_“but I cannot find the right weight anywhere on the net”_ Isn’t the weight of the Canada Type font the same as your sample? The Jaspert “Encyclopaedia of Type Faces” says that Annonce Grotesk “is one of the most widely distributed typefaces” and was in the type catalogs of type foundries all over Europe. It says it was issued by Wagner & Schmidt 1914 under the name “Grotesk V” and later “Krupp Hallo”. (“Annonce” was the Dutch name.) The description says nothing about other weights, except that an italic weight was added in 1959 by Ludwig & Mayer. - Mike Yanega
