virusfonts release new 32 weight font family BOURGEOIS

BOURGEOIS FONT
+_+_+_+_+_+_+_+_+_+_+_+_+

FONT RELEASE: BOURGEOIS
This clean looking sans was originally drawn in three weights for the Mori Art Museum in Japan. ( www.mori.art.museum), Bourgeois has been now been released as a major 32 weight family. It is not the most experimental font we have designed (it has a very light virusfonts touch to it) but it is certainly one of the most usable, well crafted and mature. We hope you will find that suitable for a number of uses, both text and display, please check the virusfonts website at www.virusfonts.com for specimen sheets and pricing details.
