This is "inverted contrast" or "reverse contrast" lettering. Didn't find this as a font but still looking. A less extreme similar font: Signo [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/r-type/signo/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218042636/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/r-type/signo/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/r-type/signo/") The best known reverse contrast font is Antique Olive [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/antique-olive/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218042636/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/antique-olive/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/antique-olive/") Some that are more extreme: ITC Zipper [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/zipper/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218042636/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/zipper/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/zipper/") Stretto [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/stretto/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218042636/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/stretto/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/stretto/") Don
