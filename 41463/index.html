No, this certainly is from [Süddeutsche Zeitung Magazin](http://web.archive.org/web/20130621113432/http:/sz-magazin.sueddeutsche.de/), right? (Always mention your source, David! :°) This Munich based newspaper has had this distinctive nameplate ( [discussed](http://web.archive.org/web/20130621113432/http:/www.typophile.com/node/31857) here [previously](http://web.archive.org/web/20130621113432/http:/www.typophile.com/node/33764)) for more than 60 years now. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20130621113432im_/http:/typophile.com/files/sz_6663.png) A few years ago, the letterforms of that lettered nameplate were turned into a font, missing characters were added. I once read about that, but unfortunately forgot who had done it. [SZM](http://web.archive.org/web/20130621113432/http:/sz-magazin.sueddeutsche.de/) is a weekly supplement to the paper. [Mirko Borsche](http://web.archive.org/web/20130621113432/http:/www.mirkoborsche.com/) used to be its art director for the last three years … I don’t know if he was involved. [Typeforum.de](http://web.archive.org/web/20130621113432/http:/www.typeforum.de/modules.php?op=modload&name=XForum&file=viewthread&tid=451) doesn’t know either … guess it’s time to ask directly.
