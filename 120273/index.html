Good suggestions Bob and Kevin. Almost looks like license plate letters in a jurisdiction where the need to squeeze in more than a few letters allows this width. But the \_Q\_ is a disaster. Too close to \_O\_ -- ignore the comma, \_O\_ would look like \_C\_ with the gap closed. BTW looking at \_J\_, \_M\_, and \_W\_ leads me to believe that this is monospaced. Don
