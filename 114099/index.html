What kind of project is it? A book, a magazine, a poster, a website, Powerpoint slides? Are you looking for a text font that would work with Goudy Old Style headers, or are you picking type for other kinds of headers, captions, tables, lists, etc.? 90 of Frederic Goudy’s typefaces were issued, and many have been digitized. You might start by looking at other Goudy faces.
