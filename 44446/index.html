And check out [this thread](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/32680). Here are a couple of quotes on Chaparral, from typophiles. It seems to be quite a polarising face. I, for one, like it very much (using it for my stationery & stuff). <cite>I keep trying to use it and it always seems to fall short. I can’t articulate why.</cite> — [Patty](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/39592#comment-244492) <cite>Chaparral […] could end up as a standard children’s font for me.</cite> — [Malte](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/42632#comment-262630) <cite>Chaparral is hard to use well, for sure.</cite> — [Will](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/39592#comment-244574) <cite>Chaparral is probably the only slab that has optical sizes. I do have to say that it’s really versatile, though it’s not exactly a Scala-kind-of-workhorse.</cite> — [Bram](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/28288#comment-159471) What ONE typeface would you have rather not exist? <cite>Chaparral. Ok, you beat me up for this one.</cite> — [Thomas](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/36352#comment-222258) <cite><a href="http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Elements%20of%20Typographic%20Style" class="freelinking" rel="nofollow">Bringhurst</a> does mention Chaparral in his list of types. And since he mentions so relatively few types, that means he gave it his seal of approval.</cite> — [Reed](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/29407#comment-168386) (Bringhurst’s _The Solid Form of Language_ is set in Chaparral.) Plus: some unisonous thoughts about pairing: <cite>Auto is sort of a Chaparral Sans.</cite> — [Stewf](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/38561#comment-236149) <cite>The informal shapes of Chaparral will probably play real nice with Auto.</cite> — [Carl](http://web.archive.org/web/20140831143200/http:/www.typophile.com/node/19326#comment-118785) F
