Anonymous @ 12.Apr.2008 5.28am
You can assign quick keys to anything you see in a menu. — Open your “System Preferences” — Open “Keyboard and Mouse” — Go to “Keyboard Shortcuts” — Scroll down to “Application Keyboard Shortcuts” — Select “All Applications” — Click on the “+” — A new window appears — With “All Applications” selected, type “Stop Speaking” (It is important to spell it exactly as it is shown.) — Assign a keyboard shortcut. For instance, I used cmd-opt-shift y (Note that you should be careful not to assign a quick key combination that is already in use.) — Click “Add” — Close “System Preferences” — Restart your machine. Ta da!!
