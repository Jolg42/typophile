I would also look at the [Nouvelle Vague movie posters](http://web.archive.org/web/20090927074109/http:/www.cinesud-affiches.com/?affiches-de-cinema-NOUVELLE_VAGUE+t_66.html&urlref=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.fi%2Fsearch%3Fq%3Dnouvelle%2Bvague%2Baffiches%26ie%3Dutf-8%26oe%3Dutf-8%26aq%3Dt%26rls%3Dorg.mozilla%3Afi%3Aofficial%26client%3Dfirefox-a), because there’s was an important shift in the esthetics from the style of the previously mentioned posters to the new wave realism, end of 50’s, beginning of 60’s. When I think of French movie poster layouts of the time, I think of something like the Paris Match example Nick posted, with a similar typeface and the one-sided layout.
