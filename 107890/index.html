500 (from the Michigan Spartans University): see [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.typophile.com/node/99339) for a previous discussion on this topic (confirming Machine ITC). More alternatives: [Hoboken Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/lineto.com/The+Fonts/Font+Categories/Headline+Fonts/Hoboken+High/Sans+Serif/), [Masheen](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ingrimayne/masheen/), [Base Runner](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/base-runner/), [Octin College](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/octin-college/), [Superstar](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/superstar/), [NYC](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/k-type/nyc/) UNDER ARMOUR: to me that looks to be based on [Eurostile Extended](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/eurostile/)/ [Microgramma](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/microgramma/). Alternatives: [Eurostile Candy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile-candy/), [Alban](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/naghachian/aban/) (another rounded Eurostile), [Reversal](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/reversal/) (another Eurostile clone), [Saturn](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/dsgnhaus/saturn/) (one more Eurostile), [Reaction](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fw-identikal/reaction/), [Phantom](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fw-identikal/phantom/), [Stak](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fw-identikal/stak/), [Geometry Soft Pro Light C](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/cheapprofonts/geometry-soft-pro/light-c/), [Miedinger](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/canadatype/miedinger/), [Phat](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124044704/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fw-identikal/phat/)
