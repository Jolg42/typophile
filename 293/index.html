Anonymous @ 5 Jun 2003 — 1:42pm
\> Element:Transform:Shear -12 Many "users" wouldn't notice (at least not consciously), but I assume you realize that mechanical transformations like that produce inferior results. If you look at almost any italic (even ones called oblique that maintain the Roman structures, but especially so-called "true" italics that are totally different than the Roman) you'll see that at the absolute very least there has been some manual post-processing, but very often the whole thing is redrawn. Also, the larger the angle you use, the greater the distortion. At the very least, call the resultant fonts "Oblique", not "Italic". hhp
