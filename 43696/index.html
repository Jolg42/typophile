I would just scan it as sharply as possible (min 600 dpi), then play with sharpness, unsharp mask etc until it looks good. You might want to turn it grayscale. In other words, I wouldn’t trace it, I’d use it as a picture. And since it’s supposed to be white, just inverse it.
