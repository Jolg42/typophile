You're right Georg, Phosphor and Red Rooster's ['Phosphate'](http://web.archive.org/web/20150313163251/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/phosphate-rr/) are almost exact copies of the Erbar typeface. Letraset's ['Follies'](http://web.archive.org/web/20150313163251/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/letraset/follies/) is modified more, but still quite similar. However, the '!' mark sets the 'identical' ones apart from the Erbar design, and Michael seems right about 'Phosphor' being the answer this time. - Mike Yanega
