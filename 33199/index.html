Anonymous @ 20.Apr.2007 6.06am
U&lc was always 11 x 14.75 until around 1998 when they switched to a smaller 8.5 x 11 format. As for margins etc., the design is quite varied over their 25+ years as a print publication, so you will have to check specific issues for those details. try a university library for back issues. Also, check out the great book on the history of U&lc edited by John Berry and published by [Mark Batty](http://web.archive.org/web/20071010134640/http:/www.markbattypublisher.com/servlet/book_view?number=23).
