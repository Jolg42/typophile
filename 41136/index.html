I believe this was drawn by [Neville Brody](http://web.archive.org/web/20080125221449/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Neville%252BBrody) specifically for the FUSE logo, not from any font. You might like his [FF Typeface Six](http://web.archive.org/web/20080125221449/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/fontfont/ff_typeface_six/), though the ’S’ is very different.
