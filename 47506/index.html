In case you are looking for typefaces similar to the one in the sample you linked to: Try a no-nonsense Grotesque that has an R with a straight leg, like [News Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141108155008/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/linotype/news_gothic_no_2_com_bold/?sample_text=RUE%20DE%20RIVOLI%201e%20Arr), [Akzidenz-Grotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20141108155008/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/akzidenz-grotesk-bq/familytree.html), [Neuzeit Grotesk](http://web.archive.org/web/20141108155008/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/linotype/neuzeit_s_std_book_heavy/?sample_text=RUE%20DE%20RIVOLI%201e%20Arr), maybe [Avenir](http://web.archive.org/web/20141108155008/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/linotype/avenir_std_85_heavy/?sample_text=RUE%20DE%20RIVOLI%201e%20Arr). More recent takes on this field: [Akkurat](http://web.archive.org/web/20141108155008/http:/www.lineto.com/The+Fonts/Font+Categories/Text+Fonts/Akkurat/Fett/), or, more in the geometric line, [Proxima Nova](http://web.archive.org/web/20141108155008/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/mark_simonson/proxima_nova_semibold/?sample_text=RUE%20DE%20RIVOLI%201e%20Arr).
