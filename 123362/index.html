That’s because it appears to be custom. Closest commercial typeface is Eurostile (Extended or Extended Semibold should do the trick): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile-next/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317171451/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile-next/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile-next/") You’ll have to customize the |4| to match, or pick it up from this typeface: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/rexlia/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317171451/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/rexlia/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/rexlia/")
