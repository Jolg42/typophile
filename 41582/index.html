Cut-up slab serif used in japanese ad campaign (ca. 2006)

This font is used in a few of the dialogue in the dark ad campaigns, most notably the one in Japan seen here. The font has been clipped so that only partials of each letter are showing. The letter elements that I have been focusing on would be the slight upward hump in the D and the wide and dipping top of the A. The lower serif on the L lends itself to the courier font family… but I am stuck. Any Ideas?
