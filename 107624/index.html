That's [Alston](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317072647/http:/mirkoborsche.com/projects/049-2012-tush-typeface/05_2012_tush_fonts_bbweb.jpg), exclusive typeface for Tush Magazine, commissioned by [Bureau Mirko Borsche](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317072647/http:/mirkoborsche.com/?q=alston) and designed by [Colophon](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317072647/http:/www.colophon-foundry.org/). Alternatives: [Romana](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317072647/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/romana), [Fortescue](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317072647/http:/www.colophon-foundry.org/fonts/fortescue), [De Vinne](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317072647/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/de+vinne)
