Toronto Subway is \*a version\* of the indigenous TTC typeface. The TTC believes it has its own outline-font version. I have a few reservations about Toronto Subway; we may need a few more iterations before we arrive at a really accurate outline-font version, particularly given the fact that there is no model for the lower case. — Joe Clark [http://joeclark.org/](http://web.archive.org/web/20131023152357/http:/joeclark.org/ "http://joeclark.org/")
