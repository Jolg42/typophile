Very classy, well crafted, and very readable. Your lc "g" is a surprising success. You're calling it a Bold (standard practice for a bitmap font with 2-pixel stems), but I think this is what works for on-screen reading. Consider: 1. Calling it a Regular, and making Light and Bold (3-pixel) weights. 2. Extending the ascenders by a pixel: it will improve readability, and lighten the color a bit. hhp
