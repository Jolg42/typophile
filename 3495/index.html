Hit the wrong button I guess. Edited: Hispania, which appears in the Solotype Catalog by that name, has been digitized by others. At least two more versions exist, both of which predate Scriptorium's Esperanza. There was David Rakowski's Dobkin Script. Found [here for Mac](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018212307/http:/www.erik.co.uk/font/script.html) (the original) and [here for PC](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018212307/http:/www.dafont.com/en/search.php?q=dobkin&nb_ppp_old=10&page=1&texte=Starved%20Rock%20Lodge&nb_ppp=10&Envoyer=%A0OK%A0) (an old conversion by someone other than Mr. Rakowski). Ted Keener also digitized the typeface as Hispania KGD back in 1995. Unfortunately, Mr. Keener's website disappeared a couple of years ago, along with any online references that aren't ancient.
