try your luck at ZVAB.com and search for "Bremer Presse". A good book about the historie of the Bremer Presse is: Die Bremer Presse, Königin der deutschen Pressen, 1964 Typographische Gesellschaft München But its a bit pricey! The Bibelschrift is available at [moorstation](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124091336/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/blackletter/htm/bibelschrift.htm). I like the Antiqua and the Greek, of course a bit plumb and dark, but something differend for its time. BTW, all his typefaces are cutted by Louis Hoell. I thing his influence is a last 50%. -- [astype.de](http://web.archive.org/web/20150124091336/http:/www.astype.de/)--
