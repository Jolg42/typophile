Anonymous @ 2 Jul 2003 — 10:54am
I think it would be far more readable if you didn't marry the left half of the _n_ into the _a_. The ambigram/inversion thing can very definitely work for this word. Try using the middle of the right stroke of the _n_ as your centre of rotation, curving left at the top as normal, and curving into the _t_ at the bottom, something like a cursive join. Then the left stroke of the _n_ can invert to the _t_. A half serif on the end and a slightly descending stroke will give you your _t_.
