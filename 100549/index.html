"Brand Lager" and "The Famous Cinci": I don't have an exact match but you may be able to approximate it using **[Blue Highway](http://web.archive.org/web/20130823215651/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Typodermic&ffam=Blue%20Highway%20-%20Regular&fstyle=i&fsize=60&fid=3b7e7e678645357a77e3fe6c440e44ab&wrap=2&text=THE%20FAMOUS%20CINCI%20BRAND%20LAGER)** by Ray Larabie (Typodermic) by modifying /H & /M and probably some more letters "BRANDIN"S": The closer I can see are some condensed forms of **[DIN 1451](http://web.archive.org/web/20130823215651/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Adobe&ffam=DIN%201451%20Std%20-%20Engschrift&fid=1cf07fdc01da97ba3a1410d18951d28f&fsize=60&text=BRADING'S&fit=1)**
