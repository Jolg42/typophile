Sounds probably "lazy" but to me a slightly edited [Times New Roman](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/times-new-roman) might work for 'Hennessy' and 'COGNAC' mainly squooshing it and curving top /n serif. [Starling](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/starling) FB might be a good option to consider too. No match for the script. Some close: [Englische Schreibschrift](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/Englische+Schreibschrift), [Symphony](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/profonts/symphony-pro), [English 157](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/english+157), [Sonata](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/profonts/sonata-pro) BTW, this logo is freely available to download as vector [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.brandsoftheworld.com/search/logo?search_api_views_fulltext=hennessy).

URW’s Danmark is actually a pretty good match for the overall letter shapes/proportions—with a little manual condensing: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/danmark/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/danmark/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/danmark/") Thicken the crossbar and refine the serifs on the |H|, add serifs to each |s|, and add a ball finial to the tail of the |y|, and you are pretty close. Agree with Ryuk about Times New Roman for |COGNAC|—a little artificial widening, and it is a better than 95% match. Nimbus Roman by URW is a close second: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/nimbus-roman/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/nimbus-roman/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/nimbus-roman/") For the script face, Ballantines Script by Elsner+Flake is good for everything except the initial caps: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/ballantines-script/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/ballantines-script/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/ballantines-script/") Novia isn’t bad, either: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/novia/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404231930/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/novia/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontbureau/novia/")
