This Columbus. The original was created by Hermann Ihlenburg in 1892 and sold by MacKellar, Smiths & Jordan /​ American Type Founders as Columbus. Thomas Phinney has launched a project for a quality revival under the name Cristoforo [http://www.thomasphinney.com/tag/hermann-ihlenburg/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317104733/http:/www.thomasphinney.com/tag/hermann-ihlenburg/ "http://www.thomasphinney.com/tag/hermann-ihlenburg/") Don
