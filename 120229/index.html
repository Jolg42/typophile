All letters, except /a & /M looks very close (if not an exact match) to a manually slanted **[Lucida Sans (Bold)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317114642/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Linotype%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Lucida%20Sans%20Std%20-%20Bold&fid=62280376d21c8e58e4020e56533ff067&fstyle=i&fsize=60&text=Johns%20Manville&fit=1)** The problem with Lucida is that /a has a curly terminal and /M middle is not touching the baseline.
