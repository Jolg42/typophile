It's been said before: InDesign won't recognize kerning unless there is at least one GSUB element (ligatures, fractions, ordinals, something). In short, if you ain't got no GSUB, you don't get no GPOS. Make up one substitution feature & recompile. You should now get kerning in InDesign.
