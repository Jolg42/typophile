Whoa! There is no one font that matches this, surely? It has two differing constructions for ’a’ for a start, and two differing widths for the last two words. You could start with something like [Clearface](http://web.archive.org/web/20080312104828/http:/www.linotype.com/616/itcclearface-family.html) and mess about with it yourself. Another one that reflects the style of the period is [Windsor.](http://web.archive.org/web/20080312104828/http:/www.linotype.com/90458/windsor-family.html) And you could probably find something at [http://www.letterheadfonts.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20080312104828/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/ "http://www.letterheadfonts.com") that comes closer to this than either of them, but I don’t know what it would be called.
