Pierre, try working with a work that is in Public Domain. I suggest Alice's Adventures in Wonderland. Its abstract enough for you to go wild with your creative imagination and create a new spin on an old look.
Anonymous @ 18 Sep 2005 — 4:34am
You might even try a long poem such as T.S. Eliot's "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufock," or a series of shorter, concise poems like hiakus or something. If it's portfolio work, you probably don't have to choose something in public domain, but the comment above is a good one. If you plan to reproduce the work, publish it on the internet or sell the work in any way, be very careful of copyright laws. You might read [10 myths about copyright explained](http://web.archive.org/web/20131014054947/http:/www.templetons.com/brad/copymyths.html) by Brad Templeton. ChuckGroth
