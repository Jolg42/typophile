At first, I thought the font wasn't designed by Erik, since it didn't appear to match any of his output. Then, I discovered this: [http://usshop.gestalten.com/hello-erik.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150419154056/http:/usshop.gestalten.com/hello-erik.html "http://usshop.gestalten.com/hello-erik.html") The font is called Real, and was designed by Spiekermann specifically for this project. You get it free when you buy the book.
