or if you are not a purist. Import or scan your base ’type’ design. Needs to have a photographic background or similar to work, like in your example Copy the ’type’ layer in Photoshop four/five+ times. Blur each layer to different amounts, nudge them about a bit, alter the colours, impose different blends on each color dodge/screen/overlay. Basically experiment to you find what you are looking for.

Yep, along with belleisle advice there’s lots of ways to achieve the neon glow effect in Photoshop. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20071010133948im_/http:/typophile.com/files/GlowLines_4921.JPG) One real simple way is to use the Pen tool to create your shapes and on a new layer above it fill with colour and set the blending mode to Color. You could copy your shape to a new layer below it and apply to slight gauss blurr to achieve a diffused effect. In my example I just used two layers on a black bg. clauses, groovy linkage. Check out camera tossing on Flickr: [http://www.flickr.com/search/?q=camera+tossing](http://web.archive.org/web/20071010133948/http:/www.flickr.com/search/?q=camera+tossing "http://www.flickr.com/search/?q=camera+tossing") \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ Hiro
