Achieving true shared baselines is very hard. You can never be sure which typefaces are used to display a webpage & most browsers use the top offset of a font to render type, and not the baseline like most page design programs. The different x-heights mess up sharing baselines. Alistapart published an informative article about this: [http://www.alistapart.com/articles/settingtypeontheweb](http://web.archive.org/web/20130516052555/http:/www.alistapart.com/articles/settingtypeontheweb "http://www.alistapart.com/articles/settingtypeontheweb") Rob Goodlatte built a small bookmarklet to help check whether you are on the right track: [http://robgoodlatte.com/2007/07/31/syncotype-your-baselines/](http://web.archive.org/web/20130516052555/http:/robgoodlatte.com/2007/07/31/syncotype-your-baselines/ "http://robgoodlatte.com/2007/07/31/syncotype-your-baselines/") Hope this helps. . . .
