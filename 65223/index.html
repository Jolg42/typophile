I'd definitely say keep going with this. Looks like you haven't decided on a variable-width or constant-width style. The _c_, _s_, _o_ and the tails of the _g_ and _e_ are constant-width, and the rest are variable-width. I think the variable-width looks better. The top of the vertical in _b_, _d_ and _h_, and the bottoms of the _p_ and _q_ seem like they should be flatter.
