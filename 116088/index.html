Try using PhotoShop levels to limit gray range and tighten them up - then run poster edges and then covert to one bit. This will give you a nice engraved look. Process: 1. Convert Photo to Grayscale. 2. Run Artistic/Poster Edges Filter at 1 for Thickness, 6 for Intensity and 2 for Posterization. 3. Use Image Adjust Threshold to fine tune your Sketch. 4. Convert to Bitmap using Diffusion Dither.
