Lettering, probably based on a mix of Art Deco ideas rather than a single font. The rounded tips are perhaps the result of the sign machining process rather than a design decision. As for font ideas you might pick up the oblong shape of O and the crossbars that extend to the left of the LHS stroke from a font like Aldous/Huxley Vertical [http://www.identifont.com/similar?3TS](http://web.archive.org/web/20140124061207/http:/www.identifont.com/similar?3TS "http://www.identifont.com/similar?3TS") and the C & G from a font like Washington [http://www.identifont.com/show?9Y0](http://web.archive.org/web/20140124061207/http:/www.identifont.com/show?9Y0 "http://www.identifont.com/show?9Y0") also you might check Neutra Condensed Medium [http://www.houseind.com/fonts/neutraface](http://web.archive.org/web/20140124061207/http:/www.houseind.com/fonts/neutraface "http://www.houseind.com/fonts/neutraface") Don
