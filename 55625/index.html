Hi Sanch, welcome to Typophile! This is probably based on/influenced by [ITC Bauhaus](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021031303/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/itc/itc_bauhaus_std_by_adobe_complete_vp/&sample_text=bambo) or [Pump](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021031303/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/itc/pump_std_virtual/&sample_text=bambo). Also check out [Chalet](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021031303/http:/www.houseind.com/fonts/chalet) and [Dessau](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021031303/http:/www.fountaintype.com/typefaces/dessau/styles). In case you are interested in the finger-like terminals, have a look at [Girl](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021031303/http:/www.fountaintype.com/typefaces/girl) or [Cocon](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021031303/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_cocon/). BTW, such requests should better be posted on the [Type ID Board](http://web.archive.org/web/20131021031303/http:/typophile.com/forum/29). F
