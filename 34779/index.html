Spot/Process Question

Sometimes (actually, all the time) in InDesign when I’m working in two spot colors, and I have it generate the instructions, it lists four process inks too, even though, to the best of my knowledge, I haven’t used any. How can I prevent this from happening?

You can delete the colors you don’t use from your swatches palette, manually, or by pressing the little arrow and choose ’select all unused’ and delete. Chances are that it still lists CMYK colors if you for example generate a PDF of it, but since you do not use those colors, that should not matter. You can always instruct your printer to ignore those plates, if they do not already, since there is nothing on them. Also, the color ’Registration’ (for crop marks and such) is built up from CMYK (or maybe the colors that are present in your document, I’m not sure).
