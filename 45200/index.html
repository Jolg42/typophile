Anonymous @ 10.May.2008 7.20pm
It’s by Hendrik Werkman, a Dutch designer working in Groningen, Holland pre WWII. He was an interesting guy who, late in his life, began doing very experimental and artistic work using a letterpress. This image was part of a periodical he published called the \*Next Call.\* He was killed by the Nazis when Allied forces invaded Holland. You should look for more of his work, it’s really phenomenal.
