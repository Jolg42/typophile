The bowl of /p and, to a lesser degree, /q, looks like it's leaning backwards. I'm not convinced of the flattish bottoms of /b /d. The /m and /w look somewhat asymmetric; I think the apex of the second arch in /m and first arch in /w might not be centered. Maybe even the two arches are of different widths? Hi, thanks for the great feedback again I wasn't happy with p,q,d,b either but they are difficult to master because it should look somewhat symmetrical and effortless but still be balanced. It's especially difficult width g,p,q because I want to accentuate the corner were the stem is cut of but that causes them to lean a little bit so it's a matter of finding the right compromise. m+w were perfectly symmetrical (photoshop rendering isn't always 100% accurate) but now I've tried making the m asymmetrical (apex, not width).
