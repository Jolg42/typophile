Need a printing advice

Trying to round up options for printing this business card. I am quite new to this, so I would appreciate some guidance.

First, the paper is Touche from Fibermark. Amazing, suede-like texture. Somewhat similar to Plike, but bolder.

I want to preserve the texture of course, so the inverted printing is not an option. So white needs to be printed on black.

The four options I have arrived at are:

  • Metallic foil pressing. Non-metallic foil allegedly flakes and doesn’t look sharp enough when finer details are present. An example is here.
  • Letterpress in metallic ink. An example is here.
  • Engraving. An example is here.
  • Silkscreening. An absolutely amazing example is here.

I would rather not do the metallic ink if possible as I need to get flat white for the type. No shimmer and such.

I would rather not do pressing or engraving as these leave the imprint on the back. Though I guess this can be remedied by duplexing or using thicker stock.

Engraving guys said the white is not a problem, but opaque yellow ink is a special order, which takes a while and costs a lot (yellow is used for the middle stroke between two E’s).

I am inclining towards the silk-screened version, but I absolutely cannot find anyone who can do it. Leave alone with very tight registration in two colors. Tried emailing the owner of Flickr photo, got no response. Does anyone know if there are any printers that could do this ?

Also the sharpness of small type on that Flickr photo looks very impressive, but I suspect it is very hard to achieve. Am I right ?

Thermography is another option, but local guys are saying that their white inks are not opaque enough to work on black. So I was wondering if doing termography over a foil would work - any thoughts on this ?

Thanks for any pointers and/or comments. I could really use some :-)

An Unhealthy Obsession

Let me begin by saying that I am no pirate. I am actually so vocal on the subject that I have on occasion bullied others. So, it is with that said that I must voice my current predicament. I truly hope that within that context, there is some sort of resolution.

Sometime in the spring of last year, I was taken with a beautiful font that frequented the ID forum on some book covers: ITC Didi.

Unfortunately, (not to rehash the facts) Didi was digitized by Image Club Graphics in the late 80’s-early 90’s, but was later discontinued. So, I let my crush on the beautiful but unattainable Didi fade. In the company of other type dorks, I would sometimes mention this star-crossed font, waxing poetic about what might have been. *le sigh*

I was moving on in my life, still giddy with my recent purchase of Archer, when an acquaintance of mine brought up my old flame. In fact, she emailed me the object of my desire; the actual file for Didi. I have opened it, and studied the glyphs. However, I feel like this is somehow worse. Before, it was simply out of reach. Now, it is within reach, but only on the “dl”.

How could I possibly ever use this? I am sure that the file in question was attained through dubious means. I would be willing to pay for the rights to actually use it, but how would I go about broaching that subject? Is what I propose even possible?

As an ex smoker, I compare this to having a cigarette and no means by which to light it.

Should I delete this file and forget I ever had it?
