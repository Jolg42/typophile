William Thorowgood

Indicies : Designers : William Thorowgood

In 1794 Robert Thorne had purchased the foundry of Thomas Cottrell, a former employee of William Caslon, which had been founded in 1757 when Cottrell and Joseph Jackson were fired in a wage dispute. Upon Thorne’s death in 1820 the foundry was purchased at auction by William Thorowgood using money he had won in a lottery. Though he was never involved in the type founding business before this Thorowgood made the foundry initially successful by publicizing Thorne’s typefaces. Many of the types identified as Thorowgood’s are actually the designs of Robert Thorne. Thorowgood went on to issue new specimens and added more typefaces including Frakturs, Greeks, and Russian types which he obtained from the Breitkopf and Härtel foundry of Leipzig, Germany. In 1828 he also purchased the Edmund Fry foundry which had a large collection of foreign language types as well. Robert Besley became a partner in the firm in 1828, and on Thorowgood’s retirement in 1849 Besley took over the foundry.

Robert Thorne

Indicies : Designers : Robert Thorne

In 1794 Robert Thorne purchased the foundry of Thomas Cottrell, a former employee of the original William Caslon, which had been founded in 1757 when Cottrell and Joseph Jackson were fired in a wage dispute. By 1798 Thorne had replaced all of Cottrell’s types with his own designs and in 1801 was the first type founder to begin showing the fat face types. He went on to design many popular display typefaces. He also moved the foundry to Fann St. renaming it the Fann Street Foundry.

Upon Thorne’s death in 1820 the foundry was purchased at auction by William Thorowgood using money he had won in a lottery though he was never involved in the type founding business. Subsequently many of the types identified as Thorowgood’s are actually the designs of Robert Thorne.

Schule für Gestaltung

The Schule für Gestaltung (School for Design) Basel is located in the north-west of Switzerland. It has a rich history in typographic education. Teachers like Jan Tschichold, Emil Ruder, Armin Hoffman and Wolfgang Weingart were instrumental in making this school a center for modernist typography and education. The Vorkurs (foundation year) is still unique in it’s very thorough examination of cross discipline basics following the Bauhaus methods.

The SfG has 270 full time students and the public courses are visited by over 1000 participants every year. The summer school offers an intensive three week study of typographic basics.

www.sfgbasel.ch

ESDI

Indices : Education : Escola Superior de Desenho Industrial

Founded in 1963, Escola Superior de Desenho Industrial (ESDI) was the first design college in Brazil and in Latin America.

The undergraduate course consists of a five-year programme of full-time study, covering both product design and graphic design.

ESDI’s Master’s Degree Course started in 2005.

Michael Abbink

Indices : Designers : Michael Abbink

Mr. Abbink left Wolff Olins (NYC) in June 2006 after 3 years and is now trying his hand at freelance and developing his own design practice. He was co-founder and creative director of Method, a design firm based in San Francisco doing graphic design, and web & interface design. He formerly worked at Meta Design and Apple Computer, and is the designer of FF Kievit and FF Milo typeface families.

FontShop Bio
Mike Abbink earned two BFAs: one in Fine Arts, and one in Graphic Design and Packaging from Art Center in Pasadena. He has been designing letter forms since 1992, and his first completed typeface, FF Kievit, is used around the world and has won awards from the ISTD and ATypI. In 2000 Mike designed FF Milo which became part of the FontFont Library in 2006.

Hannes Famira

Indices : Designers : Hannes Famira

Type designer and lecturer. Educated at the Royal Academy of Art in The Hague (Den Haag, The Netherlands), he has taught type design and new media at the SfG, Schule für Gestaltung in Basel, CH as well as Lettering and Typedesign at the NJCU New Jersey City University. Hannes runs the website www.typEdu.org and the kombinat-typefounders. He currently teaches Digital Typedesign and Experimantal Typography at the Cooper Union and Typography at CUNY.
