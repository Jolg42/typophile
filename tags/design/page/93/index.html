Footnote issue

Hello again!

I have a question about footnotes that has been bothering me for a while now:

I’m working with footnotes on a book, and it happened that a word which needs a footnote is sitting on the bottom line of the page. When I put the actual footnote in place, the word referenced goes to the next page; if I put the footnote in the next page, the word obviously goes back to the previous page. What can I do in these cases?
I asked my professor about it and he said I could increase the size of the text slightly – 10pt to 10.1pt, for instance – on that page only and not touching the line spacing at all. According to him, the reader won’t notice the difference in size, the word will go to the top of the next page, and I’ll be able to set the footnote very comfortably.
But I’d like to know, is that a common practice? I don’t feel this like a good thing to do, even if it solves the problem.
Also, the endnotes possibility is discarded. I just hate to go back and forth a book in order to understand it.

I’d love to know your opinions on this issue!

Regards,
Henrique
