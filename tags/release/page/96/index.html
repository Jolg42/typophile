Ten Year Itch: Device Fonts celebrates 10 years with lavish new book

FONTWORKS NEWSLETTER // FEBRUARY 2006

This month’s newsletter is a Rian Hughes special celebrating the 10th birthday of Device Fonts and the release of the stunning new “Ten Year Itch” reference book to celebrate this landmark, available from Fontworks.

Featured below:

  • New Device Fonts Releases: check out the fonts which comprise Rian’s “Zephyrmen” Collection 8
  • “Ten Year Itch”: details on this brand new 160 page must-have type reference book
  • Special Offer: spend 99 GBP or more on Device Fonts at type.co.uk and receive the book absolutely free

================================

  • NEW DEVICE FONTS COLLECTION 8: “ZEPHYRMEN”
    Hot on the heels of Collection 7 (“Pagodaland”) comes another 15 Device families released under the code name “Zephyrmen”. As we’ve come to expect from Rian it’s an extremely varied and versatile collection of type, ranging from distressed (Roadkill, Zond Diktat) to playful (Catseye, Custard), historical (Mulgrave is based on cast-iron Victorian street signs) to futuristic (Gentry, Gridlocker). The 7 weight “Ministry” typeface is a notable revival, created after viewing the Ministry of Transport signage designs in the National Archives in Kew. All new releases are magnificently detailed in the new Device book “Ten Year Itch” (see below).

View and buy the Device 8 Collection:
http://www.fontworks2.type.co.uk/fw/doc?d=public/displaycase/device8

  • TEN YEAR ITCH: The definitive Device Fonts resource
    Workaholic type designer and illustrator supreme, Rian Hughes celebrates ten years of font releases the only way he knows how, by sweating blood on a mammoth 160 page type specimen documenting the entire Device collection. Stuffed to the brim with samples, text settings, background info on each font and with a foreword by Erik Spiekermann this book is a visual feast for type fans. If you’ve admired Rian’s work over the years the book will be indispensable. The softback version has fold out flaps featuring a cross section of logos designed for various clients and if you’re really quick you may be able to purchase the extremely limited edition (only 150 exist) hardback volume with full colour dustjacket and a lime green embossed hard cover, signed and numbered by Rian. The book is officially unveiled in March but Fontworks have advance copies available to purchase now; e-mail sales@type.co.uk to place your order.

Chicago's Society of Typographic Arts presents Archive06

Archive06 www.sta-archive.com is the competition developed to recognize outstanding achievement in Chicago graphic design during 2005. It’s different from other competitions because it:

• promotes winners to Chicago’s design community,

Copinger and Skone James on Copyright

While doing other legal research, I came across the case “Masson, Seeley & Co., Ltd. v. Embosotype Manufacturing Co.”, (1924) 41 R.P.C. 160 [1923-28] Macg.Cop.Cas. 99, mentioned in “Copinger and Skone James on Copyright”. According to this case based on the 1911 Copyright Act and dealing with a catalog of type, it would be illegal to depict fonts as it is done in books on typography and at various typographic websites. In my opinion, this old decision was a misjudgement, inasmuch as the court held that an alphabet of type, though it has no meaning in language, is a “literary work”. If this were so, then any font depicted at Typophile, e.g. as attachment to postings, would constitute a copyright infringement of a “literary work”.

WWW.FONTPARTNERS.COM: NEW TYPO-WEBSITE!

FONTPARTNERS.COM: NEW TYPO-WEBSITE!

This new released fontsite presents customfonts, FontFonts and current typography-news from the two Danish founders and partners, Ole Søndergaard and Morten Rostgaard Olsen, whose designs include FF Signa, FF Max, FF Olsen and others. Fontpartners.com also presents examples of fontdesigns and their implementation, and offers creative and technical solutions within most areas of fontdesign and typography.

Ole Søndergaard studied graphic design at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts, Copenhagen. After working as a graphic designer at an architect’s office, he established his own design practice. Ole has also taught graphic design at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts, Copenhagen. Between 1985 and 1995 he was co-founder and partner of Eleven Design A/S, designing signs and signposting systems for banks, office blocks and public buildings in Denmark, Sweden, and the Middle East. He has designed corporate identity programmes, logotypes, annual and environmental reports, brochures, packaging and magazines for Danish and international companies, as well as designing complete alphabets and pictogram systems.

Morten Rostgaard Olsen is an graphic designer living and working in Copenhagen. After five years studing at The Danish School of Art and Design, he now runs a design studio where he supplies solutions for corporate design, typography, and typefaces. He’s also become a teacher in all these subjects. Among his clients at home and abroad are The Ministry of Education in Copenhagen, the first user of his typeface FF Olsen.
Morten is a member of The Association of Danish Designers MDD.

Project Diacritics Updated - Now with Galleries

I have expanded the Project Diacritics website. Now you can easily attach pictures to particular languages and diacritical marks. We welcome your submissions of fine examples of correct accents. Let’s help other designers to create accents for Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, and other scripts.

Diacritics project is a free on-line database of knowledge and
experience - how to design correct diacritics (what size, shape and position
an accent should have). Text concerning the history, use, languages, and
also some technical information is related to each diacritical mark. The
project is on-line at http://diacritics.typo.cz.

Everyone is invited to check it. If you would like to edit
the text, simply Register and Login - every page can be edited, corrected or
enhanced with pictures by anyone (Wikipedia model).
