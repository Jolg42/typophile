It most certainly is hand drawn. If an existing typeface was the model, someone could come up with a match. In the meantime, looking for “railway” at MyFonts, brings some interesting results. Perhaps unsurprisingly, Western Railway ( [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/western-railway/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416125149/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/western-railway/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/jnlevine/western-railway/")) has a similar flavour to the words (it needs some condensing for “Municipal”), while something from Greater Albion Typefounders ( [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/birmingham-new-street/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416125149/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/birmingham-new-street/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/birmingham-new-street/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/great-bromwich/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416125149/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/great-bromwich/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/gatf/great-bromwich/")) could give you the |F|.

The winged \_M\_, heavy letters and big serifs have some similarity to Heirloom Artcraft Black ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416125149im_/http:/typophile.com/files/HeirloomArtcraftBlackAsMunicip_3950.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/baselinefonts/heirloom-artcraft/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416125149/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/baselinefonts/heirloom-artcraft/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/baselinefonts/heirloom-artcraft/") compare to a slick lighter version of Artcraft ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416125149im_/http:/typophile.com/files/ArtcraftAsMunicip_6126.gif) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ascender/artcraft-pro/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416125149/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ascender/artcraft-pro/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ascender/artcraft-pro/") Don
