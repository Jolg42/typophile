They've been using Officina for a while, but now only for really large settings on the cover, so you must mean the new face for headings: it's a custom version of FF Zine (Spiekermann was involved in the recent redesign). There are some things I love about Zine, but to me it seems to lack some polish. As for the body text, I think it didn't change with the recent redesign, but I forget what it is. I find it a bit mannered. hhp

Anonymous @ Tue, 2003-11-04 15:12
The old text face was simply called _Economist_ or _Economist 101_ and was designed, I believe, by Briem. You can see a sample here [http://www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=243431](http://web.archive.org/web/20050526221214/http:/www.fonts.com/findfonts/detail.asp?pid=243431) or in the Precision Type Reference Guide (5.0) if you have that at your disposal. The redesigned face is called _EcoType_; it's clearly a derivative of _Economist_, but is a little lighter and slightly more condensed in comparison. But James is right: Erik is the source. Jon
