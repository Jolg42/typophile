Iris, I don't understand your question. What does "ofc" and "w&w" mean? Weights beginning with a 4 are light, Weights begininig with a 5 are regular Weights beginning with a 6 are medium, Weights beginning with a 7 are bold, styles ending with a 5 are upright roman Styles ending with a 6 are italic Styles ending with a 7 are condensed, In other words. Univers 55 is regular weight standard width roman Univers 65 is medium weight standard width roman ChrisL
