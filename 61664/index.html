Anonymous @ 6 Sep 2009 — 6:11pm
Well, if you're after the boldness and high contrast you might want to take a look at this [collection](http://web.archive.org/web/20150227021643/http:/www.fontshop.com/fontlist/genres/didone_extra_bold/). Hoefler & Frere Jones's [Didot](http://web.archive.org/web/20150227021643/http:/www.typography.com/fonts/font_styles.php?productLineID=100004) with optical sizes is also worth a look. There's also [Leitura](http://web.archive.org/web/20150227021643/http:/www.dstype.com/index.php?id=11) and [Leitura Display](http://web.archive.org/web/20150227021643/http:/www.dstype.com/index.php?id=11) by Dino dos Santos. It's not a didone, like Bodoni, but it has a rather high contrast and the display cut is very expressive. I'm a sucker for [Bodoni Egyptian](http://web.archive.org/web/20150227021643/http:/www.veer.com/products/typedetail.aspx?image=SHT0000006) (predominantly for text), by Nick Shinn, which would pair nicely with most other Didones.

Don't know if this is close enough to Poster Bodoni for you (or bold enough), but speaking of Nick Shinn, his Scotch Modern has a nice display cut, but also a text size and even a "micro" cut that seems very readable even at seriously small sizes.
