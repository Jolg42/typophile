a couple centuries late - but the 17th century manuscript 'Miracles of Mary" produced at a single scriptorium in Gondar, Ethiopia, is on display in Chicago. A page is turned every 2 weeks for viewing. Not sure if you can really study the rotation online. But the site is www.artic.edu The sheets supposedely still have the scored lines that guided the calligrapher in aligning text - could be intersting.
