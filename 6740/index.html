- Try increasing the mi spacing combo. - The right leg of the m needs more weight. - Maybe a subtle little flat rectangular dot on the i (not far off the stem). It would clear up that sequence and wouldn't hurt legibility on the m the other way. - The tricky part is the JA LY. Try adding a small flourish on the tail of your y moving right. It would add the left stroke on the top of the J and solve the d confusion. Scotts suggestion could work too. - Try the Ja connector at a little more of an angle and slightly thicker.
