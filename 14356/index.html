Hi,

Is there a typeface found in Windows PCs that would be an acceptable substitute?

Scott, [Century Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044423/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/century-gothic/) is somewhat close to Futura and is included with some versions of [Microsoft Office](http://web.archive.org/web/20150218044423/http:/office.microsoft.com/en-us/assistance/HA011872311033.aspx). So if your company has Office installed on the machines give that font a try.
