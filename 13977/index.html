Here's some more info on the LogoWorks issue: Just wanted to pass on some information about the "logo design" company LogoWorks. The firm has been getting a lot of major glowing press lately: Wall Street Journal [http://tinyurl.com/9o4w5](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/tinyurl.com/9o4w5 "http://tinyurl.com/9o4w5") Entrepreneur Magazine [http://www.entrepreneur.com/logocontest/0,7353,,00.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/www.entrepreneur.com/logocontest/0,7353,,00.html "http://www.entrepreneur.com/logocontest/0,7353,,00.html") Over the past few days more and more people have discovered that numerous LogoWorks designs have actually been blatantly ripped off from other designers. You may want to take a look at some of the discussion and evidence at some of these links: The Origins of Brands Blog - written in praise of LogoWorks. The comments offer a great deal of info: [http://tinyurl.com/bs8gb](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/tinyurl.com/bs8gb "http://tinyurl.com/bs8gb") BadDesignKills [http://www.baddesignkills.com/logoworks/index.htm](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/www.baddesignkills.com/logoworks/index.htm "http://www.baddesignkills.com/logoworks/index.htm") ThePreparedMind [http://tinyurl.com/bbv9c](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/tinyurl.com/bbv9c "http://tinyurl.com/bbv9c") Morea's Photos [http://tinyurl.com/dmhrr](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/tinyurl.com/dmhrr "http://tinyurl.com/dmhrr") HOW Design Forum Discussion [http://tinyurl.com/bej4q](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/tinyurl.com/bej4q "http://tinyurl.com/bej4q") A HOW Forum "discussion" with a LW designer [http://tinyurl.com/a9rry](http://web.archive.org/web/20120623033237/http:/tinyurl.com/a9rry "http://tinyurl.com/a9rry") I'm sure this will get more interesting as the week continues. Personally, I feel such "logo mills" do a huge disservice to the graphic design industry. The outright stealing of the work of other designers is another issue and its blatancy is incredible to me - and should outrage all designers and the clients being taken for what initially may be an inexpensive ride to get a new logo. How many stolen designs have been repackaged and sold to unexpecting clients by LogoWorks? - J.
