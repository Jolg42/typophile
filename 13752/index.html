I think that this is probably a custom lettering (artwork) job, but the font shown as Diana in the Solotype Catalog, and sold as [Ballantines Script](http://web.archive.org/web/20060604052314/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/ballantines-script/ef-bold/testdrive.html?s=Thompsons&p=48)might be a fairly close substitute. At least it would be a place to start, and with a few modifications, you could come fairly close. You could also browse the Casual Flowing Script samples in [Part 7 of the Script Font ID Guide](http://web.archive.org/web/20060604052314/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/Script07Pg1.html) for similar styles. - Mike Yanega
