Carolus was originally made in 1953 by the Swedish type designer Karl-Erik Forsberg and released by "Berlingska stilgjuteriet i Lund". He was a gifted calligrapher and over the years he made some hundred hand drawn book covers apart form designing typefaces. It's a pain to see that someone just ripped it off and put it up on "Dafont". More info here; [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Erik\_Forsberg](http://web.archive.org/web/20100323041006/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Erik_Forsberg "http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl-Erik\_Forsberg") Cheers /Stefan H
