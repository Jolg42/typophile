I'm thinking it's probably a font, rather than a completely custom job. A custom job would never have passed up on the opportunity for an FFI ligature! It is likely, however, that the letters got a few tweaks along the logo design path. Beton is very close. [Geometric Slabserif 712 from Bitstream](http://web.archive.org/web/20100202111215/http:/www.fontshop.com/fontlist/families/geometric_slabserif_712_extra_bold/) is another close one, but I have yet to find an oblique. If you'll indulge the diversion, here are a few products that didn't pass up the chance to class up their act by sneaking in a 3-letter ligature. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20100202111215im_/http:/typophile.com/files/jiffylube_5466.gif) ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20100202111215im_/http:/typophile.com/files/Jiffy_Pop_3577.jpg)
