Anonymous @ 29 October, 2006 - 5:31am
The short answer is keep it as a suite, bearing in mind that a letterhead obviously has a different purpose to a comp. slip and a business card. The relationship of letterhead to comp. slip is much closer than the business card in that they will need the same information, postal address, registered address and registered number, probably with a generic telephone, fax, e-mail and url, wheras a business card has some of this information but with the name of the individual, with a direct line and e-mail and is not primarily intended to be written on. The logo should be the same unless you have a clear reason why it shouldn’t be, although you might consider if you have a particularly vertical logo you might require a horizontal logo for some applications, for example van graphics or website masthead. The treatment of the text needn’t be exactly the same although I would keep to the typeface family, the proportions of point size and leading the same. It really depends on the design and requirements of the client, as a student (and I am guessing this a college project) you are probably best off starting with keeping the elements the same. Try searching for stationery projects in some design agency portfolios. Tim
