Slighty OT Does anyone here have any information about [Jock Kinneir](http://web.archive.org/web/20110812175213/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Jock%20Kinneir) and [Margaret Calvert](http://web.archive.org/web/20110812175213/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Margaret%20Calvert)'s [Rail Alphabet](http://web.archive.org/web/20110812175213/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_Alphabet)? In the mid 70's I went to Maidstone College of Art in Kent, UK. I recall lots of British Rail brand guideline posters all over the walls in the commercial design studio. In those days, I was committed to drawing, painting and latterly, photography and had little more than a passing interest in graphic design and typography. Given the quantity, and quality, of material on the walls, I suspect there was a connection between the design team and the college. I wish I had paid more attention at the time.
