That’d be Nick Curtis’ [Tucson Two Step](http://web.archive.org/web/20060523005408/http:/www.moorstation.org/typoasis/designers/curtis/pages/t.htm). Thanks to Sander de Voogt’s [archive of all things Nick](http://web.archive.org/web/20060523005408/http:/home.planet.nl/~voogt053/), we can read these references any time and, for some fonts, even see images of the [original material](http://web.archive.org/web/20060523005408/http:/home.planet.nl/~voogt053/Pics/TucsonTwoStep.jpg).
