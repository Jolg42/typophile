Linotype’s LinoLetter for January 2006 – A Look Back at 2005’s Top Fonts

Check out the full version of this month’s newsletter at the Linotype website: http://www.linotype.com/8-38-8-19227/issue200601.html

Content Preview: Fresh fonts for the design year 2006 and an inspiring look back at the most-wanted fonts of 2005 await you inside this month’s LinoLetter. With Frutiger Capitalis, the newest decorative family from Adrian Frutiger, and Bickley Script, a smoothly elegant script face designed by Alan Meeks, we have two atypical fonts on display that are sure to enlighten the new year. Our brand-new High Noon Value Pack, a collection of first-class Wild West fonts, also leaves little to be denied. This edition also includes a glance at the chart-topping fonts from the past year – can you guess which typefaces our customers loved the most? Rounding out this month’s news is a peek into photos from the most recent birthday party of type legend Hermann Zapf.

Announcing: Registration has opened for the first major conference on Arabic Calligraphy and Typography in the Middle East.
