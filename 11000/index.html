In response to Tiff: This typeface was created based on a modular form with minimal elements juxtaposed with varied calligraphic components. I wanted to create an aesthetic that reflected a sordid, but confidently structured attitude. The second line uses the "snap-on" dots I'm working on; depending on the form of the word it may be desirable to have dots on a character. Also, I may employ snap-on ascenders/descenders that face the opposite direction. Any suggestions? P.S. I'll try to check out Industria. Thanks.
