If the |A| is what make you think it's not Ronda, depending on the age of the sample, it could well be a non digitised alternate glyph. Edit: confirmed here: [http://fontsinuse.com/uses/8676/official-california-visitor-map](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415095532/http:/fontsinuse.com/uses/8676/official-california-visitor-map "http://fontsinuse.com/uses/8676/official-california-visitor-map") It looks that, like many 1970s' typefaces, there were plenty of variations that never made it to digital: [http://fontsinuse.com/typefaces/10234/itc-ronda](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415095532/http:/fontsinuse.com/typefaces/10234/itc-ronda "http://fontsinuse.com/typefaces/10234/itc-ronda")
