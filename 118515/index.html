Original name should be **Tasmin Bold**. It should appear on Dan X Solo 1979 collection "Sans Serif Display Alphabets" (p. 96). I know there's a freebie floating around but can't say about its legitimacy. Note it also carries the other /S (not the one of your sample). ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051525im_/http:/typophile.com/files/tasminbold_5551.jpg)

There is also Tasmin by David Crosby. I don't know who copied whom. Previous discussion [http://typophile.com/node/107791](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051525/http:/typophile.com/node/107791 "http://typophile.com/node/107791") I have no info on the origin of the pre-digital font, other than that Dan X. Solo gave the name of Tasmin to this design, which he may have purchased as metal type. This Art Nouveau design is a variation on Quaint by the Dickinson Type Foundry of Boston. Aka Desdemona by Karl Brendler & Söhne of Vienna. Desdemona [http://luc.devroye.org/KarlBrendler+Soehne-Desdemona.jpg](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317051525/http:/luc.devroye.org/KarlBrendler+Soehne-Desdemona.jpg "http://luc.devroye.org/KarlBrendler+Soehne-Desdemona.jpg") Both are circa 1900. I don't know which came first. Several good digital versions. Don
