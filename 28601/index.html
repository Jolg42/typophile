We usually like to offer some suggestions for alternatives, and here are three that might be similar in feeling, in addition to the ones Yves mentioned. I found these looking in the Sign Lettering section of the Script Font ID Guide: [Geis](http://web.archive.org/web/20061111004730/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/galapagos/geis/) from Galapagos, by George Ryan [Cyrano Modern](http://web.archive.org/web/20061111004730/http:/www.unifonts.com/c_texhtm/cyranomodernregtex.htm) by Victor De Castro, Unifonts. [Arjorie](http://web.archive.org/web/20061111004730/http:/www.unifonts.com/a_pages/arjoriemedium.htm), also by Victor De Castro, Unifonts. In my opinion both these guys are artists, who put a lot of personality into their fonts. - Mike Yanega
