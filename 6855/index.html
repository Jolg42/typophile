(x) Hil St. Soul - Pump (customized) {Mike Y}

I think this was made using Blippo Black or Pump. The ‘h’ is made from an ’l’ attached to a reversed ’s’. All the connections are added on. The tracking is closed so that letters touch. If you give me a few minutes, I can use Pump to show you what I mean.
