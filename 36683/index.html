I got a sneak peak at some of the questions ahead of time (included at end of post). I’m told that the drawing mode has several levels. First you have to draw basic handwriting-like glyphs to make sure you know how to use the stylus . Then the second level is drawing sans serifs, third level humanists, forth didots, fifth angle-weighted serifs, and the bonus round is fraktur. It also has training modes for teaching sutterlin and charlamagne handwriting. They plan on coming out with more editions for teaching other scripts, although when they do the Ge’ez version, they use a terrible font that looks like a Times being shoe-horned into it (or maybe it’s the other way around) that’s unreadable to everyone in Ethiopia. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20081202183226im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/fonts_typographic_minigames_4474.png) _«El futuro es una línea tan fina que apenas nos damos cuenta de pintarla nosotros mismos».
