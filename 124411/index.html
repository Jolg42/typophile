Suggestions for good monospace font?

I’m not a designer myself, but I am a fan of nice fonts. It’s been quite interesting to see how much interesting is being paid to typefaces these days.

By profession, I’m a programmer, so I need to use monospace fonts for nearly everything. While I’ve heard people gushing on about Helvetica, I’ve never seen much discussion of fonts that could replace Lucida and Courier.

Can any of you point me to some excellent, readable, beautiful typefaces that follow the monospace standard?

I see that none of the examples has tried as hard as IBM did with Bookface Academic, or Diplomat (not available for Selectrics in the U.S., but it was available for U.K. typewriters) to be a monospaced typeface that doesn't look like a monospace typeface. Of course, trying to make a monospace version of a traditional Roman typeface _does_ run the very strong risk of making a monospace typeface that looks even worse than one that doesn't do that, but IBM in those two examples seems to have managed fairly well. If you scroll down, you can see a little bit of Diplomat on [this page](http://web.archive.org/web/20150421193738/http:/munk.org/typecast/2011/04/25/1964-nomda-blue-book-ibm-font-styles/).
