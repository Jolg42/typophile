Anonymous @ 8.Oct.2005 2.24pm
»Gravur Condensed« has rounded tips. The logo you link to does not have rounded endings. The logo font is much more like a »DIN 1451« or a variation thereof. In fact, it is not DIN, however, it is so close as to call it a variation of DIN. The upper bowl of the capital B is smaller on the logo. DIN’s capital B has identically sized bowls both upper and lower. Also, the endings on the capital S finish with a straight edge on the logo, whereas DIN’s capital S does not have such a feature. More searching is needed. For a DIN example of this company name, see: [http://flickr.com/photos/luxuryluke/3081004809/](http://web.archive.org/web/20090614065525/http:/flickr.com/photos/luxuryluke/3081004809/ "http://flickr.com/photos/luxuryluke/3081004809/")
