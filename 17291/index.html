Just a little design history. My source told me that John Downer did the last version the now defunct logo. <cite>"My main objective, back in 1991-1992, when I was<br> working in SF, was to center the v in "Chevron" and<br> I nearly accomplished it to my own satisfaction. It<br> was just a tad off, due to the fact that C has to be<br> wider than r. In short, the h matches the width of<br> the n; and the e matches the width of the o. The<br> balancing act is definitely not a merit of the new<br> incarnation. Its symmetry is rather poor, in fact."<br> </cite> It is nice to here such effort being put into something. He was their signage consultant for a while as well. I can see that the new Chevron logo is softer, but if the goal was to move away from military-like ribbons, I think they didn't quite make it.
