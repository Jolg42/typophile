A little too wide, but otherwise rather close -- except for the squared corners on \_C\_ and \_S\_ and the leg on \_R\_ -- is YWFT Matter [https://www.youworkforthem.com/font/T0264/ywft-matter?affid=109571](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/https:/www.youworkforthem.com/font/T0264/ywft-matter?affid=109571 "https://www.youworkforthem.com/font/T0264/ywft-matter?affid=109571") The font is a digital version of Information Extra Bold Wide (Breitfett) which was designed by Friedrich Karl Sallwey in 1958 as an addition to the Information family. It is probably best known today from its use in Pablo Ferro’s title sequence for “Bullitt”. [Source Stephen Coles [https://www.flickr.com/photos/stewf/11794966995/in/photostream/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/https:/www.flickr.com/photos/stewf/11794966995/in/photostream/ "https://www.flickr.com/photos/stewf/11794966995/in/photostream/") ] Don

Pretty convinced it has never been a font as it, rather a customization of an existing pre-digital sans. Some I like as alternative: FF [Softsoul](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/softsoul) Black (not bad, some corners needing to be squared and it'd be not so far), [Sneakers](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/positype/sneakers-pro) (more work), [Mansfield](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/filmotype/mansfield) (nice too), [Rika](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/thinkdust/rika) (hardly squooshed but why not), [Iranica](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/naghachian/iranica), [Nima](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/naghachian/nima), [Philosophy](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.dafont.com/philosophy.font) (almost perfect), [LeftOvers II](http://web.archive.org/web/20150310151814/http:/www.ffonts.net/LeftOvers-II.font)
