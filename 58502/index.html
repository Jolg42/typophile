That K is different than in the Thermotype shown in Mac McGrew's book. So is the C, G, and the M and N. Are you sure this is ATF Thermotype? If this is an 'interpretation' then they took a lot of liberties with the original. A similar font that does exist in digital form is [Hollywood Deco](http://web.archive.org/web/20140804042255/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/hollywood-deco-sg/) (also an ATF typeface, from 1932, a year after Thermotype). - Mike Yanega
