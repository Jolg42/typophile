According to Landor, it's based on a 1920s typeface (unnamed) that they then modified: http://landor.com/#!/work/case-studies/council-on-foreign-relations/ Futura (designed in 1927) comes very close, save for the angle of the stroke terminals on the C. There is a companion CRF monogram that appears to be based on Bernhard Script and/or Baskerville.

I agree that the overall result is quite pleasing. As long as it holds together visually, I don't mind the Dr. Frankenstein approach--provided the stitches and bolts aren't visible.
