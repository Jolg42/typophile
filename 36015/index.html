The whole thing is almost certainly custom drawn, but it's a textura blackletter. You could try [LTC Goudy Text](http://web.archive.org/web/20111219111314/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/lanston/ltc-goudy-text) or [Old English Text](http://web.archive.org/web/20111219111314/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/agfa/old-english-text) to get decently close.
Anonymous @
Another common one that is very close to most of it is called Wedding Text.
Anonymous @
I think the lower case looks most like a Fraktur, because of the 'a', and [Fette Fraktur](http://web.archive.org/web/20111219111314/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/fette-fraktur/) seems pretty close. A simplified version of the caps of [Engraver's Old English](http://web.archive.org/web/20111219111314/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/engravers-old-english/) might do for the initial letters. - Mike Yanega
