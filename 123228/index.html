Chesterfield was designed by Alan Meeks for Letraset in 1977. The glyphs in the digital Chesterfield, including \_T\_ and \_F\_, seem to match the original, as shown in my Letraset product catalog from 1985. Chesterfield was also digitizatized by Uwe Borchert as Mops Antiqua (Pug Serif) [http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-56498.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317114954/http:/luc.devroye.org/fonts-56498.html "http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-56498.html") It does not have the modified \_T\_ and \_F\_. So I agree with Fivos that the letters in\_FOUNDATION\_ are customized. Don
