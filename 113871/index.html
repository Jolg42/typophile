Excellent character in the lettering on this sign, presumably Austria, and after circa 1910. Don't know of a digital match, or a pre-digital for that matter. The style shows a mixture of influences, including Art Nouveau and the Arts & Crafts movement. Some of the fonts in this link might be of interest.
