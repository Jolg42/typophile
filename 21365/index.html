Hi Chuck Litho Antique doesn't ring any bells directly – maybe someone else will know. [Venus](http://web.archive.org/web/20111208233233/http:/www.ftbauer.com/bswebs.G3?log%3Apkid=2&log%3Acompany=22&log%3Acontract=1&log%3Aserviceid=1&xtd%3Alanguage=es&pkid=2&idcompany=22&idcontract=1&idservice=1&language=es&behaviour=PRODUCT&menukeys=TYPES_BUSCADOR&buscador=venus) is an older grotesk from Bauer, I think, maybe contemporary to Akzidenz Grotesk, Standard and Basic Commercial. It is in Jaspert's Encyclopedia of Typefaces and also Rookledge's TypeFinder, so there is a known date of release for it. Not too sure about the designer though.
