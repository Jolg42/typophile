Gentium and Charis updated

Two free SIL fonts have been updated.

Charis:
Overview at http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?item_id=CharisSI
Download and List of Changes at http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?item_id=CharisSIL_download
Note the Charis SIL Compact; the “Compact” refers to the leading.

Gentium:
News: http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?item_id=Gentium_news
