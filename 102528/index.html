Hi Matthew and welcome to Typophile. Don't be surprised, most of the brilliant design educators who had the greatest impact on me were not the greatest designers as I imagined them to be. Regarding online resources; [I Love Typography](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/ilovetypography.com/) is a great starting point. Few other websites which I have in my mind at the moment are: [typefoundry.blogspot.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/typefoundry.blogspot.com/), [briem.net](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/66.147.242.192/~operinan/index.html), [100types.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.100types.com/), [kupferschrift.de](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/kupferschrift.de/cms/), [paulshawletterdesign.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.paulshawletterdesign.com/), [Microsoft.com/typography](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.microsoft.com/typography/default.mspx), [typotheque.com/articles](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Fwww.typotheque.com/articles), [fontfeed.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/fontfeed.com/), [typedia.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/typedia.com/) and [typographica.org](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/typographica.org/). [The Elements of Typographic Style](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/typographica.org/typography-books/the-elements-of-typographic-style-4th-edition/) by Robert Bringhurst appears to be a popular read amongst designers and typographers, some other books which I enjoyed reading were: [Counterpunch: Making Type in the 16th Century, Designing Typefaces Now](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.goodreads.com/book/show/463387.Counterpunch) by Fred Smeijers, [Anatomy of a Typeface](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.amazon.com/Anatomy-Typeface-Alexander-S-Lawson/dp/0879233338/ref=pd_sim_b_1) by Alexander S. Lawson, [Alphabet: “The History, Evolution, and Design of the Letters We Use Today”](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.amazon.com/Alphabet-History-Evolution-Design-Letters/dp/0823001709/ref=sr_1_4?s=books&ie=UTF8&qid=1366871880&sr=1-4&keywords=allan+haley) by Allan Haley. All of the books by [Doyald Young](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.doyaldyoung.com/) are a must in my opinion, if you can afford them. Additionally, see also journals and magazines like: [Codex,](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/codexmag.com/) [Eye](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.eyemagazine.com/), [Baseline](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/www.baselinemagazine.com/) and [Creative Review](http://web.archive.org/web/20140720165650/http:/typophile.com/wiki/www.creativereview.co.uk).
