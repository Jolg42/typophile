The only versions I could find were also TTF. I think this font was used as a film typeface. I show a number of old scans as well as some current multi-line fonts in the [Lined Fonts ID Guide](http://web.archive.org/web/20130118024312/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/LinedFontGuide01.html). That might help you find an alternative. Sources are listed in the Guide. Another possbility, which may not work well, is to convert the font from TTF to Type1. I say it may not work well, because the TT outlines may not be good quality, and that would make 'garbage in- garbage out' situation. - Mike Yanega

Prisma was originally produced by the [Klingspor](http://web.archive.org/web/20130118024312/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Klingspor) foundry. It is a special version of [Rudolf Koch](http://web.archive.org/web/20130118024312/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Rudolf%20Koch)'s [Kabel](http://web.archive.org/web/20130118024312/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Kabel).
