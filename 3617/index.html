It looks like it's based on an old metal/wood typeface called Hellenic Wide or Antique Extended. See the links in [this interview](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011113921/http:/curbcontrol.com/interview/bob_dahlquist/) with Bob Dahlquist. There's a free version by Pelle Piano called Stereo and a very good revival by the Letterror boys called [Zapata](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011113921/http:/www.letterror.com/foundry/zapata/index.html). Also, see Aboutype's [Antique Central](http://web.archive.org/web/20131011113921/http:/www.myfonts.com/FontFamily28396.html).
