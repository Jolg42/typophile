You could get pretty close to \_BRISAN\_ with PAG Bankas ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317055933im_/http:/typophile.com/files/PAGBankasAsBrisanLogoTypophile114879_3486.jpg) with the bottom of the \_S\_ swept to the left. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317055933im_/http:/typophile.com/files/BrisanLogoTypophile114879b_5682.jpg) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/prop-a-ganda/pag-bankas/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317055933/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/prop-a-ganda/pag-bankas/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/prop-a-ganda/pag-bankas/") As for \_Carriages\_ the g and s, and perhaps other letters are significantly modified, but you could use a script like Amazone which has similar \_r\_ and \_s\_ [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/tilde/amazone/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317055933/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/tilde/amazone/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/tilde/amazone/") or pick a script with all letters but \_s\_ such as Commercial Script [http://www.myfonts.com/search/Commercial+Script/fonts/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317055933/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/Commercial+Script/fonts/ "http://www.myfonts.com/search/Commercial+Script/fonts/") Palace Script [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/palace-script/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317055933/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/palace-script/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/palace-script/") or Sackers Italian Script [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/sackers-italian-script/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317055933/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/sackers-italian-script/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/mti/sackers-italian-script/") I trust that another font detective will post something closer for the script, or perhaps even an exact match.
