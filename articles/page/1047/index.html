brasilian text faces

i’m after a certain kind of brasilian text face, and i was after some recommendations. i’ve checked out the very useful wiki http://typophile.com/wiki/brazil and some of the sites listed on it. there’s also http://cg.scs.carleton.ca/~luc/brasilfonts.html and http://www.typophile.com/node/13220

but this post relates to something a little more specific and i was hoping you guys could help me out. here are the details:

–i’m after a brasilian-designed, or failing that s. american face.

–it’s for text i’m setting on the brasilian diva elis regina (not for publication–i am setting the text just as a learning excersise). so it could somehow reflect some of the character of brasilian music, or better still, of the fiery singer, known as ’little pepper'.

–specifically, i would like something taut and beziery, with M-formula-type curves; perhaps part of that certain recent trend of south american faces some have termed ‘fauve’. http://typophile.com/node/5303

–must be suitable for setting extended text, must have OS figs, pref SC also. OTF preferably. an extensive range of weights wd be nice, but is rather less important.

–budget is also a consideration, as this is just a personal project, and i’ve spent all my money on brasilian music:)

miguel, is your quetzal available? that fits the bill totally!

any suggestions welcome

Calacalacity - Invitation

This post is an invitation to take part in Calacalacity.

Calacalacity is an installation and a website project that take place in Lille, France.
As part of “Bombaysers de Lille”, a cultural event dealing with indian arts,
we were asked to realise a project hosted at “La Maison de l’Architecture
et de la Ville”.

The project is composed of a fresco and the calacalacity.fr website.

The fresco aims to be the towncenter of this imaginery indian city.
Visitors have the opportunity to create and develop it through a flash interface
and to see their creations placed on the map.

It is indirectly link to typography as you may see in the image attached.

Please feel free to have a look around and to participate.

http://www.calacalacity.fr

The télescopique crew
