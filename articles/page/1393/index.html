Photoshop CS prevents currency scanning

According to BoingBoing.net, “Photoshop CS automatically detects images of US currency and prevents the manipulation, copying and pasting of same.”

The article is linked from a Slashdot article, which links to the Adobe forum on the subject.

Interestingly, it prevents users from engaging in what the US government defines as legal usage of currency images. This is both depressing and scary. Apparently, it’s restricted to only certain denominations, but isn’t restricted to just US currency. Also, there are many caveats to what exactly will and will not open. Does anyone else know anything about this?

Now it has been some time since I have read print – and by read I actually mean ‘reading’ – or any other ‘design’ magazine. I have recently started taking home these periodicals that have been piling up at the studio for the past few years. Tonight been one of the previously mentioned ‘sleepless nights’, I decide to pull out the latest issue and give it the once through. The issue contains a few good articles, one notably on clearview (the font)and its designers. But back to my original rant, has anyone seen the art direction of this magazine? More clearly the type setting? You would think a magazine of design – printed design – and one expressing ‘opinions’ on type and type design would in the least be set half way decent? I would provide scans, but as previously noted, Im in bed . Im not even asking for anything great, like using type that does not come bundled with Adobe apps, but maybe something as simple as hanging punctuation. Is this too much to ask? I can’t remember exactly what print looked like a year or so ago, but I don’t rmemeber anyhting this lifeless, and dare I tout ‘unprofessionally set’?
