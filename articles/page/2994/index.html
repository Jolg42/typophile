Heroic Condensed by Silas Dilworth at TypeTrust

Here’s my latest, Heroic Condensed, a geometric sans in eight weights plus obliques.

http://www.typetrust.com/font/heroiccondensed

…and the PDF…

http://www.typetrust.com/fonts/specimen_pdfs/TypeTrust_HeroicCondensed.pdf

Regular price is $240 for the 16 fonts set. It’s on sale for $160 until June 11, 2008. Individual weights are half off.

About the typeface…

Heroic Condensed is the graphic embodiment of the idealized, no-nonsense, narrow grotesque. From poster design to editorial layout, Heroic Condensed is intended for a range of uses—a worthy addition to any designer’s font collection.

Not just a quickly generated spin-off of a text family, Heroic Condensed has been crafted from scratch with a structural logic of its own: a fusion of pure geometry and optical balance. At its core you will find a geometric skeleton, but mathematics alone does not make attractive type. Heroic Condensed has been honed by and for the acute and sublime workings of the human eye. The finest adjustments have been applied to every curve and every angle of its letterforms for even color and visual equality across a versatile range of eight weights. Even regarding usability, Heroic Condensed exhibits technical refinement. With careful attention paid to the economy of metrics, the need for extensive kerning tables has been alleviated by a hand-tuned matrix of sidebearings in conjunction with already streamlined glyph designs.

Heroic Condensed is further bolstered with Opentype Small Caps, Tabular Figures, Fractions, and the standard TypeTrust character set which includes a full array of Latin diacritics.

Karel Martens to speak at St Bride Conference (Update)

Submitted by Miss Tiffany : 12.May.2008 1.12pm

Seeking Inspiration: creative thinking around the design process

Seventh annual St Bride Library conference
15-16 May 2008
At St Bride Library, Bride Lane, Fleet St, London EC4Y 8EE

The greatly respected Dutch book designer Karel Martens will be speaking
at this year’s conference.

Karel will start by showing three (very) short films: ‘notforresale’, ‘lost
and found’, ‘homage to the alphabet’. This will lead on to a
conversation between Karel and Robin Kinross, with unpredictable results.

Karel Martens is a graphic designer, specializing in typography. He has
won numerous awards – a 1996 prize resulting in the publication of a
monograph of his work Printed matter. He has taught graphic design for
over thirty years and is a much-revered mentor of Dutch Graphic Design
inspiring younger generations towards an approach to design which
prioritises intelligence and integrity. He is a co-founder of the
Werkplaats Typografie in Arnhem, a two-year workshop education which he
now oversees with fellow designer Armand Mevis.

Robin Kinross is a typographer and editor. He runs Hyphen Press, whose
authors include Christopher Burke, Fred Smeijers, Gerrit Noordzij, Jost
Hochuli, and David Wild. He has collaborated as writer and publisher
with Karel Martens on the books Printed matter and Counterprint. A third
edition of Printed matter is in preparation now.
