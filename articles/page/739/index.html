How to Create Modular Scales

Designers:

The ‘Bringhurst’ offers four examples of modular scales, including the Whole and Half Pica modulars used be Le Corbusier and a Medieval Interval Scale.

The first is easy to understand. The second and third are baffling. My question regards the third: how does it work?

After a certain point, the values on the left side of the stick are doubles of those on the right. Easy enough. But even though one of the two ratios (2:3 and 1:2) suggests doubling (1:2), this is not consistent from one side to the next. Doubling ‘1’ does not yield ‘4’. The values on the right side of the stick don’t seem at a glance to have anything to do with the (2:3) ratio (e.g. 1, 3, 4.5, 7, 10).

My goal is to understand this so as to be able to construct such scales generally. Basic mathematical competence, I am sure, is all that is required.

All help welcome.

Thanks.
