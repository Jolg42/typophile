PR/ Agfa Monotype Releases Fonts from Australian Type Foundry, Patricia Lillie and Nerfect Worldwide on Fonts.com

Submitted by Jared Benson : 18.Sep.2003 10.06am

Agfa Monotype Releases Fonts from Australian Type Foundry, Patricia Lillie and Nerfect Worldwide on Fonts.com

Wilmington, Mass. and Salfords, Surrey, England, Sept. 18, 2003 - Agfa Monotype Corp. has introduced font collections from three type foundries, adding 79 new fonts and font packages to Fonts.com. This is the third installation of foundry collections in three weeks. The fonts from Australian Type Foundry, Patricia Lillie and Nerfect Worldwide follow the releases from Fontsmith, Baseline Fonts, Bannigan Artworks, Chris Costello and Joe VanDerBos. The collections can be viewed at www.fonts.com/foundries.

“When expanding our offering on Fonts.com, we evaluate typefaces for their originality, creativity and their ability to solve practical design applications,” said Allan Haley, director of words and letters at Agfa Monotype.
