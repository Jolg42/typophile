And then comes the author’s comment: ‘[The sans serif used was Gill Sans, the serifed face was Jenson. The slight preference recorded by adults for the serifed face could be explained simply by the fact that most of their reading will have been of serifed faces.]’

Since I’m not a native English speaker, I’m a bit confused, for me there seems to be a contradiction between the two sentences. The first says the sans-serif is more legible, but the second sentence says the serif face was it preferred by adults. Is it a contradiction or am I missing something due to my English? If it’s a contradiction, which statement is right in your opinion? As far as I know serif faces are a tiny bit more legible.
