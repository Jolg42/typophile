I second the suggestion of “Dutch Type” by Jan Middendorp. It’s really quite packed with images, biographical info, specimens, and history. Beautifully designed. It is not a typical fluffy coffee-table book, it’s much better.
Anonymous @ 2.Feb.2006 9.10am
Something antique might be a good idea. Perhaps an issue of “The Colophon - a Book Collector’s Quarterly” from the 1930s. The whole set, on sale at Oak Knoll for $1500. They were beautifully printed (and hardcover bound), and each issue used a variety of classic letterpress fonts, on a variety of interesting stocks. fabulous design and illustration. And the articles are fascinating, still.
