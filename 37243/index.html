Typophile Film Fest 4, San Francisco

Submitted by Joe Pemberton : 16.Oct.2007 12.00pm

Punchcut presents

Typophile Film Fest 4, San Francisco
at the Swedish American Hall

Punchcut’s screening of the Typophile Film Festival 4 is best described as a three-hour party centered around a rare and unequaled selection of short typographic films. Hailing from Portugal, The Netherlands, Austria, Canada and the US, the films create a visual mashup of motion design, typographic animation and short stories. It includes broadcast motion design, documentaries and typographic eye candy from Trollbäck + Company, Strange Attractors, Heebok Lee, Nick Shinn, Juan Leguizamon as well as exclusive interviews and cuts from the acclaimed documentary film Helvetica. Thanks to FontShop for helping make this presentation possible.

After the films, we’ll keep the food, drinks and music going until the Swedish American Hall kicks us out.

Doors: 8 - 11pm
Screening: 8:30 - 9:30pm
Date: Thursday, October 25

The Swedish American Hall
2170 Market Street
San Francisco, CA 94114

The Swedish American Hall, upstairs from Café Du Nord, is located on the north side of Market Street, between Church Street and Sanchez Street. It is easily accessible by the F-Line bus stop at the corner of Sanchez and Market or the Muni N, M, L and J lines at the Church Street Station.

Admission: $12

Reserve tickets now!

We expect a full house. Please reserve tickets early.
