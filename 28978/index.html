Liber Librorum

Is there anyone out ther that owns a copy of the typographical item described here and shown below?

I’m interested in the typography of Genesis 1:1–2:4a, and would love it if someone would supply me with scans of the 42 offerings in the Liber Librorum package. Other exemplars are welcome as well.

Years ago I did a set of typographical exercises with the passage as base, using Futura and layering, Stefan Themerson’s principle of Internal Vertical Justification, variations of font size, weight and colour to reflect levels of discourse and patterns of repetition / disruptions of strict repetition.
