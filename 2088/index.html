Speaking of an international audience and implied W's, the rebranding of [Nortwest Airlines](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018220431/http:/www.nwa.com/) comes to mind. Their [old logo](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018220431/http:/www.identityworks.com/Reviews/2003cases/nwa.html) had a subtle w, which was quite well done, implying a compass pointing to northwest as well as the letters N and W, but in order to sound less regional (Northwest) their mark is now just "nwa" worked into the frame of the old logo.
