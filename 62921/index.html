Anonymous @ 10 Oct 2009 — 12:37pm
Stanyan is nice. I think the popularity of this style and the sign painter style is that young people, in particular, respond well to authentic, one-of-a-kind, hand-drawn writing and art. There is an authenticity to handmade stuff that's harder to get with an all-digital approach. And the funny thing is that it's almost always easier to actually draw/write this style by hand than it is to search for a "handwritten" font. Out of the thousands of handwritten fonts, only a few do it well. And they are all so particular. Each handwritten font has its own distinct personality.
