Microgramma/Eurostile has the same basic shape (in bold extended), but includes a lowercase (no stencil cutouts, however) [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile/bold-extended-2-63893/](http://web.archive.org/web/20121130041510/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile/bold-extended-2-63893/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/eurostile/bold-extended-2-63893/") To me, "tough" and "stealth" seem mutually exclusive... I could see "stealth in a VERY light weight sans serif, but "tough" usually tends toward somwthing like Goudy Oldstyle extrabold; you might compromise on something like Glaser Stencil ( [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/glaser-stencil/](http://web.archive.org/web/20121130041510/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/glaser-stencil/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/glaser-stencil/")), there's a "rough" version here too.
