Sans serifs with many stylistic alternates

Hello, I’m looking for a sans-serif font with a lot of stylistic alternates similar to what Whitney and Concourse have. For example so you can swap out the default glyphs for single-story a, more humanist or more geometric t and M. Sense and Sensibility Neology Deco and Neology Grotesque Having invested a considerable amount of time in drawing alternates for most of the alphabet in these, I’m afraid I couldn’t summon the commitment to do condensed and small caps as well! And the Neology has no italic. However, it is always possible to do a good faux by using the stroke command, coupled with a little extra tracking and perhaps a touch of horizontal scaling. And there may be some instances where the next weight up can be used as a small cap. Hypatia Chalet
