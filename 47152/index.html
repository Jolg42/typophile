House industries release Studio Lettering Fonts

Submitted by Miss Tiffany : 7.Jul.2008 1.04pm

After Ken Barber of House Industries took home 3 TDC2008 Awards for Studio Lettering Sable, Sutdio Lettering Slant, and Studio Lettering Swing, I’ve been chomping at the bit to see the entire set.

The folks at House don’t disappoint. They’ve added more temptation beyond the fonts including an ampersand mousepad, ampersand t-shirts (for the whole family no less!), cast iron and cast aluminum ampersands, as well as the Studio Lettering Book. Isn’t there a Christmas in July tradition?
