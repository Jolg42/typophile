1. |UNION MADE| is Archive Tilt: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/archivetype/archive-tilt/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/archivetype/archive-tilt/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/archivetype/archive-tilt/") The other type is too small to positively ID, by may be Wearetrippin Display: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/t26/wearetrippin/display/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/t26/wearetrippin/display/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/t26/wearetrippin/display/") 2. Again, awfully small and low-res sample. |SOLID| resembles Haymaker, though not an exact match to the |S|: [http://www.losttype.com/font/?name=haymaker](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.losttype.com/font/?name=haymaker "http://www.losttype.com/font/?name=haymaker") |HANDMADE| etc. could be one of these: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/penumbra/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/penumbra/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/penumbra/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/hypatia-sans-pro/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/hypatia-sans-pro/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/hypatia-sans-pro/") Something from Harold's Monograms may help you recreate|BAT| and |MFG|: [http://haroldsfonts.com/portfolio/harolds-monograms/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/haroldsfonts.com/portfolio/harolds-monograms/ "http://haroldsfonts.com/portfolio/harolds-monograms/") 3. |CARUSO| appears to be Corki Tuscan: [http://www.typedepot.com/corki](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.typedepot.com/corki "http://www.typedepot.com/corki") |Since 1978| appears to be the free Google web font Damion: [http://www.google.com/fonts/specimen/Damion](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.google.com/fonts/specimen/Damion "http://www.google.com/fonts/specimen/Damion") Rest of the type may be another Google font, PT Sans: [http://www.google.com/fonts/specimen/PT+Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317154932/http:/www.google.com/fonts/specimen/PT+Sans "http://www.google.com/fonts/specimen/PT+Sans") 4.
