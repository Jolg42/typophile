TRAIN & HARTY and INSURANCE used a typeface called Columbus Bold. Beaumarchais is a digitization of Columbus (not bold). The only digital version of Columbus Bold I know of is Brendel Informatik's [Columbus](http://web.archive.org/web/20120104164104/http:/www.qbf.de/cart/cart.exe?gif=tc.gif/Stc02155.gif).
Anonymous @
'Civil Engineer and Architect' was done, I think, in a typeface called Obelisk. I'm not aware of any digital versions, though. Alternatives might be Letterhead Fonts' digitization of Asteroid which they call [LHF Ambrosia](http://web.archive.org/web/20120104164104/http:/letterheadfonts.com/downloads/index.shtml). This is squared and upright like Obelisk, but perhaps too heavy. A font with a similar weight, but fancier, would be [Jugend WF](http://web.archive.org/web/20120104164104/http:/www.waldenfont.com/content.asp?contentpageID=10) from Walden Fonts.
