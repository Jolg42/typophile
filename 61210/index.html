In fact FontFont IS embedding-friendly for apps, documents, and even devices. § 2.2 of [FF’s EULA](http://web.archive.org/web/20140307100340/http:/www.fontshop.com/help/licenses/fontfont/) requires a **subsetted font** used in a **secure format** for permitted **non-editable embedding**. As Sii already mentioned, no format is absolutely secure, but as a foundry you have to define your understanding of security. So ‘secure’ means here: encrypted which may be related to the font file and/or the document containing the embedded font file. Formats with encryption/protection of the whole document are allowed as the fonts are protected within the document. Flash is such a format.
