[...] What I am concerned about is whether I will be able to select my text and switch from RusHelvetica-Bold to the new font without any issues._ Probably not. From what you say, I suspect that the font you have is \_not\_ Unicode-compliant. If that’s so, then you will have problems when using an Unicode-compliant font, like OpenType fonts tend to be. You can, however, make your text Unicode-savvy, by using this tool: [http://www.2cyr.com/decode/?lang=en](http://web.archive.org/web/20081014145533/http:/www.2cyr.com/decode/?lang=en "http://www.2cyr.com/decode/?lang=en")
