The whole point of a custom typeface is that it’s unique; the following suggestions are decent substitutes based on overall form and proportions, but may miss on some of the details (DIN comes closest on both the numerals and the letters). ITC Conduit: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/conduit/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317134200/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/conduit/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/conduit/") Modus: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/garagefonts/modus/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317134200/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/garagefonts/modus/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/garagefonts/modus/") DIN Text Condensed, by Parachute: [http://www.parachute.gr/typefaces/allfonts/din-text-condensed-pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317134200/http:/www.parachute.gr/typefaces/allfonts/din-text-condensed-pro "http://www.parachute.gr/typefaces/allfonts/din-text-condensed-pro")
