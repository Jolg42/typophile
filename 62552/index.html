It's a difficult question. I'm slightly familiar with the Batak people since my manager used to live in Sumatra and is married to a Batak woman. I'm not sure if you're producing an academic essay, a trip report or something larger so I won't advise on layout. I worked on a project a couple of years ago for an international peace organisation that was concerned with endangered communities in Colombia.
