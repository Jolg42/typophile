I’ve been using Tribute a lot lately and found a character I didn’t recognize. It’s in the Italic face and is the Nr. character. I assume it’s an abbreviation for “number” (like No.), but I wanted to consult with the experts.

"Nr." is the German equivalent of "No." in English. Tribute was designed by Frank Heine (a German). See this [wiki article about "No."](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417051054/http:/en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No.). This is the second [German-specific](http://web.archive.org/web/20150417051054/http:/typophile.com/node/62727) font feature I've seen posted about today.
