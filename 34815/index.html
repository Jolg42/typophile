Sure, When you type this: iåiäiö with Galliard (and other “older” fonts) – you clearly see that the diacritics are very much jumping up and down. In the swedish language we use these letters alot in a different combinations and it doesnt look good if the diacritics are not adjusted (as they mostly are today in “newer” or recently updated fonts) Type iåiäiö here: [http://www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=galliard](http://web.archive.org/web/20080623194042/http:/www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=galliard "http://www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=galliard") And also compare Adobes garamond with other Garamonds here, on these letters. [http://www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=garamond](http://web.archive.org/web/20080623194042/http:/www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=garamond "http://www.myfonts.com/search?search%5Btext%5D=garamond") So Matthew Carter did a special Scandinavian edition of his Galliard which is called Galliard Scandinavian.
