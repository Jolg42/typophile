This is not a font: - The letters are in 3D design _and_ overlap. You can have either in a font but not both. (Fonts are no 3D objects) - The two O's are similar but different. Maybe the free font Xanax will do for you.
Anonymous @ 12 Jul 2009 — 11:59am
Actually, while the two Os are different, one looks like the other one but squooshed. So, it COULD be a font (or based on a particular typeface). The 3D effect is certainly something one can do in a font (it's not actually 3D) and the overlap could easily be achieved via negative letterspacing. Not saying it \*is\* a font, but those two things don't preclude it from being one.
Anonymous @ 12 Jul 2009 — 1:13pm
Take a look at the thorn inside the bowl of the O - to me it looks larger in the "condensed" version. It should be smaller if it was simply condensed, shouldn't it? As for 3D: Of course you can imitate 3D effects in a font, just like you can do perspective in a drawing on a flat piece of paper. But as soon as two such letters overlap the effect is gone. The rendering engine does not know which letters you mean to have "on top", so the outlines of your letters "below" will shine through. Which is obviously not the case in the image. (Remember, I wrote "You can have either in a font but not both.")
