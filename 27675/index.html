Jiri Rathousky is not the primary author of Metron. Prof. Petr Tucny showed me dozens of initial sketches from 1968-9 convincing me about his authorship. The drawings are meticulously done on transparent papers, signed and dated. I newer saw something like that in Rathousky’s inheritance. There are also many witnesses confirming the version of Mr. Tucny. We are working on a new, extended story to Metron and solving copyright issues. It’s going slowly, Mr. Tucny has over 80, and I’m working simultaneously on another projects, holidays, etc... Thank you all for patience.
