Anonymous @ 9 Dec 2014 — 6:03am
In addition to the "F" problem... Illustrator allows line segments and stray points to be scattered around, which works fine for most things that most people use Illustrator to do. Making a font, however, means adhering to a few more exacting expectations, like clean, closed contours that define filled shapes. FontLab has a few features that help facilitate clean-up chores under the Contour menu heading. If all your glyphs are riddled with these sorts of issues, though, you might have a job ahead of you that will require a large degree of manual fixing.
