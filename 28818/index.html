Anonymous @ 15 October, 2006 - 9:17pm
You will need thicker strokes on the U’s, C and B. I would also look at a narrower B and broader Fs and Ts. You might also add some overshoot on the curved letters. This might give you some direction for the S middle stroke, think of it as drawing it with a broad nib. [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/britannic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20061127143809/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/britannic/) Tim
