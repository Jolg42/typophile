Excellllent. Some suggestions: [Storm Jannon](http://web.archive.org/web/20141018173239/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/storm/jannon/). Based on an antique French model, excellent update by Frantisek Storm. [Christian Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141018173239/http:/www.typophile.com/node/9245). No, it's not released, but I'd be happy to let you work with a beta version if you felt it was right. [Della Robbia](http://web.archive.org/web/20141018173239/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/della-robbia/). It's based on an Italian model rather than French, but has the right flavor of antiquity and matches the low stroke contrast but sharp features of the card you showed. [Beaufort](http://web.archive.org/web/20141018173239/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/shinn/beaufort/) by Nick Shinn. A beautiful modern update to the same genre as Della Robbia, and by a designer who's still breathing. Most of all, have fun! Go with whatever works best visually with your artwork.
