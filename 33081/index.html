Since I don't read Hebrew I can't attest to the <cite>relative</cite> legibility between the two scripts\*, but the Arabic itself is plenty legible. The only potential problem (which the Hebrew probably has too) is the possible confusion with the circles; you might try making them different - maybe make the Arabic ones diamonds. A bigger problem however is how the logo will look without color, since you're relying on that heavily for legibility. \* Which probably is only of academic interest however.
