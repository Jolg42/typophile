To me, looks like a custom job based on [Handel Gothic](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/urw/handel-gothic/) (/e eye closed, /o redrawn from /d bowl and the all thing slightly extended then slanted). [ITC](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/itc/handel-gothic)] has a version with italics but /a is off. More alternatives: [FF Trademarker](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/fontfont/ff-trademarker), [Domyouji](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/domyouji), [Aspirin Advance](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/t26/aspirin-advance), [Traffic Wide](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/fonts.gestalten.com/category/display/traffic-wide.html), [Hypocrisy](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/shyfoundry/sf-hypocrisy), [Zekton](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/zekton), [Korataki](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/korataki), [Axion RX-14](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typeinnovations/axion-rx-14), [Snasm](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/snasm), [Forgotten Futurist](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/forgotten-futurist), [Vipnagorgialla](http://web.archive.org/web/20141113073536/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/typodermic/vipnagorgialla)
