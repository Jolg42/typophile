If you can get your hands on it, Shadow's [_Live in Austin_](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017203230/http:/www.tunes.co.uk/tunes/featured/6855.html) is an excellent album. There's more turntablism than in his studio albums, but some of the tracks are indicative of his recent touring, where he's been mixing his own stuff. It's incredible to hear. You'll get _Preemptive Strike_ and _Endtroducing_ with _U.N.K.L.E._ mixed together. There's also a lot of his earlier stuff, including the first record he ever made. It's pretty damn brilliant. In fact, he's releasing a DVD, _In Tune and On Time_,(you can see the trailer [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20131017203230/http:/www.djshadow.com/landing.html)) that's got a lot of this stuff in it.
