PRESENT and BY appear to be some form of Bank Gothic (though very hard to tell given the tiny size of the sample; when scanning, focus only on the type you need identified and scan as large as possible): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/bank-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140901231335/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/bank-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/bank-gothic/") My best guess for AUDRA and LANIE is Trade Gothic Condensed: [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/trade-gothic/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140901231335/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/trade-gothic/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/trade-gothic/") The text time is garden variety Times (without a larger size sample, hard to determine whether it's Times, Times New Roman, etc.): [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/apple/times/](http://web.archive.org/web/20140901231335/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/apple/times/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/apple/times/")
