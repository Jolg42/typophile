Suggestions for an alternative to Helvetica Neue

Hi all,

I’m a signage designer (know a little about typefaces but not an expert) doing a favor for a friend in redesigning their very bland name-style, which at the moment is simply the word iLAB set in Helvetica Neue. I was hoping to suggest something to them with a little more style to it - they are after all (lighting) designers. Any suggestions would be most welcome, thanks.
