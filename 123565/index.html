Scala/Scala Sans equivalent with Cyrillic support

At the moment it’s looking like I’m going to have to make a list of all of the humanist faces and then check which have the necessary glyph support – not an especially appealing prospect!

Does anyone have a suggestion of a better way to carry out my search – or possibly even firsthand knowledge of a suitable font?
