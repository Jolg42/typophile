Available under at least 2 names: **[Digital Sans EF](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415214656/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=Elsner%2BFlake%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Digital%20Sans%20EF%20-%20Medium&fid=e03883b0881dd4a00353653e3268a728&fsize=60&text=MegaFileUpload&wrap=2)** by Elsner & Flake [1999] **[Digital Serial (Bold)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415214656/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=SoftMaker%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=DigitalSerial-Bold%20-%20Bold&fid=dea9b07ea9eaf36ed9b5f3be16bf1538&fsize=60&text=MegaFileUpload&wrap=2)** by SoftMaker [1991] The 2nd one is a bit closer to your sample but although is available for purchase from myfonts.com, take into account that it's probably a clone and not an original design as most of SoftMaker's fonts under the '-serial' suffix/trick. **[Sol Pro (Medium)](http://web.archive.org/web/20150415214656/http:/www.findmyfont.com/index.php/fonts/font-preview?fset=CanadaType%20%2F%20%28Commercial%29&ffam=Sol%20Pro%20-%20Medium&fid=a776335d7538528c359ca5c03b045308&fsize=60&text=MegaFileUpload&wrap=2)** by Canada Type [2010] is another similar. There are also at least 2 similar freebies out there (probably illegal clones).
