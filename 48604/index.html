I think this looks like a version of Garamond, but I'm not sure which foundry. As for the ligatures like st and ct, most of the OpenType versions of classic serif families have them and some of the older (PS/TT) families do as well, usually when they are from the major foundries. - Mike Yanega

Anonymous @ 21 Aug 2008 — 3:42am
I'm sure Stephen's list is just a starting point. As I said earlier, most of the Open Type releases, especially of new serif families, will probably include these ligatures. For example Adobe's [Minion Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022062121/http:/store1.adobe.com/type/browser/pdfs/MINP/MinionPro-Regular.pdf), [Garamond Premier Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022062121/http:/store1.adobe.com/type/browser/pdfs/1737.pdf), [Arno Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022062121/http:/store1.adobe.com/type/browser/pdfs/1739.pdf), [Brioso Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022062121/http:/store1.adobe.com/type/browser/pdfs/BIOQ/BriosoPro-Regular.pdf), [Jenson Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022062121/http:/store1.adobe.com/type/browser/pdfs/1716.pdf), [Warnock Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20131022062121/http:/store1.adobe.com/type/browser/pdfs/WARP/WarnockPro-Regular.pdf) and probably most of their other "Pro" releases all have st, ct and sp ligatures (although Garamond Pro has only the ct, as Stephen stated). - Mike Yanega
