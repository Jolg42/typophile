Modern Cyrillic: Type Design Competion by ParaType

Submitted by paul d hunt : 11.Jun.2009 11.46am

The competition is held by ParaType with support by the Federal Agency for Press and Mass Communications of Russia. It’s dedicated to the Tercentenary of Russian Civil Type — Peter the Great’s historic reform of Russian typography (1708–1710).

Cyrillic typeface projects and completed typefaces created and/or released after January 1, 2006 are eligible.
