The legit version is called Revue, which is currently distributed by ITC. They probably got it along with the other Letraset typefaces in their library. It should be available wherever ITC faces are sold, including [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907031819/http:/www.linotype.com/1404/revue-family.html).
