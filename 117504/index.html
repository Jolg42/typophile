Bob's find of Yorkville is as close as I have seen. But, based on a reasonably thorough search, my opinion is that this probably does not exist as a publicly available font. In particular I have not spotted the combination of a vertical leg on the \_R\_ like Helvetica but without the little kick at the bottom, \_G\_ with a rounded lower rhs and no spur like a compressed Futura, \_W\_ with a vertex that is at only about two-thirds of the letter height, and an \_S\_ with extended terminals similar to Helvetica. Plus the the base of \_A\_ is as wide as \_O\_. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140701152436im_/http:/typophile.com/files/UnknownSansTypophile117504D_6116.jpg) Futurvetica anyone?
