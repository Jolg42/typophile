New fonts from TypeTogether

TypeTogether is proud to announce the recent release of two new font families:

Cora, designed by Bart Blubaugh, is a sans serif with an experimental bent, offering a large x-height, some contrast of stroke weight, and capitals inspired by classical lettering. It has 6 weights, Regular, Medium, Bold and the accompanying Italics.
Bree, designed by Veronika Burian and José Scaglione, is a fluid upright italic with a set of alternate letters when a more classical look is desired. It is based on TypeTogether’s logotype and contains 5 weights, Thin, Light, Regular, Bold and Extrabold.
