Type Story on CBS Sunday Morning

Did anyone else catch the short item on CBS Sunday Morning yesterday about type? It was a few minutes long and featured interviews of Steven Heller and Jonathan Hoefler. It was predictably shallow, but fairly well informed. It reminded me a bit of the famous Erik Speikermann " Typomania" video (but without the Tom Tom Club music).

I don’t normally watch this program, but my parents do and thoughtfully TIVOed it for me so I could see it. I checked the CBS website and they don’t have the item viewable online.
