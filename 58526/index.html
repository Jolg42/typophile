Paul, I believe a musical notation font is what Dot said they don't need. This is interesting. Frode is right but it's not quite un-tricky. Many Bodonis/Didots don't have the soft/"scripty" instrokes in the italics; but then [some](http://web.archive.org/web/20150306084653/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/baskerville/) [Baskervilles](http://web.archive.org/web/20150306084653/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/emigre/mrs-eaves/bold-italic/waterfall.html), which would seem like good matches otherwise, have the semi-ascending "p". Here are a few faces whose bold italic cuts look like they might work: [Scotch Modern](http://web.archive.org/web/20150306084653/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/shinn/scotch-modern/) [Century 731](http://web.archive.org/web/20150306084653/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/century-731/) [Berthold Bodoni Old Face BQ](http://web.archive.org/web/20150306084653/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/berthold/bodoni-old-face-bq/) (quirky "p") Also nice: [Teimer](http://web.archive.org/web/20150306084653/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/suitcase/teimer/) (no foot serif on the "p")
