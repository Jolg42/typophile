Larger image: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150304194517im_/http:/typophile.com/files/WWF_6078.jpg) Not Clarice Black and probably a custom design. The base letters may have been derived from a font like Radiant Black [http://www.myfonts.com/search/radiant/fonts/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150304194517/http:/www.myfonts.com/search/radiant/fonts/ "http://www.myfonts.com/search/radiant/fonts/") or Castle Ultra [http://www.identifont.com/show?2MG](http://web.archive.org/web/20150304194517/http:/www.identifont.com/show?2MG "http://www.identifont.com/show?2MG") using overlapping Vs to make a W with the center strokes meeting in a "T" to the left, and rounding the corners of the Ws and F to make soft and "friendly" letters. Hi damn1990, Definitely not Clarice Black and Don's probably right about it being a custom logotype (registered ®), but pretty simple though - all straight lines and circles.
