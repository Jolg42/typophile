Anonymous @ 6 Jul 2004 — 4:24am
I don't think it's based on any particular face such as Futura. You can see bits of Erbar, Bernhard Gothic, Kabel, etc., in it, plus some unique features. No Gill. There were a lot of "mash-ups" in that era, as different type designers from different foundries from different countries did their own version of the look du jour, the geometric sans. It would be like saying that all present day sans serif faces that are slightly squarish, semi-condensed and open-fitted, with a 4-letter name, are versions of Meta, forgetting other sources like Quay Sans, and the general lettering aesthetic of the day.
