Fountain Releases Three New Typefaces

Submitted by Joe Pemberton : 9.Jul.2003 4.01pm

The Fountain catalogue has been updated with the 3 new type families:

Alita - a text face placed somewhere between renaissance and transitional Antiqua, designed by Peter Hoffmann. [http://www.fountain.nu/catalogue/alita.asp

Paracelsus - a modern take on the old gothic type form called Schwabach, designed by Lars Bergquist. [http://www.fountain.nu/catalogue/paracelsus.asp ]

Waldstein - a Scotch typeface named after young Beethoven’s mecenat, this is also designed by Lars Bergquist. [http://www.fountain.nu/catalogue/waldstein.asp

We have added a new section to the site called - “Visual Overview” - where you can take a fast peek on our whole collection of typefaces.

The second release will be next week when we release 2 new fonts by Martin Fredrikson, plus an update of some of his earlier typefaces.

Peter Bruhn, principal designer & owner

http://www.fountain.nu/
