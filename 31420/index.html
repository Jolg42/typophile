Because identical letters are not identical it points to a hand-drawn logo, or something hand-customized. Similar fonts could be [Mason Serif (or Sans)](http://web.archive.org/web/20140513182346/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=verity.search&font_categories_search=font_name_search&searchstr=Mason&image.x=18&image.y=8) from Emigre. Another called ['Blackfriar'](http://web.archive.org/web/20140513182346/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontdetail&displayfontid=C.100732.0.0) actually has horns on the 'g'. For some reason [Percolator](http://web.archive.org/web/20140513182346/http:/www.fontshop.com/?fuseaction=catalog.fontpackage&searchby=manufacturer&displayfontid=C.100897.0.0) seems to show up in a lot of devil-ish contexts, maybe because it 'perverts' normal letters? Another font with somewhat arcane letterforms is Font Bureau's [Saber](http://web.archive.org/web/20140513182346/http:/www.fontshop.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=catalog.fontdetail&displayfontid=FB.111998.0.0). There are many others that just need some imagination and creepy shading to look sinister. - Mike Yanega
