The reason Flash became a quasi-standard back then was not because Macromedia had a monopoly (which it didn't, with Adobe always being the bigger player), but because it was the decisively superior technology at the time (over competitions like SVG, which Adobe was trying to back, though in the most pathetic manner imaginable).
