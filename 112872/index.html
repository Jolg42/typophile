An almost identical A -- with the angled cross stroke -- is found in F. W. Goudy's National Old Style and Nabisco fonts of 1916 and 1921. National: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432im_/http:/typophile.com/files/GoudyHalfCentOfTypeV1p99NationalOSCharset_6105.jpg) Nabisco: ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432im_/http:/typophile.com/files/GoudyHalfCentOfTypeV1p119Nabisco_5013.jpg) National Old Style is available as a "prop font," i.e. an unedited autotrace from HPLHS [A Lovecraft fan and gaming group] [http://www.cthulhulives.org/toybox/propdocs/propfonts.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432/http:/www.cthulhulives.org/toybox/propdocs/propfonts.html "http://www.cthulhulives.org/toybox/propdocs/propfonts.html") National is shown in detail at pages 300 to 305 of the ATF Catalog for 1923. Luc Devroye has an informative page [http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-26299.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432/http:/luc.devroye.org/fonts-26299.html "http://luc.devroye.org/fonts-26299.html") The distinctive leg on the R is very similar to the one on Nicolas Jenson’s Roman. See [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/jenson/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/jenson/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/adobe/jenson/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/nicolas-jenson-sg/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/nicolas-jenson-sg/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/spiecegraphics/nicolas-jenson-sg/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/jenson-classico/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/jenson-classico/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/jenson-classico/") [http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/jenson-classico/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150404010432/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/jenson-classico/ "http://www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/jenson-classico/") Don
