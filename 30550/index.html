Personal requests to Yves Peters (Bald Condensed, Head Moderator Type ID Board)

Moderating this board is lots of work — mostly behind the scenes — and eats up large amounts of my own time. Remember this is a voluntary job. I don’t get paid for this, and time spent here is time I can’t devote to my family, writing for Unzipped or Typographer.org or other websites, designing for my personal clients, rehearsing and gigging with my band Troubleman or — hey — relaxing.

I don’t mind doing this, make no mistake. Fact is, I even like it. But if you want a typeface identified or need advice or information, please don’t send me a personal e-mail. Post it here, that’s what this board is all about.

Thank you. ;^)
