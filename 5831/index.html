Anonymous @ 30 Jul 2004 — 10:47am
Well, "NET WT. 1.76 OZ" is [Goudy Oldstyle](http://web.archive.org/web/20130111093436/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/goudy/old-style/testdrive.html?s=NET%2BWT.%2B1.76%2BOZ&p=48), but beyond that, I think most of the package is hand-lettered -- "ALTOIDS" is almost certainly custom lettering. "CURIOUSLY STRONG PEPPERMINTS" is definitely hand-lettered (three different P's, two different S's, probably more inconsistencies than that), but Letterhead's [LHF Full Block](http://web.archive.org/web/20130111093436/http:/www.letterheadfonts.com/downloads/index.shtml) is a similar style, and it's free! "The Original Celebrated" is also probably hand-lettered -- the face that this reminds me the most of is [Windsor](http://web.archive.org/web/20130111093436/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/windsor/)... if you tweaked one of the weights of Windsor and added some strokes, you could probably get very close. "MADE IN BRITAIN", of course, is Helvetica Medium, maybe Bold. =)
