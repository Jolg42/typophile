I call that the Ribbon Device, as does a major soft drink company, I believe. TF22Raptor, you need to practice a bit more at critically assessing a piece of design and what it achieves. You don't have to have a name for everything you do, but you will need a good reason justifying it to the client and a clear explanation of how it helps them achieve their stated goals.
