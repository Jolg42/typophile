Demolition in Doosan borchure.

This is a generic sans – I don’t care which one – with special treatment to represent demolition.
I find the restrained treatment to be more effective than the customary extreme defacing of a font.
I got it from a Doosan heavy equipment brochure. It also showed more words with the same treatment, which I did not get.
I would appreciated any pointers as to the font name.
I know it’s not the one in a previous Demolition thread:
http://typophile.com/node/84703

This one is very close if not matching to [Uptown](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/uptown.font) by Anthem Type. More in the same vein: [Impacted](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/impacted.font), [Rock's Death](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/rocks-death.font), [Raw](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/device/raw), [You Are Loved](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/you-are-loved.font), [Mr Black](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/hipopotam/mr-black), [Dead Kansas](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/dead-kansas.font), [Letterpress](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/facetype/letterpress) Headline, [Dirty Bag](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/dirtybag.font) Having a look to some sections at DaFont could be helpful too, full of eroded post-treatment, most of time very well executed: [Fancy \> Eroded](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=107), [Fancy \> Destroy](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.dafont.com/theme.php?cat=109). Best one so far for your 2 requests is [Rust Bucket BA](http://web.archive.org/web/20140405003056/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bagraphics/rust-bucket) by Bob Alonso.
