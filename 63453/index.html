Anonymous @ 24 Oct 2009 — 3:19pm
What it matches exactly is the alternate ampersand of Pistilli Roman (a film type shown in the 1985 Phil's Photo book called "Homage to the Alphabet"). The closest digital font I know to Pistilli Roman is [Carousel](http://web.archive.org/web/20131018094951/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/carousel-lt/regular/characters.html), however Carousel does not have this alternate ampersand. It also has differences mainly in the lower case (e, g, k, v, w, x, y, z). Uppercase K and Q are different. - Mike Yanega
