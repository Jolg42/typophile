It’s almost identical to a script called ’Hemlock’ that was one of the film type brush scripts (many are shown in the Solotype Catalog). I think it was digitized as ’Gideion’ in the Expresiv Brush Script Collection. A German company called Typo Bach is listed as a supplier in the Script Types book I found the font in. The only difference I see is that the ’i’ has no serif in Hemlock. - Mike Yanega

I went and hunted through the Photo-Lettering catalogs, and they call this Adpata Script. They don’t always credit the designers, but Ed Benguiat was one of their lettering artists. Frank Bartuska was another whose scripts look very similar to Benguiat’s. The ’Hemlock’ name is, I suspect, a Solotype re-naming. since he seemed to group these scripts in alphabetic clusters, with H and J for brush script styles. I suppose Hemlock is as good an answer as any. - Mike Yanega
