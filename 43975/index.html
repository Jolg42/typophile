For the serif: [Dederon](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.suitcasetype.com/index.php?lmut=en&part=fonts&font=9) is similar to Dolly and has a lot of characters, but still, it unfortunately doesn’t support cyrillic. [Fedra Serif](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.typotheque.com/site/fonts.php?id=6) does, but it isn’t very similar. Just sharing a little bit of Dutch flavour.
Anonymous @
More not-so-usual serif typefaces with cyrillic characterset: [Anselm Serif](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.stormtype.com/typefaces-fonts-shop/families-86-anselm-serif-pro), [Quadraat](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_quadraat_cyrillic/), [Swift](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.paratype.com/pstore/default.asp?fcode=PT_SWF), [Raleigh](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.paratype.com/pstore/default.asp?fcode=PT_RLG), [Warnock](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.adobe.com/type/browser/P/P_1709.html), [Octava](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.paratype.com/pstore/default.asp?fcode=PT_OCT), [Maiola](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/downloads/fontfont/ff_maiola_cyrillic/). For the techy one: [Etelka](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.stormtype.com/typefaces-fonts-shop/families-15-etelka), maybe? [Consolas](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ascender/consolas/), [OCR One](http://web.archive.org/web/20100629171130/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/paratype/ocr_one_regular_cyrillic/)?
