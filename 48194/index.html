Anonymous @ 7 Aug 2008 — 9:50pm
some fonts, such as Bickham Script, doesn't work well because it has delicate pothooks that connect on both right and left side of a glyph. Radio, from Magnus Rakeng, has a thicker, right-side-only pothook that joins at the stem. So Radio seems to work fairly well. ( [www.vllg.com](http://web.archive.org/web/20130329131417/http:/www.vllg.com/ "www.vllg.com")) It can never hurt to try some lettering — gives you the advantage of customizing each particular letter combination. best of luck.
