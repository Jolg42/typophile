University of the Arts London

Indices : Education : The University of the Arts London

The University of the Arts London was until recently called the London Institute.

It consists of five colleges:
London College of Communication (formerly the London College of Printing)
Camberwell Collge of Arts
Central Saint Martins College of Art and Design
Chelsea College of Art and Design
London College of Fashion
