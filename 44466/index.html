There are some basic typographic issues I found with your new design. For example: I found few Widows in your text. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821154154im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/a_5261.jpg) Your text blocks has some poor rags as well. These creates disturbing white shapes on the margins. Turning off the Hyphenation is not enough, you need some manual lines breaks and the paragraph returns where needed. ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20080821154154im_/http:/www.typophile.com/files/B_6695.jpg) Have you followed any grid in your layout?
