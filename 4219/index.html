you should be commended for doing something from scratch. ![:-)](http://web.archive.org/web/20131108143137im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/clipart/happy.gif) i'd second Scott's suggestion for your curves. Maybe the left side of the 't' crossbar could be reined in a bit. also, in context, the m feels a bit wide. the n + n usually needs to be narrowed.
