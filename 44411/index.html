Non-Extractable Outlines in PDF

Is there a way to create a PDF and not have the outlines extractable? I’m not too worried about it because extracting and composing back into a workable font would be alot of work, I’m just curious about this, I’m sure it’s been discussed before.

The rule of thumb, actually quite logical one is: if the computer can take some vectors and turn them into bitmaps so they can be shown on screen, then there are some vectors there. If they are there, they can be transformed into other vector forms. It’s a question of cost and effort. As far as I know, there still is no “out of the box” software for exctracting fonts out of PDF files. There are some semi-manual methods but they’re good for kids who have lots of spare time on their hands, and will end up with incomplete character sets and zero kerning tables anyway. A.
