The SacredNipple, Inc. is a South African based digital type foundry. Founded in 1998, by Brode Vosloo and Lyall Coburn, it is the only South African type foundry to make a concious effort to promote the splendours of african design and typography. The SacredNipple, Inc. holds exclusive license to over 40 original typefaces designs created by a selection of some of South Africa's brightest type designers.
