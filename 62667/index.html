Ex-Hallmark artist Rob Leuschke is a source of script styles that at times look like some of the Sudtipos collection. His [Qwigley](http://web.archive.org/web/20120415203813/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/typesetit/qwigley-rob/) is somewhat like your sample. He does a lot of custom work, and this might be one of his. - Mike Yanega
