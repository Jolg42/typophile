It's by Hideakai Otani, a web/graphic designer from Tokyo. The target audience seems to be graphic designers -- MdN is the publisher of a popular Japanese graphic design magazine. It has 128 pages, half in color. To me it looks great. Contents Visual prologue - Essay on Helvetica typography by Hiroshi (?) Sato, based on material from Haas, with designs by the author. 1. Information about Miedinger and Linotype's Helvetica. 2. History of sans serif. Features a digital redrawing of Haas Unica by the author. 3. Comparison of Helvetica, Univers, and Akzidenz Grotesk. 4. Tips and tricks from Japanese designers. The author also designs fonts. One of them is [here](http://web.archive.org/web/20120228125810/http:/fontgraphic.jp/2004/pdf/fgm_1.pdf). I like the [at] mark. The person who was asking recently about 3-sided [at] marks might want to look at it.
