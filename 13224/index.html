Dot accent is a quite common diacritical mark in some European languages. It is used in Maltese (over ‘c’, ‘g’, ‘z’), Lithuanian (over ‘e’), Polish (over ‘z’) and Turkish (they distinct between i/İ — ı/I — i with and without dot). And actually, the dot as a diacritical mark can be found in every language using Latin script over ‘i’. More at [Project Diacritics](http://web.archive.org/web/20050624004857/http:/diacritics.typo.cz/index.php?id=15)

Catalan (u/c & l/c I and U for diaeresis and u/c & l/c L for dot) and Turkish (u/c & l/c O and U for diaeresis and u/c I for dot) use both but not on the same character and Hungarian uses the diaeresis (umlaut) and double acute (Hungarian umlaut). Macron seems to be Lithuanian and Latvian also used for transliteration of Arabic. Tim
