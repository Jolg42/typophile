[Miller](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019003856/http:/fontbureau.com/fonts/Miller) is the obvious print analogue to Georgia. [Lucida](http://web.archive.org/web/20131019003856/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/ef/lucida-sans/) started life as a print face so it can do double duty, onscreen and off.
