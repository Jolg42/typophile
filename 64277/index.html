That is Chicago Modern, Alf Beckers 44th demonstration lettering from _Signs of the Times_ magazine from August of 1935. It is available in digital format from MyFonts at... [http://new.myfonts.com/fonts/fontry/arb-44-chicago-modern/](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414234005/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/fontry/arb-44-chicago-modern/) or [_...from the Fontry_](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414234005/http:/www.thefontry.com/)

Apparently not the first time someone has digitized this design. Steve Jackaman claims he saw an Adrian Williams typeface like this. If so, Adrian must have found inspiration in Alf Becker's book. See Red Rooster's [Saint Louis](http://web.archive.org/web/20150414234005/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/redrooster/saint-louis-rr/). I notice the 'a' is a different style, but otherwise the resemblance is startling. - Mike Yanega
