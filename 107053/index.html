No problem. This kind of fake is easy to do actually (using Chrome and a PC at least). Find [a tweet](http://web.archive.org/web/20150317132522/http:/typophile.com/wiki/https%3A/%252Ftwitter.com/SergioRamos/status/394207206339796992), right click it, inspect element, double-click element on elements panel and replace with your words.
