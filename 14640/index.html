Hey Forrest, I am sorry to say that it doesn’t work for me. The type is somewhere near okay (the lowercase e looks smallish, though), but the twig just doesn’t have the right feel. Too literal, in my opinion. I think the rather straight shape of the words collides with the loose treatment of the twig. The two should be a unity. The twig refers to the organic, but I think some reference to jewelry might be better. Keep up the good work! —————————————————————————— My work is a game. A very serious game [M.C. Escher]
