Yes, I would say that is the right category, but there is no match I could find at Dafont, and none I could locate at MyFonts. This pushes me towards the idea that this is an amateur font that is perhaps a custom font made by the 'artist' who did the lettering. In case you cannot find the original font, [Klang](http://web.archive.org/web/20130416020917/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/klang/mt/) is pretty similar, and could be backslanted a bit to make it more vertical. - Mike Yanega
Anonymous @ 10 Aug 2009 — 8:37am
If you want some choices that are even less formal than Klang, Robert Schenk of Ingrimayne Type has some hand-lettered pen fonts (at MyFonts): Gretchen Hello, Xahosch, Xaltid, Balboat and Hermainita. The last one is perhaps the neatest lettering, and most professional looking. The others are less formal. - Mike Yanega
