Hello PDM, welcome to Typophile. I recommend you reading the 2-epsiode introduction to the [work of a typedesigner](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/www.fontshop.be/details.php?entry=230) by [Yves Peters](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Yves%252BPeters), and [The Value of Type](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/www.letterror.com/content/value/index.html) by [Erik van Blokland](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Erik%252Bvan%252BBlokland). Furthermore, have a look at the [FAQ Free](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/typophile.com/wiki/FAQ%252BFree). Microsoft Windows comes with a handsome sans-serif font family by [Joshua Darden](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Joshua%252BDarden), the designer of Freight. [Corbel](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/typophile.com/wiki/Corbel) has four weights, very wide language support (including Cyrillic and Greek), small-caps, various figure styles and more. Note that Corbel is [not a free font](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/typophile.com/node/38272). BTW, here’s a link to [Freight Sans](http://web.archive.org/web/20090511044240/http:/www.dardenstudio.com/typefaces/freight_sans). F
