Fonts from the Newspaper

I am designing a mini-book that i will be typesetting my senior paper into, which is about crossword puzzles, and I am really struggling with the typeface choice. I am currently using Ehrhardt but it seems too heavy on the grey paper I am printing on. I think i started with Century, which again was just too heavy. I don’t quite want to go with the obvious Times New Roman. Does anyone have any suggestions on faces the would lend the “newspaper” look to my mini-book?
