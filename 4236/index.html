Nope. There's [Helvetica Extended](http://web.archive.org/web/20140802053753/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/linotype/neue-helvetica/helvetica-53-extended/) or [Berthold Akzidenz-](http://web.archive.org/web/20140802053753/http:/www.philsfonts.com/showing.html?sku=BQ900380X1P1&start=1) [Grotesk Extended](http://web.archive.org/web/20140802053753/http:/www.philsfonts.com/showing.html?sku=BQ900380X1P1&start=1), but no Arial Extended. I once suggested it to David Saunders way back in 1993 who was then with the Monotype Drawing Office (before the merger with Agfa). I literally told him "Why not make an extended version of Arial? The world can use some more extended faces." That was shortly after I had found out there were only very few "wide" faces in the FontBook. They didn't follow the advice of a one-year-in novice at FontShop Belgium, can you imagine that?! ![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140802053753im_/http:/www.typophile.com/forums/clipart/bigsmile.gif) Nonono, I'm not pulling yer leg, this is a true story, I kid you not. This is _SO_ funny you bring this up after all those years.
