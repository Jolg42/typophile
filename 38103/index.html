<cite>by publishing date and/or some kind of regional influence</cite> Are you talking about [Font Explorer](http://web.archive.org/web/20071220030548/http:/www.linotype.com/2393/fontexplorer.html) (FE)? Not to be confused with the rather new FontExplorer X (FEX)! The former can <cite>[f]ind fonts fitting each historical era</cite> and <cite>[s]elect a mix of emotions and impressions and find the fonts that suit them all</cite>. Yet, I don’t think this app is suited for _managing_ fonts. For that, the latter is the right thing – however the Windows version of that is currently not available due to revision. There’s a [FAQ on the Best Font Management](http://web.archive.org/web/20071220030548/http:/typophile.com/node/15290). Though there are tags like ‘designer’ or ‘description’ in FEX, you have to do the sorting yourself, I’m afraid.
