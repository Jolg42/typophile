I think the Hudson's Bay Company logo and the lettering in the banner are custom lettering. The main logo is a simplified blackletter script, somewhat like [Cloister Black](http://web.archive.org/web/20110514170304/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/bitstream/cloister-black/) or [Agincourt](http://web.archive.org/web/20110514170304/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/letraset/agincourt/), but it doesn't match any blackletter font I could find. I think the sans serif lettering at the bottom of the sign looks like Helvetica. - Mike Yanega
