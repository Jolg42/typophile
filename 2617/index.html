Anonymous @ Thu, 2004-01-08 05:35
Kristin, Have you looked at my [Sans Serif Font Identification Guide](http://web.archive.org/web/20050516141942/http:/bowfinprintworks.com/SansIDPage.html)? There are now over 750 families classified by letterform features of 7 key letters (a, e, g, G, M, R and y). I will leave the genre classification to others (Grotesk/Grotesque, and such terms are too vague and often misused) and try to use a scheme that doesn't depend on such judgments. Look at how much difficulty experts in this group are having agreeing about which are which. In my opinion a classification scheme should be independent of such labeling attempts, because there are too many 'grey' areas in the digital era fonts.

FF Super Grotesk is a lovely typeface, in fact it's one of my faves in its genre. That said (and as if to prove Mike Yanega's assertion that type classification is entirely in the eye of the beholder) Super Grotesk is essentially Futura (Super Grotesk A) with an alternate three-storey g (Super Grotesk B) and a full set of ligatures (Super Grotesk C), so for me it's basically a geometric face with extra features, but without any obvious connection to classic Realist grotesques such as Akzidenz Grotesk.
