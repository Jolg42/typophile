![](http://web.archive.org/web/20140803140100im_/http:/typophile.com/files/PeignotDemiModified_4826.jpg) Here are your letters in the lower case of Peignot Demi -- a font designed in 1937 by A.M. Cassandre with Charles Peignot for Deberny & Peignot -- with the ascenders on l, k and h clipped to the x height; the base stroke on the l extended to match the base stroke on the e; the spur on the g raised to the baseline; and all letters widened 120%. Cassandre's "space age" design would do the job. Don
