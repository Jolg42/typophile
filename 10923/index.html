Roxy Fonts : a brand new foundry from Mexico

After almost one year in the making, Roxy Fonts, the first mexican digital type foundry is here with a monthly offering of six new fonts designed in and with love by Victor Yb Delgado.

For our inaugural issue of March 2002 we have selected some of our most original work : the elongated but strange elegance of Moneyca Boulevard, a huggy bear feeling with Polara, the fun and rhythm of Kofika, plus the sheer novelty of Zarza and Exboy families.

And don’t miss our free download of the month, Weirdiac, a manic drugressive dingbat font!

Roxy Fonts is an indie space where words like repetitive, derivative and, most of all, expensive are never spoken.

…a little type corner you will definitely want to bookmark.

Roxy Fonts, for the love of type
