Stephen — Clarification: The going rate for FB fonts is $40 per weight _for singles_ and _through resellers_. Font Bureau’s own direct Retail site gives quantity discounts. So at 35 styles the per font cost goes down to $35 (thus the $175 rate quoted above), and 6 or more styles drop to $30 per. Further discounts above 16 styles. These are the single workstation rates, and styles may be mixed across families. (More info at [http://www.fontbureau.com/help/quickpricingchart](http://web.archive.org/web/20080907014803/http:/www.fontbureau.com/help/quickpricingchart "http://www.fontbureau.com/help/quickpricingchart")) — K.
