Kia ora!
KLIM is a typographic design studio run by Kris Sowersby. The KLIM studio has been set up to deal specifically with typographic issues within the realm of graphic design. If you need something that is typographic by nature, then I am at your service.

A thing well made…
…is a song by legendary New Zealand band The Mutton Birds. ‘When a man holds a thing well made, there is a connection, there is completeness…’ This philosophy is at the very heart of my approach to typographic design—for art is long and life is short.
