Hi Jimmy, most likely, these are not fonts, but hand-rendered letters. For a digital font similar to ‘INVEST’, check out [Prenton Ultracondensed Bold](http://web.archive.org/web/20090330100043/http:/new.myfonts.com/fonts/blhd/prenton-rp/ultra-cond-bold/) by [Roy Preston](http://web.archive.org/web/20090330100043/http:/typophile.com/cms/wiki/Roy%252BPreston) – it has that open ‘S’ with vertical terminals. Also: [Gill Sans Bold Condensed](http://web.archive.org/web/20090330100043/http:/www.fontshop.com/fonts/singles/monotype/gill_sans_adobe_std_bold_condensed/&sample_text=INVEST). F
