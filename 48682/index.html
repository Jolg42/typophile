Typophile Film Fest 4, St Petersburg

Submitted by Joe Pemberton : 21.Aug.2008 11.30pm

Punchcut presents the Typophile Film Fest 4 at ATypI ’08 St. Petersburg. The Film Fest is an unequaled selection of short typographic films hailing from designers in Portugal, The Netherlands, Austria, Canada and the US. The films are a mashup of motion design, typographic animation and short stories including broadcast motion design, documentaries and typographic eye candy from Trollbäck + Company, Strange Attractors, Heebok Lee, Nick Shinn, Juan Leguizamon as well as exclusive interviews and cuts from the acclaimed documentary film Helvetica.

THANK YOU FONTSHOP

Typophile owes its sincere gratitude to FontShop for making this presentation at ATypI possible. Thanks also to ATypI for including the Film Fest in the 2008 conference programme.

ABOUT ATypI
ATypI, Association Typographique Internationale, is a global forum and focal point for the type community and business. ATypI’s annual conference draws hundreds of worldwide participants. // http://www.atypi.org

ABOUT TYPOPHILE
Typophile is an international collaborative community of graphic designers, typographers and type designers. Typophile is driven by sharing and the idea that we’re always learning. Now in its eighth year, it is one of the oldest design communities online. Typophile is a Punchcut brand. // http://www.typophile.com


ABOUT FONTSHOP
Erik Spiekermann, Joan Spiekermann, and Neville Brody founded FSI FontShop International in 1990 in Berlin, Germany. FontShop is the original independent font retailer, carrying over 100,000 fonts from more than 50 international type foundries, including FontFont, FSI’s own boutique label of innovative fonts for designers. // http://www.fontshop.com


ABOUT PUNCHCUT
Punchcut is a US-based user interface design and development company that specializes in improving device user experiences. Punchcut works with device manufacturers, operators and leading entertainment brands to design for people in motion. // http://www.punchcut.com
