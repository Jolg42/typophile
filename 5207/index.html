Peter, I applaud three things: 1, The caps are a good height - shorter than ascenders. Too often designers of pixel fonts forget what's been learned from traditional typography. 2, The extensive character set. I suppose we should expect a good number of accented glyphs from a Swede. ;) 3, The uc 'W'. It's nice. It's great to see you here. I admire your work and often point to Mercury's little 'g' when people ask why I love typography so much. Welcome. Stephen

I like this a lot. To echo Stephen, the UC height is great. The numerals are nice too. The 'serifs' on the lc f and l as well as the UC I and J are a nice touch. The lc r is also nice.
