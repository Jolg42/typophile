You nailed it, it s the exact font, the A and R are actually lower cases. The old free Bitsumishi is also usable, the font was stretched horizontaly + 162% for that logo . Second line (BIOSYSTEMS) seems to be Helvetica LT Std Roman with tracking at +600 and horizontal stretch similar to the bitsumishi (Which show that Helvetica is really everywhere....)
