You could try Monotype's [eText version of Amasis](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416024653/http:/www.fonts.com/font/monotype/amasis-etext). [Giza RE](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416024653/http:/www.webtype.com/font/gizare-family/) and [Ibis RE](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416024653/http:/www.webtype.com/font/ibisre-family/) from Font Bureau's <cite>Reading Edge</cite> collection could work very well. Nick Shinn's [Bodoni Egyptian Pro](http://web.archive.org/web/20150416024653/http:/www.myfonts.com/fonts/shinn/bodoni-egyptian-pro/webfont_preview.html) also looks really good at small sizes.
